# The second reading of the rows that were to be raised

Nineteen rulings passed the five tests on the first reading. One more reader
then took those nineteen without the first reading, its blocks or the list of
what had been raised, in a worktree cut at the commit before any row was
raised, and wrote a block per row as the first readers had. The classes are the
same three, and a row that is his in one part and not in another is `split`.

Each block has one more line than a first reader's, `facts:`, which answers
five questions of the row and gives the file and lines for each yes. Whether
his words on the claim carry a hedge of his own is `hedged`. Whether the row
states a general rule where he answered one question is `generalised`. Whether
it is an instruction for one stretch of the run is `one-occasion`. Whether the
row or the file around the passage marks his words as an acknowledgement and
not a ruling is `ack`, and this reader also put under it the places where he
says a statement is intent and not an absolute. Whether a later row of his
corrects or replaces it is `corrected`.

The blocks are the reader's own and are kept as it wrote them. A path
beginning `op-responses/` is a capture in the workspace's store of his
responses, which is outside this repository. `NOTES.md` and `PROVISIONAL.md`
beside this file say what was done with the result.
