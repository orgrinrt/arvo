## 3. The steps

One helper each. Every step is a mockspace design round on its branch, pulls `dev` first, lands on green. Steps that overhaul `arvo-numeral` run one after another; N6, N9 and P0 touch other crates and can run beside them. Common proving checks, not repeated below: each operation equal to the oracle, exhaustive at widths 1 to 8 through the value and 1 to 12 through each rung, an edge lattice and a committed seed above that; one mutant per rung per operation with its red output committed; a compile-fail twin for each refusal; the outside consumer crate with no feature line reaching every new public item.

P0. `work/claim-the-value-rows`. Registry only. Two cold reads, sequential, each from a worktree cut before the other's commit, then the coordinator's gate (`ruling.toml:1392`). Subjects: `a_value_is_the_sole_occupancy_placement_of_a_declared_signature` (one cold read so far, per `ruling.toml:1852`), `a_value_admits_a_constant_quantum_a_zero_phase_and_a_deterministic_mode`, `the_debug_check_is_a_parameter_the_consumer_names`, the three exact-operation rows, and the cells of `Hot`, `Cold`, `Precise` (each FIXME in `arvo-strategy/src/lib.rs`). Briefs name the question and withhold the proposals' wording. Needs nothing. Tier: opus medium per seat.

N1. `work/numeral-ring`. Overhauls `arvo-numeral`; adds `points::IFixed` to `arvo-format`. `sub`, `neg`, `mul` on `Num` as inherent `const fn`, each the format's own (`arvo_format::sub`, `neg`, `mul`), with verdicts `fits_sub`, `fits_neg`, `fits_mul` asked beside the total operation under `DebugCheck`. The product takes the double-width intermediate per rung (128-bit at the 64-bit rung) and applies the signature's rounding, so every one of the five deterministic modes is exercised. `Identity<Multiplicative>` computed from the format's predicate, refused as a failed constant where one is not a member. Aliases `Fixed`, `Signed` over `UFixed` and `IFixed`. Checks: the common set per operation; `UFixed<6,3>` and `IFixed<6,3>` exhaustive for the product in all five modes, both policies; a tie case per mode at a negative product; the two associativity laws for the product (`law.toml:84`, `:114`) reproduced with the registry's counts. Needs nothing. Tier: opus high.

N2. `work/numeral-contracts`. Adds to `arvo-numeral`. First a committed sketch with three arms: plain traits beside the inherent `const fn`s; `const trait` behind an opt-in feature with the plain traits still present; `const trait` only. Outcome per arm judged on one thing: does a consumer with no feature line and no unstable gate build a function generic over "a numeral that adds, orders and has an additive identity", and does its code equal the concrete one in disassembly. Then the contracts, each a trait whose law is the oracle equality, `Num` their instance, and one outside instance in the consumer crate to show the set open. Covers family 8's names (sum, difference, product, order, identity, sign predicates, absolute value, constant construction); reciprocal and square root are named as contracts with no instance until N4 and floats. Checks: a law suite generic over the contract, run against `Num` and against a deliberately wrong outside instance that must fail it. Needs N1. Tier: fable low for the contract shaping, opus medium for the build.

N3. `work/strategy-names-an-adaptation`. Adds to `arvo-strategy`; moves `NamesAdaptation` to the lowest crate that reaches its types (it returns an `Adaptation`, which is `arvo-format`'s). Each preset whose cell is claimed implements it by its own `Strategy::Adaptation`, one impl per marker (a blanket over `S: Strategy` is an orphan), so `Uint<8, Hot>` is a legal spelling. `Warm` gets no impl and the aliases no default until his answer; the step leaves a one-line change for it and a compile-fail test that `Uint<8>` is refused today. It also states, with a sketch, the two homes the default can take: `arvo-numeral` taking an edge onto `arvo-strategy` (which changes `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl:70-72`), or a crate above both that owns the defaulted aliases. The second is the public-name question and goes to him at the port. Checks: a test-only strategy naming each adaptation, the consumer source of sketch `202611130550-m67x_an-alias-that-takes-a-strategy-later` built unchanged before and after. Needs P0 for the three cells. Tier: sonnet high.

N4. `work/numeral-division`. Overhauls `arvo-numeral`. `div` over a divisor that cannot be zero by type (`arvo_format::Divisor`, `proposal-the-exact-operations.toml:18`), the remainder and Euclidean pair, even shares, and `convert` between two admitted signatures, all equal to the landed oracle (`div`, `quotient_position`, `convert`). Checks: the common set; every divisor at widths 1 to 8; conversion exhaustive across every pair of widths 1 to 6 and both slot families; compile-fail for a zero constant divisor. Builds on two one-expert rows and says so in its design, as `Num` did. Needs N1. Tier: opus high.

N5. `work/numeral-lanes`. Adds to `arvo-numeral`. `Lane` aliases: fixed point under a named rounding and policy, half to even on the product first (`obligation.toml:209`). The exact accumulator: `Acc<S, const TERMS>` built from a value, exact `add` and `sub`, back to a value only through the signature's adaptation. Its register is the narrowest that satisfies `W + ceil(log2 TERMS) <= width`, the interior-safety predicate the committed `warm-clamp-arity` family lands on to the row (`20_fog...md:208-212`), and a fold over `[Num<S>; N]` is refused at build when `N` exceeds `TERMS`. Checks: accumulate-then-adapt equals the oracle's exact sum adapted once, exhaustive for every triple at widths 1 to 6 and sampled to `TERMS = 256`; the composed saturating add and subtract of `ruling.toml:1772` reproduced through values (13,882,880 of 16,777,216) and shown absent through the accumulator; a bench arm in `mock/benches` against the existing `accfit` arm. Designed as the first chain carrier, citing `question.toml:1358`. Needs N1. Tier: opus high.
