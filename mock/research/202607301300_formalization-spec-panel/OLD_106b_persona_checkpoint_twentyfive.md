**Adopted: the convergent statement, for the ratifying text.** A bitfield is a heterogeneous product of

declaration. A deliberately aliasing view field is a real idiom, so an overlap becomes a statement the
author made rather than one they failed to notice.

**Ratified: the containment locus.** The check hangs on two mentions inside two constructors, and one

**Held: which level a bitfield's declared width is.** Named open deliberately, one pass, and it joins the
