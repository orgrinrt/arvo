## The counting split: confirmed, after seeing both readings

**Confirmed.** They are not two definitions of one number. The **site count** is how many quantiser
sites an expression has, `n - 1` for any grouping, a function of the monomorphised type, one instruction
outside the loop, and it is what gets published. The **moved count** is how many of those actually
changed a value, data-dependent, the standard's own inexact condition, seven to nine times more
expensive on a float path and destructive to unrolling, read by law verdicts and never published by
default.

## Division: all three clauses

**Confirmed.** The exact quotient solves `q · d = x`, and the solution set has three shapes:

1. **Singleton.** An ordinary quotient; the quantiser and range machinery govern as ratified.
2. **Empty with a direction** (`x/0`, `x` nonzero). The one-sided limit supplies a direction and the
   failure borrows the range event's own resolution row in that direction: clamp reaches the far point,
   a numeral carrying infinity delivers it as the absorbing far point and raises `divideByZero`, refuse
   refuses.
3. **Everything, or empty with no direction** (`0/0`, and square root of a negative). Nothing is
   privileged and there is nothing to lean toward, so the event is `invalid`, resolving to NaN exactly
   where the numeral carries one and leaving the operation partial at that input where it does not.

Both halves of IEEE clause 7 become theorems rather than citations, `Recip` at zero inherits the reading
with no list edit, and no third axis position is needed. **`Hot`'s cell goes to the consumer** as a
fallback: the record holds three independent inventions for it with no derivation among them, which by
the toolbox rule's own decision test means the consumer knows and the design does not.

The surface itself was never in dispute: `div_floor` and `rem`, exact and partial on the divisor, bound
by the compiled Euclidean law, plus general `div` implemented from the pair. The residue worth more than
the cell: **a lowering may be a derivation input or an implementation of a stated value, never the author
of one**, with the deletion test as its decision procedure.

## Elementary functions: the chapter, with the boundary stated as the family's own

**Confirmed.** Three classes by the kind of the exact carrier. Roots take a root-residue pair at linear
width and provably never tie. The radix-power exponential is decidable but exponential-width. The
transcendentals have no carrier and need the hardness const.

**The boundary is the family's own, not a weakness in the design.** Correctly rounding a transcendental
terminates for any single value, but the working precision sufficient for *every* operand is given by no
formula. It is a genuine constant of the type, since the value set is finite so a worst case exists, and
it passes the pricing pillar's test to the letter. What fails is the economics: it comes only by
exhausting the value set or citing a published search, exhaustion is capped by the const-eval wall at
model widths, and published worst cases exist only for IEEE widths and some functions. So **a numeral
promises correct rounding exactly where its hardness constant is exhausted or cited, and nowhere else.**
`exhaustively-computed-or-cited` enters the grounding registry as a provenance class.

The closure sentence lands in its admission-test form, so a reader can tell a deferred operation from a
forgotten one. And the taxonomy absorbed a whole family it was not built for without edit.

## Naming, and the assumption-not-witness sentence: both confirmed

**Confirmed.** A name freely denotes type-level content, may promise behaviour only where the design
names the verifier, and may never promise behaviour with no verifier named. Every existing name passes,
so the principle forbids only names nobody has written yet.

**And the environment sentence with it**: an environment parameter denotes the control state a lowering's

cannot-provide split is therefore required rather than preferable, since refusing a declaration because

## The truth contract: the corrected shape

**Confirmed, corrected.** The exit split is real and more forced than argued: exhaustively, the algebra
homomorphisms from an n-lane truth to a one-lane one are exactly the n coordinate projections, and
neither `all` nor `any` is one, so above one lane the exit is genuinely outside the algebra.

**But the persona's third clause was backwards.** Binding the fifteen declarations on the exit-carrying

## The bitfield: confirmed, overlap refused unless declared

**Confirmed.** The crate decision stands and both its stated grounds are replaced by the category the
design acquired since: the crate holds notation. The convergent statement enters the ratifying text:
**a bitfield is a heterogeneous product of numerals sharing one container under a declared placement
map, and a bitpacked column is the homogeneous product of one numeral under a derived one**, the two
differing only in whether the index is a `Nat` or an hlist and whether the offsets are derived or
declared.

**Overlap is refused unless declared.** A deliberately aliasing view field is a real idiom, so an overlap
becomes a statement the author made rather than one they failed to notice. Today an overlapping
declaration compiles clean and one setter silently truncates its neighbour, in safe code, with no
diagnostic. The containment locus moves to an emitted free const item, and the digest's one-word
correction to occupancy lands with it.
