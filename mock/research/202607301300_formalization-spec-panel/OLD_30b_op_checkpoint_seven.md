# Op's seventh checkpoint, after the identity half's first four

**Date:** 2026-08-03
**Position:** after `30_pesce_the_identity_half_assembled.md`. **Required reading** with the
consolidation `26_consolidation_two.md`.

## The call: D69 is overturned

**The identity contract is parameterised in mathematical coordinates, not encoding coordinates.**
Precision and the exponent bounds become primitive; total width, hidden bit and field encoding are
derived on the physical side. Two independent readings reached this, which is the threshold a call of
this kind requires, and shipped `IFixed` already carries precision-shaped parameters so the
fixed-point side of the change costs almost nothing.

The standing consequence recorded with it: the off-by-one against real hardware formats was never a
gap to patch. It was the parameterisation reporting that it was pointed the wrong way.

## Held, not overturned: D39

Membership through algebraic structure stays a decision rather than being withdrawn, despite two
readings finding that its stated mechanism does not compile and that a membership predicate over the
whole ambient set is vacuously true of everything and can never discriminate.

The reason for holding rather than overturning: both readings mostly establish what membership
*cannot* do. What it can honestly promise is still being worked out, and a decision should not be
withdrawn on the strength of two findings that agree only on a negative. The narrower reading, that
its honest content is closure over an exact and widening family, is a candidate rather than a
conclusion.

## Noted, not adopted: preset divergence

Diverging from a preset on one axis currently means restating all five of its associated types, and a
compiled generic-parameter default makes the near-preset spelling work in about thirty lines with no
unstable feature. Recorded as available. Op's call is that the wider question, how a consumer names a
composition that is nearly a preset, deserves more than the first mechanism that works, so a later
member takes it further rather than this being adopted as-is.

## The direction for the next four, and it changes their framing

Op:

> I think we want to start getting them to converge on solutions, together, building up from each
> other and strengthening the spec. Otherwise it seems a good and fruitful round.

So the emphasis shifts. Finding and refuting have been productive and stay in the floor of the work,
but they are no longer the point of a dispatch. **Members build on each other toward a spec that gets
stronger with each file.**

Concretely, for every member from here:

- **Converge.** Where a predecessor's proposal survives your attack, strengthen it and carry it
  forward as shape, rather than restating that it survived. Where it fails, replace it, and the
  replacement is the deliverable rather than the failure.
- **Build up.** Your file should leave the design more settled than you found it. A file that leaves
  three more things open than it closes has moved the wrong way, however true its findings.
- **Toward spec.** Prefer stating what the design *is*, in the form the next consolidation could take
  almost verbatim, over stating what is wrong with what someone else wrote.

The first job of the next member remains attacking the reconciliations in file 30, which have had
exactly one pass, their own author's. But the attack is in service of settling them, not of adding to
a list.

Nothing else changes. The intent outranks every instruction, is vague on purpose, and is inferred
rather than read literally, so no member resolves to a single angle. Only op's calls are final, and
even those go stale.
