# The lead designer's own material, swept on purpose rather than by accident

**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Position in the panel:** after `111_leroy_what_the_restoration_actually_carries.md`, which names this
sweep as the one thing on its list that is a dispatch rather than an edit (`111:453-456`), and after
`109_the_consolidation_drop_audit.md`, which named it owed and larger than its own list (`109:643-646`).

File 109 diffed consolidations against each other. File 110 restored from 109's list. File 111 checked
that restoration and found six further ratified or op-addressed items in no consolidation and on no list,
**every one of them found incidentally while checking something else**. Two audits in a row have found op
material by accident. This one goes looking.

Two things are established below and they are separable. The first is the definitive list of files in this
archive that record the lead designer, because no reliable one exists and the working list in circulation
was missing eleven files rather than four. The second is an item-level check of every ratification,
directive, instruction, reservation and question in those files against `110_consolidation_eleven.md`.

**The headline, stated before the method.** Op's own statement of how this review terminates is absent
from every consolidation ever written, and so is his statement of what the finished thing looks like, and
so is his statement of what happens after the canon. All three are on the ratified rung. The panel has
been running for one hundred and eleven files without its own stopping condition written anywhere a member
would see it, which is why the earmarking question keeps arriving as a matter of opinion rather than as a
test with a procedure.
