# Op's tenth checkpoint: the encoding ratified, division held, provenance made greppable

**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Position:** after `44_ringer_what_the_overturn_left_behind.md`. **Required reading** with
`40_consolidation_three.md`.

Four calls. The last one changes how every claim in this design is written from here.

## The encoding is ratified; the seal is a follow-up

**Ratified.** The value-unique encoding replaces the width chain. Everything the ninth checkpoint's
hold was waiting on is done: the rational bias is built (MATLAB's slope one, bias one half is
representable; unreduced pairs are refused), priced at 1.55 ms per composition over the dyadic case
every shipped numeral actually uses, and the API shape file 41 left undecided is settled by a compiled
generic form costing the same as the bare-alias one.

**The seal is an implementation task, not part of the ratified shape.** Sealing `Pos`/`Nat` at the
layer where they actually compose is the fix file 42 established and measured at zero compile cost. It
is owed, and it does not gate the shape.

## Division is held for a later stretch

File 43 disproved the no-finite-accumulator prediction in the ratified coordinates and sharpened it
into something more useful (division is a third growth class, at Theta(2^p) accumulator bits). It then
proposed shipping `div_floor` and `rem` as two exact-partial operations bound by a compiled Euclidean
law, with division by any fixed nonzero representable constant exact at zero new mechanism.

**Not adopted now.** The finding stands and is recorded; the operation surface waits until the rest of
the algebra settles. Nothing about the finding expires in the meantime, and the Euclidean law is
compiled, so the later stretch picks it up rather than re-deriving it.

## Every claim records what it is grounded on, and the existing ones get backfilled

**Adopted, including the backfill.** File 44's diagnosis is that three defects share one cause, and
the cause is structural rather than anybody's carelessness:

- A consolidation compresses a conjunctive claim's conclusion and discards which of its members were
  actually checked. The overflow-band sentence names four operation families, two were ever checked,
  one of those two was wrong, and a third was compiled only when someone went looking.
- A ratified coordinate change has no query for what depends on it. When D69 made adjustments rational,
  nothing could answer "what was reasoned in dyadic coordinates", so the answer arrived twice by
  accident, while members were doing something else.

So each claim carries **`grounded on: <decision>`** alongside its existing evidence class, and the next
overturn's blast radius is a grep rather than a hand sweep across forty-odd files.

**The backfill is part of it.** A member walks the current consolidation and adds the field to the
claims already in it, because a forward-only convention leaves exactly the population that has already
produced three defects unqueryable. This is also the cheapest way to catch the fourth, since writing
the field for a claim requires finding its derivation, which is the same act that catches a stale one.

## The coming four keep converging on the open list

The open items are concrete now: the float half of the overflow-band claim (never checked by anyone),
`Growth` leaving `Policy`, the evaluation-strategy sentence the design owes, `Precise`'s combinator
surface, and the missing codegen regression tests.

## Standing

The convergence directive from `30b` and the novelty posture from `34b` both hold unchanged. The
intent outranks every instruction, is vague on purpose, and is inferred rather than read literally, so
no member resolves to a single angle. Where the current shape can be kept it should be, and rewrite
cost is the tiebreaker between designs otherwise equal against the intent. Only op's calls are final,
and even those go stale, which is the entire reason the grounding field now exists.
