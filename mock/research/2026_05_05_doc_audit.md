# Arvo per-crate documentation audit — 2026-05-05

**Date:** 2026-05-05.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/*/{README,DESIGN,BACKLOG,DEEPDIVE_*,SHAME}.md.tmpl` across 16 crates (49 template files, ~6,681 lines).
**Method:** Manual read pass + targeted grep against documentation-writing + writing-style + vocabulary + ai-agent-framing workspace rules; spot-check of `pub` items vs source (`crates/*/src/`).

## Summary

The em-dash ban, ASCII-diagram ban, exclamation-mark-in-prose ban, hype/jargon ban, filler ban, agent-tool ban, and cross-workspace path ban are all clean: zero hits. The remaining damage clusters in three large categories. First, doc-vs-source drift is pervasive on three load-bearing facts (the `MultiContainer`-to-`WideBits` migration of round 202605031400, the `Width`-to-`u16` const-generic collapse of #316, and the deletion of std-parallel `Uint8`/`Int8`-style aliases): nine BACKLOG/DESIGN files still narrate "MultiContainer plumbing" as the path forward, six DESIGN files still list `const N: Width` where source uses `u16`, and the arvo facade README + DESIGN bodies both still advertise the eight per-N aliases as shipping. Second, internal contradictions inside individual files: arvo's DESIGN says Warm caps at `I+F<=32` (line 868), `I+F<=64` (line 959), and `1..=65535` (line 1052); arvo-bits/DESIGN duplicates `#![no_std]` and lists incompatible deps in adjacent paragraphs. Third, the entire body of Tier-1-rendered prose (DESIGN/README templates that compose into the public `docs/` tree) leaks Tier-2 vocabulary heavily: 80+ `mock/design_rounds/`, `mock/research/`, `.tmpl`, "doc CL"/"src CL", and round-id artefacts. Two banned-word hits ("Substrate primitives" / "Substrate-owned"). The `entry`-as-record drift the workspace vocabulary rule warns about appears in arvo-spectral / arvo-comb / arvo-graph BACKLOG entries used as legitimate matrix-vector terminology, not record drift; the only clear `entry`-as-record borderline is in the BACKLOG type-surface reminders ("new entries use…") which is changelist-entry meaning and acceptable per the carve-out.

Total findings: roughly 95 across 16 crates. Severity skew: Tier-1 leakage (50+) > stale type/trait names and prose drift (~25) > internal contradictions (8) > banned vocabulary (2) > flat-bullet TLDR opens (0; READMEs all open with prose) > minor spelling/style (5).
