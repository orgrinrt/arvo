--- WITHDRAWN, and kept as the audit trail of a wrong instrument -----------------
Two cells are wrong and both were found by reading the source against the output:

  quantum.rs assertion 2 reported CONCEPT and is HOSTING. The bound it enforces
  is `reach >= i32::MIN && reach <= i32::MAX`, which lives inside the helper
  `reach_is_representable` at quantum.rs:196-204 and therefore never appears in
  the assertion text the regex saw. The classifier reads one level and the bound
  is one level down.

  slots.rs assertion 5 reported HOSTING and is CONCEPT. Its expression carries
  `i128` casts, which the pattern matches, but the casts are there so the
  obligation cannot overflow while checking; the bound being enforced is
  `MAX - MIN < 1 << WIDTH`, a coherence condition between two declared
  coordinates with no machine limit in it. The classifier cannot tell a bound
  from a cast taken for safety.

The two errors happen to cancel in the total (6 CONCEPT / 3 HOSTING is the right
count for the wrong reasons), which is exactly why the count alone would have
been believed. Superseded by `an_admitted_refusal_that_denotes_a_number_system.sh`,
which decides the same question by exhibiting the refused candidate instead of
matching on text.
