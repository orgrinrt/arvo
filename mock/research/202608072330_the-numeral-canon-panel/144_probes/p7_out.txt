what this leaves of the second argument:
  the count has one argument that moves, the observation set, plus the axis
  set the design ships. Removing an axis from that set is a no-op wherever
  the axis is answer-invisible, and wherever it is not, the removal puts two
  denotations under one type. So a shape-to-count table is writable once the
  axis set is named, and it does not additionally wait on a denotation line,
  because the line is not free: the firewall pins every answer-visible axis
  to component one. That last step is an argument from a proposition at two
  experts, not a measurement, and it is marked as such.
