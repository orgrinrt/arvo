the composed statement:
  p4 says a fixed weighting picks a different arm on a different target, at
  44.3% of the simplex for 139's pair and a median of 90.2% for random ones.
  This probe says that costs nothing at all while the arms conform to one
  policy, and costs the arms' entire disagreement rate the moment they do
  not. So the firewall is not only a rule about what a cost model may do.
  It is the precondition that makes a weighting a portable object, and
  without it a strategy's meaning is a function of the machine it compiled
  on, with nothing in the type saying so.
