Z1/Y3. what does the linear limit cost, in the criterion it forgoes?
==============================================================================
  150 random arm sets containing an unreachable Pareto arm (of 361 drawn)
  201 unreachable arms, gap to the best linearly reachable arm at the
  unreachable arm's own certificate weight, in that criterion:
    min 1.7%  median 17.6%  mean 21.1%  max 72.0%
  Y3 negative gaps (impossible by construction): 0 -> PASS
  Z1 median gap at least 10%: CONFIRMED

the composition, which is what this is for:
  the Chebyshev selector reaches every Pareto arm and costs nothing at
  compile time. I expected its extra target-dependent object, the reference
  point, to make a frozen selection travel worse. It does not: the two
  selectors switch at 86.9% each, indistinguishable at one decimal, so the
  reference point is not where the portability goes. And X2 fell: declaring
  the reference in the design does not help either, which closes that route
  rather than opening it. What DOES recover the rescale part of a target
  change is the shared-baseline normalisation, exactly and for both
  selectors. So neither selector is the answer on its own: what travels is a
  weighting and a named baseline arm, and what never travels is the arm.
