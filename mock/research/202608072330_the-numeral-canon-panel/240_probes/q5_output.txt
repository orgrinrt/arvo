== what `adapt` returns on a position past the top of the range ==

   Exact::on_grid(1000), saturating:
     Signature<Integer<4>, ...>        -> slot 7
     Signature<Floating<3,-2,3>, ...>  -> slot 3

   For `Integer<4>` that slot names one value: 7 times the quantum at the only
   magnitude there is.

   For `Floating<3,-2,3>` that same slot names 3 different values,
     at magnitude 0: 3 quanta of q(0) = 3 * 2^-2
     at magnitude 1: 6 quanta of q(0) = 6 * 2^-2
     at magnitude 2: 12 quanta of q(0) = 12 * 2^-2

   Nothing in the returned `i64` says which, and `apply.rs` reads no magnitude:
   `MAGNITUDES` appears nowhere in it and the two occurrences of the word `magnitude`
   are both in doc comments. `RUN.md` carries the grep.

== the statement this supports ==

   `adapt` is a total map onto the representable set exactly at
   `magnitudes = 1`. Above it the codomain is the slot range, which is a
   projection of the set rather than the set, so the ratified factoring's
   second half is realised for the constant-quantum family and not for the
   magnitude-indexed one.

   findings: 1
