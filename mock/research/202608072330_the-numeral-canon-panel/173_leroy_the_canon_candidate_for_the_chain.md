### 1.2 The obligations, the grades and the window

**L5. What composition owes that a single operation does not: five things, none expressible per
operation.** [enumeration, carried in full per the discipline this unit itself measured]

From `60` section 3, with `168`'s five and `167`'s three mapped onto it (three shared, mapping at
`168` C2):

1. **An intermediate format** wider than the boundary format, or nothing exists between
   adapt-everywhere and nothing.
2. **A schedule**, since with more than one operation there is more than one place to adapt and
   the choices are semantically distinct.
3. **An association and order statement** for folds, because once any adaptation in the schedule
   is order-sensitive the fold's value depends on the reduction tree, which is where chains meet
   I10: running on `n` cores is reordering the fold.
4. **A count bound**: the headroom an exact additive accumulator needs is `ceil(log2 k)` integer
   bits and `k` must come from somewhere; a count is a static or contractual quantity, which is
   typestate-shaped.
5. **An error bound composed per adaptation point**: the drift of a chain is the sum over its
   adaptation points of the local adaptation error, each weighted by the sensitivity of the
   remaining suffix; **the schedule is the index set of the error analysis**, so fewer adaptation
   points is a structurally shorter error sum, not merely cheaper rounding.

The fifth has no counterpart in either of the tenth unit's cold files and both said so; it is
carried at `60`'s single instance and it is the enumeration the earlier consolidation dropped at
zero mentions (`168` 22), which is why it is written out here rather than pointed at.

Provenance forms: `60` blind (its sitting); `168` blind (three of five re-derived, plus depth,
shape and arity as measured predicate dimensions); `167` blind (three of five). The re-derivations
are corroboration discounted by the shared premise set (`168` 23, `169` 1, `170` 10: both exact
heading matches between the pair are dictated by shared context, so the pair counts as a
corroborating pair and not as two clean instances).

**L6. Exactness for a chain has grades, and one is fixed point's own.** [enumeration + measured]
`60` section 4: **grade a**, composite correct rounding, demonstrated constructively at `F = 8`
for a widening dot product on all 46,656 inputs with a checker that never rounds; **grade b**,
stepwise correct rounding, the only compositional grade and its ceiling, drifting up to 2 ulp
(nearest, 15,628 of 46,656 wrong) and 3 ulp (truncation, 42,892); **grade c**, bounded drift; and
**grade s**, structural exactness: fixed point's add, subtract and widening multiply are exact
integer operations, so a chain inside its width algebra is the mathematics itself, drift-free by
bookkeeping with no error analysis. Single sitting (`60`), uncontested through the tenth unit,
with `167` 4.1's gap measurement (L14) as the quantitative twin of the a/b distinction.

**L7. The window, its capacity, and its two bounds.** [measured + enumeration]
`60` section 5: addition composes headroom logarithmically, multiplication composes width
linearly, so holding a whole multiplicative chain exactly is real and not worth wanting; the
**window** (a bounded subterm whose exact result fits the container, grade s inside, one
adaptation at exit) factors a chain into `ceil(k/w)` adaptations; the fused multiply-add is the
two-operation window. Two bounds from the tenth unit and the `43` dispatch:

- **Heterogeneity** (`168` 14 B / N6) [measured, exhaustive]: the capacity formula ("a static
  function of the container width and the operand formats") holds for homogeneous windows; once a
  window mixes operations the per-step requirement is order-dependent even for genuinely
  commuting steps (`*3` needing 10 bits in one position and 11 in the other; 24 orderings of one
  four-step multiset spanning 11 to 13 bits, tightness verified both directions). The capacity of
  a mixed window is a function of the **ordered sequence**.
- **Flattening** (`43` s3) [measured, exhaustive]: nested composition derivations compose soundly
  and not tightly, one bit wide on 1201 of 4096 two-level shapes and up to two bits at three
  levels, with zero shapes where flattening is worse: **the derivation consumes the flattened
  ordered sequence, not a composition of per-part derivations.** Same phenomenon, two objects,
  same direction (`168` 24 took `43`'s answer for its own O-168-6 and this candidate carries
  that).

And the residual refinement (`167` R12, conceded and rescoped): for the **additive** window the
carried residual reaches the identical answer with the accumulator at scale `F` plus a separate
`F`-bit carry rather than at scale `2F`, a constant-factor state saving inside `60`'s window and
not a rival to it; five geometries at a 64-bit container where that saving decides
representability (`167` probes A and A2). For **product** chains the window strictly dominates
the carried residual and `167` withdrew the claim (`167` R12).

### 1.3 The licences, the theorems, and the placement

**L8. The resolution degeneracy: the size of the schedule decision is computable, and it grows
with depth for rounding resolutions.** [measured, exhaustive]
`168` 4.1: wrapping's degeneracy is 1 at every depth swept (the operational cash-out of "ring
homomorphism"); clamping over monotone non-negative additions 1; clamping mixed 3; rounding 3 at
depth 3 rising to 7 at depth 5. `60` had the existence (three schedules, three functions); the
count is `168`'s, blind.

**L9. Two independent licences delete an interior resolution, and the algebraic one is a
conjunction over every step.** [measured, with four firing controls]
`168` 4.3: **(A) range** (every intermediate provably where the resolution is the identity;
width-dependent, operation-independent) and **(B) algebra** (the resolution commutes with or is
absorbed by the composition; operation-dependent, bound-independent), each holding where the other
fails. One `saturating_sub` swapped into an affine chain revokes (B) with endpoints, widths and
depth unchanged, so **whatever carries a chain's licence must see every step and be composed as
the chain is built**; a carrier computed from endpoint types cannot express it. `60` holds both
licences as instances without separating them (`168` C4: its exactness predicate is (A); its
wrapping-fold observation is (B)), so the separation is the tenth unit's and the instances are
the `60` sitting's. Formalised at `172` section 6.

**L10. The backward-narrowing licence, and its count.** [measured; ONE EXPERT, second read owed]
`167` probes B and C: propagating the consumer's demand backward is licensed exactly for the
congruences (wrapping add, sub, mul, bitwise and: zero disagreements over 16.7M triples per
operator) and refused for saturation, division, right shift and min; the licensed rewrite is
worth 50.9% of the intermediate bits on a four-term multiply-accumulate and 72.7% on a Horner
chain; **one non-congruence operator anywhere truncates the region the backward rule reaches**,
so the licence is a property of the whole path. The chain-level rewrite available to one
resolution is unavailable to another over the identical expression, which is I9 as an arithmetic
fact. Unattacked through the whole unit and named by both replies as still at one expert; it is
the unit's most valuable unattacked claim.

**L11. The correlation finding.** [measured; ONE EXPERT, second read owed]
`167` 3.2, from a probe that failed: worst-case accumulator width is a fact about **correlation
between the terms**, not about the chain's length or declared widths; decorrelated data is a
random walk needing up to `log2(n)/2` fewer bits than the worst case; the declared extent is the
mechanism that would let a consumer say so (`112` section 8's machinery, connection offered and
untested).

**L12. The deferral optimum is a theorem.** [theorem]
Where the boundary resolution is any fixed selection from the nearest-point correspondence onto
the representable set, full deferral is pointwise optimal: every placement's output lies in the
set because the boundary resolution fires last, and the deferred output attains the argmin.
**The tie rule is irrelevant and idempotence is a consequence**; what the hypothesis excludes is a
boundary resolution that is not a function of its argument. Proof at `172` 4.1, closing `170`
O-170-1 and O-170-2. The sweeps are confirmations, and their history is part of the record
(section 3, R-i): `168` swept two placements while claiming all, `169` widened to every placement
(control moving from 91 to 317 win-chains, so the gap was real), `170` reproduced the widening on
a second instrument in a second language and added the matched wrap control and the coarser grid,
`172` confirmed at random representable sets with adversarial fixed tie-breaking. **The boundary
of the theorem is the resolution, not the chain**: non-nearest projections are beaten at 317, 395
and 443 win-chains in the three matched controls. [measured]

**L13. The no-threshold double-rounding claim is a theorem, constructive at `F any`.** [theorem]
For every `F >= 3` and every `M` with `F < M < 2F`, a two-operand product exists whose double
rounding (nearest-ties-even at `M` then at `F`) differs from its single rounding: three explicit
witness families, one per band of `M`, verified at all 65 `(F, M)` cells for `F in 3..=12` with
the on-tie non-witness control firing 10 of 10 and `169`'s exhaustive `2^(F-1)` counts reproduced
exactly (`172_probes/p2_witness_families/`). History: `167` 4.1 enumerated at three widths with
the argument beside the sweep; `169` extended to seven and found the closed form; `171` 5.2
characterised the disagreeing set exactly (tie-adjacency, `a*b mod 2^(F+1) in {2^(F-1)+1,
3*2^(F-1)-1}`); `172` 5 constructed the witnesses. Consequence, unchanged from `167`: **a
chain-level accuracy guarantee cannot be bought by strengthening the per-operation guarantee**;
it is bought by not rounding the intermediate, which is a decision about the region.

### 1.4 The axes, the shapes and the graph case

**L14. Association order is a speed lever with exactly zero accuracy content in fixed point, and
an accuracy lever worth up to 94.8x in relative precision.** [measured]
`167` section 8, with the region named by its own R12 after `60`'s order-dependence result: the

the reachability of the resolution**, or it is wrong for someone. The strategy-facing corollary
[measured, from the harness]: the reassociation win is available to an accuracy-first concern at
no accuracy cost in fixed point, so gating it behind a speed-first strategy gives up a large win
for nothing.

**L15. The association licence is per operator, not per family.** [measured, exhaustive]

algebraic licence are independent axes**. The saturating-add row is discounted for the `satfold`
contamination (`167` R1); everything else in the partition is independent.
