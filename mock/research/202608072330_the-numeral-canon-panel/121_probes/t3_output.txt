P1/P2/P3. The same primitive on two ambient spans

  policy   span                            add          sub          mul   monotone
  sat      non-negative 0..45           0/1081     885/1081        0/273       True
  sat      straddling -45..45        3120/6211    2445/6211      182/909       True
  wrap     non-negative 0..45           0/1081        0/723        0/273      False
  wrap     straddling -45..45           0/6211       0/6211        0/603      False

  The saturating rows move with the span and the wrapping rows do not,
  which is the control: the span is what separates them.

  The witness, on the straddling span:
    a=-1, b=1: R(a+b) = 0, R(R(a)+R(b)) = 1
    a=-5, b=3: R(a+b) = 0, R(R(a)+R(b)) = 3
  On the non-negative span neither argument exists, so the identity holds
  there for a reason rather than by luck.

P4. `119` 4.4's clause, tested on both spans

  saturating on non-negative 0..45    : homomorphism for ['add', 'mul']
  saturating on straddling -45..45    : homomorphism for no operation

  The clause is true on one span and false on the other, and states no
  span. On the non-negative one it is a homomorphism for two of three.

P5. The same fact as a shipped test, re-derived here

  `warm-clamp-shared/src/lib.rs` carries
  `clamping_is_a_retraction_on_non_negative_addition_at_every_swept_width`,
  which asserts that folding with a clamp at every step equals clamping
  once at the end, on non-negative addition. That is exactly the
  homomorphism identity for add on a non-negative domain. Re-derived:

    W=3: eager and deferred clamping disagree on 0/1088 folds
    W=4: eager and deferred clamping disagree on 0/8448 folds
    W=5: eager and deferred clamping disagree on 0/66560 folds

  Zero everywhere, which is the test's own claim and is the clause's
  counterexample.
