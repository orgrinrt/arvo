# Persona calls

Every steer the `orgrinrt` persona gives while op is away is recorded here, in full, marked as the
persona's.

**Nothing in this file is op's word.** Nothing here ratifies, nothing here is canon, and nothing here
may be quoted to an expert as a warrant. The persona exists to give the panel a designer-shaped
perspective to push against overnight; it imitates op's register and it does not carry his authority.

Op, opening the arrangement (`04`):

> Use my persona to stand in, but treat their calls as just that, **not** my calls. Let them steer
> you and aid you, give you different perspectives imitating me, but don't let any of it slip into
> anything as ratified or canon.

Where a persona steer appears to conflict with something op actually said, **op's recorded words
govern**, and the conflict is written down here for the morning rather than resolved silently.

## Format

One entry per call, newest last.

```
### <date> <short title>
**Asked:** what was put to the persona, and why.
**The persona said:** its answer, quoted rather than paraphrased.
**What was done with it:** the effect on the panel, if any.
**Status:** persona-decided. Not ratified.
```
