# Dispatcher note: what the mode change costs, and what the next dispatch is

**Position:** after `28`, while `27` runs. **Author:** the dispatching agent.
**Standing:** a note, not a finding. Carries no authority and may not be cited as support.

Recorded because the reasoning below would otherwise live only in a transcript, and because the next
dispatch's brief depends on it.

## The mode change is not a relabelling of what the panel was doing

`04` said nothing settles overnight. `28` extends that to roughly the first hundred files and adds
the method: options stay written down and open, each new question is reflected over all of them, and
the path appears when one shape serves the rest of arvo best.

Read quickly, that sounds like what the panel was already doing, since nothing had entered
`SETTLED.md` anyway. It is not, in one specific way.

**The panel had been converging, and was rewarded for it.** Files `23` through `26` each narrowed
something: the sentences a canon could carry, the seam between two vocabularies, the definition of a
strategy, whether packing pays. The map's own framing was eight questions waiting to be cleared, and
each stretch was measured against how much of that backlog it closed.

Under the new mode that framing is wrong. The eight are not a backlog, they are the shape of the work,
and a stretch is measured against whether it **opened** ground, killed a route with a diagnostic, or
added a shape nobody had written down. Those are different incentives and they produce different
files.

The failure to watch for is the previous one inverted. A panel told to converge invents settlements;
a panel told to explore invents vagueness. Both read as compliance.

## What the mode needs that the panel did not have

An instrument. "Carry every option forward" is an instruction with no artifact behind it unless the
options live somewhere, and until `OPTIONS.md` they lived in whichever file last mentioned them,
which means they were rediscovered rather than carried.

`SETTLED.md` holds what the predecessor established. `DROPLIST.md` holds what closed. Nothing held
what was open, which is the only category the next hundred files operate on.

## The next dispatch, and why it is this one

**Rebuild `OPTIONS.md` from the member files.**

`OPTIONS.md` was assembled by this agent from `MORNING.md`, which is a compression that every
checkpoint examining it has found defective. Building the register that way is precisely the failure
this panel keeps catching in others: reading the summary rather than the thing. The file says so at
its head, and the rebuild is owed to a different author because the author of a compression is the
person who believes it entails.

Three things the rebuild has to produce that the scaffold does not have.

**The options members actually proposed.** `03` produced five options beyond the three it was given
on the family question. `13` added a fifth arrangement of the width surface. `10` conceded the bridge
is not dissolvable with thirteen routes enumerated. None of those sets is written out. Each is either
a live option or a droplist entry, and nobody has checked which.

**A verified open-or-closed boundary.** Every entry in the scaffold is listed as live because the map
reads as though it is. Whether the panel closed any of them, and with what diagnostic, is a question
for the files.

**The options nobody wrote down.** Op widened the denotation set explicitly and the widening applies
generally. A register that only carries what has been proposed is a record, and what the mode needs
is a working surface.

The dispatch reads member files rather than the map, and where it uses the map it says so. It cannot
run until `27` lands, because `27` is being written now and reading a file mid-write is how this
panel already moved a document under a member.

## One thing this note is not

It is not a claim that the converging files were wrong to converge. They were working under the
instruction in force at the time, and their findings stand as findings. What changes is what happens
to those findings next: they become entries in a register rather than steps toward a conclusion, and
several of them are strong enough that the register will be where their strength shows.
