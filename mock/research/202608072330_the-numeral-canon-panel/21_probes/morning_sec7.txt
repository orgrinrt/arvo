## Seven: your second standing instruction, discharged, and what the repository knew

You said the headroom bench body was "one instance of evidence, completely unaudited by a second
expert set of eyes". `20` is that second set of eyes. It read the harness rather than the summaries.

**The verdict is that the bench body is sound.** Real competitors over one shared transform, not a
strawman. A genuine noise-floor control that compiles byte-identical to a real arm at every width,
asserted by tests rather than assumed. And the crate documents a **discarded first cut** where the
optimiser collapsed the chain and the numbers were thrown away, which is the shape an honest bench
has. Keeping it is the result.

Three qualifications, and the third is the one that becomes your question.

**The cost is not the number the record carries.** The record says roughly 1600 instructions against
81, corrected to 339 against 81. The harness answer is **width-banded and not monotone**: 44.2x,
0.98x, 21.0x, 7.0x, 0.99x, 2.45x across the widths measured. The split is exactly filled versus
sub-rung widths, and the mechanism is that **headroom guarantees the container exceeds the width at
every width, so it removes the case where the projection would have been free.** A larger effect sits
beside it: writing the projection once is 21.6x faster than the minimum arm at one width.

**The arm matrix is asymmetric.** Headroom measured in one configuration, minimum in four, and
headroom's is its worst. That does not change the verdict under wrapping, where a lazy headroom arm
provably cannot win. **It does not carry to clamping.**

**And the family implements `Warm` as wrapping**, while the ratified preset table gives `Warm` the
clamp and gives wrapping to `Hot` alone. So that half of the bench measures a different strategy's
resolution than the one it names. **The clamp family reverses the answer**: headroom goes from 2.2x
worse at fold arity two to **44x better at arity 256**, crossing over exactly where its own interior
safety predicate says it should.

### Six committed cells are void, and the mechanism is instructive

One bench family reports throughput **48x to 66x above the machine's roofline**. The harness calls it
"a safe default pick".

Cause, established with a probe: the saturating fold has an absorbing fixpoint, so after three
elements every lane sits at the limit and the optimiser deletes the loop. **The value stays correct,
so the cross-variant value check is vacuous there**, and the crate's own guard against exactly this
passes only because an opacity barrier hides the constant the proof needs.

Scope measured, not estimated: **6 of 691 cells.** This is the same shape as the night's other
instrument findings, arriving in the runtime evidence rather than the compile-time evidence.
