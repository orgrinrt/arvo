VERDICT
================================================================================================
  148's refutation reproduces: at one reduction site the intermediate positions coincide
  under every overflow position, wrapping included, so the congruence is not available as
  an explanation at the cell 146 attributed to it. The third contamination item is wrong.

  147's narrowing reproduces: only the unsigned saturating cell rests on the one-sidedness.

  And my z6 instrument was measuring the wrong thing. Intersecting dimension NAMES reports a
  dimension as present when the instances partition its values, and the honest intersection
  is over values.

  B5 as I predicted it is REFUTED. At the container row the empty dimensions are not
  `signedness`, which 139 simply omits and which the name-level check already dropped.
  They are `W` and `rounding`: 139 swept W in {3,5,6,7,11} where the other two swept
  W = 4, and 139 swept truncate where 141 swept floor and toward-zero. So 148's diagnosis
  is right about the mechanism and wrong about which dimension carries it here.

  B6: the defect reaches 3 of 4 rows, which 148 did not
  check and said so. The fusion row is clean.

  What that means for a clause built on one of these rows: the intersection is not merely
  an upper bound, it is EMPTY on those dimensions, so a predicate written from it claims
  nothing where they are present. A clause needs a single instance that spans what it
  wants to claim, cited for that dimension, which is 148's repair applied per dimension
  rather than only to signedness.
