What the aliases say, which is the whole result:

  general_masked = cheap_reduced_ungated
      masking after EVERY node and masking once at the root compile to
      one body. Reduction mod 2^k is a ring homomorphism and LLVM already
      proves it, so the intermediate reductions cost nothing and no
      declaration is needed to remove them.

  wrap_gated = cheap_unreduced_ungated
      the declaration licenses dropping the FINAL reduction as well, which
      LLVM cannot do on its own because nothing tells it the root stays in
      range. This is what the refinement buys under a wrapping map.

  sat_gated = general_saturating
      the same declaration under a saturating map is refused, correctly,
      and the gate leaves no residue in either direction.
