====================================================================================================
VERDICT
====================================================================================================
  144's 'nothing in the type says so' is true of an assignment set that lacks the axis and
  false of one that carries it. The join therefore has a condition, and it is checkable:

    every lowering arm the design admits realises the denotation of some assignment

  Under that condition the firewall is enforceable by declaration alone. A conforming pair
  is two arms of one assignment and the weighting picks between them freely, moving no
  answer. A non-conforming pair is two assignments and the type names which one, so the
  42.14% is a declared difference rather than a silent one. The control shows the condition
  is not automatic: an arm rounding off the axis realises no assignment, and for that arm
  the firewall has no repair available in the type.
