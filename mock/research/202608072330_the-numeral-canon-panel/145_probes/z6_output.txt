====================================================================================================
VERDICT
====================================================================================================
  The extractor sees the incident 143 names: the accumulator dimension is absent from every
  predicate in 139 and present in 141's, so the two files that agreed about the storage
  concern agreed about a region neither of them had measured one step in.

  And the firewall row carries no predicate in any of the three files that endorse it.
  Under I13 that is the strongest negative available: a claim with no dimensions listed
  holds nowhere any of them is present. The firewall is a design proposition rather than
  a measured finding, which is a legitimate thing for a canon to state, but it means the
  two-expert count on it is a count of agreements about a sentence rather than of
  instruments agreeing about a region. 144's F144-15 is the first predicated measurement
  of what it buys, and its predicate is the region the proposition has evidence in.

  What this probe can and cannot do. It matches dimension NAMES, so it catches a dimension
  one instance never varied, which is 143's failure exactly. It cannot check that two files
  mean the same thing by a name, and it cannot check that a listed dimension was genuinely
  swept rather than pinned. So an intersection it reports is an upper bound on what a
  convergence establishes, and 145 states it as such rather than as the region.
