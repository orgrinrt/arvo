D2. Are negation symmetry and equivariance mutually exclusive?
====================================================================================================
  modes carrying both: [] (must be empty)
  and the argument, which is two lines and does not depend on the sweep:
    if rnd is negation symmetric and translation equivariant, then
      rnd(-1/2) = -rnd(1/2)          by symmetry
      rnd(-1/2) = rnd(1/2) - 1       by equivariance at c = -1
    so 2*rnd(1/2) = 1 and rnd(1/2) = 1/2, which is not an integer.
  D2: CONFIRMED
  This is a SECOND exclusivity of exactly the shape 132 section 5.3 already records for
  the order bound against negation symmetry, so equivariance joins that structure.

====================================================================================================
VERDICT, and what 145 may say about a closed topic without editing it
====================================================================================================
  Translation equivariance is not an independent fifth property sitting beside the
  rounding candidate's four laws. It is bound to two of them:

    the order bound IMPLIES it, so both adjoints carry it;
    negation symmetry EXCLUDES it, by a two-line argument of the same shape as the
      exclusivity 132 section 5.3 already records;
    and among modes carrying neither it is UNDETERMINED, witnessed by
      signature (False, False, False): half_up=True, half_down=True, CONSTRUCTED half_up_mod3=False

  So the rounding topic's statement is not wrong and is not complete for this use. It
  enumerates which member carries which law; an arm relocating a rounding across an integer
  addition reads a property that the enumeration determines in two of four cases and leaves
  open in the rest. That is a gap in what the closed candidate happens to cover, reported
  here rather than edited into it, and it is the strategy topic's finding rather than the
  rounding topic's because the strategy topic is where an arm's predicate is being written.
