`the_width_invariance_control_was_toothless` carries `standing = "defective"`.
`moon_phase` is declared by no `dimension` row.


### What `cargo mock --lint-only` printed

  --- registry ---
    544 rows across 10 namespaces
    warning [unknown-config-key]: ...
    schema check passed
    all lints passed

**Nothing. The lint passed the planted row.** That is worth stating plainly
rather than burying: the engine's schema check enforces field presence, types
and reference resolution, and it enforces none of the three rules this dispatch
was told to respect. The whole of that enforcement is in `arvo-checks`, so
`cargo mock --lint-only` alone is not a gate on a proposal row's substance and
must not be reported as one.


### What `cargo test -p arvo-checks` printed

  test the_committed_canon_names_only_declared_axes ... FAILED
    kind: "predicate-names-an-undeclared-dimension"
    says: "`predicate` names the axis `moon_phase`, which no `dimension` row
           declares. Declare it, or use the slug of the axis that already means
           this. An undeclared axis cannot be absent from any other predicate,
           so admitting one here weakens every predicate in the canon."

  test the_committed_canon_rests_no_measurement_on_an_unusable_instrument ... FAILED
    at:   "proposal::control_row_planted_by_189_delete_me"
    kind: "measurement-rests-on-an-unusable-instrument"
    says: "`evidence` names `probe::the_width_invariance_control_was_toothless`,

Both fired, each naming the planted row by address.
