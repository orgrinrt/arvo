-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PREDICTION WAS WRONG, AND THE REFUTATION IS THE MECHANISM STATEMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
I predicted the corner cross-term bound alone would recover the whole win and
that the [0, 1] basis would turn out incidental.  It does not, and the reason
is visible once measured: in the centre-and-radius basis the residual range is
[-r, r], SYMMETRIC BY CONSTRUCTION, so its corners are exactly -r*r and +r*r
and the corner bound reduces to the standard symmetric bound.  The corner rule
can only be tighter where a residual is one-signed, and only the [0, 1] basis
ever produces a one-signed residual.

So the two changes are NOT separable and neither is the answer alone.  The
correct statement is that the tighter cross term is what wins, and the basis is
what makes it available: the win is the pair.

This is worth more than the confirmation would have been.  A design taking
"use a corner cross term" from p9 without also taking the basis would get the
symmetric answer back and would not know why.
