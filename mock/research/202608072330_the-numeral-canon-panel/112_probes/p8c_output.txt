WHAT THE TWO CONTROLS ESTABLISH
The two aliases are what make this a comparison rather than a demonstration.
`bare_diff` is written as `(a + b) - c` with no gate at all, and `general_diff`
is written as the saturating chain with no gate at all.  The licensed arm
aliases the first and the refused arm aliases the second, so the const gate is
fully erased in BOTH directions and neither body carries any trace of the
predicate that selected it.  Without the controls, the clean two-instruction
body would be equally consistent with the optimiser having done something
unrelated.

Two instructions against seven is an ad-hoc quick spike as far as magnitude
goes.  It prices nothing, no bench harness ran, and nobody should read a speed
claim out of it.  What it establishes structurally is which bodies exist.
