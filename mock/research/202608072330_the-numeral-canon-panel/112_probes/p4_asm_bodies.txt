So FIVE symbols are one body and that body is `ret`:
  - both directions of the spurious cast (radix 2 <-> radix 10),
  - both refinement widenings (100 -> 200 and 7 -> 255),
  - and `plain_identity`, which is the control and is what makes the other
    four an observation rather than a coincidence.

And the licensed arm against the general one:

  _add_licensed_100_100:  add w0, w1, w0 ; ret          (2 instructions)
  _add_general_u8:        and / add / mov / cmp / csel  (5 instructions)

WHAT THIS PRICES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
An instruction count off one body on one target is an ad-hoc quick spike as
far as magnitude goes.  It prices nothing and no bench harness ran.  What it
establishes structurally is which BODIES exist: the clamp is absent from the
licensed arm and present in the general one, and every coercion in this file
compiled to nothing at all.

The last part is the one that bears on `110`'s contradiction.  A spurious
split's cast is FREE at runtime, so what a missed merge costs is never
instructions.  It costs writing the cast, and at a storage boundary it costs
the program, because there is no cast to write.
