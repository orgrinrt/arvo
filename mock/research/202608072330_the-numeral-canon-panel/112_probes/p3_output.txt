-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
TWO DEFECTS IN THIS RUN'S CHOICE OF WITNESS, FOUND BY ITS AUTHOR
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The numbers above are correct.  Two of the sections chose witnesses that make
them say less than they were built to say, and both are repaired in `p3b`.

1. The INSTRUMENT CHECK compares the control's conservative counter against
   `x - x`, and `x - x` has conservative 0 as well, so it prints
   "the counter is live: False" and reads as a failed check.  The counter IS
   live: `(x + y) - y` in the same run reports 120.  `x - x` was a bad choice
   of witness because its declared extents (0..7 at signed W=4) all propagate
   to [-b, b], which fits the container, so the rule discharges and there is
   nothing to be conservative about.  My prediction P2 said a repeated leaf
   gives conservative > 0 and that is wrong as stated: a repeated leaf gives
   conservatism only where the lost correlation crosses a container edge.

2. The ORACLE section runs on the two cases whose conservatism is 0, so it
   compares two rules on a region where they cannot differ, and reports 8/8
   against 8/8 and 36/64 against 36/64.  That is a true statement about a
   region chosen so that it could not be informative.  Repaired in `p3b` by
   running the oracle on `(x + y) - y` and `x * (y - y)`, where the corner
   rule is measured conservative.

Neither defect touches the unsound column, which is 0 on every shape here and
in `p3b`, nor the finding that conservatism is non-zero on some shapes, which
is what bounds `111` F111-9.
