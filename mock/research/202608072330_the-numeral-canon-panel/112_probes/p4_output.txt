p4. Where a missed merge actually costs something
==========================================================================

S1. Monomorphic sites
  sum over the radix-2 spelling  : 7
  sum over the radix-10 spelling : 7
  neither call mentions the other spelling, so the cost is zero

S2. One function over both spellings, by abstracting the parameter
  sum_any_radix is ONE generic function and both arrays above went through it
  sum_any_policy likewise accepts Obs<Sat> and Obs<Wrap>: 15 and 2
