THE INSTRUMENT CHECK FAILED, AND THE FAILURE IS INFORMATIVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The mutation at the bottom reports "a root-only check is unsound on 0/4096
extents", which reads as the per-node discipline being unnecessary.  It is not.
The mutation could not fire, for a reason that is a limitation of EVERY sweep
in this file's probes and is worth more than the mutation was.

Every declared extent in p1, p2b, p3b, p5, p7 and p7b has the form [0, b].
With every leaf's lower bound pinned at zero, the root of a chain of additions
over a non-negative domain is the WIDEST node in the term, so a root that fits
implies every intermediate fits, and a root-only check is equivalent to a
per-node check by construction.  There is no counterexample inside the swept
region, so the mutation measures nothing.

The per-node discipline IS load-bearing and `p7c` builds the witness by hand,
with a two-endpoint extent, which is the shape `111` section 12 alternative B
names as untested and `82` F6 actually uses.

The consequence for everything above: every count in this file is predicated on
one-sided extents.  A two-endpoint window is strictly more expressive and none
of these numbers is claimed for it.
