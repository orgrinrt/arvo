# 160. Formalising the primitive

My job here is construction rather than attack. The ninth unit has converged, in its reply round, on
a shape nobody has stated exactly: types as the degenerate case of lenses (`157` S-8, conceded and
adopted by `159`), and adequacy as two obligations of different kinds (`157` sections 3.2 to 3.4,
rebuilt at source by `158`). This file plugs the holes in that shape, determines the bounds each
clause holds in, and states the thing exactly with its predicates. It is not the canon candidate;
it is what the candidate compresses.
