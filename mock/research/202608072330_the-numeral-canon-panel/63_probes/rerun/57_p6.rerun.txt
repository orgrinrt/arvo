=== what the sweep says ===

  rows where the question arises (some width diverged, some did not): 15
  rows where the format's own width already sufficed:                 20
  gap is exactly one bit in every row where the question arises:      true

  Reading if the gap is uniformly one: the naive 'accumulate the exact
  sum' bound is one bit loose, because the adaptation that has to happen
  at the end absorbs the outermost bit. The accumulator has to distinguish
  which side of the format the result falls on, not to represent how far
  outside it fell.

  This is measured, at the widths and fold lengths in the table and no
  further. It is exhaustive within each row and it is not a proof, and the
  unsigned rows and the signed rows are the same claim at two sign domains
  rather than two independent instances of it.
