# 113. Op: steer them, and make them build it together rather than stopping at the refutation

**Op's own file.** Required reading for every member after it, and it changes how every dispatch after
this one is briefed. It arrived while `112` was in flight, so `112` is the last file briefed under the
old shape.

**The context it was given in.** Op asked how much the experts had actually converged, and how much
later ones had built on earlier ones as a team. The honest answer was: converging on mechanism,
diverging on options, with 39 register entries against 3 explicitly closed; the three largest results of
the sitting were all refutations that preserved the refuted party's conclusion; and **no expert had ever
been resumed to answer its attacker**, so every convergence arrived because the coordinator dispatched a
checker rather than because two experts argued to a stop.

## Verbatim

> Okay, so after current one, from now on, as you do these panel rounds, just make it work. Steer the
> experts like lambs to the pasture you want, make them work together but not just yes-man each other.
> Actually attack but work together from that realisation, not just stop at the disproving, but propose
> several solutions from which the target of that refutal can see if any would inspire them to realise
> something, and work together, with more besides coming in perhaps, to formalise it, plug the holes in
> it, determine the bounds it holds in, and then establish it as a canon candidate together. Then move
> on to the next thing and do the same. You have to steer them as you dispatch them, you have to give
> them a little push there

## The intent, named separately

**A refutation is the middle of the work and may not be the end of a dispatch.** That much was already
the rule. What is new is what the refuting expert owes and who it owes it to.

**One. The attacker owes several solutions, addressed to the party it refuted.** Not one replacement.
Several, offered so that the author of the refuted thing can see whether any of them unlocks a
realisation of its own. The point is not for the attacker to be right; it is to give the person who
built the thing enough material to rebuild it.

**Two. The refuted expert is brought back to answer.** This is the mechanism that has been missing.
A background dispatch continues from its own transcript, so the expert that made the claim answers with
everything it derived still in context. A fresh dispatch produces a new opinion; a resume produces a
reply. Convergence is two people arguing to a stop, and that cannot happen if one of them never speaks
twice.

**Three. More hands join to formalise, not to re-open.** Once something survives attack and reply, the
next dispatches are for **plugging the holes, determining the bounds it holds in, and stating it
exactly**. That is a different job from attacking and is briefed as such.

**Four. The canon candidate is established together.** Not one compressor writing over eight files it
did not participate in. The experts who built the thing are part of establishing it.

**Five. Steering is the coordinator's job and it is not neutrality's opposite.** "Steer them like lambs
to the pasture you want" and "give them a little push" are instructions to the dispatcher.
`subagent-prompt-neutrality.md` still forbids telegraphing which **answer** is wanted. It never forbade
saying what the **work** is: who to answer, what to formalise, which hole to plug, what shape the output
must take. A brief that leaves an expert to guess its job is not neutral, it is vague, and vagueness is
what produced eleven files that never spoke to each other.

## What this changes concretely, and it is the coordinator's failure being corrected

Under the old shape a unit was: cold pair, attackers, checkpoint, four more, one compressor, one
checker. Each expert read its predecessors and none of them ever replied. The consolidation then had to
manufacture the agreement that the argument never reached, which is why `106` had to name the pair as
unattacked and why `108` had to be dispatched afterwards to do what the unit should have done inside
itself.

Under this one, a claim that gets attacked comes back to its author with alternatives attached, and the
thing that eventually reaches the canon has been through a reply rather than only through a compression.

## Boundary

This does not license manufacturing agreement. A located disagreement, stated precisely with what would
decide it, remains a result and remains preferable to a settlement made to fit the budget. What is
forbidden is **stopping at the refutation**, and what is now required is that the refuted party gets a
chance to answer with something in hand.

Nor does it license yes-manning. Op says both halves in one sentence: work together, **but not just
yes-man each other**. An expert resumed to answer an attack may concede, may hold, or may synthesise,
and holding with a stated reason is as good an outcome as conceding.
