p1 (148): are the two unsigned F=0 merges one mechanism or two?
W=5, F=0, exhaustive.

=== A. the OVERFLOW merge at unsigned addition (140's refuted P3) ===
low branch taken: 0 of 1024 operand pairs
SaturateBoth and SaturateHighOnly agree: yes, on every pair
  -> mechanism is REACHABILITY: the branch that distinguishes them is never entered

=== control (i): the same two positions at unsigned SUBTRACTION ===
low branch taken: 496 of 1024 operand pairs
the two positions agree: no, they separate
  control fires: the counter counts and the positions can separate,
  so A's zero is a real zero rather than a blind one.

=== B. does the congruence proposition even apply at a single addition? ===
  single addition, Wrap: early vs late differ at 0 triples
  single addition, SaturateBoth: early vs late differ at 0 triples
  single addition, SaturateHighOnly: early vs late differ at 0 triples
  -> a single reduction site has nothing to relocate, so the two
     intermediate positions coincide at EVERY overflow position,
     including wrapping, where the congruence says nothing.

=== C. where the congruence IS the mechanism: two reduction sites ===
  multiply-add, Unsigned, SaturateBoth: early vs late differ at 0 triples
  multiply-add, Unsigned, SaturateHighOnly: early vs late differ at 0 triples
  multiply-add, Signed, SaturateBoth: early vs late differ at 11986 triples
  multiply-add, Signed, SaturateHighOnly: early vs late differ at 7664 triples

=== control (ii): signed two-sided saturation must NOT be a congruence ===
  control fires: the sweep can see a congruence failure when there is one.

=== verdict ===
The two merges at unsigned F=0 are on DIFFERENT AXES with DIFFERENT
MECHANISMS:
  139's merge: the INTERMEDIATE axis, on an operation with two reduction
    sites, mechanised by the one-sided-clamp congruence. That is the
    workspace rule's sentence and 139 says so itself.
  140's refuted P3: the OVERFLOW axis, on a single-site addition,
    mechanised by the low branch being unreachable. The congruence is
    not available as an explanation because there is nothing to relocate.
