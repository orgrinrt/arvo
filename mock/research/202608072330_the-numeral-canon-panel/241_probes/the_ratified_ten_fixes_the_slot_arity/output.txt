=== the slot coordinate, and what an arity of n would cost ===
slot axis      : MIN, MAX, WIDTH                                    -> 3 per axis
everything else: RADIX, SIGNED, BASE, SLOPE, MAGNITUDES, PHASE_NUM, PHASE_DEN -> 7
  slot arity 1 -> 10 coordinates
  slot arity 2 -> 13 coordinates
  slot arity 3 -> 16 coordinates
  slot arity 4 -> 19 coordinates

FINDING: the ratified ten reconstructs exactly, as 3n + 7 at n = 1, and the
partition into six and four is the ruling's own. An affine predicate of slot
arity 2 would need thirteen coordinates and of arity 3 sixteen, so the door
would not carry ten. The slot arity is therefore fixed at one by a ratified
COUNT rather than by a reading of the membership sentence's spelling, and the
open question of whether 'one parameterisation' meant one scalar does not have
to be settled on the wording: the count settles it.
