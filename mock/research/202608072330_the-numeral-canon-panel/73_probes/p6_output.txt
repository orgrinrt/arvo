READING. The membership test's law verdicts are not uniformly stuck at the
model width. Where the panel's own law frame supplies a geometric condition
on the range, the verdict is O(1) in the width and needs no sweep at all, so
membership for that family is decidable wherever hosting is. Where it does
not, the sweep and its transfer proviso stand. So a canon sentence about
membership owes one distinction it does not currently make: which of its
verdicts are computed and which are assumed uniform above the model width.

BOUND, stated. This does not establish the closed form at real widths; it
establishes that the closed form agrees with the sweep wherever the sweep
can run, which is the same epistemic position `63` section 4.2 holds and no
stronger. What is new is the consequence for the membership test.
