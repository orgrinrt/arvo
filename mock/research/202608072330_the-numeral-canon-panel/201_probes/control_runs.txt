=== 1. what_cannot_be_written.sh ===
BASELINE   4 answered, 46 open, 29 proposed; 18 carry an unblocks, 15 of those open.
CONTROL    a ruling edge planted at the first OPEN question, plus one at a slug
           naming no question at all:
             5 answered, 45 open, 29 proposed; 14 of the unblocks-carrying open.
           Both move the right way and the nonsense slug is not counted, or
           answered would read 6.

THE CONTROL THAT WAS NOT ONE, kept because it is the point. The first version
planted on the FIRST question in the file, which is already answered, so the
run came out identical to the baseline. That is indistinguishable from an
instrument reading no edges at all. A control has to be able to move the number,
and choosing the target by position rather than by state does not guarantee it.

A SECOND DEFECT, display only, found by reading the output rather than the
count. `unblocks` was parsed with a continuation flag set unconditionally, so a
single-line value swallowed the `note` that followed it and every quotation
would have been two fields joined. The totals were unaffected, which is why it
survived a run: it shows only when you read what it printed, and reading what it
printed was the entire purpose.
