## What this panel is

Arvo has no canon. This panel writes it: the primitives become named compositions over one format
concept, with number systems and derived algebraic laws underneath.

It is not a fresh start on the problem. It is a fresh start on the **process**, seeded with what the
previous panel actually established, so that nobody has to read 320 files to contribute. The
predecessor spent much of its life learning how to run a panel while running one. Those lessons are
written down now, in `RULES.md`, instead of being rediscovered.

## The method: keep options open, and never ratify early

Added 2026-08-08 on op's instruction, after he answered the eight questions in `28` and pointed at
the sibling panel's brief for a memo this one never got. **Read `28` in full; this section is its
summary and does not replace it.**

**This is why the previous panel failed, in op's own account:**

> They, along with me, started making calls and ratifications very early on, and then we were locked
> in a mess where nothing seemed to fit together, because calls were ratified before all its
> consumers or synergies were known, and turned out to be bad choices, but there was no way to know
> that before getting deeper into this work.

The failure was **ratifying at the wrong time**, not answering wrongly. No amount of care on an
individual question prevents it, so the method has to differ.

**What replaces it**, in his words:

> we need not, and should not, commit to any one concept yet, but rather, keep the options written
> down, open, then when we muse things for the canon, we reflect it over each option, and clearly
> say which ones would have best fit, which ones wouldn't fit well, and that will eventually give us
> a path forward, when we see if some shape serves most of the good options elsewhere the best.

Three obligations follow, and they are how a member should work.

**Write every option down, in full.** An option referenced by number is worthless and has already
cost this project a settled answer permanently. `28` records the twenty-four that were live when he
answered the eight questions.

**Carry them forward, all of them.** Later questions are evaluated **against every live option**,
and a file should say which options its finding fits well, which it fits badly, and which it kills.
Killing one is a real result and is worth as much as proposing a new one.

**The path emerges rather than being chosen.** When one shape turns out to serve most of the good
options elsewhere, that is the answer, and it will be visible rather than argued. The selection
criterion is his: the shape that **serves all other parts of arvo best**, not the shape that is best
in isolation.

**An option set is not a boundary.** On the denotation question he took the explore option and then
widened it: "don't even restrict the panel to these three. Free reign to converge by theory and
logic to the best one." A fourth answer nobody has written down is admissible on any of the eight.

**Op's statements are direction unless he names them as locked.** He has said he will name the
locks. Treating a preference of his as a ruling is the previous panel's mistake committed with his
words instead of the panel's own. His recorded instinct that there is one numeral family is exactly
this case: it is an instinct, he said so, and he said not to act on it.

### The horizon: no settlements for roughly the first hundred files

Op, 2026-08-08, extending what `04` had scoped to a single night:

> We will adopt the very same "explore, don't settle" angle for the first 100 expert stretches I
> think

**So exploration is the panel's standing mode, not tonight's exception.** `04` said no settlements
overnight; this replaces its scope while keeping its terms. A member arriving at file forty is
working under the same instruction as a member arriving at file five.

The number is deliberately approximate and he said so. It is a horizon rather than a counter, and
nobody should be watching for file 100 to arrive and treat it as a bell. What it fixes is the
**expectation**: a member is not behind schedule for closing nothing, and a stretch that opens three
well-argued directions and says what would distinguish them has done its job.

Two things follow that are easy to get wrong.

**This is not a licence to be vague.** Exploring is not the same as declining to conclude. A file
still owes findings with citations, probes that establish or refute, and a plain statement of what
its evidence supports. What it does not owe, and must not manufacture, is a ruling. The difference
is between "the evidence closes this route, here is the diagnostic" and "therefore the design should
be X".

**Killing an option is still allowed and is still valuable.** Not settling means not choosing among
live options. It does not mean carrying dead ones. When a route is closed by a compiled refusal, a
measurement, or an argument nobody can answer, say so and put it on the droplist with what would
have to be overturned to reopen it. The option space should be **shrinking from the bottom** the
whole way through, even while nothing is chosen at the top.

Two failure modes this method has in a panel, both seen in the sibling:

**Churning the vocabulary.** Everything being open is a licence to question every element, not an
instruction to replace one. Where a name or a boundary survives scrutiny, **keep it and say it
held**. Renaming reads as contribution and agreeing reads as passivity, which is why a panel told
only that everything is open will rename things and call it progress.

**Stopping at the first well-supported answer.** In the sibling panel op's instinct that a
derivation had stopped one step short was tested six times and held six times, each time because the
panel had stopped at the first answer it could support.

## What is fixed

The list is short, and it got shorter on 2026-08-08.

**Op's ratified calls**, which are fewer than the previous panel believed. See `01` section 0. Note
that `28` adds nothing to this list: all eight of the questions it answers are open.

**The acceptance criterion**, which is op's erasure gate: the consumer expresses usage in bits and
bytes, the typestate derives the matching container and numeral **representations**, it validates, and
it erases on lowering. All four at once, no caveats. Every mechanism proposal answers to this.

The plural and the word "numeral" are op's own, quoted at `seed/SETTLED_container.md:33-35`: "have the
typestate derive the matching container and numeral representations, then validate, and erase". They
had been lost through **four** generations of restatement, including in this brief, which every member
is told is required reading and part of what is fixed. `44` found it by reading the source rather than
any account of it. **How many outputs the derivation has is exactly what `15`, `16` and `44` are
about**, so a criterion that silently reads singular was prejudicing the question it exists to pose.

**The workspace discipline**: `#![no_std]`, no `alloc`, no `dyn`, no `TypeId`, sizes const, no bare
primitives at public positions, harness the type system, the writing style.

**The forbidden features**: `generic_const_exprs`, `generic_const_args`, full `specialization`, and
`-Znext-solver=globally`. Allowed: `min_generic_const_args`, `adt_const_params`, `min_specialization`
and the const-traits family. Pin `nightly-2026-05-28`, `rustc 1.98.0-nightly (57d06900f 2026-05-27)`.
A bare `rustc` outside the repository tree resolves to stable, so pass the toolchain explicitly.

**Everything else is open**, including the vocabulary, the decomposition, and any name.
