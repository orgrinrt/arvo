## The `Strategy` name collision: notko renames

**The options put to him:** (1) **arvo renames**, on the ground that the canon is being written now so
it is the cheap moment and arvo's is the consumer-facing concept; (2) **notko renames**, on the ground
that notko's is the profile-tier concept and if the two are not one mechanism then notko's name carries
the wrong connotation; (3) **neither, and the canon states the disambiguation**, cheapest in edits and
leaving every future reader to rediscover the collision.

**The intent.** The name `Strategy` belongs to arvo's concept. notko's profile-tier concept is a
different thing, he has already ruled the two are "not one mechanism" but share "synergy, nothing more"
(`144b`, quoted at `25` section 5.1), and the shared name asserts a relationship that does not exist.
The cost falls on notko rather than on arvo, and it falls there because arvo's is the one the name fits.

**What this settles and what it does not.** It settles which side moves. It does not name notko's
replacement, which is notko's business and not this panel's, and it is not a licence for this panel to
edit notko. Recorded here so the canon can rely on `Strategy` meaning arvo's concept without
qualification; the notko-side rename is a separate piece of work in that repo.

**He is right and the scope was the dispatcher's error.** He had asked for the standing inputs and the

genuinely open it is and however long it has been parked.
