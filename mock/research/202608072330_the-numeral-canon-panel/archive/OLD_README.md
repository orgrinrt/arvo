# Archived panel artifacts

**Anything in here is carried, not live.** It may be read and grepped. It may not be cited as support
for anything, and nothing in the live panel should depend on it.

The convention is the workspace's: a subdirectory named `archive/` is the marker of archived status,
the way an archived design round works. Everything flat in the panel directory is current.

## Why these three moved

The panel had seven catalogue-shaped files at the top level and the lead designer said the distinction
between them was not instinctive. It was not, and the reason was that three of them were carried
artifacts sitting at the same level as live ones, accounting for 8,699 of the panel's 10,518 catalogue
lines. Two of the three opened by declaring themselves untrustworthy.

What is left flat answers four different questions a reader actually has: what op said (`INTENTS.md`),
what is open (`OPTIONS.md`), what is closed and what would reopen it (`DROPLIST.md`), and how to work
here (`RULES.md`, with `00_brief.md`).

## What each of these was

**`SETTLED.md`.** The predecessor panel's index of what it had established, with a provenance rung per
row. Its own header states that its top rung is wrong: every row marked RATIFIED was classified under
the old reading of ratification, before op corrected it, and the rows were deliberately left
unrelabelled because which ones followed a real convergence cannot be decided by relabelling. So it is
a classification that tells you not to trust it. Importing that rung into `INTENTS.md` was a mistake
made and corrected on 2026-08-08; see `39`.

**`CANON_CANDIDATE.md`.** The predecessor's twelfth and last consolidation, 7,115 lines, carried
verbatim. Its own header calls it "carried forward and not trusted", stale in named places, and "a
starting text to be re-derived from, never a citation".

**`MORNING.md`.** The map written through the night of 2026-08-07 to be presented to op in the morning.
That morning happened. Every checkpoint that examined it found defects in it: rung inflation, a doubled
count, a claim spliced across two source commits, a false "exactly when", and 295 numeric tokens
against zero line-level anchors while its sources carried 297. It is kept because it is the record of
what was presented and when, and because several corrections in the numbered files are legible only
against it.

## The one thing to take from here rather than re-derive

Nothing. If something in here matters, it belongs in a live file, restored from the source that
established it rather than copied from here.
