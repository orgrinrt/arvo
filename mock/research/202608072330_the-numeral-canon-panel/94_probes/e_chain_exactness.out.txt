  reading: the add fold is essentially free, since a fold of 2^h terms costs
  h bits. The multiply chain is what runs out, and it runs out linearly in the
  chain length, which is a compile-time-visible quantity wherever the chain is
  written out. That is the predicate the arm is gated on.
