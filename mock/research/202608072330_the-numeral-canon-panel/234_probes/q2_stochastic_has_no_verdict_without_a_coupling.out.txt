== WHAT THIS REFUTES ==
`228` F4, the `fails` side clause "stochastic by construction since the
eager step is then not a function of the triple". The premise is true and
the conclusion does not follow: the comparison becomes a question about a
joint distribution, two defensible couplings give two answers, and neither
`228` nor any instrument in this panel names one. The clause therefore does
not discharge the `construction` warrant that
`ruling::the_warrant_is_a_token_and_a_clause_on_the_values_side` defines,
which obliges an instrument that varied the axis and found NO movement.
This instrument varied it and found movement.
