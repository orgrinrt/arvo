============================================================================================
READING
============================================================================================
A conversion function does not by itself determine a mixed operation, and the cells
above say exactly when it does. What determines the operation is how many adaptation
points the schedule contains and where they sit, which is `63` C9's statement that the
schedule is part of the function's meaning, arriving at the place two systems meet.

So `66`'s two questions are one obligation asked at two arities: a conversion is the
unary case with the target declared, a resolution the n-ary case with the target
derived, and both owe the same three things, a common ambient domain, a target
representable set, and which reduction governs the loss. Only the third is a choice,
neither operand's own system is privileged to make it, and the choice stops being
observable exactly in the cells where coherence and nested absorption both hold.
