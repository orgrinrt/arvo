READING
================================================================================================
The two properties a narrowing crossing can have are the two law families the concept
already carries, one each, and all four combinations are inhabited, so neither implies
the other. The crossing therefore demands nothing new of a number system: it consumes
C4's classification. That makes crossings a THIRD consumer of the two families beside
the two `67` section 5 names.

And the sufficiency half fails: coherence of the target reduction makes the crossing
commute only while the source's own reduction has not acted. Once it has, two
adaptations sit in the schedule and the square does not close. That is `63` C1's
unfused condition arriving at the crossing layer, and the count of adaptation points,
not the choice of reduction, is what decides the answer.
