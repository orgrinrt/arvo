READING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The blank cell has a value: 192/256 under two's complement, 0/256 under
  excess-K, and it is the same under either policy in both encodings.

  PART (c) OF THE PREDICTION IS REFUTED, and this is why the cell was worth filling.
  The pattern-level number is IDENTICAL across wrap and saturate for a fixed encoding,
  asserted above rather than eyeballed. The container's own operation is modular
  addition whatever the type says, so the pattern level CANNOT SEE THE REDUCTION.

  That is the same fact p1's index-3 row reports from the other side: restrategising
  preserves the pattern-level operation at 256/256 precisely because the pattern level
  is blind to the coordinate being moved. Two instruments, one structural fact.

  PARTS (a) AND (b) HELD. Under two's complement the levels coincide at 192/256
  against 192/256; under excess-K they part at 192/256 against 0/256.
  Nothing about the crossing changed between those two rows. The ENCODING did, and the
  encoding is a coordinate this crossing does not move.

  So the correction to p1's table is not one number. The PATTERN column is not
  determined by the index at all: it is determined by the coordinates both sides carry,
  and it is blind to the reduction. p1's table read as though the index fixed both
  columns, and it fixes only the first.
