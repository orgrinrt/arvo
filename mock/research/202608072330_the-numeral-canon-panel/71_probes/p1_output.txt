==============================================================================
THE FIVE CROSSING CLASSES, addition
==============================================================================
index                   value map   pattern map   VALUE ops       PATTERN ops
1  ambient domain       none        identity      108/256 vs xor  n/a
2  representable set    inclusion   sign-extend   192/256         not measured
3  adaptation           identity    identity      192/256         256/256
4  encoding             identity    bijection     256/256         0/256
5  container            identity    shift         256/256         256/256

Crossings preserving the VALUE-level operation at 100%: ['4  encoding', '5  container']
Crossings preserving the PATTERN-level operation at 100%: ['3  adaptation', '5  container']

PREDICTION HELD. `67` section 4's universal, 'No crossing preserves operations at
100%', is refuted at the two indices it did not measure. The universal was drawn
from three of five classes.

And the mirror pair, which is the structural point:
  index 4 preserves the VALUE operation totally and destroys the PATTERN operation
    (256/256 against 0/256)
  index 1 preserves the PATTERN map totally and has no value operation to preserve
  index 3 preserves the pattern map AND the untyped pattern operation and destroys
    the value operation (256/256 against 192/256), which is exactly
    why it is the crossing that looks free: it is free at every level a machine can
    see, and not free at the level that decides the answer.
