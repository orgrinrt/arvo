the settlement, in the author's own words
====================================================================================
  I meant the earned reading and I should have said so. The gate exists because I had
  just published a false positive, so the thing it guards is the POSITIVE claim: an arm
  counts as non-dominated only if it beats every rival somewhere by more than the noise.
  Guarding the negative claim instead, which is what both of z2's readings do, admits
  arms that merely escape an established domination, and that is the wrong direction for
  a finding whose failure mode is claiming an arm is special when it is not.

  What 145 gets right and I owe it: the direction is not stated anywhere in 144, the
  finding is unusable without it, and z2 is the file that made that visible.
