== what this establishes ==
  Five promotions, five stated tests, and every one of them is an ORDERING
  or an EXCLUSION test rather than a judgement of how good an argument is:
  a blind commit landing before the reading commit; mutual non-ancestry;
  promotion on the gate rather than the count; agreement traceable to a
  shared reading excluded from what the promotion rests on; and a seat whose
  blindness leaked withdrawing as a blind instance while its earlier
  commits still count.

  The fourth of those covers the case 246 and 247 hand back. A seat that
  agreed with a conclusion because it read the conclusion is agreement from
  a shared reading, and the corpus already excludes that from a promotion.
