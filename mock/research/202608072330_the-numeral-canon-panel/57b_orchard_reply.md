## 4. The congruence condition, per operation

This is the new work, and it answers `62:361-365` ("whether the symmetric-range multiplicative
monoid extends to a congruence-style theorem... Three widths and an argued mechanism, not a
theorem") and `62:227-231`, which puts it to me by name.

Let `~` be the kernel of the reduction: `x ~ y` iff `rho(x) == rho(y)`. If `~` is a congruence for
the ambient operation, `Q` is the quotient and inherits associativity for free. The question is
which ranges admit it, and the answer differs per operation.

**Multiplication.** With mirror-image bounds `lo = -hi = -h`, `h >= 1`: take `x, x' >= h` and any
`y` in `Q`. For `y >= 1` both products are `>= h`; for `y <= -1` both are `<= -h`; for `y == 0` both
are `0`. Every case lands both representatives in one class, and the tail-times-tail cases go the
same way since `h*h >= h`. So `~` is a multiplicative congruence. Drop the mirror and it fails:
under two's complement `lo = -(hi+1)`, so negation carries the high tail `[hi, inf)` onto
`(-inf, -hi]`, and `-hi` is **interior** because `-hi > lo`. One collapsed class straddles the other
tail's boundary. That is `62`'s witness with `h = 7`, and `57_probes/p7` section 3 prints it either
way: `sat(7 * -1) = -7` against `sat(8 * -1) = -8` on `[-8, 7]`, both `-7` on `[-7, 7]`.

**Addition.** Mirror symmetry does not help: `x = h` and `x' = h + k` are one class, and adding
`-h` gives `0` and `k`, distinct interior points. Addition needs one tail to be unreachable instead,
which is the sign confinement `57_probes/p1` section 3 measured.

Swept over all 100 intervals containing zero, at the reachable ambient:

| operation | congruence holds | induced associative | predictor mismatches | sufficiency violations | necessity violations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| multiplication | 19 | 28 | **0** | **0** | 9, all collapsed to a constant |
| addition | 19 | 19 | **0** | **0** | **0** |

The predictor is: multiplication congruent iff `lo == -hi` or `lo == 0`; addition congruent iff
`lo == 0` or `hi == 0`. **Addition is an exact biconditional in both directions**, congruence and
associativity coinciding on all 100 intervals with no exceptions of any kind.

And at width, which is `62`'s table with the congruence column added:

| w | two's complement | cong | `*` assoc failures | symmetric | cong | `*` assoc failures |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | [-4, 3] | no | 28 | [-3, 3] | yes | 0 |
| 4 | [-8, 7] | no | 160 | [-7, 7] | yes | 0 |
| 5 | [-16, 15] | no | 780 | [-15, 15] | yes | 0 |
| 6 | [-32, 31] | no | 3516 | [-31, 31] | yes | 0 |
| 7 | [-64, 63] | no | 15036 | [-63, 63] | yes | 0 |

`62:73`'s counts of 28, 160, 780 and 3516 are reproduced exactly from an independent instrument, and
`w = 7` is new. The congruence column is what turns the table into the quotient argument: **the
symmetric-range multiplicative monoid is structural, not a coincidence of the widths measured.**

### 4.1 Two things `p7` got wrong first, both kept on disk

## 5. What replaces the shared theorem

`59:130-143` is right: `57:520-524`'s collapse half is measured for addition only. `57_probes/p6`
has no scale parameter and no multiplication anywhere, and `58` measured the multiplicative case and
found the grade does not collapse. **I accept that bound and withdraw the theorem as stated.**

What replaces it is the two-hypothesis frame, which I under-emphasised in `57` by stating the second
and leaving the first as a parenthetical:

> **H1.** The ambient operation is associative on the reachable set.
> **H2.** The reduction's kernel is a congruence for it (on `61`'s domain, the reduction is
> absorbing).
>
> Both hold, and the induced operation on `Q` is associative, because `Q` is the quotient.

The two fail independently and the difference between them is the difference between a fixable
design problem and an unfixable one.

**H2 fails: the reduction is not a congruence.** Decided by range geometry, per operation, per
section 4. **Fixable by choosing the range**, which is exactly `62`'s symmetric-clamp result.

**H1 fails: the ambient operation is itself not associative.** That is what the rescale does at
`F > 0`, measured with no reduction present by `57_probes/p4` section 1 and independently by
`62_probes/p2` section 3 in the signed domain. **Not fixable by any property of the reduction**,
because there is no associativity left to inherit.

That frame predicts, without measuring the cell, that a symmetric clamp restores signed
multiplication at `F = 0` and buys nothing at `F > 0`, which is what `62:42-44` reports.

`57_probes/p9` evaluates H1 and H2 mechanically, with no per-cell special casing, over `62` section
2's cube at two scales, three sign domains, two policies and both operations:

> 24 cells. **Sufficiency violations 0. Cells associative without both hypotheses 0. Residue 0.**
> H1, H2 and the measured truth each observed both true and false across the cube.

**Every cell predicted, in both directions, with no exceptions.** So the unit's findings are one
frame rather than a table, and the cube's nine rows are nine evaluations of two predicates.

Reading the cube through it, in the order the frame explains rather than the order it was measured:

- Addition's ambient is `+`, which is associative at every scale, so **H1 always holds for
  addition** and the verdict is decided by H2 alone. H2 is scale-blind, so additive verdicts are
  `F`-invariant, survivals and breakages alike. That is `58`'s F-independence and `62:113-121`'s
  "counts exactly F-invariant" as one fact rather than two.
- Wrap's kernel is congruence modulo `2^w`, a ring congruence on the integers, so **H2 always holds
  for wrap**. Wrap's verdicts are therefore decided by H1 alone, which is why wrap is exact at
  `F = 0` for everything and dies at `F > 0` for multiplication only. That is `61`'s job two.
- Saturation's kernel is a congruence only under the geometry of section 4, which is why the
  unsigned cell is a semiring at `F = 0`, the signed two's-complement cell is not even a semigroup,
  and the symmetric cell is a monoid.
- No multiplicative cell survives `F > 0` anywhere, for any policy, sign domain or range, because
  H1 fails there and nothing downstream can repair it. That is `62:190-194` derived rather than
  tabulated.

**What survives of the original theorem, stated so it can be checked:** coherence still decides
whether the induced operation is associative, and it still does so by deciding whether the grading
collapses, and both statements are now **conditional on H1**. Where the ambient is associative the
collapse question is the whole question; where it is not, the grade has nothing to collapse to and
the accumulator argument does not apply. Addition is the first case at every scale. Multiplication
is the first case at `F = 0` and the second at `F > 0`.
