-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
TWO SECTIONS OF THIS RUN ARE VACUOUS, FOUND BY ITS AUTHOR
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. P4's "no-mul" rows report 0 unsound over 0 checks-differ.  With every
   declaration one-sided from zero, the root of an add/sub chain over a
   one-signed domain is the widest node, so the root-only and per-node checks
   are the same predicate by construction and the zero says nothing about arm
   W1.  That is `112` F112-23 biting a probe of mine for the third time.

   The mul rows are NOT affected: 540 to 1091 checks-differ, and 1984 to 12020
   unsound at F > 0 against 0 at F = 0.  Those rows carry the finding.

2. P5 draws its operands from `P.values()`, which are all already in range, so
   `R(a) = a` and the homomorphism identity is trivially satisfied whatever the
   grid is.  Reporting 0/256 at the FINE grid, where the P1 table reports
   1234/2116 over a wider ambient span, is the tell.  It measures nothing.

Both repaired in `p4b`: two-endpoint declarations for P4, and an ambient span
wider than the container for P5.
