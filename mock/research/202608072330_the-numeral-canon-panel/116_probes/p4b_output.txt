-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
P5's PREDICTION WAS WRONG IN ITS MECHANISM AND RIGHT IN ITS CONCLUSION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
I predicted the homomorphism would be restored once the operands sit on a grid
coarse enough that their products need no requantisation.  The step-1/2 row
refutes that: every product IS already on the fine grid ("True"), and the
homomorphism still fails on 144 of 529.

The correct condition is stronger and is visible in raw units.  At fraction
width F a value v has raw index v * 2^F, and a product's raw index is
raw_a * raw_b / 2^F.  Replacing raw_a by raw_a + k*n adds k*n*raw_b / 2^F,
which is a multiple of n exactly when raw_b is a multiple of 2^F, that is when
b is a whole multiple of the UNIT.  So:

  the multiplicative homomorphism at F > 0 is restored exactly when the
  operands are declared to lie on the UNIT grid, not merely on a grid whose
  products avoid requantisation.

Step 1/4 fails on 1234/2116, step 1/2 fails on 144/529, step 1 fails on 0/144,
which is that condition and not the one I predicted.
