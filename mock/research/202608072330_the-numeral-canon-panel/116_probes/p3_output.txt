-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE COLUMN OF THIS TABLE IS UNDERSIZED AND MUST NOT BE READ AS A RESULT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "root-only unsound" column sweeps 4800 cells over one-sided declarations
derived from a single bound per leaf, which is far smaller than p1's sweep of
the same question.  It reports 0 at `uW3/sat`, which is a FALSE NEGATIVE: p1,
over 76896 cells with a systematic term enumeration, reports 228 unsound at
that exact primitive.  The `uW3/zero` and `uW3/reflect` zeros in that column
are undersized in the same way and no claim is made from them.

That is the hand-picked-rows failure appearing in my own probe, and it is
worth leaving visible: a column can be live somewhere (`iW3/sat` shows 14) and
still be a false negative on the row a reader is most likely to quote.

WHAT THE FILE ACTUALLY RESTS ON, which is unaffected:

  - the HOMOMORPHISM column, exhaustive over the whole ambient range the
    operands can reach, 0/1452 at wrap and 189 to 376 at sat;
  - the MONOTONICITY column, exhaustive over the same range;
  - the INTERVAL column, exhaustive over every well-ordered pair, 0/1296 at
    sat and 546/1296 at wrap;
  - the P2 search, exhaustive over every map from the window, which is what
    establishes that no non-constant map has both properties.

The licence columns are corroboration and their authoritative values are p1's.
