what this says about the three notions:
  definitional   degenerate at the trivial refinement (the whole
                 carrier, and every rational argument to R)
  reachability   degenerate on the image of the term algebra
  declared       degenerate on a consumer-supplied extent
  All three are the same predicate over different extents. What
  differs is who supplies the extent and when it is known.

and the condition that makes a merge usable:
  the extent must be closed under the signature, or the merged
  identity is not a congruence and substitution walks out of the
  region the licence was granted on. That is one condition covering
  109's 'closure is prior to every law' and 110's P8 congruence
  check, and it is what a const predicate on an arm has to assert.
