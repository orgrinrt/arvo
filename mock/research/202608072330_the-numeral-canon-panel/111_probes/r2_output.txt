What each rule costs to carry, since the composition turns on it.
The corner rule carries two numbers per node whatever the term is.
An affine form carries one coefficient per leaf plus one per
non-constant multiplication, which 112 p8b states and which is a
property of the term rather than of the width.

  term                        corner state  affine state  leaf repeats
  x + y                                  2             2         False
  (x + y) - y                            2             2          True
  (x + y) * z                            2             4         False
  x * (y - y)                            2             3          True
  fold of 2 adds                         2             2         False
  fold of 4 adds                         2             4         False
  fold of 8 adds                         2             8         False
  fold of 16 adds                        2            16         False
  fold of 64 adds                        2            64         False

  On a fold, which is the shape satfold and warm-clamp are built
  around, no leaf repeats, the predicate fires, the corner rule is
  exact, and the affine form costs one coefficient per element for
  nothing. On a correlated term the corner rule loses licences and
  the affine form is what recovers them. That is two arms with one
  const-checkable predicate between them, not a better rule.
