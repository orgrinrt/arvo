reading:
  There is no invariant extent to find, and P5 was wrong to look for
  one. The equivalence is indexed by a DERIVATION: each node carries
  the extent its operands' extents imply, and the merge is checked
  against that node's extent. Composition works because the extent
  propagates, not because it is preserved.

  That is the same move 109 section 8 makes for chain accuracy, one
  level down: the operator is not an endomorphism, and once its
  result is allowed to be a different primitive the property falls
  out of the typing instead of needing a policy.
