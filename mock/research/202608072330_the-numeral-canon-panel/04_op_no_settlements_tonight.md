# 04. Tonight is a breadth pass. Nothing settles.

**Date:** 2026-08-07, as op goes to sleep. **Status:** standing for the night. Required reading for
every dispatch until op returns.

## Op's instruction

> I will be going to sleep, so no more ask user tool; Use my persona to stand in, but treat their
> calls as just that, **not** my calls. Let them steer you and aid you, give you different
> perspectives imitating me, but don't let any of it slip into anything as ratified or canon. All the
> work you do tonight with the panel is prepping and going through a lot of different things, angles,
> concepts, theory, ready to be presented to me when I come back in the morning.

## What tonight is for

**Breadth.** Angles, concepts, theory, directions, alternatives, evaluations. Getting on the same map
and re-establishing the grounding, after a predecessor panel that spent 320 files learning how to
work while working.

Go wide by **category** before going deep by variant. Enumerate the families of approach that have
not been touched at all, then instantiate inside them. Permuting the axes already in hand is not
exploring the design space, and a prior session in this workspace produced two overnight runs of
nothing but permutations before a single fresh agent listed whole categories nobody had opened.

## What tonight is not for

**No settlements. None.** Not by an expert, not by agreement between experts, not by the persona, and
not by the dispatcher. This is stated absolutely because the failure it prevents is the predecessor's
signature failure: a thing filed as closed, built upon, and reopened later at cost.

A file may say *this appears to hold*, *these three readings survive*, *this route is closed and here
is the diagnostic that closed it*. A file may not say *this is settled*, and nothing written tonight
enters `SETTLED.md`.

The ack correction in `01` section 0 already implies this, since ratification requires op and follows
convergence. Tonight makes it explicit and extends it: **even convergence between experts does not
settle anything tonight.** Convergence is a result to present, not a conclusion to adopt.

## The persona, and the exact limit on it

The `orgrinrt` persona stands in overnight so the panel has a designer-shaped perspective to push
against. Its value is real: it steers, it offers angles, it argues in op's register, and it catches
things a domain expert will not.

**Its calls are the persona's, never op's.** They do not ratify. They are not canon. They are not
quoted later as a warrant, and no expert is told that the persona decided something. Every persona
call goes in `PERSONA_CALLS.md`, marked persona-decided with the date, so a later reader can never
mistake one for op's word.

If the persona's steer and op's recorded words appear to conflict, op's words govern and the conflict
itself is worth writing down for the morning.

## What is delivered in the morning

A map, not a verdict. Op returns to:

- the angles opened, and what each one looks like from inside,
- the concepts and theory that turned out to matter, and the ones that did not,
- the routes closed, each with the diagnostic or measurement that closed it,
- the alternatives, described well enough that he can choose between them,
- the questions that are now sharper than they were, and the ones that dissolved,
- everything the persona said, marked as the persona's.

None of it presented as a decision.
