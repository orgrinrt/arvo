**D1. Section 0.2 attributes to my file a count my file contradicts.** `132` 0.2 (and `131` 0.2, and

concurrent load, and that record, not a completed 123, is what `127` inherited." The inheritance
chain should carry the non-termination, because a gate count that silently absorbs an unterminated
crate is the false green wearing attribution's clothes.

**D2. Section 5.2's "every deterministic member is order-preserving" is false as quantified, and my
own committed control is the counterexample.** A deterministic quantisation in the candidate's own
sense (5.2 covers "every deterministic member" of the axis) is a grid-fixing retraction, and
`125_probes/p1`'s parity mode is a committed retraction with 1750 measured inversions. What is true,
and what the degeneracy argument actually needs, is sharper and worth having: **a deterministic
quantisation is order-preserving exactly when it is a per-cell threshold rule**, the round-up set
within each cell being a suffix. `s2` part A verifies both directions exhaustively over every
retraction restriction at three cell resolutions (monotone count equals suffix count equals `m`, of
`2^(m-1)`), with the parity control failing. This is also the deterministic shadow of 5.6's up-set
argument, so it unifies rather than complicates: the monotone deterministic members are exactly the
realisations of the realisation-monotone stochastic family. **Replacement:** "Every member of the
threshold family, which includes every standard mode, is order-preserving, and the threshold shape is
not incidental: a deterministic quantisation is order-preserving exactly when it is a per-cell
threshold rule, so monotonicity is a structure the admitted members share rather than a property
determinism grants." The predicate should also split, since the monotonicity half carries no
operation dimension while the additive half needs `operation = addition`.

**D3. Section 5.3's "no member carries more than one of the first three" is false against the
findings it cites, and the negation attribution drops a member.** `s2` part B, on a fresh instrument:
floor carries the order bound **and** exact staged composition; toward_zero carries exact composition
**and** negation symmetry; half_even carries negation symmetry, which 5.3 attributes to toward_zero
alone against my T5 row. The sentence appears in neither source file; it entered at `131` section 6
and was compressed forward, which is the invented-plausible-exclusivity failure a signature check
exists to catch. There **is** a true exclusivity and it is worth stating instead: **the order bound
and negation symmetry are never carried together**, because negation swaps the adjoints, confirmed in
the same probe. **Replacement:** "Four exact laws are available, each carried by a proper subclass,
and the subclasses overlap: the two adjoints carry the order bound and compose exactly across
precisions; the toward-zero member composes exactly and carries negation symmetry, at the price of
respecting no translation; the even-tie nearest member carries negation symmetry and the optimal
error bound, with neither exact composition nor a one-sided bound; and the additive law in
expectation is carried only by leaving the category of functions. No member carries the order bound
and negation symmetry at once, because negation exchanges the two adjoints, and no member carries
all of the first three."
