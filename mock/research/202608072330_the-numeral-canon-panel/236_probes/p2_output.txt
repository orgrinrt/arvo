VERDICT
  `Biased<4, 1, 1>` is a shipped format of the shipped inventory,
  it is half-step-biased by construction (PHASE_DEN = 2),
  it carries no additive identity, and it carries the value one.
  So `format::has_additive_identity`'s doc clause "and takes one
  off with it" is false of a format this crate ships.

  And arm D is a second, independent defect in the same function.
  `has_additive_identity` tests `PHASE_NUM == 0` rather than whether
  the phase is a whole multiple of the quantum, so at a phase of one
  whole step it reports no additive identity while zero is on the
  grid. `PHASE_DEN` is read by no function in the crate at all.
