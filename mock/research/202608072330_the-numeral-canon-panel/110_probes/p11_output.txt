P11. congruence is free; lifting is not
==============================================================================
OBLIGATION ONE: does EQUALITY transport through a construction?
  each base is compared against a RELABELLED twin (identical tables,
  different construction path) and against a SABOTAGED twin (one
  operation genuinely changed). the sabotaged arm must fail, or the
  relabelled arm's zero means nothing.

  construction   relabelled: same?    sabotaged: same?
  product2                     4/4                 0/4
  complex                      4/4                 0/4
  dual                         4/4                 0/4
  interval                     4/4                 0/4

  congruence failures on the relabelled twin: 0
  sabotaged twins that slipped through as 'same': 0
  the control fails as it must and the real arm passes, so the zero
  is a measurement rather than a default.

OBLIGATION TWO: does a PREDICATE transport through a construction?
  predicate: the base is EXACT on the extent {0..e}, no reduction fires.
  candidate rule: componentwise, which is the obvious rule and the one
  a design reaches for when it has already seen congruence hold.

  base           construction  extent   unsound pairs
  uW3/wrap       complex         <= 1            3/16
  uW3/wrap       complex         <= 2           26/81
  uW3/wrap       dual            <= 2            1/81
  uW3/sat        complex         <= 1            3/16
  uW3/sat        complex         <= 2           26/81
  uW3/sat        dual            <= 2            1/81
  iW4/wrap       complex         <= 2            1/81
  iW4/wrap       dual            <= 2            1/81
  iW4/sat        complex         <= 2            1/81
  iW4/sat        dual            <= 2            1/81
  rule fired 48 times; unsound in 10 of them

THE POINT:
  congruence failures:  0
  lifting failures:     10

  Every construction transports EQUALITY and some do not transport
  the PREDICATE, over the same bases and the same constructions.
  The two obligations are independent and the first is no evidence
  for the second.

  The reason is structural rather than incidental. A construction is
  a function of the base's operation tables, so equal tables in give
  equal tables out however much the construction mixes components.
  A predicate is a claim quantified over a region, and a construction
  that mixes components mixes the regions, so nothing carries it
  across for free.

  A design that reads 'the construction respects the base' off the
  congruence and lifts a predicate on that basis makes exactly the
  error `112` measured. The two look like one property and are not.
