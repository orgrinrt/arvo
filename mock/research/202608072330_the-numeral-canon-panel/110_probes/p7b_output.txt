reading:
  product2 has no precondition. componentwise construction preserves
  every equation the base satisfies, so it is total on primitives and
  a lane-wise composite is licensed for exactly the rewrites its
  scalar was, with no separate measurement.

  interval is PARTIAL. it computes on endpoints and needs the base's
  operations to preserve order, and a wrapping base does not, so the
  result is not even a carrier: an ordered pair comes back inverted.
  that is a construction carrying a const predicate on its base,
  which is the same shape as every other arm in this design.
