C2 read out: which constructions preserve which laws, over these bases
    product2   breaks: nothing the base had
    complex    breaks: ['distrib', 'mul_assoc']
    dual       breaks: nothing the base had
    interval   breaks: ['distrib', 'mul_assoc']

the componentwise construction is the interesting control:
    product2 reproduces the base's law set exactly, on every base.
    that is the classical fact that equational theories survive
    products, and it means a lane-wise composite inherits every
    rewrite the scalar was licensed for, with no new measurement.

    the twisted constructions do not, so the law set of a composite
    is a function of the base's laws AND the constructor, and is
    read off rather than chosen, exactly as P2 found for the base.
