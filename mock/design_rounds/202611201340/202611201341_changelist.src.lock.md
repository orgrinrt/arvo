**Date:** 2026-11-20
**Round:** `202611201340`, job `arvo-numeral-doors`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611201340_changelist.doc.lock.md`.

## `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/member/step.rs`

CHANGE: `Sides::POWERS` reads `u` and `v` from `placing::powers`, each narrowed to the host's unsigned
integer by `Term::narrowed` through `Wide::unsigned`, and the step's own `power` function goes (doc
decision 2).

ADD: `arvo_mutant_member_step_guard`, which drops the check in `Sides::QUOTIENT` that the widest member
times `u` is held (doc decision 1).

## `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/member/step/tests.rs`

ADD: `above_zero!`, called by `at_the_edge!` at every width, radix and range it already covers: the
product at the last exponent at or above zero where the square of the widest member times `r^e` is below
`2^127`, and refused one past it, or refused at zero where the square alone is not; the quotient at the
last exponent where the widest member times `r^e` is a 128-bit integer, and refused one past; the
conversion from that range's last such exponent onto zero, and from zero onto the last exponent where
`r^e` is a 128-bit integer, each refused one past. The edges come from `edge::held`,
`edge::square_below_2_127` and `edge::at_least_zero`, the tests' own arithmetic (doc decision 1).
