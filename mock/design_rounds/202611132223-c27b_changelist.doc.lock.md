## Canon

`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` is about the shipped code and does not reach a test
module. The canon says nothing about how the suite is compiled; the shape of a sweep is design, and it
belongs where the design states what the tests owe.

## CHANGE: the laws section says how a sweep is built

At the end of `## The laws this crate owes tests for`, one paragraph: a sweep over formats or signatures
is a list of calls, each type's walk kept out of line, because inlined walks become one function whose
counters chain through every loop and overflowed the compiler's stack in an optimised build, and out of
line a sweep can grow without the build depending on its size.
