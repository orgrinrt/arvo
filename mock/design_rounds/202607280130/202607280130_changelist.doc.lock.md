# Doc changelist: correct what the design claims about SignedIdentity

**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Phase:** DOC
**Source topic:** `202607280100_topic.review-findings-on-the-identity-round.md`
**Status:** living until locked

Two documents claim `IFixed` carries `SignedIdentity::NEG_ONE`. No such impl exists at any width, and none
ever has. The source changelist adds the impls; these edits stop the documents describing them as already
present, and state the condition that makes them unconditional.

## CHANGE: mock/crates/arvo-strategy/DESIGN.md.tmpl

The `SignedIdentity` paragraph currently says it is implemented "for `i8`..=`i128` and `isize`", which is
true, and its surrounding prose in the trait's doc comment claims substrate wrappers too, which is not. The
document gains the wrapper impls explicitly: `Bits<N, S, Signed>` forwards to its container, and
`IFixed<I, F, S>` carries raw `-(1 << F)`.

It also states why the impls are unconditional where `Identity<Multiplicative>` is not, since that is the
whole reason the two traits are separate. `IFixed<I, F, S>` spans `[-2^I, 2^I)`. At every `I` including
zero, minus one is inside that range, and at `I == 0` it is exactly the container minimum. One is outside it
at `I == 0`. So the representability projection gates the multiplicative identity and must not be copied
onto `NEG_ONE`, and the document now says that as a property of the ranges rather than as an assertion.

## CHANGE: mock/crates/arvo/DESIGN.md.tmpl

The line "IFixed gains `SignedIdentity::MINUS_ONE` separately (signed-only)" describes a state that did not
exist when it was written. It is replaced with the impl as it now ships, naming the raw encoding, and with
the same range argument for why no condition guards it.

The constant is `NEG_ONE`, not `MINUS_ONE`. The document has been naming a constant that is not on the
trait, which is the kind of drift the `design-doc-source-mismatch` lint catches only for backticked type
names and not for member names.

## Not changed

The locked changelists of round `202607270015`. They are frozen, and the corrections to their two inaccurate
claims live in this round's topic instead.

The five other crate design documents. Their `Identity` spellings were corrected in the previous round and
none of them mentions `SignedIdentity`.
