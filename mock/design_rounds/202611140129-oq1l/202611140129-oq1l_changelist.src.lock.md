# Source changelist: the digest rebuilt on the operations its owners already have

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140129-oq1l`, job `arvo-digest`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140129-oq1l_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-bits/`, `mock/crates/arvo-digest/`, `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/`, the
digest bench variants under `mock/benches/variants/`, and the lockfiles that follow.

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/`

Remove what round `202611140014-l3t2` added: the byte and word section of `host.rs` (`Byte`, `Bytes`,
`At`, `HostWord`, `Word` and their tests) and their re-export from `lib.rs`. The crate keeps the
`from_index` and `index` door `work/arvo-values` brings, unchanged.

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-bits/`

1. `Cargo.toml`: notko as the one dependency; the description names xor and the byte run.
2. `src/lib.rs`: `Bits::xor`, `Bits::<64>::halves`, and `mod bytes` with `pub use bytes::Bytes`. The
   module documentation names the two new doors and the notko dependency.
3. `src/bytes.rs`: `Bytes<'a>` with `of`, `host` and `split_first`.
4. `src/bytes/tests.rs`: the walk over the empty run and every byte value, a planted wrong walk, the
   `xor` laws at widths 1, 8, 32, 63 and 64, and `halves` over the four placements of set bits.

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-digest/`

1. `Cargo.toml`: `arvo-format` joins, for the adaptation `Fnv1a<32>`'s state names.
2. `src/digest.rs`: `Carrier` names `Bits<32>`, `Bits<64>` and `[Bits<64>; 2]`; `bits` is `cast`.
3. `src/xxh3.rs`: `Bytes` from `arvo-bits`; results through `Bits::masked` and `Bits::halves`.
4. `src/fnv1a.rs`: `Fnv1a<32>` on `Uint<32, Adapt<TowardZero, Wrap>, Quiet>::mul` and `Bits::xor`,
   crossing through `index` and `from_index`. `Fnv1a<64>` is removed and marked by a `FIXME` citing
   `arvo_format_admits_the_wrapping_product_over_the_unsigned_64`.
5. `src/switched.rs` and `tests/switched.rs` are removed, and `src/lib.rs` marks the switched digest
   with a `FIXME` citing the same ticket.
6. Tests: FNV-1a at 32 bits against its reference values, against the host's own arithmetic over
   every prefix of a 512-byte run, in const context, and a planted wrong vector; XXH3 against the
   sanity vectors with the 128-bit halves joined back; the narrowing tests on `Xxh3<64>`; the ui
   tests importing `Bytes` from `arvo-bits`.

## CHANGE: `mock/benches/variants/digest-*`

Each variant takes `Bytes` from `arvo-bits` and reads the digest through `Bits::raw`. The
`digest-fnv1a64` variant carries a `FIXME` citing the same ticket, and the `digest-crossover` bench is
not run in this round.
