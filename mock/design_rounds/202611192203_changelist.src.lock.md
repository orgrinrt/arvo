# Source changelist: the numeral contracts, the numeric tier

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Round:** job `arvo-contracts`, branch `work/numeral-contracts`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611192146_changelist.doc.lock.md`.

## `mock/crates/arvo-contracts/`

ADD: the crate, `no_std`, `const_trait_impl` on, depending on `arvo-format` alone. `src/lib.rs`
re-exports `Sum`, `Difference`, `Product` (`src/arithmetic.rs`), `Order` (`src/order.rs`),
`Identity`, `Additive`, `Multiplicative` (`src/identity.rs`), `Sign`, `Magnitude` (`src/sign.rs`),
`Constant` (`src/constant.rs`), `MonotoneCompletion` with its impl for `Saturate`, `MonotoneSum`
(`src/monotone.rs`), and `Reciprocal`, `SquareRoot` (`src/named.rs`). The traits with methods are
`const trait`s; `Order` provides `lt`, `le`, `gt`, `ge`, `min`, `max` over `order`.

## `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/`

CHANGE, `Cargo.toml`: depends on `arvo-contracts`.

CHANGE, `src/lib.rs`: `const_trait_impl` unconditionally; `Identity`, `Additive` and
`Multiplicative` re-exported from `arvo-contracts` in place of the crate's own.

CHANGE, `src/identity.rs`: the trait and the two markers go; the two impls for `Num<S, C>` stay as
they are, against `arvo_contracts::Identity`.

ADD, `src/contracts.rs`: `const impl`s of `Sum`, `Difference`, `Product`, `Order`, `Sign`,
`Magnitude` for `Num<S, C>`, each the inherent `const fn` of the same operation; `Constant<V>` as
`constant::<V>()`; `MonotoneSum` where the signature's overflow policy carries `MonotoneCompletion`.

ADD, tests:

- `tests/contract_laws/mod.rs`: the laws, generic, each returning its first counterexample, and the
  test-side vocabulary `Members` and `Oracle`.
- `tests/the_contracts.rs`: against `Num` at widths 1 to 8, signed and unsigned, wrap and saturate,
  and two fixed-point points; the oracle equality of every method everywhere, the algebraic laws only
  inside the regions of the law rows the doc changelist names, the abort through a contract, the
  constant door.
- `tests/the_wrong_instances.rs`: a four-bit outside instance using no `Num`, right under wrap and
  under saturation, and wrong in one law per selector, each failing that law.
- `tests/consumer/`: crates built by `trybuild` with no feature line, reaching every public item of
  `arvo-contracts`, with an outside instance using no `Num`.
- `tests/ui/`: `MonotoneSum` refusing a wrapping value beside a saturating twin, and `Constant<V>`
  refusing a `V` naming no member beside a twin at the end of the range.
