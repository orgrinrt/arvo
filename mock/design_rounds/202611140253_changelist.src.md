# Src changelist: the conversion seen from the source, the positive ends, and the mutants off the manifest

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140253`, job `arvo-values-fin2`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140253_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/Cargo.toml`, `src/` and `tests/`, and the mutants' red runs under
`mock/research/sketches/202611140253-gl7r_value-mutants/`.

## CHANGE: the mutants are flags

`Cargo.toml` loses the twelve `mutant-*` features and the comment on them. `[lints.rust]` declares the
thirteen flags to the configuration check as `unexpected_cfgs` with `check-cfg`, so a misspelt flag is
a warning.

Every `cfg!(feature = "mutant-<name>")` in `src/` becomes `cfg!(arvo_mutant_<name>)`: `ratio.rs`
(`div`, `convert`), `host.rs` (`index`), `num.rs` (`under`), `rung.rs` (`sub`, `neg`, `mul`, and the
rungs' own, which the rung macro now takes as a configuration predicate rather than a feature name),
`count_const_path.rs` and `count_plain_path.rs` (`count`). The doc comments that name a mutant say the
flag.

`ratio.rs` gains `arvo_mutant_convert_policy` in `convert`: where the source's overflow policy is
saturation the conversion completes by saturation, read through `WithOverflow`, and otherwise by the
target's policy.

## CHANGE: the source signatures across a step that loses range

`tests/common/exponents.rs` gains the step from exponent zero onto minus one beside the step from minus
one onto zero, each its own macro. `tests/the_conversion_under_every_source_signature.rs` keeps the first
step and `tests/the_conversion_under_every_source_signature_past_the_range.rs` takes the second, one
binary each, since the two in one binary build past the container's memory. Each asserts the signatures
it ran, and the second also that some member left the target's range under each pair of policies.

## CHANGE: the positive ends of the families

`tests/every_shape/product.rs`: `top` is the highest exponent at which every product of two members is
a position a slot index holds, computed in the tests' own arithmetic, with no cap at one.

`tests/every_shape/quotient.rs`: the family is `top`, zero, minus one, half and deep, where `top` is the
highest exponent at which the remainder's denominator fits at both ends of the range.

`tests/common/edges.rs` holds the edge functions the families and the conversion share, and a macro
that asserts, at one width, both ranges and one radix, that the format admits an operation at its edge
and refuses it one past. The radix files of the product and the quotient each run it at every width.

## CHANGE: the conversion at the positive edge

`tests/common/edges.rs` holds the conversion's two positive edges, the source's and the target's, and
`tests/common/exponents.rs` the sweep at them. `tests/the_conversion_at_the_positive_edge_{binary,decimal,ternary}.rs` run it at
every width and pair of ranges, and hold both edges against `is_convertible`.

## CHANGE: the mutants' red runs

`mock/research/sketches/202611140253-gl7r_value-mutants/` holds `FINDINGS.md` and one output per run:
`arvo_mutant_convert_policy` against the two source-signature binaries and the rest of the conversion
sweeps, and every other mutant against its own tests under the new flag, showing the flags still turn
their tests red.

## CHANGE: the order of the members in a file of its own

`src/num.rs` passes five hundred lines. Its comparison and what reads it, `cmp`, `eq`, `lt`, `le`, `gt`,
`ge`, `min` and `max`, move unchanged into `src/order.rs` as a second inherent block on `Num`, reading
the carrier through `carrier`. `num.rs` re-exports the dispatch macro inside the crate so that both files
use the one. No item changes name, signature or meaning.

## No change

No public item of `arvo-numeral`, and no file of `arvo-format`, `arvo-placement`, `arvo-strategy` or
`arvo-bits`.
