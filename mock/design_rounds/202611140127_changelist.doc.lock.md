## CHANGE: what the product refuses

The obligation is the format's, forced by `product_position` and asked by `is_multipliable`. `mul`
forces the same conditions on its declared signature, the carried condition only where the signature
does not wrap. Wrapping reads the floor modulo the range's count and the remainder, both formed by the
step in its four limbs past the index too, so `mul` reduces a floor past the index onto the range before
`adapt`.

## CHANGE: the 64-bit ranges

`UFixed<64, k>` at `k >= 0` is not yet admitted under saturation or clamping, and is admitted under
wrapping at every `k`; its top corner at `k = 0` wraps to 1, as the host's wrapping product on 64
unsigned bits does.

## CHANGE: the shared ending

A position past the index keeps its floor and residue, and the shared module reduces the floor onto the
range once for every step that wraps past the index. The product is the one step that reads it on
this branch, which carries no `land`.

## CHANGE: the open item

The unsigned 64-bit product stays open under saturation, clamping and `product_position`. Wrapping is
admitted, and saturation and clamping, sound by the same reading, are task `44c483a9f0d5`. The design was
silent on how a wrapping completion is admitted without widening the slot coordinate; this is the
minimal admission the topic names.
