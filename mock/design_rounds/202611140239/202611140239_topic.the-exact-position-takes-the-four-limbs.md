## What the brief asks

Verbatim from the job brief: "Job 8 (the slot widening) changes what every computation over a slot
reads. Keep `Slot::at` and `Slot::index` if the design allows; where it does not, port the three IEEE
suites and two MATLAB suites in the same branch, and DM `coord` the changed shape before the round locks
so `arvo-values` hears it."

The intent: the product, the quotient and the conversion are admitted wherever their exact step is
formed, so the three tickets close, and the inverse round trip reaches width 64. Keeping `Slot::at` and
`Slot::index` is the brief's preference, conditional on the design.

## Where the width goes

The design says two things about the locus. "Where the exact step happens, which is not in the set it
lands in" says the step's intermediate "is not a slot index and is not required to be one; only the
answer is", and that carrying it "in a form that holds every position the step can reach removes the
case rather than catching it". The first open item says admitting the product is "a widening of the slot
coordinate past `i128`, which every other computation over a slot would then owe too". The first is the
one derived from the spine, and the open item, which the tickets copied, disagrees with it. The section on the exact
step governs: the width goes on the exact position, not on the slot.

So `Exact` holds its floor in the four limbs the exact steps already form it in, `Slot` stays an `i128`,
`Slot::at` and `Slot::index` keep their shapes, and no standards suite is ported. The plan names two other
shapes: `Slot` itself four limbs wide, which widens a coordinate for widths the canon holds open
(`question::one_word_or_two_for_is_a_number_system`), and the carrier chosen per format at the type level,
which changes every signature for a cost nobody measured. Neither is taken.

## What follows

A formed step has a position. A numerator in the four limbs over a denominator of at least one has a
floor in the four limbs, and a remainder over a denominator of at least two puts the floor at most at
half the limbs' reach, so the step a rounding adds fits. The fourth condition of the product, quotient and
conversion obligations, that the slot index carries every position, stops being a condition: each
obligation is the three it was before, and a format is admitted wherever its step is formed.

`Exact`'s `past` distance goes with it, since a carry of at most `2^63` from any slot is an ordinary
four-limb value, and `between` is total with no pinning. `slot()` answers the floor where the index holds
it and the index's end on the position's side where it does not. Rounding answers with the floor and a
step; completion compares the floor against the range's ends, wraps by reducing the floor and the lowest
slot modulo the span separately, and saturates by the floor's side.

Wrapping past the index then needs nothing of its own. Round `202611140127` added `placing::wrapped`,
`adapt::wraps`, `MultipliableUnder`, and round `202611140209` the reduction modulo twice the count and
`is_multipliable_under`, all to wrap a floor the index could not hold. With the floor held, every policy
reads it the same way, so those go, and the verdict on a declared signature is the verdict on its format.
The every-mode wrap sweep and the host comparison stay as the regression net they already are.

## What does not move

`Slot`, `Slots`, the 64-bit bound in `Slots::ADMITTED`, addition and its reach, `Reach`, the symmetry
module and the standards. The width 65 and 128 cells of the law ticket need a declared range past 64 bits,
which is the open question above; that ticket stays open, narrowed to them.
