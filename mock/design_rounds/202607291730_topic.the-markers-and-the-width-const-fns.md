## D32. The marker family splits on what it describes

**Decision (op, 2026-07-29).**

`IntegerLike`, `FractionLike`, `FloatLike` and `BoolLike` go to **`arvo-numeric-contracts`**. Each
names what kind of number a type is, which is a numeric contract and nothing else.

`BitPresentation` goes to **`arvo-bits-contracts`**. It describes how a value presents as bits, which
is the bit surface's question.

The split is on subject matter rather than on where they happen to sit together today, which is the
same correction D27 applies to `arvo-storage`.

## D33. The width const fns follow their subject

**Derived (agent), on op's instruction that the destination is obvious from each name.** Recorded
explicitly so that "obvious" is written down rather than re-derived by the next reader.

| Function | Subject | Destination |
|---|---|---|
| `ufixed_bits`, `ifixed_bits` | total container width of a `UFixed` / `IFixed` | `arvo-numeric` |
| `logical_width_unsigned`, `logical_width_signed` | logical width from I and F | `arvo-numeric` |
| `is_fractional` | whether `I == 0`, a property of the fixed-point shape | `arvo-numeric` |
| `width_le_64` | a width predicate read by container selection | `arvo-strategy` |

`width_le_64` is the one that goes elsewhere, and the reason is that it asks a question about
*container dispatch* rather than about a fixed-point value: it is the strategy crate's business which
widths fit which carrier. The other four are statements about `UFixed` and `IFixed` and travel with
them.

**`is_fractional` carries a second obligation.** It is one of the three GCE sites, appearing as
`[(); 1 / is_fractional(F)]:` at `ufixed.rs:274` and `ifixed.rs:308`. D3 already settled the cure
(`const { assert!(...) }`, stable, no feature), so it moves and is rewritten in the same pass rather
than being carried across as-is.
