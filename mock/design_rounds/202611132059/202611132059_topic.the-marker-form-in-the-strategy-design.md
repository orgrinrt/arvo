# the-marker-form-in-the-strategy-design

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** the correction round after the first self-review of `work/arvo-lints`, which found a claim in the closed round `202611132036` to be false.

## What the review found

The doc changelist of round `202611132036` says a grep of the templates for the carried preset defaults finds no instance. That is wrong. `mock/crates/arvo-strategy/DESIGN.md.tmpl` says at line 68 that the other three presets' choices are marked `// FIXME:` at the declaration, and at line 186 that each choice is marked `// FIXME:` at each impl. The module doc of `mock/crates/arvo-strategy/src/lib.rs` says at line 28 that each impl is marked `// FIXME:` at the declaration.

The closed round put a ticket id in each of those markers and added the lint that refuses a marker without one. The design and the module doc still describe the bare form in so many words, so the design prescribes what the repository's own gate refuses.

## What the sentences mean

Each one says that the preset's choice is a carried default and not a settled value, and that a marker at the impl says so. That claim stands. Only the form of the marker changes: it names its ticket in parentheses before the colon.

## What this round does

The two template sentences and the module doc sentence say the marker names its ticket, in the form `FIXME(<ticket id>):`. The ticket for the four presets is `arvo_s_four_preset_adaptation_defaults_are_carried_rather_th`, and the sentences stay general because the ticket belongs to the markers and not to the design. The generated `docs/STRATEGY.md` regenerates from the template.

The closed round cannot be edited, and its doc changelist is not an addendum target. Its false sentence is corrected here: this round's changelists state what the first one got wrong and name the lines that were missed.

## Open

Whether a design should name the marker form at all, or say only that a choice is carried and tracked, is not decided here. The sentences keep their claim and gain the form.
