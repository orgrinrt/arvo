# Src changelist: the bit tier on `arvo-bits`

**Date:** 2026-11-20
**Round:** `202611200155`, job `arvo-numeral-doors`. Overhauls `arvo-bits`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611200157_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Sketches:** `mock/research/sketches/202611200200_bit-tier-const-trait/` (the shape builds and refuses
past the width), `mock/research/sketches/202611200230_bit-tier-mutants/` (the red runs).

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/src/ops.rs`

ADD: the inherent `const fn`s `bit::<I>`, `with_bit::<I>`, `and`, `or`, `xor`, `not`, `is_empty`,
`shift_up::<K>`, `shift_down::<K>`, `rotate_up::<K>` and `rotate_down::<K>` on `Bits<N>`, and the private
`Within<N, I>` whose `ADMITTED` refuses an index or an amount at or past the width, forced in each method
that takes one. `not` and `shift_up` mask; `rotate_*` take the carrier's own rotation at sixty-four bits
and two shifts within the width below it.

ADD: five mutant flags, each off unless a build sets it: `arvo_mutant_bits_index` admits the width
itself, `arvo_mutant_bits_with_bit` sets without clearing, `arvo_mutant_bits_not` leaves the bits above
the width set, `arvo_mutant_bits_shift` leaves a shifted-out bit in the carrier, and
`arvo_mutant_bits_rotate` rotates within the carrier at every width.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/src/tier.rs`

ADD: the `const` traits `BitWidth` (`const WIDTH: Width`), `BitAccess: BitWidth + Copy`, `BitLogic: Copy`
and `BitSequence: Copy`, and their `const` impls for `Bits<N>`, each method calling the inherent one.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/src/lib.rs`

CHANGE: `xor` moves to `ops.rs`. `#![feature(const_trait_impl)]` for the `const` traits; the modules
`ops` and `tier`; the four traits re-exported at the root. The module doc names the tier and the
dependency on `arvo_format`.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/Cargo.toml`, `mock/Cargo.toml`

CHANGE: `arvo-format` joins the dependencies, the description names the bit operations, and the five
mutant names join the declared cfg names. The workspace manifest's comment calling `arvo-bits` a leaf
with no sibling dependency is dropped.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/tests/`

ADD: `common/mod.rs` (the mask in 128 bits, the values a sweep takes, a rotation moved one bit at a
time); `the_bit_access.rs`, every index of the widths 1 to 8, 16, 28, 32, 63 and 64 over every value up
to eight bits and the edge and seeded values above; `the_bit_sequence.rs`, every amount at the same
widths, against the host's shift and the one-bit rotation, with the round trips and the rotation up by
`K` against down by `N - K`; `the_bit_logic.rs`, every width from 1 to 64 over every pair of those
values, with De Morgan, commuting, associating on a smaller set, and the intersection test;
`the_bit_tier_in_a_constant.rs`, the tier through `const fn`s generic over `[const]` bounds evaluated in
constants and `WIDTH` at every width. Every check goes through the inherent method and through a function
generic over the trait.

ADD: in `compile_fail.rs` and `tests/ui/`, `bit`, `with_bit`, `shift_up` and `rotate_down` at the width
through the inherent method, and `bit` at the width through a `const fn` generic over `BitAccess`, each
`E0080` at the width assertion.

## `mock/benches/bit-set/`

ADD: the bench `bit-set`, arms `bit-set-host` (baseline, on `u64`) and `bit-set-tier` (through a
function generic over `BitLogic` at `Bits<64>`), each exporting `bit_set_meet`, `bit_set_union` and
`bit_set_is_empty` by name; their disassembly under `results/bit-set/disasm/`.

## `mock/benches/digest-crossover/`

CHANGE: `baseline` is spelled `digest_fnv1a64`, the arm's `bench_name`, which is what the bench
validation checks it against; the hyphenated spelling refused every `cargo mock bench` command in the
tree, `disasm` on `bit-set` among them (ticket
`bench_baseline_is_checked_against_the_underscored_arm_name`). Its three arms' lockfiles take
`arvo-format`, now under `arvo-bits`.

## `mock/research/sketches/202611200230_bit-tier-mutants/`

ADD: the runner for the five flags against the tests that hold each, and its red runs.
