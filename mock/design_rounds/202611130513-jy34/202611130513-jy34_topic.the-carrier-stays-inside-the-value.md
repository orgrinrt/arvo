# Topic: the carrier stays inside the value

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** a follow-up to rounds 202611130342 and 202611130501-vt8a on branch `work/numeral-integers`,
from the first review of that branch, whose canon finding two independent reads have now confirmed.

## What this round is for

`arvo-numeral` shipped two doors typed in the machine carrier, `raw` and `from_raw`, and made the
carrier's type nameable through `Sole::Repr`, `Carried::Repr` and `Machine`. The design contradicted
itself over it: its canon line cites
`ruling::behaviour_is_stated_per_declared_signature_and_the_premise_dissolves` for the carrier being
observable through nothing the value declares, and its doors section then calls `raw` the one place the
crate shows a machine integer.

The row says every operation the design declares is a function of the declared width and never of the
machine carrier, and that the footprint is observable through exactly one observation the design does
not own, the host's layout observation. `ruling::the_derivation_is_a_placement_and_the_operation_set_is_an_admission_rule`
admits an operation exactly when it is a function of the declared signature, and the carrier is the
placement's output rather than a coordinate of the signature.
`ruling::the_numeric_door_carries_the_coordinate_set_and_the_two_type_bound_is_not_canon` has the door
carry out the coordinate set in types the stack owns. Two reads agree that `raw` and `from_raw` are
operations the design declares, typed and valued in the carrier, so the canon line stands and the
doors go.

## The doors

Bits come in by the declared adaptation from an exact point, `adapted`, and as a member by
`constant`; they go out as the slot coordinate, `slot`, an `arvo_format::Slot`. No door takes or
gives the carrier. Where a consumer needs the bits as bits, the shape the row allows is an encoding
stated over the declared width, an exact-width container such as `arvo-bits` holds, and never the
carrier. None is added here: nothing in this crate's tests or its consumer needs one, and the consumer
that will is the one to ask for it.

`from_host` and `host` on `USize` and `ISize` stay. Their point is the platform-width member of the
format family, so the host's integer at that width is the declared width's own encoding, and the copy
between them is the identity on members. They are built from the carrier inside the crate rather than
through `from_raw` and `raw`.

## The carrier cannot be named

`Num<S>` still stores the carrier the map names. What changes is who can name it. The map `Carried`
and the machine integers `Machine` move into a module the crate does not export, and `Sole` gives up
its `Repr`: it reaches the map only through a bound in its own supertrait list,
`DeclaredSignature<Format: Format<Slots: Carried>>`. The compiler elaborates that bound wherever
`S: Sole` holds, so `Num<S>` is well formed in every generic consumer, and a consumer has no route to
the carrier's type: `S::Repr` is not found, `<X as Sole>::Repr` does not exist, and the path to the map
is private.

`mock/research/sketches/202611130513-jy34_an-unnameable-carrier/` probes exactly this encoding against
`arvo-format`, with one crate per attempt, and it holds with no feature gate. Its one finding on the way,
that a derived `Copy` bounds `S: Copy`, does not apply here, since the value's traits are already
written by hand.

Each route a consumer could take to the carrier becomes a compile-fail case with its twin: the
projection through `Sole`, the fully qualified projection, and the map and the machine integers by path.

## Hash and Debug

`Hash` and `Debug` were written over the carrier. A hash over a `u16` and over an `i32` writes different
bytes for the same member, so the hash depended on the carrier, which the row forbids of any operation
the design declares. Both go through the slot: `Debug` prints the slot, `Hash` hashes its index. Equality
and the order keep reading the carrier, because the carrier is canonical and signed exactly where the
slot family is, so their results are the slots' and the reading is only the mechanism.

## The named surface

The design's section on where the host integers are named, and the comment on `arvo-numeral`'s row in
`[primitive-introductions]`, shrink to what is still named: the carrier map, the machine integers and
the bit copy, all inside the crate and unnameable outside it; the rung arithmetic and its dispatch; the
host doors on `USize` and `ISize`; the slot index; and core's trait signatures and the tests. The row
itself stays, because the rung arithmetic and the host doors still name host integers.

## What follows in the tests

The round trip through `raw`, the `from_raw` tests and their assertion tests go, with the twins. The
consumer crate stops reaching the carrier and the map and reaches the surface as it now stands. The
mutant outputs of round 202611130501-vt8a were taken with `from_raw` present, and the runs are taken
again on the new surface.
