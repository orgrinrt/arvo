## What is wrong

The stack audit found the cheap per-width adaptation a value's ingest and egress want sitting private,
and the triage names it "the public per-width adaptation door" as this job. That adaptation is
`arvo-numeral`'s, over the host's integers, and this crate holds no machine container by design, so the
door cannot live here. T3 shipped it on the value side at `4beb5c52` on `work/arvo-values`
(`Num::from_index`, `holds_index`, `index` in `arvo-numeral/src/host.rs`). The coordinator closes that
ticket against `4beb5c52` when `work/arvo-values` lands.

What is this crate's is the other door T1's side of the border names (`briefs/arvo-door-shape.md`, need
1). `adapt` lands a position on one slot index, and a magnitude-indexed format is a union of grids, one
per magnitude. The IEEE parity suites find the landing magnitude themselves, calling
`panic_on_overflow` per magnitude from the finest up (`tests/ieee754/mod.rs`, `land`), and the design
says so: "where a result lands is the caller's composition". A consumer that lands a value on a floating
format writes that walk again, with its own position arithmetic in the host's integer, which leaves the
four limbs this crate already forms positions in.

## The shape the coordinator settled

Proposed to the coordinator and answered at 21:04 with two changes, which are taken here.

`Scaled::of(num, den, power)` is a point of the ambient domain with no magnitude in it,
`num / den * radix^power`. Its denominator is taken the way `Remainder::of` takes its own: the same
unsigned 128-bit type, and a zero reads as zero rather than being refused, since `Remainder::of` and
`Fraction::of` both read a zero denominator as `ZERO` and refuse nothing. The coordinator's words: "Take
it the way Remainder::of and Fraction::of already take theirs (the same type or the same refusal,
reused, not a second convention)". A negative denominator is not a value the type holds.

`land::<S>(Scaled, Dither) -> Maybe<Landing>` returns the finest magnitude whose rounded position is in
the slot range, and that position as an `Exact`. The rounding and the range question are
`panic_on_overflow`'s, asked per magnitude, so there is one implementation of each: "land rounds by S's
declared rounding through the same path adapt uses, one implementation". The slot is
`adapt::<S>(landing.exact(), dither)`, so `adapt` and `panic_on_overflow` do not change.

Nothing past the top magnitude, and nothing where no magnitude holds the position. What completion means
past the top of a union of magnitude ranges is the canon question `arvo-registry`'s row answers, so
`land` carries `FIXME(arvo_landing_a_value_on_a_magnitude_indexed_format_is_a_call)` there until it
stands, and the three magnitude placeholders stay as they are.

## The nesting, which needs no check

The proposal had `land` check at build that each coarser quantum is a whole multiple of every finer one,
which is what makes the finest grid in range the nearest member of the union. The quantum concept admits
only the affine law `radix^(BASE + SLOPE m)`, and the ambient's obligation admits only a radix of two or
more, so the step at one magnitude divides the step at every coarser one for every law that builds. A
check over that is a declaration nothing constrains, so the design states it as a consequence of the two
obligations and `land` checks nothing.

## The walk, and where it starts

The position at a magnitude is the conversion's placement with the point in place of a source member:
with the phase `p / q` in lowest terms and the shift `t = power - step` split into `U = r^max(t, 0)` and
`V = r^max(-t, 0)`, it is `n q U - p d V` over `D = d q V`, formed in the four limbs and placed by
`placing::over` and `positioned`. A magnitude where `D` passes `2^128 - 1` or the floor leaves the slot
index holds nothing.

Walking every magnitude from the finest would cost a binary64 point around a thousand placements before
the first that can be in range. A bound on bit lengths skips those: with `|n| >= 2^(bits(n) - 1)`,
`d < 2^bits(d)` and `r^t >= 2^(t floor(log2 r))`, a shift at which
`t floor(log2 r) + bits(n) - 1 - bits(d)` reaches `max(bits(range), 64) + 2` names a position past every
range the slot index holds, the phase and the rounding's one slot included. The first magnitude past
that is computed, and the walk starts there.

## What tests it

An oracle walking every magnitude in the host's integer, keeping those `panic_on_overflow` holds and
taking the finest, against `land` over every mode, on a rising and a falling binary law, a phased law, a
decimal law and the constant family, and on 64-bit slot ranges where the walk starts within a few
magnitudes of the landing. A point past what the host integer positions, a zero denominator, and the top
magnitude's edge are spot cases. Beside those, an integration test lands the IEEE parity suites' own
samples through `land` and through the suites' search, every direction, and requires them to agree on
every sample the search lands below the top, before the search is replaced.
