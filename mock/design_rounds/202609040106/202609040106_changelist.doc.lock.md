## Current state

`crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl` states the applied map's laws under
"The laws the applied map owes tests for" and lists seven: totality, identity on
the grid, order transport, determinism, direction, separability of the two
regions, and an outside policy reaching the map. It says nothing about what the
map does when a position is translated by a whole number of quanta or reflected
through zero.

The canon does. `law::fusing_a_multiply_add_preserves_the_answer_under_unsigned`
says in its statement that the property is translation equivariance of the
rounding position on the domain the cell reaches, and
`law::fusing_a_multiply_add_preserves_the_answer_under_signed_wrapping` is the
same property on a domain that includes the negatives. Both sit above this design
and neither has an expression in it, so the design cannot be checked against them
and the crate ships a map whose behaviour under the one transformation the canon
names about it is undocumented and untested.

The design also carries the fused multiply-add as a worked case of the admission
rule, and says this crate neither ships that arm nor forecloses it. That sentence
is correct and this round does not change it.

## Changes proposed

One new top-level section at the end of `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`,
appended rather than woven into the existing ones, and no other document change
anywhere. The section is self-contained the way the applied map's own section is:
it carries its subject, the laws it owes tests for, and the surface it names.

### CHANGE: add the section on the grid's two symmetries

`## The two symmetries of the grid, and where the map commutes with them`, with
six subsections. It states the two laws, what decides each of them, and why they
are properties of the adaptation rather than of any operation.

The relocation law is `adapt(position + c)` against `adapt(adapt(position) + c)`
for a representable translation `c`, and it quantifies over every exact position
rather than over the ones some operation produces. That is what makes it
stateable in a crate with no arithmetic in it, and it is why a fusion verdict is
this law read at one operation's reach rather than a separate question. The
reflection law is the same shape at the grid's other symmetry, `adapt(-position)`
against `adapt(-adapt(position))`, with the right side re-adapting because the
negation of an admitted slot need not be admitted.

Each decomposes into one property per region and the conjunction is the whole of
it. The rounding region commutes with translation exactly where whatever a mode
reads besides the residue is absent from the domain, and the section states the
three answers the six names give and the two facts they are made of. The
completion region commutes with translation always under wrapping and under a
clamp exactly where no translation can point back at the range from the side an
excursion left on, which is narrower than saying unsigned and is narrower in the
direction that matters.

The section also records that the two symmetries partition the six shipped modes,
that this is a measured fact about those six rather than a theorem, and that a
nearest rule whose tie goes toward positive infinity would break it, which is why
the reflection fact is carried per name rather than derived from the other one.

### CHANGE: the laws that section owes tests for

Six, in a subsection of its own rather than appended to the applied map's list,
because they are about the new subject and the existing list is about the
regions.

The map relocates and reflects where the predicate says, over the cross, with the
two directions of disagreement counted separately: a licensed law the map refuses
is a defect and a refused law the map honours is a cost. The classification of a
mode agrees with the map, derived by walking the map rather than restated. The
rounded slot is the position's own slot or the one above it, which is the bound
the reach's derivation of the excursion sides rests on. The two symmetries
partition the six names. And every sweep establishing any of it reaches both
answers, a tie, a negative position and a value outside the range on both sides.

### CHANGE: the surface that section names

`Reach` with `Reach::of`, `translated_by`, `without_ties` and `Reach::EVERYTHING`;
`Reads`, `When`, `Behaviour` and `behaviour_of`; and the six predicates,
`rounding_is_translation_equivariant`, `completion_is_translation_homomorphic`,
`adaptation_relocates`, `rounding_is_reflection_equivariant`,
`completion_is_reflection_equivariant` and `adaptation_reflects`. Every one is a
`const fn` or a coordinate a `const fn` takes, and every yes-or-no answer is a
`Bool`, on the same reading as the rest of the design's surface.

`Reach` is the coordinate that carries what a declared signature cannot: which
exact positions an operation produces before the adaptation sees them, which
translations it applies, and whether an exactly-half position is among them. A
consumer that knows none of it declares `Reach::EVERYTHING` and gets the
conservative answer, which licenses only the cells where both regions commute
whatever the reach.

### CHANGE: state why this is not the predicate the crate deleted

`overflow.rs` and `rounding.rs` each carry a note saying two predicates stood
there and were deleted rather than improved, because each was a `matches!` over
an enumeration that no code read, so their tests reached a declaration and
stopped. That is the right rule and this round has to say why it does not catch
what it adds.

Two things separate them, and the section says both. The predicates here take a
region, so an answer is a function of five independent facts about a domain
rather than a restatement of one enumeration, and every one of those facts was
shown by measurement to move a verdict. And the test is a reader that runs the
machinery: it derives the classification by walking the map over domains that
differ in one restriction at a time and asserts the shipped one equals it, which
is the repair this design already prescribes for an assertion that reaches a
declaration and stops.

The section also says plainly what remains true, which is that no code in this
crate reads the predicates, because the consumer that would is an operation and
there are none here.

## What the design does not gain

No operation, no fused multiply-add, no arm gated on one. The sentence saying
this crate neither ships that arm nor forecloses it is unchanged and stays true:
an arm needs a multiply and an add, which are not here, and the coordinate that
separates the fused realisation from the stepwise one, which the admission rule
already says to declare rather than to refuse.

No answer to what `half_up` denotes. Everything the section states is a statement
about the function this crate computes, measured against that function, so it
survives that question either way.

No sharpening of the panic bound, no answer to the stochastic mode's seeding or
keying. The stochastic mode's verdict is stated at a fixed dither, where the mode
is a function of the position, and the tests require the verdict to be the same
at each of four dithers.

No emphasis in the new section, and no sweep of the emphasis already in the file.
The style rule says to take it out of what is being edited rather than opening a
sweep that buries the change travelling with it, and this change is an append, so
nothing existing is being edited.
