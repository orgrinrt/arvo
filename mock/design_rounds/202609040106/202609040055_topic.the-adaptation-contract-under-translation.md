# the-adaptation-contract-under-translation

The ratified format spine says arithmetic on a format is an exact operation in
the ambient domain composed with a named total adaptation onto the representable
set, and that the adaptation is a first-class object with its own laws. This
crate names two of those laws in `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl` under the
applied map's own heading, and the list there is totality, identity on the grid,
order transport, determinism, direction, separability of the two regions, and an
outside policy reaching the map. It does not name what the map does under a
translation, and that is the property the whole fused-multiply-add family in the
registry turns on: `law::fusing_a_multiply_add_preserves_the_answer_under_unsigned`
states in its own sentence that the property is translation equivariance of the
rounding position on the domain the cell reaches, and its signed twin says the
same. So the canon names a law of the adaptation that the design does not, and
the suite therefore cannot check the crate against it.

This round writes that law down and the one beside it that nobody has asked
about, states each as an arm over the region it holds in, and ships the const
predicate a consumer gates on. It does not ship an operation, it does not ship a
fused multiply-add and it does not foreclose one, which is what the design
already says about that arm and what stays true.

The reason the fusion question reduces to a property of the adaptation alone is
worth stating before anything else, because it is what decides where the work
belongs. Take an exact position, translate it by a whole number of quanta, and
ask whether adapting the translated position gives the same slot as adapting the
position and then translating. Written out that is `adapt(position + c)` against
`adapt(adapt(position) + c)`, and it is a law about a map rather than about an
operation. A fused multiply-add is the case where the position happens to be a
product and the translation happens to be the addend. So an operation decides
which region of that law it sits in and decides nothing else, and a crate with no
operations in it can still state the law, measure it and ship the predicate.

## The canon gate

Aligned, and the alignment is direct rather than inferred.
`ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon` is ratified by op and by the experts, and it
ratifies `proposal::arithmetic_on_a_format_factors_as_an_adaptation_of_an_exact_operation`,
whose sentence is that the adaptation is a first-class object with its own laws.
`ruling::arms_over_regions_are_the_fundamental_heart` is ratified and says the
space is filled by small arms carrying const predicates over the region each
holds in. `ruling::the_canon_does_not_police_what_shape_a_law_takes` says a law
is expressed so that it actually works, typestate or const expression, and that
the requirement is functional: it reaches one lowered path.
`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` bars a runtime check, and
everything this round adds is a `const fn` over associated items fixed at
monomorphisation.

Two questions the design lists as not its own were checked and neither covers
this. `question::does_narrowing_compose` and
`question::what_the_double_rounding_mechanism_is` are both about composing
adaptations onto grids of different coarseness, which is narrowing. Relocation is
one adaptation at one signature applied on two schedules, so the exclusion does
not reach it.

One thing is ambiguous and it is handed back rather than answered, in the section
below on what `half_up` denotes.

targets: 179 pass, none fails, and three are ignored, each carrying a catalogue

`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/mod.rs` and

that a wrong rule would be caught. `the_edge_answers_are_the_ones_worked_out_by_hand`
writes down two values derived on paper. Both `_over_the_matrix!` macros run all

tautological and nothing samples a mode set.

One arm does not keep its name.
`a_dither_at_the_edges_still_selects_between_two_neighbours` at
`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/mod.rs:551` computes two answers under two
dithers and asserts only that each is inside the declared range. Under `Wrap` the
completion returns `lo + (slot - lo).rem_euclid(span)`, which is inside the range
for every input by construction, so the assertion holds whatever the stochastic
branch computes: replacing that branch with a constant leaves the test green. The

neighbours, is never checked. It is not tautological, it is under-asserting, so
it gets the missing assertion rather than the delete a tautology earns.

## What the two laws are, and what decides them

The rounding region commutes with translation exactly where the thing a mode
reads besides the residue is not present in the domain. Translating a position
moves the slot and leaves the residue alone, so a rule reading only the residue
commutes by construction, and a rule reading anything else commutes only where
that thing does not vary. Over the six names the vocabulary carries there are
three classifications and they are measured rather than derived: floor, ceil and
stochastic read nothing besides the residue; toward-zero reads the sign of the
slot at every position off the grid; half-up reads the sign at a tie; half-even
reads the parity at a tie. Sign is defeated by a domain with no negative
position, parity is defeated by a domain of even slots or by no tie at all, and
the even-slot domain is what separates the two rather than leaving one inferred.

The completion region commutes with translation always under wrapping, because
reduction modulo the span is a homomorphism of the additive group. Under a clamp
it commutes exactly where no translation can point back at the range from the
side an excursion left on. That is narrower than "unsigned", and the difference
is load-bearing: on an unsigned range every translation is non-negative, so no
high excursion can be undone, and a low excursion can be. The witness is a value
five below the bottom translated by ten, which is five directly and ten staged.

The whole law is the conjunction and nothing else. Measured over 432 cells and
30,756,672 triples, the law holds in a cell exactly when both region properties
hold over the sets that cell reaches, and neither property alone decides it.

The second law is the grid's other symmetry and nothing in the registry carries a
row about it. Reflection through zero: adapting a negated position against
negating the adapted one. Toward-zero, half-up and half-even commute with it and
floor, ceil and stochastic do not; wrapping commutes with it on every range,
because negation is an automorphism of the cyclic group, and a clamp commutes
with it where the range is symmetric about zero, which a two's complement range
is not. The whole law is the same conjunction one symmetry over, 54 of 54 cells.

And the two symmetries partition the six shipped modes. Every mode has exactly
one of them, none has both and none has neither. That is a measured fact about
these six rather than a theorem: a nearest rule whose tie goes toward positive
infinity reads nothing beyond the residue and still commutes with reflection away
from a tie, so the equality is recorded as a law with its own test rather than
built into the classification.

## Where the evidence is

`mock/research/sketches/the-adaptation-contract-under-translation/`, five steps,
thirty-nine verdict lines, every output committed under `out/` and reproducible
with `./run`. Outcome: WORKS, in the sense the sketch discipline asks for, and
the substance of it moves into the crate's own tests in this round's source
changelist rather than staying there. Nothing in it is a benchmark, no timing is
taken, and the release profile is a runtime convenience.

Two refutations came out of it and they are the useful half.

The first is mine. The predicate's first revision was unsound in six cells and
step 05 caught it before a line of it was proposed here. It asked its sign
question about the positions a caller declares, and the law rounds the translated
position too, so a non-negative position with a negative translation reaches a
negative position the declaration never named. Toward-zero and half-up under
wrapping on the two ranges that admit a negative slot. The reach is quantified
over the union now, which is what `translated_low` is for.

The second is against a structural argument in the corpus.
`202608072330_the-numeral-canon-panel/259_fallin_whether_the_fused_result_composes.md`
section 3 has saturation commuting on a non-negative domain, on the ground that
every translation is non-negative so a high excursion stays high. That covers one
side of the range. Its instrument never reached the other because an unsigned
multiply-add produces no negative product, so the argument and its measurement
agree with each other and both are narrower than the sentence reads. That matters
here and did not matter there, because a predicate a consumer gates on cannot
assume what its operation produces.

## The locus challenge, and what this round refuses to build

The design says of the fused multiply-add that this crate neither ships that arm
nor forecloses it, and that stays true. An arm cannot be built here: the crate
ships no multiply and no add, and the admission rule gives a criterion rather
than an operation list. What a fusion arm would need beyond this round's
predicate is the operation and the coordinate that separates the fused
realisation from the stepwise one, and the admission rule already says what to do
about that pair, which is to declare the coordinate rather than refuse the
operation. Neither is this crate's, so neither is here.

What is here is the half that is this crate's, and it is the half the whole
family reduces to.

## What `half_up` denotes, handed back

`Mode::HalfUp` in this crate sends a tie away from zero, its rustdoc says so, and
`half_up_goes_away_from_zero_on_a_tie_and_half_even_goes_to_the_even_slot` pins
it. Under that reading the mode reads the sign of the slot at a tie and does not
commute with translation on a domain reaching negatives, which is what this
round's step 01 measures and what its predicate says.

`law::fusing_a_multiply_add_preserves_the_answer_under_signed_wrapping` puts
`half_up` in its holding region, and that region needs a nearest rule whose tie
goes toward positive infinity, which is a different operation. So a canon row and
the shipped crate use one token for two operations, and which one the token names
is not settled anywhere I can find: the vocabulary ruling names `half_up` without
fixing its tie direction, and `crates/arvo-format/src/standards.rs` carries a
`FIXME` pointing at a question about an unqualified nearest that is about a
different value in different rows.

This round does not answer it and does not need to. Every predicate it ships is a
statement about the function this crate computes, measured against that function,
so it stays true whichever way the word is decided; if the crate's `Mode::HalfUp`
changes, the classification changes with it and a test measuring the
classification against the map is what fails. The call is a panel's and op's, and
the reason it is worth making is that a consumer reading that law row today and
reaching for the `half_up` this crate provides is licensed to fuse where fusing
changes the answer.

## What this round does not do

It does not touch either fusion law row or the ruling above them. Those are canon
and the round below canon does not edit them. The observation that this round's
instrument agrees with seat 259's on the mechanism, and that both are narrower
than one row's holding region reads, is recorded here for whoever revisits those
rows.

It does not add a strategy, does not touch `arvo-strategy` and does not touch
`arvo-placement`. The scope was checked against both: a strategy selects an
adaptation and does not decide what an adaptation's laws are, and a placement
carries no adaptation at all, so neither crate has a seam this feature crosses.

It does not sharpen where a panic may fire, does not decide the seeding or keying
of the stochastic mode, and does not close the rounding-vocabulary question. The
stochastic mode is measured at four fixed dithers, with the verdict required to
be the same at each, which is what makes the question answerable for a mode whose
result is not a function of the value alone. That is a statement about a fixed
dither and not an answer to either open question.
