## `mock/crates/arvo-numeral`

ADD, `src/room.rs`: the exact position of the product, the quotient and the conversion, each chosen by
a constant of the instantiation. `Sides<F>` reads `u`, `v` and the range's reach; `PRODUCT`,
`QUOTIENT` and `Crossing<F, T>::HOLDS` are the bounds: the largest numerator a member reaches and the
denominator in `u128`, their quotient below `2^127`, at a zero phase and a constant quantum. Where the
bound holds, `Ratio::exact` floors toward negative infinity on the sign, in the machine's 64-bit
integer where `PRODUCT_NARROW`, `QUOTIENT_NARROW` or `Crossing::NARROW` says both terms fit it and in
`u128` otherwise, and hands the residue over the denominator as it is, unreduced, with
`Exact::on_grid` where nothing is left over; elsewhere `product_position`,
`quotient_position` and `converted_position` as before. Under `arvo_mutant_room` a negative ratio with a
residue is floored without the step down. A test module shows each bound true one exponent inside its
edge and false one past it: the product at every negative exponent of the 64-bit widths and not at a
positive one, the quotient down to `-63` and not at `-64` at both 64-bit ranges, the conversion from
`UFixed<64, 0>` down to `-63` and not at `-64`, and the 64-bit division taken at `UFixed<16, -8>` and
`UFixed<32, 0>` and not at `UFixed<33, 0>` or the 64-bit quotient.
CHANGE, `src/lib.rs`: `mod room`.
CHANGE, `src/num.rs`: `mul` at a non-zero exponent is `adapt` over `room::product`, and `fits_mul`
reads `room::product`; the `const` block forcing `product_position` stays; the `FIXME` and the
`mul as mul_slots` import go.
CHANGE, `src/ratio.rs`: `div` is `adapt` over `room::quotient`, `fits_div` reads it, `convert` is
`adapt` over `room::converted`, `fits_convert` reads it; both `FIXME`s and the `div` and
`converted_position`, `quotient_position` imports go; the module comment says where the ratio is
formed.
CHANGE, `Cargo.toml`: `arvo_mutant_room` in the declared cfgs.
ADD, `tests/the_quotient_by_a_value.rs`: every member of `UFixed<8, E>` and `Fixed<8, E>` at `E` 0,
-3 and -7, under the ten adaptations, by every value of a positive range 1 to 127 and, signed, a
negative range -128 to -1, landed by `Member::adapted` and taken by `Divisor::member`, against
`oracle::quotient` on the slot and the verdict, with the zero branched on before; the sweep's size and
the zero branch's count asserted.
CHANGE, `tests/ui/a_product_past_the_four_limbs_is_refused.stderr`: the same refusal with the same
message, one `erroneous constant` note fewer, since `arvo_format::mul` is no longer instantiated.
