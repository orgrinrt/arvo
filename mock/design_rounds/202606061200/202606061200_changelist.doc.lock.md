### CHANGE: note the contract-trait re-export in the Bit-matrix section

The Bit-matrix section introduces the `W` bound
(`BitSequence + BitAccess + BitLogic + Copy + Default`) but does not say
where a consumer names those traits. Add a short paragraph after the
`BitMatrix` struct block stating that arvo-bitmask re-exports
`BitSequence`, `BitAccess`, and `BitLogic` from arvo-bits-contracts, so a
consumer writing a `BitMatrix<W, C>` field or a row-word-generic function
spells the bound through the L2 layer it already consumes, without a direct
arvo-bits-contracts dependency.
