## Current state

`arvo-numeral` depends on `arvo-format`, `arvo-placement` and `notko`, enables no unstable feature,
and implements no trait of another crate's for a value beyond the standard library's. A list of
`notko-hlist` has no length in a value.

## Changes proposed

### The workspace

`mock/Cargo.toml`: `notko-hlist` among the workspace dependencies, from the repository `notko` comes
from, without its default features. `mock/Cargo.lock` follows, and moves `notko` to the same revision.

### `arvo-numeral`

`Cargo.toml`: the optional dependency, and the features `hlist`, `hlist-const` and `mutant-count`.

`src/lib.rs`: the crate attribute that enables `const_trait_impl` under `hlist-const`, and the two
modules, one per path, each behind its feature.

`src/count_plain_path.rs` and `src/count_const_path.rs`, new: `Cardinal` for `Num<S, C>`, zero the
additive identity and the successor `add` with the multiplicative identity. Two files because a const
impl is refused at parse time without its feature. The mutant sits in the successor in both.

`tests/count/mod.rs`, `tests/the_count.rs`, `tests/the_count_without_const.rs`: the shared lists and
checks, and one target per path. `tests/ui_count/`: the two compile-fail pairs of the const path, with
their diagnostics.
