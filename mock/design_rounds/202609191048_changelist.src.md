## CHANGE: `Exact::between`'s rustdoc and the module header FROM saying the carry cannot leave the index TO where it pins

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`

`between`'s rustdoc says the constructor saturates where the carry leaves the index, what it stores, and
that the map adapts the stored position. The module header stops saying the step's intermediate "lives
inside two private functions": `Rounded` and `round_slot` are crate-visible and addition reads them.

Verification: `grep -n 'like every other position\|two private functions' crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`
returns nothing.

## CHANGE: the carry FROM asserted only as a stored slot TO pinned through the map at both ends

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_top_of_the_index.rs`,
`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_edges.rs`

`a_carry_past_the_index_pins_at_the_constructor_and_the_map_adapts_what_is_stored`:
`between(Slot::at(i128::MAX), 9/4)` stores the slot `i128::MAX` and a quarter; under `Ceil` and `Wrap`
over `AtTheTop` it answers the lowest slot, not two above it, and the overflow verdict reports it.
`between(Slot::at(i128::MIN), -7/4)` stores `i128::MIN` and a quarter; under `Floor` and `Wrap` over
`AtTheBottom` it answers `i128::MIN`, not the slot the named position `i128::MIN - 2` wraps to. Each
carries the same construction one slot inside the index as its control, where the carry lands exactly.
`the_edges.rs`' comment on the carry says what it pins rather than that it "saturates the same way".

## CHANGE: the translation law FROM offsets 0 to 255 against a reference at zero TO references off zero on both sides

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_translation_law.rs` (new),
`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_top_of_the_index.rs`, `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/mod.rs`

The law moves out of `the_top_of_the_index.rs` into its own file. Four pairs, each the same span with
an even shift and one sign on both sides, each reference's lowest slot not a multiple of its span:

- `[1000, 1255]` against `AtTheTop`, offsets from the lowest slot -300 to 255;
- `[-2000, -1745]` against `AtTheBottom`, offsets 0 to 555;
- `[2^64 + 1000, 2^65 + 999]` against `[i128::MAX - 2^64 + 1, i128::MAX]`, offsets -300 to 300 and
  within 300 below the top;
- `[-2^65 - 1000, -2^64 - 1001]` against `[i128::MIN, i128::MIN + 2^64 - 1]`, offsets 0 to 300 and
  within 300 either side of the top.

Through `adapt` and `panic_on_overflow` over all 18 mode and policy signatures, four remainders and
three dithers. The law is also a function returning the first disagreement for a given completion, and
`the_law_reports_each_broken_completion` feeds it the shipped completion (no disagreement) and three
broken ones, each of which it must report: a wrap that subtracts before reducing, saturating; a wrap
anchored at zero instead of the lowest slot; an in-range test with no step onto the lowest slot. Each
broken one is also shown to pass the old offsets 0 to 255 against a reference at zero where it does,
so the test says which new region catches it.

Cross-end positions by hand, at spans 256 and `2^64`: `i128::MIN` into the range at the top, on the
grid and with a quarter under `Floor`, `Ceil` and `TowardZero`, over the three policies; and `i128::MAX`
plus a quarter into the range at the bottom under `Floor` and `Ceil`, over the three policies.

Negative control run once by hand besides the committed ones: the wrap's reduction of `lo` removed in
`apply.rs`, the new file's cases fail, restored and `touch`ed.

## CHANGE: the platform-width spelling FROM guarded by nothing TO a lint

File: `mock/lints/the_platform_width_points_read_the_pointer_width.rs` (new)

A crate lint on `arvo-format`, `HARD_ERROR`: each of `pub type USize` and `pub type ISize` must exist
and its right-hand side must read `usize::BITS`. Missing either alias is a finding too, so deleting one
does not pass silently. Tests: name, not declared off, registered, severity; the shipped spelling is
silent; a literal width fires for each alias; a missing alias fires; another crate is not read.
