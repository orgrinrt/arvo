## CHANGE: `Exact::between`'s rustdoc and the module header FROM saying the carry cannot leave the index TO where it pins

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`

`between`'s rustdoc says the constructor saturates where the carry leaves the index, what it stores, and
that the map adapts the stored position. The module header stops saying the step's intermediate "lives
inside two private functions": `Rounded` and `round_slot` are crate-visible and addition reads them.

Verification: `grep -n 'like every other position\|two private functions' crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`
returns nothing.

## CHANGE: the carry FROM asserted only as a stored slot TO pinned through the map at both ends

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_top_of_the_index.rs`,
`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_edges.rs`

`a_carry_past_the_index_pins_at_the_constructor_and_the_map_adapts_what_is_stored`:
`between(Slot::at(i128::MAX), 9/4)` stores the slot `i128::MAX` and a quarter; under `Ceil` and `Wrap`
over `AtTheTop` it answers the lowest slot, not two above it, and the overflow verdict reports it.
`between(Slot::at(i128::MIN), -7/4)` stores `i128::MIN` and a quarter; under `Floor` and `Wrap` over
`AtTheBottom` it answers `i128::MIN`, not the slot the named position `i128::MIN - 2` wraps to. Each
carries the same construction one slot inside the index as its control, where the carry lands exactly.
`the_edges.rs`' comment on the carry says what it pins rather than that it "saturates the same way".

## CHANGE: the translation law FROM offsets 0 to 255 against a reference at zero TO references off zero on both sides

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_translation_law.rs` (new),
`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_top_of_the_index.rs`, `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/mod.rs`

The law moves out of `the_top_of_the_index.rs` into its own file. Four pairs, each the same span with
an even shift and one sign on both sides, each reference's lowest slot not a multiple of its span:

- `[1000, 1255]` against `AtTheTop`, offsets from the lowest slot -300 to 255;
- `[-2000, -1745]` against `AtTheBottom`, offsets 0 to 555;
- `[2^64 + 1000, 2^65 + 999]` against `[i128::MAX - 2^64 + 1, i128::MAX]`, offsets -300 to 300 and
  within 300 below the top;
- `[-2^65 - 1000, -2^64 - 1001]` against `[i128::MIN, i128::MIN + 2^64 - 1]`, offsets 0 to 300 and
  within 300 either side of the top.

Through `adapt` and `panic_on_overflow` over all 18 mode and policy signatures, four remainders and
three dithers. The law is also a function over a `Map`, a completion and a verdict, returning the first
disagreement as a `Maybe`. `the_law_holds_of_the_shipped_map_and_reports_each_broken_one` feeds it the
shipped map, which neither the old offsets 0 to 255 against a reference at zero nor the new law
reports, and a wrap anchored at zero instead of the lowest slot, which the old law passes and the new
one reports. A wrap that subtracts the lowest slot before reducing, with wrapping arithmetic as a
release build of the naive code would, passes the new law too: `i128::MIN` and `i128::MAX` are
congruent to every range end modulo a power of two, so at spans 256 and `2^64` it answers what the
shipped wrap answers. Where it differs is a span that is not a power of two, and the far-end file
below is what catches it.

`a_verdict_missing_the_step_onto_the_lowest_slot_is_the_same_at_both_ends` is its own test, because
that defect is the same under every translation and no translation law can see it: the broken verdict
passes the new law, and for every range in the file a `Ceil` from a quarter under the lowest slot is
judged in range by the shipped verdict and out of range by the broken one, with the shipped wrap
answering the lowest slot.

Cross-end positions by hand, in `the_far_end_of_the_index.rs` (new), over `AtTheTop` and `AtTheBottom`
at span 256, `WideTop` and `WideBottom` at `2^64`, and two ranges of span 200 at each end:
`i128::MIN` into the range at the top, on the grid and with a quarter, under `Floor`, `Ceil` and
`TowardZero` and the three policies; `i128::MAX` plus a quarter into the range at the bottom under
`Floor`, `Ceil` and `TowardZero` and the three policies, each with the verdict out of range.
`a_wrap_that_subtracts_first_goes_wrong_only_where_the_span_is_not_a_power_of_two` runs nine of these
through the shipped map and the subtracting one and asserts they disagree exactly on the out-of-range
cases at span 200.

Negative control run by hand besides the committed ones: the wrap's `lo.rem_euclid(span)` replaced by
`0` in `apply.rs`. Fourteen tests failed, the four translation tests and both far-end wrap tests among
them. Restored to the exact line and `touch`ed. The committed `anchored_at_zero` map is the same break
kept as a test.

## CHANGE: the per-target arms FROM one per pointer width TO none

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/tests/the_platform_width.rs`,
`crates/arvo-placement/src/tests/the_platform_width.rs`

The `cfg(target_pointer_width)` arms and their `// FIXME:` markers go in both crates, the `msp430`
sentence with them. The placement test reads the pointer width as
`declared_slot_width::<<USize as Format>::Slots>()`, which `arvo-format`'s host tests pin against
`usize::BITS`, asserts `ISize` declares the same, and asserts carrier, access and stride equal it with
sole occupancy. The comparison against a literal-width point selected by `cfg` goes, and so do the
asserts that read the declared width back against itself.

## CHANGE: the platform-width spelling FROM guarded by nothing TO a lint

File: `mock/lints/the_platform_width_points_read_the_pointer_width.rs` (new)

A crate lint on `arvo-format`, `HARD_ERROR`: each of `pub type USize` and `pub type ISize` must exist
and its right-hand side must read `usize::BITS`. Missing either alias is a finding too, so deleting one
does not pass silently. Tests: name, not declared off, registered, severity; the shipped spelling is
silent; a literal width fires for each alias; a width read from `u64::BITS` fires; a missing alias
fires; a commented-out alias counts as missing; a longer name such as `USizeLike` is not the alias;
another crate is not read.
