## What the leakage greps found in the work that just closed

The round before this one widened the additive-identity predicate and added two
test files. Running the tier-one leakage greps over what it touched turns up
comments naming the process that produced the code rather than what the code is.

Shipped tests are a tier-one surface. They go in the package and are browsable
beside the rendered documents, so a comment in one narrating which design round
found something is the same leak as one in a readme. That is stated in the
workspace's documentation rules and it is what these break.

Two classes, and only the first is new to this crate.

**Research paths in shipped source.** Two comments in `tests/identity.rs` cite
`mock/research/sketches/phase-over-the-magnitude-range/` by path. Nothing in this
crate did that before, the directory is internal, and a reader of the published
package cannot open it. The substance survives the removal in both cases: what the
comments are actually saying is that a separate instrument checked the predicate
against an independent enumeration, and that the extreme coordinates were measured
rather than reasoned about, and neither needs an address to be worth saying.

**The ceremony's own vocabulary.** `round` naming a design round, and `ratified`
naming a canon status, in comments whose subject is the code. Seven sites across
the two new test files and one in `slots.rs`. Each is describing how the project
came to know something, which is exactly what a design and its tests do not carry.

## What this round does not touch, and why it is worth naming

**The same two words are already through the older files, and those predate this
work.** `apply.rs` and `overflow.rs` carry `ratified` and `op's own correction`,
`tests/mod.rs` carries a construction attributed to a reviewer, and
`DESIGN.md.tmpl` carries `the earlier round` and `ratified` in about nine places,
all of which render into the published document.

That is a real defect and it is a different one: it is the whole crate's register
rather than a slip in one change, it reaches a document a stranger reads, and
fixing it means rewriting paragraphs whose subject is the design rather than
deleting a clause. It wants its own round with the greps run across every tier-one
surface at once, and doing it here would bury the correction that is actually this
round's in a diff four times its size.

So this round fixes what the last one introduced and leaves the rest named. The
count is what makes it actionable later: nine in `DESIGN.md.tmpl`, four in
`apply.rs`, two in `overflow.rs`, one in `tests/mod.rs`, one in `width.rs`.

## How the fix reads

The research citations go and what they were carrying stays, said plainly: that
the enumeration check is a separate instrument rather than this file, and that the
extreme cases are measured. A reader who wants the instrument finds it the way
anybody finds anything internal, which is not from inside a published package.

The ceremony words go by rewriting the sentence around them. A comment saying a
previous round missed a case becomes one saying what the case is and why an arm
that does not reach it cannot see it, which is the useful half and the half that
stays true when nobody remembers there were rounds.
