# The proof is invariant

**Date:** 2026-11-20
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `arvo-bits`, the root design, `mock/tools/the-positions`
**Source:** `mock/research/202611200325_review-arvo-bits-tier/PASS_3.md`, the third and last review of `work/arvo-bits-tier`, at `1fd4d78e`; the round is on `work/arvo-bits-third-review`, cut from `dev` at `3c78ee81`

The third review returned merge after fixes, with four findings to fix before landing and six that may be follow-ups. This round takes the four and what of the six is cheap and in this crate's lane; what it leaves is a ticket.

## 1. The root design says no crate but two enables an unstable gate

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl` says the contracts a numeral satisfies take `const_trait_impl` by default in `arvo-contracts` and `arvo-numeral`, and that no other crate enables an unstable feature by default. `arvo-bits` enables it by default, for its six const traits. Decided: the paragraph names `arvo-bits` beside the two, and says how a consumer stays clear of the gate: calling an inherent method or the tier from an ordinary function needs no feature on its side, and only a consumer evaluating the tier through a `[const]` bound in its own `const fn` enables `const_trait_impl` itself, which is what `obligation::the_unstable_machinery_does_not_reach_a_consumer` asks for.

## 2. The proof is covariant in the container, so a coercion carries it across

`Within<B, const I: u32>(PhantomData<B>)` is covariant in `B`. Two containers related by subtyping, `Wrap<for<'a> fn(&'a ())>` at 128 bits and `Wrap<fn(&'static ())>` at 8 bits, let a proof made for the wide one coerce to the narrow one and index it at 100. Decided: the field becomes `PhantomData<fn(B) -> B>`, which is invariant in `B`, and which is also `Send` and `Sync` whatever `B` is, so the proof's auto traits stop following the container's. The case is a `compile_fail` ui case, `a_proof_carried_to_a_narrower_container_is_refused`, pinning the mismatch the invariant field gives.

The sentences saying no container can take an index past its width are narrowed to what the proof holds: an index or an amount below the width the container declares in `BitWidth::WIDTH`. That the declared width is the width the container's storage holds is the implementor's, as an inherent method's arithmetic is; for `Bits<N>`, the only container here, `WIDTH` is `N`. The sentences are in `src/tier.rs`, `src/lib.rs`, the crate's `DESIGN.md.tmpl` and `README.md.tmpl`, and the root design's table row.

## 4. `the-positions` still files the names this branch ships as gone

`mock/tools/the-positions/src/supply.rs` lists `Bits` and `BitAccess` as names arvo no longer exports, and a passing test asserts it. The class is wider: `USize`, `Unsigned`, `Capacity` and `Dim` ship again as well, in `arvo-numeral`, `arvo-format` and `arvo-capacity`. Decided: all seven move to the shipped list, the test asserts what is still gone and what ships again, and a control reads every crate's `lib.rs` by literal path, so a name exported on a `pub` line cannot stay on the gone list; a second control holds that reading against a re-export, a declaration and two near misses, so the first cannot pass by reading nothing.

## 5. A container of its own is held for access alone

The `Pair` cases cover `BitAccess`. Decided: `Pair` gains `BitSequenceBy` in a case of its own, refused past its width for a shift at run time, `a_container_of_its_own_shifted_past_its_width_is_refused`; an impl of `BitSequence` by hand, `an_impl_of_the_callers_sequence_by_hand_is_refused`, and an impl of `BitAccess` on a type with no `BitAccessAt`, the seal's other branch, `an_impl_of_the_callers_trait_without_its_supertrait_is_refused`, join the refusals.

## 6. Three oracles restate the implementation

`tests/the_bit_access.rs` reads a bit as `(v >> I) & 1`, and `tests/the_bit_sequence.rs` takes a shift as `(v << K) & mask` and `v >> K`: the same expressions `src/ops.rs` computes. Decided: each is taken bit at a time, the way `written` and `rotated_up` already are, so the oracle is a definition the source does not share.

## 7. The constant test evaluates four of eleven operations

`tests/the_bit_tier_in_a_constant.rs` evaluates `and`, `is_empty`, `rotate_up` and `bit` through `[const]` bounds. Decided: it evaluates all eleven, so the design's sentence that the tier evaluates in a constant holds of every operation it names.
