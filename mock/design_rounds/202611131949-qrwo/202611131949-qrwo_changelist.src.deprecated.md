# Source changelist: the ring operations through the value

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** 202611131949-qrwo, branch `work/arvo-values`.
**Design:** `202611131949-qrwo_changelist.doc.lock.md`, and under it
`mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`.

## Current state

`arvo-numeral` has `add`, `fits_add`, the order and `Identity<Additive>` on `Num`. `rung.rs` has, per
carrier width, the two adaptations, the sum, the index moves and the order. Its cargo features are the
four rung mutants. `arvo-format` has `mul`, `product_position` and `is_multipliable`, no function
naming the member that is one, and no signed fixed-point point under a name of its own.

## Changes proposed

### `arvo-format`

`src/multiplication.rs`: `multiplicative_identity::<F>() -> Maybe<Slot>`, as the design states it,
built on the module's private step. `src/lib.rs`: its re-export, and `points::Fixed<BITS, FRAC>`, an
alias of `Biased<BITS, FRAC, 0>`. `src/multiplication/tests.rs` or a sibling test file: the identity's
law, exhaustive at widths one to six, both families, exponents minus seven to two, the three phases
and radix ten, in both directions.

### `arvo-numeral`

`src/rung.rs`: `sub_s`, `sub_u`, `neg_s`, `neg_u`, `mul_s`, `mul_u` per carrier width, with the three
operation mutants behind `cfg!(feature = ...)`.

`src/num.rs`: `sub`, `neg`, `mul`, `fits_sub`, `fits_neg`, `fits_mul`. `mul` forces
`product_position`'s obligation in an inline constant, takes the rung at a quantum exponent of zero and
`arvo_format::mul` elsewhere, the latter under
`FIXME(arvo_numeral_s_fixed_point_product_goes_through_the_128_bit)`.

`src/identity.rs`: `Multiplicative` and `Identity<Multiplicative>` for `Num<S, C>`. `src/lib.rs`: the
re-export of `Multiplicative`.

`Cargo.toml`: the features `mutant-sub`, `mutant-neg` and `mutant-mul`.

`src/tests.rs`: the rungs' three operations against the oracle at the widths the sum is asked at.
`tests/common/mod.rs` and the band binaries: the three operations and their verdicts through the value
type on the pairs the sum is asked on, the unsigned 64-bit integer left out of the product.
`tests/fixed_point_product.rs`: the product over `Fixed<6, -3>`, `UFixed<6, -3>` and `UFixed<6, 3>`,
and the named tie in each mode. The identity's tests beside the additive one's. The debug-check
binaries: each new operation under `Abort` and under `Quiet`, and their release twins.

`tests/ui/`: `sub` across two signatures, `mul` on the unsigned 64-bit integer beside 63 bits, the
multiplicative identity on a one-bit signed integer beside a two-bit one, each with its diagnostic.

`tests/consumer/`: each new public item reached.

`mock/research/sketches/202611131949-qrwo_value-mutants/`: the red run of each of the three mutants,
with what each shows.
