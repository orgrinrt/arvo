# Source changelist: the override and the default it overrides

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** 202611132028-qn1r, branch `work/arvo-values`.
**Design:** `202611132028-qn1r_changelist.doc.lock.md`, and under it
`mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`.

## Current state

A value is held under one signature and has no door to a sibling but the three public ones in a row.
`NamesAdaptation` has one associated type and no parameter, and the four aliases read it. `arvo-format`
has `Signature` and `Adapt` and no alias that replaces one coordinate of a signature.

## Changes proposed

### `arvo-format`

`src/adapt.rs`: the type aliases `WithOverflow<S, O>` and `WithRounding<S, R>`. `src/lib.rs`: their
re-export beside `Signature`.

### `arvo-numeral`

`src/num.rs`: `under::<T>()`, bounded `T: Sole + DeclaredSignature<Format = S::Format>`, whose body
hands the carrier to `Num::<T, C>::from_carrier`; `with_overflow::<O>()` and `with_rounding::<R>()`,
each `under` at the alias, with the sibling's `Sole` and its format equality in the where clause. The
mutant `mutant-under` sits in `under` behind `cfg!(feature = ...)`.

`src/alias.rs`: `NamesAdaptation<F: Format>`, the impl of it for `Adapt<R, O>` over every `F`, and
`Uint`, `Int`, `USize` and `ISize` reading it at their own format.

`Cargo.toml`: the feature `mutant-under`.

`tests/the_crossing.rs`: the crossing at widths 1 to 8, both families, every ordered pair of policies
and of modes, every member, against the three-door composition and back; the named sum on each side of
a crossing; the two shorthands against `under` at the written sibling.

`tests/ui/`: a value under `WithOverflow` naming `Clamp` beside `Saturate`, a value under
`WithRounding` naming `Stochastic` beside `HalfEven`, a crossing to a signature over another format
beside one to a sibling, each refusal with its diagnostic.

`tests/consumer/`: a preset implementing `NamesAdaptation` for two formats with a different adaptation
each, an alias over `Uint` defaulting to it, an override at a type and at a value.

`mock/research/sketches/202611132028-qn1r_value-mutants/`: the red run of `mutant-under`, with what it
shows.
