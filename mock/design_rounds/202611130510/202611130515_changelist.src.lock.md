## CHANGE: the exact step's ending is one crate-private module

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/placing.rs`, `src/multiplication.rs`, `src/lib.rs`.

`placing` holds `Reduced` and `reduced_phase` (the phase in lowest terms, `d` positive, or nothing
where it does not denote), `Powers` and `powers` (`r^max(e, 0)` and `r^max(-e, 0)` in the four limbs),
`times` over two maybe-values, `Placed`, `over` (the floor and residue over a `u128` divisor),
`is_held`, `saturated` and `positioned`. The product's `step`, `placed` and `product_is_carried` read
them; its own `Placed`, `corner_is_held`, `saturated` and `positioned` go. What the product computes is
unchanged, and its 25 tests pass over the move.

## CHANGE: division

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/division.rs`, `src/lib.rs`.

`Div<S>`, arity two. `Divisor<F>` holds a slot, `Copy`, with `constant::<B>()` forcing
`ConstantDivisor<F, B>::ADMITTED` (`B` in range by `slot_in_range`, and not the slot
`cancelling_slot` names at the smallest magnitude), `of(slot)` forcing `NoZeroMember<F>::ADMITTED`
(`has_additive_identity` false), and `slot()`. A `#[cfg(test)]` `assumed(slot)` builds one with no
obligation for the sweeps, which divide by every non-zero member of formats whose range holds the
zero. `Divisible<F>::ADMITTED` asserts the four conditions in the design's order with their messages,
the last three carrying `FIXME`s under `arvo_quotient_and_conversion_over_a_magnitude_indexed_quantu`,
`arvo_s_exact_position_carries_no_remainder_denominator_past` and
`arvo_quotient_and_conversion_positions_past_the_slot_index`. The step forms `n + a d` and `n + b d`,
the per-divisor denominator `d |n + b d| u` narrowed to `u128`, and the numerator
`d (n + a d) v - n (n + b d) u`, negated with the denominator when `n + b d` is below zero. A zero
divisor factor saturates toward the positive end; a denominator past `2^128 - 1` or a numerator past
the limbs, both only for a non-member divisor once the obligation holds, saturates on the sign of the
two factors' quotient, which the rustdoc on `quotient_position` states. `extreme_divisors` names the
range's ends and the pole's two neighbours from `div_euclid`, and `quotient_is_carried` places each
against the dividend's two ends. `quotient_position`, `div`, `is_divisible`, and a `#[cfg(test)]`
`unforced_position`. The crate root re-exports `Div`, `Divisor`, `div`, `is_divisible` and
`quotient_position`.

## CHANGE: conversion

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/conversion.rs`, `src/lib.rs`.

`Convert<F, S>`, arity one, `Signature = S`. `Convertible<F, T>::ADMITTED` asserts both phases
denote, both quanta constant, `STEP` formed (the denominator `d_F V_F U_T d_T` under `2^128`), and both
range ends held, the last three with the same tickets as division. `STEP` holds the source's reduced
phase, `U_F V_T d_T` as a maybe, the target's term `n_T d_F V_F U_T` and the divisor. `placed` forms
`n_F + a d_F`, scales it unless it is zero, subtracts the term and calls `over`. `converted_position`,
`convert`, `is_convertible`, and a `#[cfg(test)]` `unforced_position`. The crate root re-exports
`Convert`, `convert`, `converted_position` and `is_convertible`.

## CHANGE: the quotient's suite

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/division/tests/`.

`the_oracle.rs`: every member pair with a non-zero divisor at widths one to eight over eleven formats
(radix two at `e` of 0, -3 and 2 over both families, a phase of a half and of minus a third, radix ten
at -1 and 1 and at -2 with a phase of two thirds), every mode and shipped policy, against the applied
map's oracle fed `Ratio`; then a planted step that leaves the phase's term in, which the same machinery
reports at two phased formats, with a zero-phase control at which the planted step is the shipped one
and nothing is reported. `the_divisor.rs`: `has_additive_identity` against the oracle's own count of a
zero member at eight formats, both doors against `Ratio`, the non-member zero saturating toward the
positive end beside an ordinary neighbour, and every condition on both sides of its boundary.
`the_edges.rs`: every width nine to 64 of both families over eight grids, the divisors at the range's
ends, the pole's neighbours (found by stepping, not by `div_euclid`) and the members beside each,
against dividends at the ends and around zero, checked against an oracle in `Big`, and the verdict
against the oracle's reading of the eight corners; exactly two formats are held out, the 64-bit
ranges at `2^-64`.

## CHANGE: the conversion's suite

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/conversion/tests/`.

`the_oracle.rs`: every member at widths one to eight onto six targets, finer, coarser, narrower,
from a phase, onto a phase, and from radix ten, every mode and policy, against the applied map's
oracle; the identity onto the source's own grid at four formats; a planted step that forgets the
target's phase, reported, with a control onto a target with no phase. `the_edges.rs`: every source
width nine to 64 onto six 64-bit targets, the range ends and the slots beside them against `Big`, the
verdict against the oracle's reading of the two ends; exactly one pair is held out.

## CHANGE: the inverse round trip

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/multiplication/tests/the_inverse_round_trip.rs`.

`law::the_multiplicative_inverse_round_trip` through `mul` under wrapping at unsigned widths one to
63, every member up to 20 bits and the ends, their neighbours and a stride of 4094 members past that,
with the inverse plus two as the case that has to break at every width from two. Width 64 at exponent
zero is asserted not multipliable; 64, 65 and 128 are carried by
`arvo_law_the_multiplicative_inverse_round_trip_is_unreachabl`.

## CHANGE: the compile-fail pairs and the root surface

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/compile_fail.rs`, `tests/ui/`.

Five pairs, each a refused file with its committed stderr and a twin differing in the offending token
that compiles: a constant divisor at zero against one; `Divisor::of` over a range from zero against
one from one; division over `Indexed<-3, 4>` against `Constant<-3>`; conversion from the same pair of
quanta; and conversion from `UFixed<8, -64>` onto `UFixed<8, 64>` against `UFixed<8, 63>`. One file
that has to compile names every new item from the crate root.
