# Src changelist: the identity quantifies over the magnitude range

Doc changelist: `202609011445_changelist.doc.lock.md`.

## The arithmetic this implements

From `phase + i * quantum(m) = 0` with `phase = (PN/PD) * r^BASE` and
`q(m) = r^(BASE + SLOPE*m)`, dividing by `r^BASE` and clearing the denominator:

```
i * PD * r^(SLOPE*m) = -PN
```

`BASE` cancels and does not appear, which the sketch checks by moving it and
watching no answer move, and which a test pins. The remaining work is one exact
integer division per magnitude, and the existential is a search over the admitted
magnitudes.

## CHANGE: free fn `cancelling_slot` ADDED
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`
`cancelling_slot::<F>(magnitude) -> Maybe<i64>`, the slot that cancels the phase
at one magnitude when an integral one exists and the arithmetic stays in range.
Named rather than inlined because the identity question is a conjunction and a law
over the conjunction cannot say which half a failure came from.

**It carries `notko::Maybe` rather than the core carrier**, which the
`no-bare-option` lint refuses outright and which was how the first cut of this
function was written. The lint caught it at the commit gate. The internal
`checked_mul` went with it: the overflow test is now an explicit comparison
against the width's ends, which also removes a division by a radix of zero that a
check-time evaluation could reach.

## CHANGE: free fn `has_additive_identity` FROM evaluating at magnitude zero TO searching the admitted magnitudes
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`
Was `PHASE_NUM % PHASE_DEN == 0 && slot_in_range(-(PHASE_NUM / PHASE_DEN))`, which
reads no coordinate of the quantum. Becomes a search for an admitted magnitude
whose cancelling slot is in the slot range. A format admitting no magnitude now
answers false, because the loop does not run.

**Two arithmetic cases went with it that the round did not set out to fix.** The
replaced expression diverges at the least numerator over minus one, where the
remainder overflows, and at that numerator over one, where the negation of the
quotient overflows. Both are reachable through the open trait, neither through the
shipped points, and both are measured in
`mock/research/sketches/phase-over-the-magnitude-range/extreme_phase_output.txt`.
Carrying the division one width up makes both defined, so the repair is the
widening rather than a guard, and four arms pin the answers.
Verification: `grep -n "MAGNITUDES" mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs` returns
a hit inside `has_additive_identity`, where there was none.

## CHANGE: const `MAGNITUDE_SEARCH_BOUND` ADDED
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`
The scaling stops at 127 and that is exact rather than approximate. The running
product is one width up, which holds magnitudes below `2^127`, so at a radix of at
least two it leaves that range within 127 steps and every later magnitude answers
`Isnt` anyway. A radix below two never scales at all and the loop is skipped
outright there, so the bound governs the reachable case only.
Verification: `the_search_bound_is_past_where_a_radix_of_two_can_still_answer` and
`the_search_bound_loses_nothing_where_the_quantum_does_not_move` are the two halves
of that derivation, pinned.

## CHANGE: `Slots::ADMITTED` gains a `compile_fail` doctest and its control
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/slots.rs`
Not planned and not optional. The three new obligations needed a way to express
their refusal, and the answer turned out to be a doctest, because `trybuild` runs
`cargo check` and the obligation is a const evaluated at monomorphisation, so a
`trybuild` case reports a refused program as compiling.

**`Slots` had carried that obligation since it was written with nothing testing
it**, and its documentation described the refusal as a fact. Same class, same
file, so it is closed here rather than left as the one untested member of four.
Measured: `rustc --emit=metadata` accepts a program whose obligation fails while
catching an ordinary type error in the same invocation, and a full build refuses
it with `E0080`.

## The tests this adds, and what each is for

The existing phase arms all run through `Biased`, which fixes the denominator at
two, the quantum at `Constant` and the slots at `Signed`. So they measure one
point of three axes. A test-local format generic over both phase coordinates, the
ambient domain, the quantum and the slot range is what lets the sweep move them.

**The cancelling slot, on its own**, in both directions, with a control asserting
the two verdicts differ so a function stuck at one value fails.

**The denominator sweep**, one to sixteen plus a spread past it, against numerators
that are and are not multiples. Two was the only denominator any arm used before.

**Every quantum family and every slot family**, and a radix other than two, which
no arm had ever used for this law.

**The whole phase whose cancelling slot is out of range**, with the control that
widens the range and gets the identity back.

**The two magnitude-range witnesses**, each with the control that cuts the
magnitude range to one and asserts the answer goes back, so the search is what
found it rather than the first magnitude.

**The identity answer against membership at zero**, over a matrix of twenty
formats, which is the design's own claim about what this predicate is.

**The extremes of the phase coordinates**, four arms including the control that
divides to a phase of one and does have an identity.

**The search bound**, both halves of its derivation.

**The obligations**, each with the control that admits every shipped instance and
a third arm asserting the two verdicts differ.

## Would any of it have caught the defect

Measured rather than assumed, and recorded in
`mock/research/sketches/phase-over-the-magnitude-range/mutation_the_search_is_cut_to_one_magnitude.md`.
Cutting the search to one magnitude fails eight arms. **None of the three arms the
suite already had is among them**, so the previous cut could not see this class at
all: every phase it tried sat in the constant family, where there is one magnitude
and the search has nothing to search.
