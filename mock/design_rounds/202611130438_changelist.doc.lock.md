# Doc changelist: the remainder carries a 128-bit denominator

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** step AR6 of the roadmap, its first round, on `work/oracle-fraction-wide`.
**Topics consumed:** `202611130436_topic.the-remainder-carries-a-128-bit-denominator.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`. The readme and the registry are not edited; the
proposal row for the stance is written in the source changelist's round.

## Canon

`ruling::the_derivation_is_a_placement_and_the_operation_set_is_an_admission_rule` governs every
admission touched here, unchanged: each condition moved is still a function of the declared signature.
`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` is why the new bounds stay in `ADMITTED` rather
than in a branch. The registry is silent on the width of the stored remainder, so the width is a stance
of this round, stated with its compromise below and recorded as a `one_expert` proposal.

## CHANGE: the exact position names two coordinates

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `## The exact position, which is what the map takes`.

The two paragraphs that called the remainder a `Fraction` are replaced. A caller hands `Exact::between`
and `Dither::at` a `Fraction`, which stays as it was. The remainder an `Exact` stores is a coordinate
of its own: a numerator below an unsigned 128-bit denominator, in `[0, 1)`, crate-private. The section
says why it is wider (a quotient by a 64-bit member and a product's `d^2 r^max(-e, 0)` both pass what a
positive `i64` holds), that the midpoint test reads `num` against `den - num` and the dither test is a
192-bit cross product, and that no public constructor takes a wide remainder yet.

## CHANGE: addition's remainder refusal narrows to a phase that does not denote

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, addition's `### What it refuses`.

The third refusal is now only a phase that does not denote, a refusal by design. Every denoting
phase's reduced remainder, up to `2^63`, is held, built in the wide coordinate directly. The
placeholder and its ticket leave this section.

## CHANGE: addition's laws name the pair at the least value

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, addition's `### The laws this section owes tests for`.

"whose phase remainder the position cannot hold" becomes "whose phase does not denote". The twin that
compiles is the phase with odd numerator over `2^63`, checked against the oracle at every member pair
of a small range, at the range ends, under every mode.

## CHANGE: the product's exact step and denominator condition move to 128 bits

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the product's exact step and `### What it refuses, and
what is not yet admitted`.

The factor bound becomes `2^127`, the phase term is formed in the four limbs, and the one division is
by `D` under `2^128`: four limbs by one where `D` is under `2^64`, one bit at a time otherwise. The
condition on `D` moves from `2^63 - 1` to `2^128 - 1`. It reads `e >= -127` at radix two and
`e >= -38` at radix ten at zero phase, every phase at a non-negative exponent, and a phase denominator
up to `2^32 - 1` at `e = -64`. The comparison with addition's condition is dropped, since addition no
longer has one.

## CHANGE: the product's edge sweep is named at `-127`

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the product's laws. The fractional exponent named in
the edge sweep moves from `-62` to `-127`.

## CHANGE: the open items trade the widening for the missing constructor

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `## Open items`.

The item naming the widening of the remainder is replaced by the one this round leaves: no public
constructor takes a remainder over an unsigned 128-bit denominator, under ticket
`arvo_has_no_public_constructor_for_an_exact_position_whose_r`. The product past `2^128 - 1` stays held
under `arvo_s_exact_position_carries_no_remainder_denominator_past`. The count of items stays six.
