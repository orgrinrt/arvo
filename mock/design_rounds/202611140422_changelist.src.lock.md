# Src changelist: the IEEE 754 module without the allow

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140422`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140422_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Topics consumed:** `202611140422_topic.the-ieee-module-without-the-allow.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/`: `ieee754/mod.rs`, the new `ieee754_sweep/mod.rs`, and the three
IEEE 754 parity suites.

## CHANGE: the shared module carries only what all three suites read

`tests/ieee754/mod.rs`: the `allow(dead_code)` and the comment explaining it come out, and so do `inputs`
and `misses`. What stays, `held_below` and `underflow` among it, is read by every suite that declares the
module.

## CHANGE: the whole-range sweep is a module of its own

`tests/ieee754_sweep/mod.rs`: `inputs` and `misses`, unchanged, reading the shared module through `super`.
`tests/ieee754_binary_parity.rs` and `tests/ieee754_decimal_parity.rs` declare it and take `misses` from it.

## CHANGE: ties to away among the planted misses below the smallest subnormal

`tests/ieee754_underflow_parity.rs`: `a_wrong_direction_below_the_smallest_subnormal_is_reported` asserts
that decimal64's underflow inputs landed toward positive miss against ties to away, which rounds them to zero.

## NO CHANGE: the rest

No case is dropped or changed. The three suites build with no warning and pass: binary 14, decimal 5,
underflow 7.
