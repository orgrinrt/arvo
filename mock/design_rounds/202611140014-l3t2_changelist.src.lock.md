# Source changelist: arvo takes a digest

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140014-l3t2`, job `arvo-digest`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140014-l3t2_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** the new crate `mock/crates/arvo-digest/`, the host door in `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/`,
the workspace manifest, `deny.toml`, and the `digest-crossover` bench under `mock/benches/`.

## Comparison to deprecated changelist

`202611140014-l3t2_changelist.src.deprecated.md` took `&[u8]` and held the digest in `u32`, `u64` and
`u128`, which the numeric lints refuse outside an introducing crate. This one adds the host door in
`arvo-numeral` (topic D7 and D8) and moves the digest onto it: the input becomes `arvo_numeral::Bytes`,
the carrier `arvo_numeral::Word`, FNV-1a walks the run with `split_first` over `Word` arithmetic, the
crossover becomes a `shorter_than` const argument rather than a `usize` constant, and the sealed trait
in `digest.rs` is named `DigestWidth` so it does not repeat `arvo-numeral`'s `Sealed`. The bench, the
licence and the workspace changes are unchanged.

### CHANGE: the workspace

Unchanged: the same member, the same `arvo-digest` and `xxhash-rust` workspace dependencies, and
`BSL-1.0` in `deny.toml`. `arvo-numeral` gains `notko` for `Maybe`, and its consumer crate's lockfile
follows.

### CREATE: `mock/crates/arvo-digest/`

The same modules and tests. `Carrier` now names an `arvo_numeral::Word` rather than a host integer,
every entry point takes `Bytes`, `raw` returns the `Word`, and `Digest<32>::bits` reaches
`Bits::masked` through `Word::widen`. The crate depends on `arvo-numeral` and `notko` as well.

### CREATE: the `digest-crossover` bench

The same three variants, sizes and `ByteRoutine` entries in the driver; each variant wraps its slice
in `Bytes` and reads the digest through `Word::host`, and depends on `arvo-numeral` for it.

## CHANGE: the workspace

`mock/Cargo.toml`: `crates/arvo-digest` joins `members`; `workspace.dependencies` gains `arvo-digest` by
path and `xxhash-rust = { version = "0.8.19",
default-features = false, features = ["xxh3", "const_xxh3"] }`.

`deny.toml`: `BSL-1.0` joins `[licenses] allow`, with a comment naming op's answer.

## CREATE: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/host.rs`

`Byte` and `Bytes<'a>`, each `repr(transparent)` with `of` and `host`; `Bytes::split_first` returning
`Maybe<(Byte, Bytes<'a>)>` and `Bytes::shorter_than::<const N: usize>()` returning `Bool`. `At<const
BITS: u32>`, the sealed `HostWord` with `type Host` at 32, 64 and 128, and `Word<const BITS: u32>` with
`of` and `host`, `xor_byte` and `wrapping_mul` at 32 and 64, and `widen` from 32 to 64, which
`Digest<32>::bits` needs to reach `Bits::masked`: the design's "further word arithmetic joins when a
consumer needs it". All `const fn`. `src/lib.rs` gains
`pub mod host` and re-exports `Byte`, `Bytes`, `HostWord`, `At` and `Word`. A test module covers the
round trips, the walk, the length comparison, and both operations against hand-worked values with a
planted wrong value shown red.

## CREATE: `mock/crates/arvo-digest/`

`Cargo.toml`: depends on `arvo-numeral`, `arvo-bits` and `xxhash-rust`, `trybuild` as a dev-dependency for the
refused widths.

`src/lib.rs`: the crate documentation, `#![no_std]`, the modules and their re-exports.

`src/digest.rs`: `Width<const BITS: u16>`, the `Carrier` trait sealed by `DigestWidth`, naming the
`Word` of each of 32, 64 and 128,
`Digest<const BITS: u16>` with `raw()`, equality and copy, and `bits::<N>()` on `Digest<32>` and
`Digest<64>` with the compile-time refusal of `N` wider than the digest.

`src/fnv1a.rs`: `Fnv1a<const BITS: u16>`, `of` as `const fn` at 32 and 64, taking `Bytes` and walking
it with `split_first`, `xor_byte` and `wrapping_mul`.

`src/xxh3.rs`: `Xxh3<const BITS: u16>`, `of` and `of_const` at 64 and 128.

`src/switched.rs`: `Digest::<64>::of` and `of_const`, and the crossover as one private `const fn` asking
`shorter_than`, its value and the
findings file it comes from.

`tests/`: `fnv1a.rs` (the reference values, the planted wrong one), `xxh3.rs` (the sanity buffer and
vectors at 64 and 128 bits, const against runtime, the planted wrong one), `switched.rs` (each side of
the crossover, const against runtime), `bits.rs` (the masked cast), and `trybuild` cases for a width
`Width` does not admit and for `bits::<N>()` wider than the digest.

## CREATE: the `digest-crossover` bench

`mock/benches/variants/digest-fnv1a64/`, `digest-xxh3-64/` and `digest-xxh3-64-const/`: one cdylib
each, calling `Fnv1a::<64>::of`, `Xxh3::<64>::of` and `Xxh3::<64>::of_const`. `mock/benches/bench.toml`
gains the `digest-crossover` section over the length sweep, with `may_differ` set because FNV-1a and
XXH3 are different functions, and the driver's `routine_for_n` gains its `ByteRoutine` entries. The
run's CSV, meta and findings are committed under `mock/benches/`, and the decision note goes in
`mock/research/<stamp>_digest-crossover/NOTES.md`.
