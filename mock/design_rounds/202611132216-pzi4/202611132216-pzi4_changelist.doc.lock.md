## Changes proposed

### `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

The section "The exact accumulator" is removed, with every sentence elsewhere that names the type:
the canon paragraph, the debug check's sites, the aliases' note on splitting a saturating sum, the
proof paragraphs, the mutant, the compile-fail list. The canon paragraph says instead that
`question::which_chain_carrier_ships` is open and that the crate ships no chain carrier. "What is
not here" says the same and no longer describes a capacity type in terms of `take`.

`holds_index` leaves the list of host doors, which becomes two. The debug check paragraph says
`from_index` asks a private verdict. A sentence cites `ruling::the_ingest_door_waits_on_a_known_use`
as the reason no public verdict on a host integer is shipped.

"What is proved" gains the wide sweeps: the quotient, the conversion and the fractional product on
the edge lattice and seeded pairs at widths 9 to 64, against the tests' own arithmetic, and what
those leave out because the arithmetic is done in `i128`. The named divisors at widths 5 to 8 gain
an even one. The consumer paragraph says the rerun is a branch of the one test.
