# Source changelist: the standard targets, after review

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** `202611132100`, job `arvo-standards`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611132100_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/standards/ieee754.rs`, the crate's `tests/`, the root
`README.md`, the `gap` of one obligation, and the regenerated `docs/`.

## CHANGE: the directions are type aliases

`src/standards/ieee754.rs`, `rounding_direction`: `RoundTowardPositive`, `RoundTowardNegative`,
`RoundTiesToEven` and `RoundTowardZero` are `pub type` aliases of `Ceil`, `Floor`, `HalfEven` and
`TowardZero`, replacing the renamed `pub use`. The module documentation says why. Every path that names
them is unchanged. `arvo-numeral`'s `tests/compile_fail.rs` is the check: its eighteen cases print
`arvo_format::rounding::TowardZero` again.

## CHANGE: the significand's own obligation

`src/standards/ieee754.rs`: `Significand<A, P>` gains an inherent const `SIGN_AND_MAGNITUDE`, which
asserts `A::SIGNED` and `P >= 1`, and its `Slots` impl forces it from both `MAX` and `MIN`. The three
summary lines past 80 characters are shortened. `tests/ui/an_unsigned_interchange_is_refused.rs` and
`tests/ui/a_precision_of_zero_is_refused.rs`, with their `.stderr`, are the compile-fail cases, run
by `a_significand_that_is_not_sign_and_magnitude_is_refused_where_used` in `tests/compile_fail.rs`.

## CHANGE: one rule and one landing for the IEEE suites

`tests/ieee754/mod.rs`: `land` is written over a `Landing` trait, `overflows` and `read_off`, with a
blanket impl for every `DeclaredSignature`. `Direction`, `Standard` with `BINARY16`, `DECIMAL32` and
`DECIMAL64` at the standard's numbers, `rule`, `inputs`, `misses` and `agrees` move here, the rule
written over any radix and over the five directions. `tests/ties_away/mod.rs` gains `overflows` beside
`read_off`, sharing one tie test.

## CHANGE: decimal under all five directions

`tests/ieee754_decimal_parity.rs`: `TiesToAway<F, O>` lands through the alias; each format runs the
five directions against the rule; the planted arms add `HalfUp` for ties away and a precision one
short. The exponent-range arm compares the format against the standard's numbers the rule reads.

## CHANGE: binary16 and binary64 under the four binary directions

`tests/ieee754_binary_parity.rs`: binary64 runs the four directions against the host stepped by bits
(`host64`, `against_exact`, `step64`), with four planted arms. Binary16 runs the four against the rule
at radix two beside its encodings, with an exponent-range arm and planted arms. The `Floating` test is
replaced by the binary32 sweep over `Floating<25, -149, 254>`, asserting exactly one miss, and the
input at that miss landing finite at `-2^128`.

## CHANGE: the fimath sweep over a matrix of shapes

`tests/matlab_fimath_parity.rs`: a `Shape` computes the range and the wrap; a macro writes one module
per shape, thirteen of them, signed and unsigned, widths 1 to 64, fraction lengths from -4 to 15, each
with every method and the Round alias under both actions and the planted arms. The method swaps run
under Wrap, which keeps two rules apart wherever they differ.

## CHANGE: the readme and the obligation

`README.md` names IEEE 754 beside MATLAB. The `gap` of
`obligation::every_standard_convention_expressible_as_an_alias_over_the_primitives` says what the
suites now hold and calls the standards ruling ratified, which it is.

## Not changed

The casts in the impl bodies stay: the coordinate types are newtypes with constructors and readers and
no arithmetic, and the crate's other impl bodies compute the same way.
