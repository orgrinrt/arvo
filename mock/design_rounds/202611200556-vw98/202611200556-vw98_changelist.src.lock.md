## Comparison to deprecated changelist

The deprecated changelist moved `Fields` and `Holds` into a private module and dropped them from the re-export in `src/lib.rs`, so a consumer could not name them. This one keeps both public and re-exported beside `Field`, `Lane`, `Layout` and `Word`, and seals them with a private supertrait instead, because a function generic over a field, such as the tests' round trip over an `Id`, a `Bool` field or any other, must write `L::Fields: Holds<F>` to call `Word::get` and `Word::with`. Its `src/lib.rs` entry is gone, since the re-export no longer changes. Everything else is the same: `placed` refusing a width or a `BELOW` the carrier cannot take before `Word::TILED` compares the cover, the masked write, the two mutant flags, the `Bits<64>` layouts, the alias, the three refused cases, the mutant sketch, the clean disassembly rerun and the report.
