## Canon

changelist. Every change below is the design's sentence carried into source; nothing here says anything
the design does not.

## CHANGE: `Mode::HalfUp` and `HalfUp` rustdoc FROM ties away from zero TO ties toward positive infinity
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/rounding.rs`
Both doc comments say a tie goes toward positive infinity, `floor(x + q/2)`, and carry the note that it is
not Java's or Python's `HALF_UP`. The module doc gains the same note beside the retired-word note.
Verification: `grep -c "away from zero" mock/crates/arvo-format/src/rounding.rs` names only the note's
statement of what the name is not; `grep -c "positive infinity" mock/crates/arvo-format/src/rounding.rs`
is at least two.

## CHANGE: `round_slot`'s `Mode::HalfUp` arm FROM a sign test at a tie TO `2 * num >= den`
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`
The arm reads the residue only: up when twice the remainder reaches the denominator. The sign test and
its comment go.
Verification: the `HalfUp` arm contains no `is_negative`; `apply::tests::half_up_goes_toward_positive_infinity_on_a_tie_and_half_even_goes_to_the_even_slot`
passes and asserts a tie at `-2.5` gives `-2`.

## CHANGE: a third sweep, every tie at every width to 16 and the ties at the ends of every wider range

planted tie rules through the map's regions over ranges pinned to both ends of the index, around zero and

are fed; each planted rule is required reported, under the mode it plants and, for the ties-away and
toward-zero plants, at a tie of the sign it breaks. Coverage arms count ties by sign and parity, ties past
each range end, and the two ties past the index.
Verification: the file's arms pass, and each planted rule is reported.

## CHANGE: a lint refusing the magnitude reading of `half_up`
File: `mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule.rs` and its directory, new.

`.md.tmpl` under the mock directory outside `research/` and `design_rounds/`, and the registry's prose

uses and gives it a magnitude reading, unless a negation lies between the two or the clause marks itself

documents. Tests plant every spelling and every phrase, the escapes, and a control per escape.
Verification: `cargo mock` reports it passing over the tree; its tests pass.
