# A member is a type, and the divisor takes one

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Scope:** `arvo-format`'s slot range section, the divisor, and division's laws.
**Source:** round two of `mock/research/202611132345_slot-coordinate-plan/PLAN.md`; ticket
`arvo_a_runtime_divisor_over_a_whole_phase_range_without_the`.

A divisor read at run time is admitted today only where no slot of the format names the zero, which is
wherever the reduced phase's denominator is past one. A format at a whole phase whose range leaves the zero
out, the slots 1 to 255 on a byte's grid, has no zero member either, but `Divisor::of` cannot take a slot of
it, because a slot is not typed as a member of its range and the slot `-n` could be handed in whether the
range holds it or not. The `FIXME` at `NoSlotNamesTheZero` names exactly that gap.

The shape the plan recommends, its M2, adds a type rather than changing one. `Member<F>` is a slot of `F`'s
range, with a private field, two doors and one accessor. `Member::<F>::constant::<B>()` is refused at build
outside the range. `Member::<F>::adapted::<S>(exact, dither)`, for a declared signature over `F`, is `adapt`,
and is in range by its totality, which `apply/tests/totality.rs` holds. `slot()` reads it back. No door
classifies a slot read at run time: `ruling::the_ingest_door_waits_on_a_known_use` sets a known use as the
bar for one, and nothing here shows one. Like `adapt`, it serves a constant-exponent quantum only.

The divisor gains a third door, `Divisor::<F>::member::<G>(Member<G>)`, admitted at build where `G` has `F`'s
radix, constant exponent and reduced phase and no member of `G` names the zero: the phase is not whole, or
its zero slot `-n` is outside `G`'s range. With `G = F` it is the ticket's case. With `G` a zero-free part of
`F`'s grid, a byte divided by a member of slots 1 to 255, it is the typed form of a non-zero operand of
another format. `Divisor::constant` and `Divisor::of` are unchanged, and the whole-phase restriction on `of`
stops being a compromise, since the member door is where such a range divides.

The sketch `mock/research/sketches/the-member-divisor/` builds both doors as associated-constant
obligations on the pinned toolchain and shows each refusal fail with its own message: a constant past the
range, a range holding the zero, another exponent, another phase, another radix.

The shape went to `coord` and to the values lane before the doc. The values lane agreed with no change
needed on its side: its tier admits only whole ranges at zero phase today, so it has no caller of the door
yet, and if it later admits a zero-free `G`, `Member::<G>::adapted` on a grid position is exact and no
unchecked door is wanted.

## The member's range lies inside the divisor's

Reading the quotient while drafting the doc: `quotient_position` is exact for two members of `F`, and a
divisor slot outside `F`'s range, which only reaches past the four limbs at the far end, saturates at the
end on the quotient's sign instead. `Divisor::of` already admits such a slot where `d` is past one, but a
member door over a `G` wider than `F` would carry that inexactness into a door whose whole point is a
typed operand. So the door also asks that `G`'s range lie inside `F`'s, and every divisor it builds is a
member of `F` as well as of `G`. This narrows the shape sent to `coord` and the values lane rather than
widening it: the ticket's case, `G = F`, and the values lane's, a zero-free part of `F`'s range, both meet
it. The sketch gains the planted case, a byte divided by a member of the slots 1 to 65535, which fails with
its own message.
