# Src changelist: a member is a type, and the divisor takes one

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140438`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140438_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Topics consumed:** `202611140438_topic.a-member-is-a-type.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/`: `lib.rs`, the new `member.rs` and `member/tests.rs`, `division.rs`,
and the tests named below; `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/compile_fail.rs` and `tests/ui/`.

## CHANGE: the member type

`src/member.rs`: `Member<F: Format>` with a private `slot` and `format`, `Clone` and `Copy` by hand as
`Divisor` has them. `Member::constant::<B>()` forces `ConstantMember<F, B>::ADMITTED`, which asserts
`slot_in_range::<F::Slots>(Slot::at(B))` with a message naming the range and `Member::adapted`.
`Member::adapted::<S: DeclaredSignature<Format = F>>(exact, dither)` returns `adapt::<S>(exact, dither)` as a
member and checks nothing. `Member::slot` returns the slot. A test-only `assumed` builds one with no
obligation, for the planted constant door. `src/lib.rs` declares `pub mod member` and re-exports `Member` at
the root.

## CHANGE: the divisor's member door

`src/division.rs`: `Divisor::member::<G: Format>(member: Member<G>)` forces `MemberDivisor<F, G>::ADMITTED` and
holds the member's slot. The obligation asserts the five verdicts of `member_conditions::<F, G>()`, in order,
each with its own message: `radix::<F>().equals(radix::<G>())`; `same_constant_exponent`, both quanta a
constant family at one `BASE`; `same_phase`, both reduced phases denoting and equal; `range_inside`, `G`'s
`MIN` and `MAX` within `F`'s; `no_member_names_the_zero::<G>()`, the reduced denominator past one or the slot
`-n` outside `G`'s range. The module doc and `Divisor`'s rustdoc name three constructors. The `FIXME` naming
`arvo_a_runtime_divisor_over_a_whole_phase_range_without_the` is removed from `NoSlotNamesTheZero`, whose
rustdoc and refusal message name `Divisor::member` as the door for such a range.

## CHANGE: the member door's tests

`src/apply/tests/totality.rs`: at every fed position the walk reads `Member::adapted` beside `adapt` and
requires them to agree, and places each fed edge slot in range on itself through `adapted` at
`Exact::on_grid` under three dithers, at every admitted width of both families and every mode and policy. The
position count takes the 5 in-range slots under 3 dithers per cell.

`src/member/tests.rs`: the constant door at each end of a signed, an unsigned and a zero-free range, none
outside; a planted constant door with no range check, one past each end of `OneTo255`, reported by the
perimeter check, with the same door inside the range as its control.

`src/division/tests/the_oracle.rs`: the sweep's inner loop is extracted into `pair::<S>(a, divisor, planted)`
and `Tally::add`, which the sweep calls with `Divisor::assumed` as before.

`src/division/tests/the_member_door.rs`: `pair` driven by divisors reached as a consumer reaches them,
`Member::adapted` at `Exact::on_grid` and then `Divisor::member`, for the zero-free range 1 to 255 divided by
its own members and for the bytes divided by them, at every mode and shipped policy, with the counts checked.
A table of ten format pairs states the five verdicts by hand; the shipped verdict agrees with all of it, and
each condition flips alone in its own row. Three planted verdicts, the zero check reading the divisor's format,
the grid without the exponent and the verdict without the range lying inside, are each reported by the table.

`tests/compile_fail.rs` and `tests/ui/`: `a_member_constant_past_the_top_is_refused` beside
`a_member_constant_at_the_top_is_admitted`, and `a_member_divisor_from_a_range_holding_its_zero_is_refused`,
`_from_another_radix_`, `_from_another_exponent_`, `_from_another_phase_` and
`_from_a_range_past_the_divisors_is_refused`, each one token from
`a_member_divisor_from_a_zero_free_range_on_the_grid_is_admitted`, which names `Member` from the root. Each
refusal's `.stderr` holds its own message alone. `a_runtime_divisor_over_a_whole_phase_is_refused.stderr`
takes the reworded refusal of `Divisor::of`.

## NO CHANGE: the rest

`Divisor::constant`, `Divisor::of`, the exact step, `quotient_position`, `div` and the quotient's obligation
are unchanged. The sweep in `the_oracle.rs` checks what it checked before through the extracted `pair`.
