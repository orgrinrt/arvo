## What lands

Three edits to one document, and the generated document regenerated from it. No
other crate is touched and nothing about the two laws changes: what changes is
what the design says about where they came from and what its own mode names
correspond to.

## CHANGE: the canon declaration block FROM ten rows TO twelve

The block names ten rows and neither of the two law rows the symmetry section
descends from. `grep -c 'fusing_a_multiply_add' crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
returned zero over the whole file before this round.

That is not a missing footnote. The declaration is the mechanism the mutation
order runs on: a canon row changing names the designs it invalidates by finding
the ones that declared it, so as the file stood, editing either law row would
invalidate nothing and this section would survive its own governing canon. Both
slugs go in, with the sentence saying what the two rows state, which is the
translation equivariance the section expresses.

## CHANGE: the section's canon paragraph FROM naming no slug TO naming both, with the vocabularies written out

The paragraph said the canon names the first of the two symmetries and cited no
row, and the two mode vocabularies were left to look like one.

They are not one. The canon's six are `floor`, `ceil`, `toward_zero`, `away from
zero`, `half_up` and `half_even`. This crate's six are `Floor`, `Ceil`,
`TowardZero`, `HalfUp`, `HalfEven` and `Stochastic`. `cargo mock
rounding-vocabulary` reports `away from zero` as outside the six ratified names
in exactly the two law rows, and says in its own output that whether the
vocabulary is incomplete or a row names a mode the design does not ship is a
canon question rather than an edit.

So the cardinalities agreeing is a coincidence over different sets. The unsigned
row holds over five of six and the section's region holds over five of six, and
a reader checking one against the other finds corroboration that is not there.
What lands is both slugs, the two sets written out, the two non-shared modes
named, and the comparison stated only where it can be made: on the unsigned row
the five shared modes agree, and on the signed wrapping row the comparison turns
on whether the domain that row was measured over reaches a tie, which the row
does not say. That one is left open rather than guessed.

## CHANGE: the parity paragraph FROM naming a region TO saying it is not expressed

The rounding region paragraph says half-even is defeated by a domain whose
translations are all even. The reach a caller hands the predicate carries no
parity of the translation band, so the predicate has no disjunct for it and
answers no where the law holds.

Conservative rather than unsound, so nothing built on it is wrong, and a region
an arm could gate on is going unused. The paragraph now says so, and the source
carries a `FIXME` naming what expressing it would take.

## CHANGE: `docs/FORMAT.md` FROM ending at the industrial conventions TO carrying the symmetry section

The previous round appended 174 lines to the template and never regenerated. The
published document is tracked, so it shipped a design document that stops before
the work the round was about. Regeneration, no hand edit.
