# the-review-findings-on-the-symmetry-round

A senior review of the pull request carrying the previous round returned two
blockers and seven findings. The round closed before any of them could be
folded in, so they open this one, and the scope is exactly what the review
named plus the controls each repair owes.

The two blockers are both surface rather than substance. The published
`docs/FORMAT.md` ends at the section the template ended at before the symmetry
work, because the round never regenerated it, and a stale generated document
blocks a merge on its own. And the design section's own canon declaration names
ten rows and neither of the two law rows the section descends from, so editing
either law would invalidate nothing and the section survives the canon it
answers to. Both are one edit each.

The third finding is the one worth the most thought. The section says the canon
names the first of the two symmetries and cites no slug, and the mode
vocabularies on the two sides do not correspond. The canon's six are floor,
ceil, toward_zero, away from zero, half_up and half_even; this crate's six are
those less `away from zero` and `half_up` as canon spells it, plus a stochastic
decision. `cargo mock rounding-vocabulary` reports `away from zero` as outside
the six ratified names in exactly these two rows, and says in its own words that
whether the vocabulary is incomplete or the row names a mode the design does not
ship is a canon question. So the cardinalities agreeing, five of six unsigned
and three of six signed wrapping on both sides, is a coincidence over different
sets, and a reader checking the derivation finds corroboration where there is
none. What lands is the two slugs, the correspondence written out, and the two
non-shared modes named. What does not land is a mapping between `away from zero`
and anything here, because that is the canon question and this round does not
get to answer it.

The fourth is a real hole in the cross and it is the interesting one. The
completion region's low side is decided by whether the reach leaves the range
underneath it or by whether any translation in the band is positive, and the
second disjunct is never exercised true anywhere in the 432 cells: `cell_reach`
hands every cell the range's highest slot as its upper translation bound, and
that is positive on all three ranges. Its mirror on the high side is exercised
both ways, through the restriction axis that turns negative translations off. So
a defect confined to that one disjunct passes the entire sweep. A fourth
restriction axis would double the cross for one cell's worth of information; one
arm reaching the case directly is what this round takes.

The fifth is a region named and not expressed. `Reads::Parity` documents that
the half-even case is defeated by a domain whose translations are all even, the
design repeats it, and nothing in the reach carries the parity of a translation
band, so the predicate answers no where the law holds. Conservative rather than
unsound, and unmarked, which is what makes it a finding rather than a known
limitation.

The sixth and seventh are both about the previous round's own artifacts. Its
source changelist cites five sketch steps and a verdict file that no longer
exists under that name, because the sketch grew two steps after the changelist
locked; a locked changelist is immutable, so the repair is an addendum naming
what is there now. And its mutation run is cited as evidence while saying of
itself that it is not reproducible from the tree, which under the evidence rule
makes the claim void rather than merely unverified. Committing the mutated
declaration and its failing output costs kilobytes and turns it back into
evidence.

The eighth and ninth need no round. One is a commit subject over the character
cap, already pushed and so not amendable, handled at the merge by overriding the
squash subject. The other is the test module passing the file size limit, which
the split in the fourth finding resolves on its own.

## What is not in scope

The attribution scan refuses this repository on two commits from April whose
bodies quote the forbidden tool-advert suffix because the commit's subject is
the rule that forbids it. That is a defect in the scan's quoting exemption,
which is line-oriented and so misses a quotation wrapped across two lines, and
it is filed where the tooling is tracked. Nothing on this branch reaches it.
