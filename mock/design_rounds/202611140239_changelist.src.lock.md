# Src changelist: the exact position takes the four limbs

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140239`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140239_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/`, `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/compile_fail.rs`,
`mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/ui/`.

The source follows the locked design: the exact position holds its floor in four limbs, the map reads that
floor, and the fourth obligation condition goes from the product, the quotient and the conversion, with the
wrapping special path that stood beside it. The tests that encoded the old shape are ported where what they
protect still holds, and deleted where they only pinned the pair and the distance.

## CHANGE: the exact position holds its floor in four limbs

`src/apply/position.rs`: `Exact` is `floor: Wide` and `part: Remainder`; `past` is gone. `on_grid` and `at`
read the slot into the floor, `between` forms its carry with `Wide::of_sum`, so it is total with nothing
pinned, and `floored` builds a position from a floor and a remainder for the steps. `slot()` answers the
floor where the index holds it and the end on the position's side where it does not. `floor()`,
`is_negative` and `is_even` read the floor.

`src/wide.rs`: `of_sum` adds two `i128` without leaving the four limbs, `end` is the limbs' end on a side,
`residue` reduces the floor modulo a span below `2^127`, and `is_even` reads the lowest limb.

## CHANGE: the map reads the floor

`src/apply.rs`: `Rounded` is `down: Wide` and `up: Bool`. `lands_within` compares the floor with the
range's ends in the four limbs. `complete_slot` lands a position in range by `reduced(lo, span)`, wraps by
the same reduction of the floor and the lowest slot modulo the span separately, and under `Saturate` and
`Clamp` answers the lowest slot where the floor is under it and the highest otherwise. `round_slot` reads
the floor of the exact position. `src/addition.rs` reads the step through `step()`.

`src/placing.rs`: `Placed` is `Unformed(bool)` or `At(Wide, Wide)`, `over` always answers `At`, and
`positioned` builds the position from the floor. `is_held` and `wrapped` are gone; `saturated` is the
limbs' end on a side, which only a step not formed reaches.

## CHANGE: three conditions for the product, the quotient and the conversion

`src/multiplication.rs`: `Multipliable::ADMITTED` asserts the three conditions and the `product_is_carried`
assertion and its FIXME are gone, with `product_is_carried` itself. `MultipliableUnder` and
`is_multipliable_under` are gone, and `mul` positions the step and adapts it under every policy alike.
`is_multipliable` folds the three. `src/lib.rs` re-exports `Mul`, `is_multipliable`, `mul` and
`product_position`.

`src/division.rs`: `conditions` is three verdicts, the carried assertion and its FIXME are gone, with
`quotient_is_carried`, `extreme_divisors` and `is_nonzero`, which only they read.

`src/conversion.rs`: `conditions` is three verdicts, the carried assertion and its FIXME are gone, with
`conversion_is_carried`.

`src/adapt.rs`: `wraps` is gone, which only the wrapping special path read.

## CHANGE: the tests

Ported to the floor: `apply/tests/the_carry_past_the_index.rs`, `the_cross_end_saturation.rs`,
`the_edges.rs`, `the_ratio_coordinate.rs` (the carry test is renamed to keeping the whole floor),
`the_wide_remainder.rs`, `the_oracle_sweep.rs` and `the_wide_oracle_sweep.rs` (the band counts read the
floor against the index's ends).

`apply/tests/the_broken_maps.rs`: the maps are written over the four-limb floor, each still differing from
the shipped map in one place. Three join, each the shape a step that held its floor in the index's integer
would take: `narrows_the_floor`, a wrap over the floor narrowed to the index; `saturates_past_the_index`, a
wrap that saturates a floor past the index; and `half_even_reads_a_narrowed_parity`, a tie whose parity is
read from the narrowed floor. Both oracle sweeps report all three, each only past the index.

`multiplication/tests/the_edges.rs`, `division/tests/the_edges.rs`, `conversion/tests/the_edges.rs`: the
oracles answer the exact position with its floor whole, the verdict admits every format the sweeps reach,
and positions past the index are compared exactly rather than as the end. The control step that drops the
remainder keeps the floor, so it is still never wrong where the denominator is one.

`multiplication/tests/the_step.rs`: the test that a position past the index saturates becomes one that it
is exact and that only a step past the limbs saturates; the test that the obligation holds out a product
the index cannot carry becomes one that it reads nothing of the index, with the numerator's boundary at
`2^253`, `2^254`, `10^76` and `10^77`. `multiplication/tests/the_wrap_past_the_index.rs` admits the unsigned
64-bit product under every policy, saturation and clamping at the corner past `2^127` included, and
`the_inverse_round_trip.rs` reaches width 64 through `mul`. `division/tests/the_divisor.rs` admits
`MIN / -1` at `2^-64`.

New: `division/tests/the_far_quotient.rs`, a narrow range at a fine quantum, `MIN / -1` at `2^-64` wrapping
to slot 0 and saturating to the top, and a tie past the index at a phase of a half whose odd floor every
mode reads under wrapping, with the positions at `a = 1` and `a = -1` inside the index as the control;
`conversion/tests/the_far_conversion.rs`, the 64-bit top onto an 8-bit grid at `2^-70` wrapping to slot 0
and saturating to the top. The exact positions past `2^127` these adapt are compared with the `Big`
oracles in the edge sweeps, and the map over floors past the index with the wide oracle sweep.

`tests/compile_fail.rs` and `tests/ui/`: the refusals of the unsigned 64-bit product, and of it under
saturation and clamping, become pass cases, and the one bit narrower pass case is deleted, since the
boundary it marked is gone. The quotient and the conversion past the index join as pass cases.

## The failing controls

On the tree before this round, with the tests above in place, the build refuses with E0080 at the carried
assertions: `conversion.rs` line 97, `division.rs` line 211 twice and `multiplication.rs` line 132 twice,
each "not yet admitted ... slot coordinate". On the tree after it, the three planted maps are each
reported by both oracle sweeps, and before the step tests were rewritten they failed at
`!is_multipliable::<UFixed<64, 0>>()` and at the saturated corner, which the new floor answers exactly.

## NO CHANGE

`Slot`, `Slots` and the 64-bit range bound; addition, whose own carried condition is left for its own
round; the symmetries; the standards.
