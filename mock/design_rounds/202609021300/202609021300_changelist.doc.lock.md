# Doc changelist: the word form the first sweep did not match

Four sentences, in two files, plus the regeneration that carries them to the
pages people read.

## `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

Three sentences.

Line 4, the canon declaration block. `the four propositions it ratifies` becomes
`the four propositions under it`. The block stays and every slug in it stays;
the chain requires a design to declare its canon and this is that declaration.

Line 118. `the one closed enumeration in the design, closed because a
ratification closed it:` becomes `the one closed enumeration in the design,
fixed at these six:`. The six names follow it unchanged.

Line 306. `` `Mode` is closed because a ratification closed the vocabulary at
six; `Policy` is not.`` becomes `` `Mode` is closed at six names; `Policy` is
not.``

## `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

One sentence, and the file the previous round never opened. `The rounding
vocabulary is the one closed enumeration, closed because a ratification closed
it at six names.` becomes `The rounding vocabulary is the one closed
enumeration, fixed at six names, so a const predicate over it is exhaustive and
a seventh mode is a different design rather than a new row.`

The replacement is longer than the original on purpose. What the original was
carrying, under the provenance, was that the set does not grow; saying why that
matters is what the sentence is for.

## Regeneration

`cargo mock`, and `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/FORMAT.md` commit with it. The
previous round's own gap was that two prose fixes lived only in the template,
so the page a stranger reads still carried the sentence the round had replaced.

## Verification

`grep -rniE 'ratif'` over every design template, both root templates, every
crate readme, `README.md` and the rendered `docs/` tree, expecting zero, with
this round's own topic file as the positive control, expecting non-zero.

The pattern is `ratif` rather than `ratified` because the last verification used
`-w ratified`, which does not match `ratifies` or `ratification`, and those are
the two forms every surviving site used. A check calibrated to one word form
measures that word form. What is being checked is that no published page reports
how the project came to know a thing.
