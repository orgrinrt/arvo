# Four sites the first sweep could not have found

The round before this one took the provenance framing out of the three per-crate
design templates and verified the result with a grep. The grep was
`-ciwE 'op|ratified|reviewer|panel'`, and `-w ratified` matches `ratified` and
nothing else. `ratifies` and `ratification` are not the same word to `grep -w`,
and those are the two forms the surviving sites use.

So the sweep reported zero and four sites were standing. Three are in
`arvo-format`'s template and one is in `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, which the round
never opened at all, because the finding it started from named the crate
templates and the class was measured over the set the finding named.

The four:

- `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl:4`, the canon declaration block, which
  says the ruling and the four propositions it ratifies.
- `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl:118`, the rounding vocabulary, closed
  because a ratification closed it.
- `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl:306`, the same sentence about `Mode`
  against `Policy`.
- `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl:43`, the same sentence again at the root, rendering into
  `docs/DESIGN.md`.

## What is wrong with them, which is what was wrong with the thirty-four

Three of the four justify a closed enumeration by naming the act that closed it.
A reader wants to know that the rounding vocabulary is six names and does not
grow, because that is what lets a const predicate over it be exhaustive. Whether
somebody ratified that is a fact about the project's own process and cannot be
acted on.

The fourth is the canon declaration block, and it is a different thing: the chain
requires a design to declare which canon it descends from, so the block stays.
What goes is the word, because the relation it names is that the propositions sit
under the ruling, and that is sayable without the process verb.

## What stays

Every slug. The declaration block. The technical content of all four sentences,
which is correct and is not what changes.

## The verification, and why the last one was not enough

`ratif` as a substring rather than `ratified` as a word, run over every design
template, both root templates, every crate readme, the repository readme and the
rendered `docs/` tree, with a positive control that returns non-zero on this
file. A check calibrated to a word form is a check of the word form. The property
is that no published page reports how the project came to know a thing, and the
pattern has to be wide enough to be a check of that instead.
