## CHANGE: one private helper builds a position, one grades a reach

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/addition.rs`.

`positioned(at, parts)` builds the exact position from a slot sum carrying the whole part and the
parts' remainder, and `graded(reach, parts)` gives a reach the grid state the remainder decides.
`sum_position` and `addition_reach` call them, behaviour unchanged, and the new steps call the same
two, so the construction is written once.

## CHANGE: `Neg`, `Sub` and their steps, compositions and reaches

Files: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/addition/subtraction.rs` (new), `src/addition.rs`, `src/lib.rs`.

`Neg<S>` of arity one and `Sub<S>` of arity two implement `Operation`. `negation_position::<F>(a)` is
`-a - 2 * phase` and forces the addition obligation; `difference_position::<F>(a, b)` is `a - b - phase`
and forces it together with the slot range's `ADMITTED`. `neg::<S>(a, dither)` and
`sub::<S>(a, b, dither)` compose them with `adapt`. `negation_reach::<S>()` and
`subtraction_reach::<S>()` assert the arity, run from `-MAX + k` to `-MIN + k` and from
`MIN - MAX + k` to `MAX - MIN + k`, and carry no translation. A private `saturating_difference`
saturates the three-term slot sum once. All are `const fn`, re-exported from `addition` and the crate
root.

## CHANGE: the apply oracle is reachable from the crate's other tests

Files: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`, `src/apply/tests/mod.rs`, `src/apply/tests/the_oracle.rs`.

The test module `apply::tests` and its `the_oracle` become `pub(crate)` under `cfg(test)`, and the
oracle's items `pub(crate)` rather than `pub(super)`, so the signed steps are checked against the same
rounding and completion oracle as the applied map instead of a second copy of it.

## CHANGE: the step tests run over a `Step` shape

Files: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/addition/tests/steps.rs` (new), `tests/mod.rs`, `tests/the_step.rs`.

`Step` names which of a signed step's operands enter negated, as the crate's `Bool`, and its crate
functions, with `Sum`, `Negation` and `Difference`.
The denotation (with its value-units control), the largest-denominator case and the reach-against-step
check are generic over it and run at all three; `Table::of_step` builds a table of any binary step,
`Table::of` is it at `Sum`. New check-time constants bind both steps and both reaches.

## CHANGE: the signed steps' laws, oracle sweep and the composed count

Files: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/addition/tests/the_signed_laws.rs`, `tests/the_signed_oracle.rs` (new).

The identity's slot is neutral on the right of `sub` and fixed by `neg` at every signature and dither;
`a - a` lands at minus the phase; negation undoes itself on members; a difference equals the sum with
the negation wherever the negation is on the grid. Every member tuple at widths one to eight, both
families, every phase of the shared list, every mode and shipped policy, four dithers for the mode
that reads one, equals the apply oracle fed the position computed in exact rationals, slot and
overflow verdict both; a planted negation a copy of the phase short is reported by the same sweep and
not at phase zero. Every admitted width is fed its ten edges, members against the oracle and the rest
inside the range. `(a + b) - c != a + (b - c)` on 13,882,880 triples at eight unsigned bits under
saturation, and on none under wrapping, signed or unsigned.
