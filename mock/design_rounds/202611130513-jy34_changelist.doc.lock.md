## CHANGE: how the value holds its carrier

`## How the value holds its carrier`. `Num<S: Sole>` stores the carrier the map names for the
signature's slot family. `Carried` and `Machine` sit in a module the crate does not export, `Sole`
declares no associated type and reaches the map only through the bound
`DeclaredSignature<Format: Format<Slots: Carried>>`, so the carrier's type is fixed by the signature,
stored, and nameable by no consumer, and its size is observed only through the host's layout. The
paragraph on dispatch speaks of the carrier rather than of `S::Repr`.

## CHANGE: where the host integers are named

`## Where the host integers are named`. The list shrinks to the carrier map, the machine integers and
the bit copy, all inside the crate and unnameable outside it, with `Num`'s private field; the rung
arithmetic and its dispatch; the host doors `from_host` and `host` on `USize` and `ISize`; the slot
index; and core's trait signatures and the tests. The doors to the carrier leave the list.

## CHANGE: the doors

`## The doors`. `from_raw` and `raw` go. Bits come in by `adapted` from an exact point and by `constant`
as a member, and go out by `slot` as the coordinate; no door takes or gives the carrier. `from_host` and
`host` stay, as bit copies between the host's integer and a member at the platform width.

## CHANGE: the operations

`## The operations`. `Hash` hashes the slot's index and `Debug` prints the slot, so neither depends on
the carrier. `Clone`, `Copy`, `PartialEq` and `Eq` stay over the carrier, which is canonical. The
sentence on a conversion from a host integer stops naming `from_raw`.

## CHANGE: what is proved

`## What is proved, and how`. The round trip through `raw` and the assertion tests at `from_raw` leave
the text; the assertion is observed at `add` and `adapted`. The compile-fail list gains the routes to the
carrier: the projection through `Sole`, the fully qualified projection, and the map and the machine
integers by path. The consumer reaches every public item of the surface as it now stands.

## CHANGE: what is not here

`## What is not here` says a bit door, where a consumer needs one, is an encoding stated over the
declared width, and is not in this crate.
