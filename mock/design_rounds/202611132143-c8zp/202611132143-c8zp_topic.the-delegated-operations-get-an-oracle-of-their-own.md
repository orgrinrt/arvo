## What the round is for

A fresh seat read the branch and found defects. Each is a defect found and fixed, and they are taken
together because they were found together. Five of them change source or the crate's design and are
this round's. The rest are answered in `RESPONSE.md` beside the reading.

The first is the one that matters most. The quotient, the conversion and the product at a quantum
exponent other than zero are not computed by the value. The value calls the format's own function on
slots and wraps what comes back. The tests of those three compared the value against that same
function, so both sides of each comparison were one call, and a wrong quotient in the format would
have stayed green here. The same held for `fits_div`, `fits_convert` and `fits_mul`, each asserted
equal to the expression that is its body. The design told the tests to do this, so the design is
changed with them.

The second is the accumulator at a capacity of zero. It was admitted, and `take` at that capacity sums
nothing and hands back the whole slice, so a loop over `take` never ends.

The third is the consumer test of the round before this one. It found the build directory three
levels above the test binary, which is wrong when the build names a target, and the test beside it
passed whether or not the paths were read at run time.

The fourth is small: test names that say less than the tests do, a check written twice, and unused
imports in the passing halves of the compile-fail pairs.

The fifth is what the design says of the accumulator beside the open question on chain carriers.

## What the canon says

`question::which_chain_carrier_ships` is open, and its decider is the panel. Its first option is the
named accumulator, which carries the budget only. Its bound says that several carriers, each covering
the shapes it is optimal for, is the design, and that the choice among them is made on what each costs
at run time. Nothing in this round answers the question. The accumulator this crate ships is a type a
consumer may reach for whichever carriers the answer names: it carries a capacity and an exact sum and
no schedule. The design said so in one sentence without citing the bound, and now cites it. The
reading asks for a second seat on whether shipping the type under this name leans on the answer. That
is filed as a question and is not decided here.

The value definition, `proposal::a_value_is_the_sole_occupancy_placement_of_a_declared_signature`,
stands on a provisional stamp and is not promoted.

`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` bears on the zero capacity: the refusal is a
failed constant, at build.

## The oracle

The tests get arithmetic of their own, in the test crate, that calls nothing of `arvo-format` to
compute: a quotient of two wide integers rounded by a mode, and a completion of a wide integer onto a
range by wrapping or by saturation. The mode, the policy, the range and the quantum's exponent are
read from the signature's declarations, which are what is declared and not what is computed. With
those, the expected slot of a quotient, a conversion and a fixed-point product is written from the
members' values: the dividend over the divisor scaled by the grid, the member scaled from one grid to
the other, and the product scaled back onto its grid.

The sum, the difference and the negation keep the format's functions as their oracle. There the value
computes in its carrier and the format computes on wide slots, so the two are two computations and
their agreement is evidence.
