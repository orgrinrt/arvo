## Canon

ruling the lint refuses a field of, which is what decides the ending below.

## Which ending, and why

The brief for this round gave two ends: show the class the review named closed, or demote the lint to
a warning, catalogue every sentence still wrong as known-red, and say in the lint's module doc that the
gate is incomplete in both directions. The second is taken. The reason is one field. Run over every
ratified row of `ruling.toml`, the reader refuses the governing ruling's `promotion`, whose clause
"270 read blind, ... and first answered ties away from zero on the external names" opens on a seat
number, a subject the reader has no word for, so the name carried from the clause before it reaches the
reading. A hard-error gate that refuses a sentence of the canon it defends is the one state the brief
rules out. The call is mine, under the brief's hard stop, and no human has ratified it.

## CHANGE: a reading is attributed to the rule its own segment is about
File: `mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule/reading.rs`, `attributed_to_another_rule` and
`another_rule_stands_last`, called from `clause_hit` and from `row`.

A reading belongs to another rule when its segment names that rule ahead of it, or names nothing and
points back, with a pronoun opening it or a relative anywhere in it, and the last rule standing before
the segment is not the mode. A segment naming the mode ahead of the reading keeps it for the mode. This
closes blocker 1: the six false positives the review listed and the two more of the same class the
topic measured are silent, and each is a pair or a walked arm.
Verification: `carry_tests.rs` arms `every_other_name_ahead_of_the_reading_in_its_segment_takes_the_reading`,
`every_relative_binds_the_reading_to_the_rule_standing_last`,
`every_pronoun_opening_a_segment_binds_the_reading_to_the_rule_standing_last`,
`a_segment_that_neither_names_nor_points_back_leaves_the_reading_with_the_mode` and
`a_table_cell_attributes_its_reading_the_same_way` pass, each walked over the whole of the list it
depends on.

## CHANGE: a conjunction opening a stretch is read past before the pronoun is looked for
File: `mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule/reading/cutting.rs`, `opens_with_a_pronoun` and
`past_a_conjunction`.

"..., and it sends a tie away from zero" points back as plainly as "..., it sends ..." does. Without
this, the attribution above never asked the pronoun what it meant, and at the clause level "And it is
nearest" dropped the carry and let a doc block's item fill a clause pointing at another rule. Topic
entry 14. Verification: `every_conjunction_opening_a_clause_is_read_past_to_the_pronoun_behind_it`
walks `CONJUNCTIONS` whole.

## CHANGE: a denied spelling is not an antecedent
File: `mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule/reading.rs`, `antecedent`, with `negated` in
`reading/escapes.rs`.

"Ties away from zero is a different operation and is not `half_up`: it is what IEEE 754's
`roundTiesToAway` computes" is the governing ruling's `says`, and the reader refused it. The pronoun
means the operation. Verification: `every_negator_ahead_of_the_mode_takes_it_out_of_the_carry` walks
`NEGATORS` whole, and the ratified-rows arm in `sentences/catalogue.rs` no longer names `says`.

## CHANGE: the denotation excuses a later reading only with no conjunction in the reading's segment
File: `mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule/reading/escapes.rs`, `denotation_before`.

Finding 8. The conjunction rule is bounded as a negator's reach is: the reading's own segment, from

Verification: `segment_tests.rs` arms `a_denotation_conjoined_with_the_other_reading_does_not_excuse_it`,
over every spelling of the denotation and every conjunction, with and without a comma ahead of the
conjunction, and `a_conjunction_in_an_earlier_segment_leaves_the_contrast_standing`, with its control.
