## CHANGE: the bits of a four-limb value

`Wide::bits`, crate-private, scans the limbs from the top and gives the position of the highest set bit
plus one, zero for zero. The walk start reads it for `K` and `D0`.

## CHANGE: the phase in lowest terms, admitted

`placing.rs` splits the reduction into a private `reduced`, under `reduced_phase`, which keeps its
`Isnt` for a phase that does not denote for the other operations, and a new `admitted_phase`, which
forces `Format::ADMITTED` and reads the phase with no branch. `land` takes the second.

## CHANGE: `land`

`land` forces `Format::ADMITTED`, `Slots::ADMITTED` and `Landable::ADMITTED`. `Distance` holds `K` as a
`Term` (formed, or past on a side), `D0` and `e`, built once by `distance` from the point and the phase.
`first_candidate` reads `bits(K)` and `bits(D0)` with no falling-phase exception, and a zero `K` starts
at the finest. `position_at` scales `K` and `D0` by the magnitude's power of the radix, decides past by
`past_the_range`, the divisor times the range's largest magnitude plus one under `2^255`, and otherwise
ends the walk with `Isnt`. Under wrapping, a floor past the index is reduced modulo the range's count
onto the range, its remainder kept, and an unformed floor lands nothing. `Scaled` drops its
`PartialEq` and `Eq` derives and says so in its documentation.

## CHANGE: the oracle and the sweeps

`placed` in `landing/tests.rs` forms the same difference from the predicate, into a `Read` of formed,
past or unknown; `walked` answers a `Want` of at, top or nothing, nothing where an unknown magnitude is
finer than the best holding one; `misses` counts every point. The `Near` comment names 30 magnitudes.
`the_members.rs` lands every member under all six modes, the stochastic one at three dithers.
`a_point_whose_denominator_passes_128_bits_still_lands` moves to the new `the_limits.rs`, beside the
falling 300-magnitude point at magnitude 135 slot 127, nothing at `2^-256` and `2^-300` with `2^-255`
landing, and `2^128 + 4`, its negation and `2^130` wrapped on `Integer<4>`. `past_the_ends.rs` clamps
on the falling law. `the_one_progression.rs` adds a falling law, a decimal law and ranges below zero,
each against the set written out.

## CHANGE: the compile-fail pair

`land_over_a_phase_that_names_no_member_is_refused.rs` with its `.stderr`, and
`land_over_a_phase_over_a_denominator_is_admitted.rs`, differing in the phase's denominator, are in
`the_landing_refuses_what_it_cannot_land_and_admits_its_twins`.

## CHANGE: the IEEE harness

The ties-to-away search in `tests/ieee754/mod.rs` positions through a `Positioned` of at, past or
unknown, reading the format's slot range for the past test as `land` does, and ends with nothing on an
unknown magnitude rather than passing it.

## Mutants

Each was applied to `landing.rs` alone and the landing tests run. Saturating a floor past the index under
wrapping reddens the wrap test. Forming the two terms 200 magnitudes finer, the cancellation the old
per-magnitude numerator had, reddens 10, the falling 300-magnitude point among them. Passing an
unreadable magnitude to a coarser one survives, and is equivalent: along the walk `m` only falls; where
`m >= 0` the divisor is `D0`, at most `2^191` since the denominator is under `2^128` and the reduced
phase's under `2^63`, and a range holding zero over several magnitudes reaches at most `2^64`, so an
unformed numerator is always provably past; where `m < 0` the divisor only grows, so a magnitude it
leaves the limbs at is followed by none it does not. The early `Isnt` is that equivalent, written
without the dead walk.
