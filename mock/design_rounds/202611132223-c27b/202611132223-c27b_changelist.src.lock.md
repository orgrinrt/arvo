# Src changelist: a sweep keeps each format's walk out of line

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** `202611132223-c27b`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611132223-c27b_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `arvo-format`'s test modules, and `mock/lints/`.

## CHANGE: every walk's `run` is out of line

Each of the 43 `impl` blocks of `PerFormat` or `PerSignature` under `arvo-format`'s test modules marks its
`fn run` `#[inline(never)]`, the line above the item. The two trait declarations in `src/tests/dispatch.rs`
are not impls and are unchanged. No test's body or assertion changes.

## CHANGE: the lint `a-sweep-keeps-each-walk-out-of-line`

`mock/lints/a_sweep_keeps_each_walk_out_of_line.rs`, a hard error, reads `arvo-format`'s test modules as
text and names every `fn run` with a body inside an `impl` of `PerFormat` or `PerSignature` that is not
`#[inline(never)]`. Its tests in `a_sweep_keeps_each_walk_out_of_line/tests.rs`: the name, not declared
off, registered, the declared severity, the marked walks silent, an unmarked walk at the line its item
starts, a walk marked `#[inline]`, `#[inline(always)]` or nothing but `#[must_use]`, every unmarked walk in
a file, a `run` outside a walk impl, and shipped source and another crate left unread.

## CHANGE: the text helpers are shared

`is_test_path`, `is_word`, `fn_keywords`, `name_after`, `has_body` and `item_head` move unchanged from
`every_function_the_format_ships_is_inline.rs` into `mock/lints/rust_text.rs` as public functions, and
both lints import them from there.

## Verification

`cargo test --release -p arvo-format --lib`, which died in LLVM before, builds and passes. `cargo test -p
arvo-format`, the lib tests, `compile_fail` and the four parity suites; clippy `-D warnings`; the lints'
own tests; `cargo mock --lint-only`. The mutant is `#[inline(never)]` removed from `Denotation::run`, which
the lint names.
