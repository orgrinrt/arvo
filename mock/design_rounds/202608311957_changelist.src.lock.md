# Src changelist: the applied map, and four tests deleted rather than improved

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Round:** the adaptation round
**Scope:** one new module in `arvo-format`, its tests, and the removal of four tests and two predicates
shipped in round `202608311902`.

## What lands

`arvo-format/src/apply.rs`, carrying `Exact`, `Dither`, `adapt`, and the two panic verdicts, with the
laws the locked design names asserted over the full matrix of modes, policies and format shapes.

## CHANGE: four tests and two predicates FROM shipped TO deleted
Files: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/overflow.rs`, `rounding.rs`, `tests.rs`
Verification: `grep -c 'is_monotone\|is_identity_inside_range' mock/crates/arvo-format/src/overflow.rs`
returns zero, and the suite still reports the order-transport and identity properties, now from
`apply.rs`.

**Deleted rather than improved, which is what the test discipline says to do with this class.**

`is_monotone`, `is_identity_inside_range`, `is_deterministic` and `is_directed` were `matches!`
expressions over an enumeration, and their four tests asserted what those expressions matched. Nothing
in the tree applied a `Policy` or a `Mode` to anything, so each test reached a declaration and stopped.

`is_identity_inside_range` named all three variants of a three-variant enumeration, so it was the
constant `true` and its test asserted that true is true.

**Measured before deleting**, in `mock/research/sketches/adaptation-gate/p1_the_shipped_law_surface_certifies_a_falsehood.rs`,
which states the monotonicity predicate backwards, claiming wrapping transports order and saturation
does not, and shows that with its own test updated to match the backwards version is green. Its control
builds a real wrapping and a real saturating map and measures order transport against them, disagreeing
with the backwards declaration at all three policies.

**The shipped declarations were correct.** That is worth stating rather than burying: the defect is
that nothing checked them, not that they were wrong. Every property they declared is now asserted
against the applied map, where being wrong is possible.

`is_deterministic` and `is_directed` go the same way and for the same reason. Their replacements vary
the dither and inspect the returned slots.

## CHANGE: the adaptation gains an application FROM named-only TO applied

Verification: `cargo test -p arvo-format` reports the new module's tests passing, and

One map, two regions, rounding then completion. An `Exact` is a slot and a remainder as a numerator

## The finding: `Clamp` needs a coordinate the declared signature does not carry

`Clamp` is documented as pinning to a declared bound that need not be the range's own end. **The
declared signature has nowhere to put that bound**, so with no bound to read, clamping degenerates to
saturating and the two policies compute the same function at every input.

**This is the admission rule firing, not a defect in the implementation.** The rule says an operation
is admitted exactly when it is a function of the declared signature, and where two realisations of one
name disagree, the signature is missing a coordinate. Here two names agree where they should differ,
which is the same diagnosis from the other side: the coordinate that would separate them is absent.

**The implementation does not paper over it.** `Clamp` and `Saturate` compute identically, a test
asserts they do, and that test carries the reason so nobody later reads the agreement as a law. Adding
the bound is a change to what a declared signature carries, which is a design question and not this
round's to answer.

## What is deliberately not here

**No preset bound to a policy.** `ruling::wrap_or_clamp_stays_open_and_both_get_priced` is open and
says the bench decides.

**No claim about what a saturated result denotes.** `question::what_a_datum_stands_for` is open. The
map lands on the top slot; what that slot means is not stated.

**No seeding and no keying for the stochastic mode.** The dither is an input. Both questions are open
in the register and this round supplies neither answer.

**No widening of the rounding vocabulary**, which a ratified row closed at six and an open question
challenges. The ratified row governs.
