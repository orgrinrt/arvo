# Src changelist: the width guard, the carry past the index, the translation law at span 200

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Round:** third review repairs on `feat/platform-width-types-on-a-wider-slot-range`
**Scope:** executes `202609191148_changelist.doc.lock.md` in source.

## CHANGE: `Exact` FROM a slot pinned at the index's end TO a pinned slot and the distance past it

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`, `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/position.rs` (new)

`Fraction`, `Exact` and `Dither` move to `apply/position.rs` and are re-exported from `apply`, so every
path that names them is unchanged; `apply.rs` is at the 500-line limit and the carry adds to it. `Exact`
gains a private `past: i64`, zero wherever the index holds the carried slot. `between` adds the carry
checked, and where the sum leaves the index it pins the slot at that end and stores
`slot - end + carry` as the distance, which is at most the carry. `slot()`'s rustdoc says it answers the
pinned slot. A crate-visible `is_negative` reads the named slot's sign from the two.

Verification: `grep -n 'saturating_add' crates/arvo-format/src/apply/position.rs` returns nothing, and
`grep -c 'past' crates/arvo-format/src/apply/position.rs` is not zero.

## CHANGE: `Rounded`, `round_slot`, `complete_slot` FROM the pinned slot alone TO the slot and the distance

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`

`Rounded` carries `past` beside `down` and `up`. `round_slot` copies it, and the sign-reading modes,
`TowardZero` and the tie in `HalfUp`, read `Exact::is_negative`; `HalfEven` reads the parity of the pinned
slot plus the distance. `lands_within` is false for a distance above zero, and for one below zero holds
only where the distance is `-1`, the slot below is `i128::MIN`, the rounding steps up and the range
starts at `i128::MIN`. The wrap adds the distance reduced by the span as a third term. Saturation pins
to the lowest slot for a distance below zero and to the highest for one above. The test-only
`Rounded::index` refuses a position past the index.

Verification: `cargo test -p arvo-format` green, including the carry tests below.

## CHANGE: the carry test FROM the saturated answers TO the named position's, shown red first

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_top_of_the_index.rs`,
`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_ratio_coordinate.rs`

`a_carry_past_the_index_lands_where_the_named_position_does`: `between(MAX, 9/4)` under `Ceil`/`Wrap`
over `AtTheTop` answers two above the lowest slot and reports out of range; `between(MIN, -7/4)` under
`Floor`/`Wrap` over `AtTheBottom` answers `MIN + 254` and reports out of range. The step back onto the
index: `between(MIN, -1/4)` under `Ceil` over `AtTheBottom` answers `MIN`, in range, where a pinned
position would answer `MIN + 1`. Half-even at a tie a slot past the top reads the named slot's parity,
not the pinned one's. The test is written before `apply.rs` changes and run red against the shipped
map. In `the_ratio_coordinate.rs` the carry test stops asserting the saturated rounding and asserts the
pinned slot, the distance and the remainder.
