# Doc changelist: fix the forcing-set reading and drop the provenance narration

## CHANGE: mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl

### CLAIM: the paragraph on where each obligation fires states two force sites, not a relation between sets

The sentence "The verbs differ per obligation and the sets are disjoint from the
slot range's" is false under either reading of what forces what, and it is the
round's own justification for the change it made, so it is the sentence most
worth checking and the one nobody checked.

Replace it with what the source does. Name the reading the section uses, name the
two direct force sites for `Format::ADMITTED`, and say that a verb carrying two
force sites belongs to both sets, which is what makes "disjoint" the wrong
relation. Say separately that the transitive reading is declaration-dependent, so
it cannot be compared per verb at all.

Nothing here is a new claim about the source. Both force sites are already
enumerated two paragraphs above and both are pinned by arms landed in the
previous round.

### CLAIM: the section states the contract without narrating the document's own past error

`documentation-writing.md` forbids provenance on a tier 1 surface, and this
template renders into `docs/FORMAT.md`, which a stranger reads. Two instances,
both new in the previous round:

"`contains` is not a route that forces nothing, and saying so was wrong" tells a
reader the document was recently wrong about the subject it is teaching. The
substance after the clause is correct and stays; the confession goes.

"Whether the design closes those routes is open and is this tier's to settle"
exposes the canon and design tier vocabulary, which is internal process. The
openness is a real fact about the design and can be stated without naming which
tier owns it.

## What this changelist does not do

It does not change what the design says the guarantee is. The guarantee was
already stated correctly two paragraphs above, over the verbs rather than over
the declaration, and that sentence is untouched.
