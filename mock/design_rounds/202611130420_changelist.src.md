## Comparison to deprecated changelist

### comparison to deprecated changelist

`202611130400_changelist.src.deprecated.md` left the ordering traits off, and that stands. Its source
then met `no-bare-numeric` and `arvo-types-only` at every carrier integer, so the design was reopened to
name the surface where the value meets the host integers, and the coordinator's yes put
`arvo-numeral = ["numeric"]` under `[primitive-introductions]`. This changelist adds that line to the
workspace change. The rung arithmetic now takes the declared width as a count, and the dispatch keys,
the width and the policy's assertion flag are read inside inline `const` blocks, because the first
disassembly reading showed `Width::count` and `Bool::get` called out of line in `add`. The disassembly
reading keeps both builds.

What the deprecated changelist named and this one keeps as it was: `Num` implements neither
`PartialOrd` nor `Ord`, since `PartialOrd::partial_cmp` returns a bare `Option`; `Wrap` and `Saturate`
assert while `Wrapping` and `Saturating` set `Overflow::POLICY` to `Policy::Wrap` and
`Policy::Saturate` and do not; `Sole::ADMITTED` is forced by every door; the map asserts against
`derive_sole` at `Access`; and the identity's `IDENTITY` asserts the format's own predicate first.

### canon

Unchanged.

### change: the workspace names the crate

Gains the `mockspace.toml` line, quoted from the coordinator in its commit.

### change: the crate root, the machine integers and the bit copy, the map from slot family to carrier, the admission

Unchanged: `Num`, `Sole`, `Carried`, `Machine`, `ValuePolicy`, `ValueRounding`, `Wrapping`,
`Saturating`, `Identity`, `Additive`, `Uint`, `Int`, `USize`, `ISize` and `Bool` as before.

### change: the rung arithmetic

The width is a count rather than a `Width`.

### change: the value

### change: the identity, the aliases, the tests, the proposal rows

Unchanged.

### change: the disassembly reading

Commits the reading before and after the constant keys.

## CHANGE: the workspace names the crate

File: `mock/Cargo.toml`. `crates/arvo-numeral` joins `members`, and
`arvo-numeral = { path = "crates/arvo-numeral", version = "0.0.0" }` joins `[workspace.dependencies]`.

File: `mockspace.toml`. `arvo-numeral = ["numeric"]` joins `[primitive-introductions]`, with a comment
saying the crate introduces the numeric category where the carriers meet the host integers, and naming
the design section that bounds it. No severity moves and no other row changes.

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/Cargo.toml`, new. Depends on `arvo-format` and `arvo-placement` through
the workspace. Features `mutant-8`, `mutant-16`, `mutant-32` and `mutant-64`, each empty, each removing
one rung's adaptation, off by default. `trybuild` as a dev-dependency. `unsafe_op_in_unsafe_fn` forbidden
as in the other crates.

## CHANGE: the crate root

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/lib.rs`. `#![no_std]`, `#![forbid(unsafe_op_in_unsafe_fn)]`, the crate
documentation, the modules below, and the re-exports: `Num`, `Sole`, `Carried`, `Machine`,
`ValuePolicy`, `ValueRounding`, `Wrapping`, `Saturating`, `Identity`, `Additive`, `Uint`, `Int`,
`USize`, `ISize`, and `Bool` from `arvo-format`.

## CHANGE: the machine integers and the bit copy

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/machine.rs`, new. `Machine`, sealed, implemented for the eight
signed and unsigned integers of 8 to 64 bits and for `usize` and `isize`, with `SIGNED: Bool`. The
crate-private `copy::<T: Machine, U: Machine>`, asserting equal size and reading the bits, the crate's
one `unsafe`, its safety comment naming the precondition the bound carries.

## CHANGE: the map from slot family to carrier

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/map.rs`, new. `Carried: Slots`, sealed, with `type Repr: Machine`.
One macro writes a row per width for `Signed<W>` and `Unsigned<W>`, `W` from 1 to 64, each row followed
by a `const` assertion that the carrier's size in bits equals what `arvo_placement::derive_sole` gives
the family's canonical point (`Integer<W>`, `UFixed<W, 0>`) under `Adapt<TowardZero, Wrap>` at
`Access`.

## CHANGE: the admission

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/admit.rs`, new. `ValuePolicy: Overflow`, sealed, with
`ASSERTS: Bool`, implemented for `Wrap` and `Saturate` (asserting) and the new markers `Wrapping` and
`Saturating` (not), whose `Overflow::POLICY` is `Policy::Wrap` and `Policy::Saturate`.
`ValueRounding: Rounding`, sealed, implemented for the five deterministic modes. `Sole:
DeclaredSignature` with `Repr`, `WIDTH`, `POLICY`, `ASSERTS` and `ADMITTED`, implemented once over every
signature whose slot family is `Carried`, whose overflow is a `ValuePolicy` and whose rounding is a
`ValueRounding`; `ADMITTED` asserts a zero phase and a constant quantum with the message the design
gives.

## CHANGE: the rung arithmetic

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/rung.rs`, new. One macro, expanded for 8, 16, 32 and 64 bits, writes
for each signedness: `adapt` onto a width, given as a count of bits, under a policy, skipped entirely under that rung's mutant
feature; `add`, the wrapping machine add then `adapt` below the carrier width, and the machine's own
wrapping or saturating add at it; `from_index` and `index`, between a slot index and the carrier; `cmp`.
Each `#[inline]`, each `const fn`, total over members.

## CHANGE: the value

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/num.rs`, new. `Num<S: Sole>(S::Repr)`, `repr(transparent)`. The
dispatch macro over `(size_of::<S::Repr>(), SIGNED)`. The dispatch keys, the declared width and the
assertion flag are read in inline `const` blocks. Doors `constant::<V>()` with an inline `const`
membership assertion, `adapted`, `from_raw`, `slot`, `raw`; operations `add`, `fits_add`, `eq`, `cmp`,
`lt`, `le`, `gt`, `ge`, `min`, `max`. The debug assertion in `add`, `adapted` and `from_raw`, under
`S::ASSERTS` and `debug_assertions` only. `Clone`, `Copy`, `PartialEq`, `Eq`, `Hash` and
`Debug` by hand through the carrier, and no `PartialOrd` or `Ord`.

## CHANGE: the identity

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/identity.rs`, new. `Identity<M>` with `IDENTITY`, `Additive`, and
the implementation for `Num<S>` asserting `has_additive_identity::<S::Format>()` first.

## CHANGE: the aliases

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/alias.rs`, new. `Uint`, `Int`, `USize`, `ISize`; `from_host` and
`host` on the two pointer-width values.

## CHANGE: the tests

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/tests.rs`, new: each rung computes every width from 1 to 8 against
the oracle, both families, both policies, every pair, with the mutant expectation per rung; the bit copy
and the map rows.

Files: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/tests/band_1_to_8.rs`, `band_9_to_16.rs`, `band_17_to_32.rs`,
`band_33_to_64.rs`, new, one binary per band through the value type, with
`tests/common/mod.rs` carrying the oracle wrappers, the edge lattice and the seeded generator. The band
of 1 to 8 is exhaustive over both families, both non-asserting markers and all five modes, and carries
the two associativity reproductions and the per-policy verdict tests. Every band checks `add`,
`fits_add`, the comparisons, the round trip through `raw`, and the identity.

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/tests/compile_fail.rs`, new, with `tests/ui/` holding each refused case
beside its passing twin and the committed diagnostic, and the consumer with no feature line.
