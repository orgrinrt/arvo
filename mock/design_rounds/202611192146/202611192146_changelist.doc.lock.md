## `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

CHANGE, section "What this crate is": the crate depends on `arvo-contracts` and takes
`const_trait_impl` unconditionally for its `const impl`s.

CHANGE, section "Identities": `Identity<M>` is declared in `arvo-contracts`; this crate keeps the two
impls for `Num<S, C>` as they are.

ADD, section "The contracts": `Num<S, C>` implements every numeric contract, each method the inherent
`const fn` of the same operation, so the slot of the result is the slot the format's own function
gives. `MonotoneSum` where the signature's overflow policy carries `MonotoneCompletion`. A wrapping
value refused at build by a `MonotoneSum` bound. `Reciprocal` and `SquareRoot` not implemented.

CHANGE, section "What is proved, and how": the law suite and the consumer crate.

- `tests/consumer/` is a crate of its own, depending on `arvo-contracts` and `arvo-numeral` with no
  feature line. It reaches every new public item, and implements the contracts for a type of its own
  that uses no `Num`.
- The law suite is generic over the contracts and runs against `Num` at widths 1 to 8 through a
  test-side vocabulary per instance, its coordinates and an enumeration of its members. It asserts a
  law only inside the region a law row states: `law::additive_associativity_under_wrapping`,
  `law::additive_associativity_under_saturation`, `law::multiplicative_associativity_under_wrapping`,
  `law::multiplicative_associativity_under_saturation`,
  `law::existence_of_a_translation_invariant_total_order`. What it measures outside a row's region is
  recorded, not asserted.
- One wrong outside instance per law, each failing exactly that law.
- A compile-fail test refuses a wrapping value at a `MonotoneSum` bound, and one refuses
  `Constant<V>` at a `V` naming no member.
