non-canonical default rather than a settled answer, and that each is marked
`// FIXME:` at its declaration. Source does not yet carry those markers. This CL

one exists (`Warm`, the cell `question::does_warm_wrap_or_clamp` already
disputes) and naming the general reason (carried default, not ratified) for the
other three, which have no open registry row disputing their specific cell but
are equally unproven defaults under the same design sentence.

No behaviour changes. No type, trait or signature moves. Four comment insertions.
