# Src changelist: admission becomes a check, and the trait stays open

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Round:** the open-trait admission round
**Scope:** `arvo-format/src/slots.rs`, its tests, and one `trybuild` case.

## CHANGE: two doc claims of totality FROM false TO what is actually true
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/slots.rs`
Verification: `grep -c 'and nowhere else' mock/crates/arvo-format/src/slots.rs` returns zero.

The module doc said "Implemented at widths 1 through 62 and nowhere else" and the
`on_unimplemented` note said "declared widths run from 1 to 62 bits". **Both are false about an open
trait**, and nothing enforced either. They now say what this crate implements and leave what exists to
the trait being open.

**Reproduced before changing anything**, at `mock/research/sketches/open-trait-admission/` with the
reviewer's values verbatim, because their probe was uncommitted and therefore uncitable. Without a
check the rogue implementor compiles and `slot_count` returns `-9223372036854775808`.

## CHANGE: admission FROM unchecked TO a compile-time check on the contract
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/slots.rs`
Verification: the `trybuild` case `tests/ui/incoherent_slot_range.rs` refuses, and
`is_admissible::<Rogue>()` returns false in the unit tests.

`Slots` gains a defaulted associated const, forced by every function in this crate that reads the
range. An implementor supplying an inverted range or a width the range cannot carry is refused where
the range is used, with a named message.

**The trait is not sealed and sealing was considered and rejected on canon.**
`proposal::the_concept_is_closed_and_the_inventory_is_open` is ratified through the format spine and a
numeral wanting a third slot-range shape has to be able to supply one.

**The same proposition is why prose was not enough.** It says a new instance earns admission by
supplying the obligations, and that closing the concept while opening the inventory is what makes
admission "a check rather than a negotiation". An obligation stated and unchecked is the construction
this round is removing, one level up.

**This is not a runtime check and not an I18 panic.** It is const evaluation at the use site, so
nothing reaches a lowered path, which is what
`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` asks. `ruling::the_overflow_panic_is_permitted_and_bounded`
is for what cannot be caught at compile time through the typestate, and its correction
`ruling::the_panic_bound_names_a_concern_not_a_marker` says the intent is the inferrable thing; this is
caught at compile time, so neither reaches it and nothing depends on a debug build.

## CHANGE: the rogue construction FROM a scratch probe TO a permanent law and a compile-fail case
Files: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/tests.rs`, `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/ui/`
Verification: both exist and both run.

Two halves, because one of them alone would be weaker than the finding.

`is_admissible<S>()` is the law, returning a verdict rather than asserting, so the wrong construction
can live permanently in the test module and be reported on without forcing the check that would refuse
it. The test asserts the law **rejects** it, which is the shape
`catalogue-edge-cases-as-tests` names for a construction that compiles and is wrong.

The `trybuild` case asserts that **using** the same construction does not compile. The law says the
design can tell; the compile-fail case says the design does refuse.

## What is deliberately not here

**No seal**, per the open inventory.

**No claim that the check is total.** It refuses what its obligations name and nothing further, and
both the design and the module doc say so rather than repeating the defect this round is fixing.

## How this changelist expects to be checked

`cargo test --workspace` in `mock/` with the new law test and the second `trybuild` case, and the same
counts under `--release`. `cargo mock check` green at strict.
