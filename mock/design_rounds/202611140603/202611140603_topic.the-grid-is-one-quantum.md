**Date:** 2026-11-14

The grid condition asked for more than the claim it serves. The door rests on slot `b` of `G` denoting the
value slot `b` of `F` does. That value is `(phase + b) q`, so what it needs is one phase and one quantum, and
`q` is `r^e`, which is one value across radixes wherever the powers agree: at exponent zero every radix has
the quantum one. Radix ten at exponent zero beside the bytes was refused with a message saying its slots
name values off the grid, which is false. The condition becomes one quantum, compared exactly: with
`U = r^max(e, 0)` and `V = r^max(-e, 0)` per format, the quanta are equal where `U_F V_G = U_G V_F`, both
read in the four limbs from the pieces `placing` already carries for the product, the quotient and the
conversion. A pair whose powers leave the four limbs is refused as not formed, with that said in the
message. The door's verdict becomes four: one quantum, both constant; the reduced phase; the range inside;
no member naming the zero. The radix refusal moves to a shape whose grids differ, radix ten beside radix
two at exponent `-1`, and radix ten at exponent zero beside the bytes becomes an admitted file and a swept
pair. The old verdict, radix and exponent compared apart, is kept as a planted verdict the table has to
report, beside one that compares the exponent and drops the radix.

The records said the compromise was removed, and it is narrowed rather than removed. A slot range is one
interval, so a zero-free range at a whole phase lies on one side of its zero slot. A whole-phase format
whose range holds the zero divides at run time by a member of a range on either side of it, but not by a
divisor read at run time that may be either sign, the ordinary signed `a / b`. Reaching that means
classifying a run-time slot as not the zero, which is the ingest door that
`ruling::the_ingest_door_waits_on_a_known_use` holds back until a known use is shown. Whether signed
division is that use is a question for op, filed as ticket
`may_arvo_divide_by_a_signed_divisor_read_at_run_time_of_eith` with four options. The design and a
superseding proposal state the gap, and the source marks it with a `FIXME` naming the ticket where
`Divisor::of` refuses such a range.

The sweep sampled the unsigned side only. The design names a plain integer range first, and no signed
dividend, negative dividend or negative member divisor was swept, so the negation the step makes where
`n + b d` is below zero was never reached through this door. `Integer<8>` divided by the members of `1` to
`127` and by those of `-128` to `-1` join the sweep, the table and the zero column's search, beside the bytes
divided by radix ten at exponent zero.
