## CHANGE: the member door's verdict is four, the grid one quantum

`src/division.rs`: `member_conditions::<F, G>() -> [Bool; 4]`, in order `one_quantum`, `same_phase`,
`range_inside`, `no_member_names_the_zero::<G>()`. `one_quantum::<F, G>()` answers false unless both quanta
are a constant family, then compares `times(f.up, g.down)` with `times(g.up, f.down)` from
`placing::powers`, equal by `Wide::ordering`, and answers false where either product is not formed.
`same_constant_exponent` is deleted, its one use gone. `MemberDivisor`'s obligation carries four asserts;
the quantum message says slot `b` names `(phase + b) q`, that the radixes may differ where `r^e` is one
value as at exponent zero, and that a pair whose powers leave the four limbs is refused as not formed. The
`radix` and `Quantum` imports leave the module.

## CHANGE: the gap is marked and `of` says what a slot past the range does

`src/division.rs`: `NoSlotNamesTheZero`'s obligation carries
`FIXME(may_arvo_divide_by_a_signed_divisor_read_at_run_time_of_eith)`, naming the divisor read at run time
of either sign over a whole-phase range holding the zero. `Divisor::of`'s rustdoc says a divisor past the
range can take the quotient past the exact step, which saturates there, in place of saying it is
positioned like any slot.

## CHANGE: the member door's suite

`src/division/tests/the_member_door.rs`: ranges `OneTo127` and `DownFromMinusOne`, formats `SignedByte`,
`UpTo127` and `Negatives`. Three sweeps join the six: `Byte` by `Decimal`, `SignedByte` by `UpTo127`,
`SignedByte` by `Negatives`. The table states four answers per row in sixteen rows: nine true throughout,
the swept pairs; `Byte, Halves` and `Halves, PositiveInTenths` false on the quantum alone; `Byte, Biased`
on the phase; `Byte, Wider` on the range; `Byte, Byte` and `SignedByte, SignedByte` on the zero;
`FromOne, SignedFromOne` on the range and the zero. The control reads four columns. The planted verdicts are
four: `ZeroOfTheDivisorsFormat`, `RadixAndExponentApart`, which the `Byte, Decimal` row reports, `NoRadix`,
which the `Halves, PositiveInTenths` row reports, and `NoInside`; `NoExponent` is gone with the five-column
verdict. The zero column's search runs at sixteen formats. The module doc names the signed and the radix
ten pairs.
