# Src changelist: the divisor and the exact door

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** the fix-up of step AR6's second round, on `work/oracle-fraction-wide`, after review pass one.
**Doc changelist:** `202611130659_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply/position.rs`, `src/apply.rs`, `src/lib.rs`,
`src/placing.rs`, `src/division.rs`, `src/conversion.rs`, the call sites of the renamed constructors in
`src/addition.rs` and the test modules, `src/division/tests/the_divisor.rs`, `tests/compile_fail.rs`,
`tests/ui`, and the regenerated `docs/FORMAT.md`.

## CHANGE: the remainder and its position are public

`Remainder` is `pub` and re-exported from `apply` and the crate root. Its constructor is
`Remainder::of(num, den)`, the residue `num` leaves over `den` with a zero denominator reading as
`ZERO`, and `numerator` and `denominator` read the pair back; `ZERO` is public. `Exact::at(slot, part)`
replaces the crate-private `Exact::held` and its `FIXME`, with no carry and nothing past the index. Every
call site of `Remainder::within` and `Exact::held` in the crate and its tests is renamed, so the
product, the quotient, the conversion and the sum build their positions through the public door.

## CHANGE: `of` is admitted only where no slot names the zero

`division.rs`: `NoZeroMember` becomes `NoSlotNamesTheZero`, asserting the crate-private
`no_slot_names_the_zero`, which is the reduced phase denotes with a denominator past one. Its `FIXME`
names `arvo_a_runtime_divisor_over_a_whole_phase_range_without_the`. The step's branch on a zero
divisor factor is removed, with the reason it is never reached stated on `placed`, and the import of
`has_additive_identity` goes with it.

## CHANGE: one array of verdicts per obligation

`division.rs` and `conversion.rs` each gain a crate-private `conditions`, the four verdicts in order.
The obligation asserts each from it and `is_divisible` and `is_convertible` fold it with `all`, a
crate-private fold over an array of verdicts in `placing.rs`.

## CHANGE: the divisor's tests and the compile-fail pair

`the_divisor.rs`: the admission is checked against the oracle's search for a slot whose value is zero
over the range and its span beyond each end, at nine formats, a reducible phase among them; a range
leaving the zero out at a whole phase is shown refused. The doors use `Halves` for `of` and a
constant for the range from one. The saturation test of the non-member zero is deleted, since that path
no longer exists; a new test places runtime divisors outside the range against the oracle; a new test
shows each held-out format fails at its own condition first.

`tests/ui`: the pair becomes `a_runtime_divisor_over_a_whole_phase_is_refused` and
`a_runtime_divisor_over_a_half_offset_phase_is_admitted`, the same range from one, differing in the
phase. The stderr of the three refusals whose assert source moved is regenerated. The root-surface
file names `Remainder` and `Exact::at`, and asserts that the position it builds for `100 / 3` is the one
`quotient_position` builds.

## CHANGE: the generated document

`docs/FORMAT.md` regenerated by `cargo mock` from the template, so the division and conversion sections
are in it and the remainder's paragraph is current.
