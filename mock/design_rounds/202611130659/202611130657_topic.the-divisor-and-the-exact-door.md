# the-divisor-and-the-exact-door

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** the fix-up of step AR6's second round, on `work/oracle-fraction-wide`, after review pass one.

## What the review found

Four blocking findings and three cheap ones, read against the closed round `202611130510`.

`Divisor::of` was admitted over any format with no zero member, a range from one included. Over such a
range the one slot that names the zero is a non-member, and `of` takes any slot, so a `Divisor` could
hold it and the step answered it with a branch that saturates toward the positive end. The type said
non-zero and was non-zero only for members, and membership is not typed. Whether that branch is the
runtime check `ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` forbids is not settled by the canon,
and no blessing is recorded, so the branch goes rather than waiting on one.

The crate readme says there is no division by zero to handle at run time, which the branch contradicted.

`docs/FORMAT.md` is generated from the crate's design template and was not regenerated in the round that
added division and conversion.

The product, the quotient and the conversion build their positions through `Exact::held`, which is
crate-private, over `Remainder`, which is crate-private too. An operation written outside the crate
cannot build what `div` builds. That is a door first-party code has and a consumer does not, which is a
second path beside the contract, and the carry rule the door was deferred on is decidable now.

Cheap ones: `Divisible::ADMITTED` and `is_divisible` state the same four conditions twice, and so do
`Convertible::ADMITTED` and `is_convertible`, tied only by boundary samples. `saturated` drops the
distance past the index. The law test does not reach the canon's failing cell at 128.

## The stance: no slot names the zero, so the step has nothing to branch on

`Divisor::of` is admitted exactly where no slot at all names the zero, which is where the phase's
denominator in lowest terms is past one. `phase + b` is zero only at `b = -n / d`, an integer only when
`d` is one, so with `d` past one there is no such slot, member or not. Every `Divisor` then names a
non-zero factor by construction: `constant` refuses the zero and every slot outside the range, and `of`
exists only where no slot is the zero. The step reads the factor and never asks whether it is zero.

The compromise is wider than before and is stated: a consumer's range that leaves the zero out at a
whole phase, a range from one, no longer divides by a slot read at run time. It divides by constants,
as a plain integer range does. Admitting it needs a slot typed as a member of its format, which is a
change to the slot coordinate and not this round's; the ticket `arvo_a_runtime_divisor_over_a_whole_phase_range_without_the` carries it.

## The stance: the exact door is public

`Remainder` becomes public, the remainder an exact position stores: a numerator below an unsigned
128-bit denominator, in `[0, 1)` by construction. `Remainder::of(num, den)` is the remainder `num`
leaves over `den`, the residue and nothing else, and a zero denominator reads as `ZERO` the way
`Fraction::of` reads one. Nothing carries, because the type is the residue rather than a ratio
normalised into one. `Exact::at(slot, part)` is the position at `slot` and `part` of the way to the
next, with nothing past the index. The product, the quotient and the conversion build through exactly
these two, so a consumer's own operation forms the same positions through the same door.

`Fraction` stays the caller's ratio for `between` and the dither, and is not widened. The two types are
two coordinates with two contracts: a ratio of any sign and any size that `between` normalises with its
carry, and a residue that needs neither.

## The cheap ones

Each obligation's conditions become one array of verdicts that the obligation asserts in order and the
verdict function folds, so the two cannot drift.

`saturated` stays as it is. A placed position past the index is one whose distance past it the step
does not form, and `Exact`'s `past` coordinate holds at most an `i64`, so the distance would need a
wider coordinate. No member reaches it, since the obligation refuses those formats.

The law's cells at 64, 65 and 128 stay under `arvo_law_the_multiplicative_inverse_round_trip_is_unreachabl`.
