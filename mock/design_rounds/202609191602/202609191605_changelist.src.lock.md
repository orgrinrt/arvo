`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_carry_past_the_index.rs`

## CHANGE: `no_phase_admits_a_range_past_the_doubling_bound_itself` pins the carry bound the doc changelist checked

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/addition/tests/the_step.rs`

At the phase furthest from zero in the direction that would extend the bound if extending it were
possible (`i64::MIN` for the top, `i64::MAX` for the bottom), a range at `i128::MAX / 2 - 1` is admitted
and one at `i128::MAX / 2` is refused; symmetrically at the bottom. Pins that `doubled_plus`'s
`checked_mul` gates the range's own bound before the phase is ever added, which is the reason the
`[-2^126, 2^126)` sentence stays as written.

Verification: `cargo test -p arvo-format --lib addition::tests::the_step::no_phase_admits_a_range_past_the_doubling_bound_itself`.
