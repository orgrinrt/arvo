# The member step's bound above a zero exponent

**Date:** 2026-11-20
**Round:** `202611201340`, job `arvo-numeral-doors`.
**Source:** the second review of `work/arvo-numeral-exact-step-clean`, `mock/research/202611201150_review-arvo-numeral-exact-step/PASS_2.md`, findings 1 and 6.

The member step's bound is held at its edge only from exponent zero downward. Above zero the three bounds
read `u = r^e`, and none of them is held at an edge there. The quotient's check that its largest
denominator, the widest member times `u`, fits the host's 128-bit integer has no test that fails when it
is deleted: at `Fixed<64, 70>` the rest of the bound reads true without it, and the denominator then
passes `2^128` in the host arm.

The step also derives `u` and `v` a second time, beside the format's `placing::powers`, which the slot
steps already read them from.

## Decision 1, the edge on both sides

The edge test holds each bound at the last exponent above zero the tests' own arithmetic puts inside it
and refuses it one past, at the same widths, radixes and ranges as below zero. Above zero the product's
edge is where the square of the widest member times `r^e` reaches `2^127`, which some ranges reach
already at zero; the quotient's is where the widest member times `r^e` leaves the 128-bit integer; the
conversion from `F` onto the grid at zero is where the widest member times `r^e` reaches `2^127`, and the
conversion onto a grid above zero where `r^e` leaves the 128-bit integer.

A third mutant flag, `arvo_mutant_member_step_guard`, drops the quotient's check on its denominator, and a
sketch shows the edge test failing under it.

## Decision 2, one derivation of the powers

The step reads `u` and `v` from `placing::powers`, narrowed to the host's unsigned integer, and keeps no
power function of its own.
