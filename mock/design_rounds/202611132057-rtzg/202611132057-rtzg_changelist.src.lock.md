# Source changelist: the fixed-point aliases and the exact accumulator

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** 202611132057-rtzg, branch `work/arvo-values`.
**Design:** `202611132057-rtzg_changelist.doc.lock.md`, and under it
`mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl`.

## Current state

The aliases cover the integer points, so a fixed-point value is written out as `Num` over a
`Signature`. A sum of many values has the signature's `add` and nothing that sums exactly.

## Changes proposed

### `arvo-numeral`

`src/alias.rs`: `Fix<BITS, FRAC, A, C>` and `UFix<BITS, FRAC, A, C>`, each reading
`NamesAdaptation` at its own format.

`src/accumulator.rs`, new: `Accumulator<S, T, CAP, C>` over a private `Num<T, Quiet>`, with the
constant `ADMITTED` that asks the two things of `T`, and `empty`, `of`, `take`, `plus`, `minus`,
`total`, `finish` and `fits`. A term enters through `Num::at_index` on its slot index and the sum is
`T`'s `add` under the `Quiet` check, since nothing can leave `T`'s range. `minus` returns
`Num<T, C>`. `finish` and `fits` are `convert` and `fits_convert` onto `S`. The mutant
`mutant-accumulator` sits in `take` behind `cfg!(feature = ...)`.

`src/lib.rs`: the module, and the re-exports of `Accumulator`, `Fix` and `UFix`.

`Cargo.toml`: the feature `mutant-accumulator`.

`tests/the_lane.rs`: the aliases against the written types, the named product under `HalfEven` and
under `Floor`, a preset naming an adaptation for a fixed-point format.

`tests/the_accumulator.rs`: `total`, `finish` and `fits` against the exact sum, exhaustive at the
small widths and seeded above; `take` around the capacity; `plus` and `minus`; the order case on the
signed saturating cell; the probe's numbers.

`tests/the_debug_check.rs` and its release twin: `finish` under `Abort` and under `Quiet`.

`tests/ui/`: the four pairs the design names, each refusal with its diagnostic.

`tests/consumer/`: a lane through `Fix` and `UFix`, and an accumulator through each of its
functions.

`mock/research/sketches/202611132057-rtzg_value-mutants/`: the red run of `mutant-accumulator`, with
what it shows.

## Comparison to deprecated changelist

The deprecated changelist was written against the design before it said what `minus` returns, and no
source was written under it.

### current state

Unchanged: the aliases stop at the integers, `Fix` and `UFix` do not exist, and nothing sums exactly.

### changes proposed

One difference. `minus` returns `Num<T, C>`, the exact difference as a value of `T`, where the
deprecated text left its return type unsaid and the design then read as though it gave an
`Accumulator`. Everything else is as it was: `Fix` and `UFix` reading `NamesAdaptation`, the
`Accumulator` over a private `Num` under `Quiet` with its `ADMITTED` constant asking the two things
of `T` on behalf of `S`, terms entering through `Num::at_index`, `finish` aborting under `Abort`, the
named product under `HalfEven` and `Floor`, and a lane written as a `Signature` no longer.
