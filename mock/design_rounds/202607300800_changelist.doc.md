# Doc changelist: six templates, all already applied

**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Round:** the restructure and formalization round opened 2026-07-27 (`fe7f56e`)
**Scope:** six `*.md.tmpl` files under `mock/crates/`, recording the predicate decisions and two
corrections. No source.

## What this changelist is

This round was a design talk that produced 52 numbered decisions across 35 topic files, two sketches
and one research artifact. Most of those decisions are not applied anywhere: they describe a
restructure and a formalization that the next round executes.

Six documentation templates did change, during the round's earlier DOC window, before
`202607290400_changelist.doc.md` was deprecated. This changelist records them, because the round
cannot reach `CLOSED` honestly with those edits unaccounted for.

A first draft of this file claimed the round changed no templates at all. That was wrong, caught by
`git diff --stat fe7f56e^..HEAD -- 'mock/crates/**/*.md.tmpl'`, which reports six files and 64
insertions. The claim was replaced rather than the check skipped.

## What is deliberately not here

The restructure D2 describes, the crate moves of D27 through D42, the formalization of
`202607300300`, and the taxonomy revisions of `202607300400`. Writing them against the pre-proposal
picture would encode a state the round superseded on its final day, and `202607300400` lists seven
earlier calls that the proposal reopens or changes in kind.

Both sketches the proposal gated on returned WORKS, so it stands rather than being parked, and the
work belongs to the round that acts on it.
