## Why this changelist is empty

This round was a design talk. It produced 52 numbered decisions across 35 topic files, two sketches,
and one research artifact measuring arvo's strategy axes against the field. It changed no
`*.md.tmpl` and no source.

The mockspace state machine has no pure-talk phase: a round reaches `CLOSED` only by opening and
locking a doc changelist and then a source changelist. This file and its source sibling exist to walk
the round to `CLOSED` honestly, recording that the answer to "what documents change" is nothing.

calls that reopens or changes in kind, including the contents of `arvo-numeric` and the identity of
`arvo-strategy`. Writing a document changelist against the pre-proposal taxonomy would encode a
picture the round itself superseded on its last day.

(`mock/research/sketches/202607300500_format-as-exponent-function/` and
`.../202607300600_law-markers-derived-over-composition/`), so the proposal stands rather than being
parked, and the work belongs to the round that acts on it.
