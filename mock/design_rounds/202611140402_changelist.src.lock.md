# Src changelist: the slot review

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140402`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140402_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Topics consumed:** `202611140402_topic.the-slot-review.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/`: `apply.rs`, `addition.rs`, `multiplication.rs`, `conversion.rs`,
`wide.rs`, the new `wide/division.rs`, and the tests named below.

## CHANGE: completion in range is the floor plus the step

`src/apply.rs`: a private `Rounded::landed` answers a position `lands_within` has put in range as the floor
narrowed to the index plus the step, and `Slot::at(i128::MIN)` where the index does not hold the floor, which
in range is only `i128::MIN - 1` stepping up. `complete_slot`'s in-range branch returns it in place of
`reduced(lo, span)`, and `reduced`'s rustdoc no longer says a position in range is its own residue. `notko::Maybe`
is imported outside tests, since `landed` reads it.

`src/apply/tests/the_far_end_of_the_index.rs`: `a_floor_one_under_the_bottom_of_the_index_steps_onto_it_in_range`
feeds the floor `i128::MIN - 1` with a half over it into the range `[i128::MIN, i128::MIN + 3]`. Ceil lands on
the range's lowest slot under every policy; Floor, its control, leaves the range and wraps to the top and
saturates to the bottom.

## CHANGE: the oracle holds every floor the map is handed

`src/apply/tests/the_oracle.rs`: the oracle's integer is the shared wide oracle, `Big`, the signed integer
below `2^256` on two 128-bit halves, behind a newtype `Wide` with `of`, `of_big`, `wide`, `plus`, `negated`,
`minus`, `is_negative`, `is_even`, `index`, `order`, `is_below` and `rem_euclid`. `order` reads two values of
opposite sign by their signs, so the difference it reads otherwise stays below `2^256`. The wrap reduces the
position modulo the span before subtracting the range's lowest slot. The module doc and the two tests that
described a pinned slot and its distance describe the floor, and `the_oracle_s_integer_holds_every_floor_a_formed_step_reaches`
checks `2^255` and `±(2^256 - 1)` against residues worked by hand, their parity, their order across the sign and
their hand-over to the step's four limbs as `end`.

`src/apply/tests/the_wide_oracle_sweep.rs`: beside the positions written from an anchor, the sweep feeds the
far floors `±2^128`, `±2^192` and `±2^255`, each with the remainders zero, one, half the denominator and one
under it over the sweep's denominators, and `±(2^256 - 1)` on the grid, through `Exact::floored`, into every
range under every mode, policy and dither, for the shipped map, every broken map, `adapt` and
`panic_on_overflow`. A case names its origin, an anchor or a far floor. The shipped map still has no
disagreement and every broken map is still reported. `the_far_feed_reaches_the_floors_a_formed_step_reaches`
counts, under Ceil, the feed's floors at both ends of the four limbs and its off-grid positions at `±2^255`.

## CHANGE: the four-limb integer's reads, and its long division

`src/wide/tests/the_reads.rs`: laws for `of_sum`, `end`, `residue` and `is_even` against values written as
literal limbs, among them `of_sum(MAX, MAX) = 2^128 - 2`, `of_sum(MIN, MIN) = -2^128`, the residue of
`-(2^256 - 1)` modulo `2^64` being 1, every residue modulo 1 being zero, and a negative odd value being odd.

`src/wide.rs`: a test-only `Wide::of_limbs` writes a value as its sign and limbs, so a test outside the
module states an expected value without the code under test forming it. `src/apply/tests/the_edges.rs` and
`src/multiplication/tests/the_step.rs` write their expected floors with it, worked by hand, where they were
formed by `Wide::of_sum` and `Wide::times`.

`src/wide/division.rs`: `by_one_limb`, `by_limbs` and `shifted_up` move out of `src/wide.rs` unchanged, the
first two `pub(super)` for `floor_divided`. `src/wide.rs` goes from 530 lines to 421.

## CHANGE: addition names its ticket

`src/addition.rs`: the refusal of a sum the slot index cannot carry carries
`FIXME(arvo_addition_s_sum_past_the_slot_index_could_ride_the_four)`.

## CHANGE: the prose the review named

`src/multiplication.rs` and `src/conversion.rs`: the placing comments say the floor is held in the four limbs
wherever it falls and that the obligation rules out a numerator not formed, and conversion's `Step::scale`
names a member past the limbs as a step not formed. `src/apply/tests/the_cross_end_saturation.rs` derives its
expected slot from the floor `i128::MIN - 2` modulo the span. `src/multiplication/tests/the_wrap_by_every_mode.rs`
says its formats cross what the wrap reads.
