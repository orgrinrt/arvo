## CHANGE: the proof section of the value's design

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `## What is proved, and how`.

The paragraph on widths 1 to 8 gains widths 9 to 12, computed in every rung wide enough to carry them,
and a sentence saying exactly which widths are exhaustive and which sampled: exhaustive at 1 to 12 in the
rungs, the 8-bit carrier's full width among them, and through the value type at 1 to 8; sampled at 13
to 64, where every full-width branch of the 16, 32 and 64-bit rungs falls.

The sentence on one verdict test per plain marker without a `#[should_panic]` is replaced by the
assertion tests: one per site, `add`, `adapted` and `from_raw`, and per plain marker, each expecting the
site's message, each site with a twin under the completing markers, and a test that the plain markers
stay quiet on what fits; compiled only under `debug_assertions`.

The mutant paragraph says the tests assert agreement under every build, so each mutant turns its band
and the rung tests red, and that the red output of each is committed beside the round.

The compile-fail paragraph loses its consumer sentence, and a new paragraph says the consumer is a crate
of its own outside the workspace, with no feature line, reaching every public item, built and run by a
test.

## CHANGE: the aliases section of the value's design

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `## Aliases`.

`Bool` stays `arvo_format::Bool`, re-exported, and the section says why it is not a value over a
declared signature: it is the type every predicate and verdict of the format and of this crate returns,
so a second boolean would be a second type for one concept, and a one-bit value's sum under a policy is
not how a boolean is read.
