# Source changelist: the value sheds what the canon reserves

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** 202611132216-pzi4, branch `work/arvo-values`.
**Design:** `202611132216-pzi4_changelist.doc.lock.md`, and under it
`mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl`.

## Current state

`arvo-numeral` exports `Accumulator` from `src/accumulator.rs` and `Num::holds_index` from
`src/host.rs`. The sweeps of `div`, `convert` and the fractional `mul` against the tests' own
arithmetic stop at 8, 16 and 6 bits. `tests/a_consumer_crate.rs` holds a test that asserts nothing.

## Changes proposed

### `arvo-numeral`, source

`src/accumulator.rs` is deleted, with its `mod` and `pub use` in `src/lib.rs` and the feature
`mutant-accumulator` in `Cargo.toml`. The doc comment in `src/alias.rs` that names the type no longer
does.

`src/host.rs`: `holds_index` loses `pub` and stays as the verdict the debug check in `from_index`
asks.

### `arvo-numeral`, tests

Deleted: `tests/the_accumulator.rs`, the accumulator's compile-fail pairs under `tests/ui/` with
their entries in `tests/compile_fail.rs`, and the accumulator's cases in `tests/the_debug_check.rs`,
`tests/the_debug_check_in_release.rs` and `tests/consumer/src/main.rs`.

`tests/the_host_door.rs`: the comparisons of `holds_index` with `panic_on_overflow` go, since the
function is no longer reachable from a test crate. What `from_index` returns is still compared with
`adapted` on every input, which is where the verdict's effect shows.

`tests/common/oracle.rs`: the three functions return nothing where an exact intermediate would leave
`i128`, in checked arithmetic.

`tests/the_quotient.rs`: the named divisors at widths 5 to 8 gain an even one. A new sweep on the
edge lattice and seeded pairs at the wide widths the design names.

`tests/the_conversion.rs`: a new sweep from sources of 32 and 64 bits on the edge lattice and seeded
members.

`tests/fixed_point_product.rs`: a new sweep at fixed-point formats of 16 and 32 bits on the edge
lattice and seeded pairs.

`tests/a_consumer_crate.rs`: `the_crate_directory_as_resolved` is folded into
`the_crate_directory_is_read_when_the_test_runs` as a branch taken where the rerun's variable is set.
