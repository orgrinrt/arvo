# The width law was sampled, and the widened lint had no arm

Three defects a review of the `arvo-bits` round found, and one stale surface it found
with them. All four are fixes rather than a feature, which is what lets them stand
together without waiting for a feature to attach to.

## The law is stated over every width and pinned at five

`crates/arvo-bits/DESIGN.md.tmpl` states the construction and cast laws over "any value"
and "every admitted `M`". The suite pins them at `N` in one, eight, twenty-eight,
thirty-two and sixty-four, and at three of the four thousand and ninety-six cast pairs.

The module header defends that sample: "There is exactly one generic code path in this
crate, unlike `arvo_format::slots`'s per-width `admit_widths!` macro, so the distinct
behaviours to cover are the boundary at `N = 1`, the special-cased boundary at `N = 64`
and the ordinary interior case."

`lib.rs` disagrees with it. `const MASK: Self = if N == 64 { Self(u64::MAX) } else {
Self((1u64 << N) - 1) };` is two paths, the design says so in the same words the header
uses to say otherwise, and the doc comment above `MASK` explains why the second exists.
So the widths were a sample after all, chosen on a premise the crate refutes, and `N`
runs over sixty-four values, which is enumerable. The precedent the header cites as the
reason not to sweep is `slots.rs`'s `admit_widths!`, which is a per-width sweep.

`N = 63`, the width immediately below the special case, is touched nowhere.

## The lint was widened and every fixture still names the other crate

`lints/a_contract_coordinate_is_not_a_host_primitive.rs` gained `arvo-bits` in
`THE_EXEMPT_CRATES`. Every fixture in its suite goes through `hits`, which is fixed to
`.with_crate_name("arvo-format", "format")`, so the widening has no arm of its own in
either direction.

It is not decorative. The round's own source changelist records the widened lint
catching a `const MASK: u64` while the round was open and driving it to
`const MASK: Self`.

## The published surface still says three crates

The root `README.md` enumerates `arvo-format`, `arvo-placement` and `arvo-strategy` and
then says "the shape above these three". It is hand-written and the round did not touch
it.

`docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/PRINCIPLES.md` say "Three crates", and `docs/STRUCTURE.md`
and the three `STRUCTURE.GRAPH` renderings do not know `arvo-bits` exists. Those are
generated, and their templates are already correct: `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and
`mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl` both say "Four crates". Nothing regenerated them, so the
round's own planned edit landed in the source and never reached the page.

## What each fix is

The sweeps go over all sixty-four widths and all four thousand and ninety-six cast
pairs, asserting against an oracle that folds the mask one bit at a time rather than
against a second copy of the closed form, so the two agreeing is evidence. The oracle
carries its own arm proving it can disagree with an off-by-one, because a sweep whose
oracle cannot be wrong establishes nothing.

The lint gains three arms: that a host-typed const in `arvo-bits` now fires, that one
spelled in the crate's own type stays silent, and that the exempt list holds exactly the
two crates with a door. The second is what stops the first passing vacuously.

The README gains a paragraph for the fourth crate, and `cargo mock` regenerates the
pages the templates already describe.

## What the control established

`MASK`'s boundary was changed to `N >= 63` on purpose. Two of the new sweeps fail and
name the width; the eight tests that were there before all pass. That is the measurement
of what the sample was covering.

## What this round does not touch

Whether the `[primitive-introductions]` entry for `arvo-bits` should be crate-wide is a
separate question and not a fix. The canon settles what a door carries out and is silent
on how many doors there may be, so it is neither granted nor refused, and silence is not
permission. It goes to op rather than into a round.
