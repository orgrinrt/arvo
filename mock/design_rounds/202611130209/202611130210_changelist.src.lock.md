## CHANGE: an independent wide integer for the edge oracle

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/multiplication/tests/big.rs`, new, declared in `tests/mod.rs`.

`Big` is a sign and a magnitude on two of the host's unsigned 128-bit integers. Its product is formed
from the 64-bit halves of each word, its division by a positive divisor is bit-serial over all 256
bits with a floor adjustment on the negative side, and a value that would pass `2^256` panics. It
shares no code with the step's four-limb `Wide`. A test checks it against the host's own arithmetic
where the host holds the answer, and `2^200` over three multiplied back.

## CHANGE: the edge sweep reads the phase and the radix

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/multiplication/tests/the_edges.rs`.

The oracle forms `(n + a d)(n + b d) r^up - n d r^down` in `Big` and floors it by `d^2 r^down`,
reducing the remainder by its common factor, so it answers at every phase and radix and not only
at radix two and zero phase. The verdict check reads the same oracle at the four corners. The
existing binary test keeps its four exponents and its counts. A new test runs the edges at every
width from nine to 64 of both families at a phase of a third, a phase of minus two thirds at `2^-5`,
radix ten at `10^-18` and `10^1`, and a phase of two sevenths at `10^-3`, asserting the points swept,
that some formats are held out and some admitted, and that the floor-only control is wrong somewhere
and never where the denominator is one. A third test runs radix ten at `10^20`, where the 64-bit
corners need four limbs, and asserts that positions past the index occur. The named edges add the
signed 64-bit range at a phase of a third and at `10^-18`, each admitted and each checked through
`product_position` at its corners. A host-typed const became a function for the lint.

## CHANGE: a division across three and four limbs is multiplied back

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/multiplication/wide/tests.rs`.

`10^45`, `3^120`, `3^150` and `7^90`, each moved by 12345 and taken on both signs, are floored by 3,
7, `10^18` and `2^63 - 1`. The remainder is below the divisor and the quotient times the divisor plus
the remainder is the numerator, 32 cases.
