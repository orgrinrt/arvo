## CHANGE: the crate's design document

File: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, new.

Sections, in order:

1. Framing prose: what a value of a declared signature is, why it is its own crate above
   `arvo-placement`, and the sum as the oracle's sum.
2. `## What a value signature admits`: `Sole`, its blanket implementation, the slot families on the map,
   `ValuePolicy` and `ValueRounding` as the admitted overflow policies and rounding modes, and the
   `ADMITTED` obligation (constant quantum, zero phase). What is refused and why: `Clamp`, `Stochastic`,
   a phase, an indexed quantum, width zero, width past 64.
3. `## How the value holds its carrier`: the map from slot family to ladder carrier with its per-row
   assertion against `derive_sole`, `Num<S>` holding `S::Repr` privately, the dispatch to the rung
   function by the carrier's size and signedness, the bit copy and its precondition, the rung's add
   below and at the carrier width.
4. `## The overflow markers`: `Wrap` and `Saturate` assert in debug; `Wrapping` and `Saturating` never
   do; the verdict `fits_add`; the doors' use of `panic_on_overflow`; the stated compromise.
5. `## The doors`: `constant`, `adapted`, `from_raw`, `slot`, `raw`, and for `USize` and `ISize`,
   `from_host` and `host`; each total or refused at build, none checked at run time.
6. `## The operations`: `add`, `fits_add`, `eq`, `cmp`, `lt`, `le`, `gt`, `ge`, `min`, `max`; the
   contract each meets, stated as equalities with the oracle; the traits `Num` implements and the
   ones it does not.
7. `## Identities`: `Identity<M>`, `Additive`, the failed-constant refusal.
8. `## Aliases`: `Uint`, `Int`, `USize`, `ISize`, `Bool`.
9. `## What is proved, and how`: the test plan by band, the mutants, the registry verdicts
   reproduced, the compile-fail pairs, the consumer with no feature line, the ad hoc disassembly.
10. `## What is not here`: sub, neg, mul, the accumulator and `Lane`, which are the next round's.
