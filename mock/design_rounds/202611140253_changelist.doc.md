## CHANGE: the mutants

The paragraph on the rung mutants is replaced. The mutants are configuration flags named `arvo_mutant_<name>`:
`arvo_mutant_8`, `arvo_mutant_16`, `arvo_mutant_32`, `arvo_mutant_64` for the rungs, and `arvo_mutant_sub`,
`arvo_mutant_neg`, `arvo_mutant_mul`, `arvo_mutant_under`, `arvo_mutant_div`, `arvo_mutant_convert`,
`arvo_mutant_convert_policy`, `arvo_mutant_index` and `arvo_mutant_count` for the operations. A mutant run
sets one through `RUSTFLAGS="--cfg arvo_mutant_<name>"`. Why a flag and not a feature: a feature is unified
across a build, so a crate that turned one on would change every other crate's arithmetic; a flag is set only
by whoever builds at the top, and no manifest can set it for a dependency. The manifest declares the flags to
the compiler's configuration check, so a misspelt flag is reported rather than silently doing nothing. Every
other sentence naming a mutant says the flag's name.

`arvo_mutant_convert_policy` is new: it completes a conversion by saturation wherever the source's policy is
saturation, and by the target's policy otherwise. It turns the sweep under every source signature red and
its red run is committed.

## CHANGE: the families of exponents

The sentence on the families of exponents says each family's two ends are the last exponents the format
admits for that operation at that width and radix, the highest and the deepest, and that a test per
operation holds both ends against the format's own verdict at every width, both ranges and the three
radixes, admitted at each and refused one past it. The product's highest is the highest exponent at which
every product of two members is a position a slot index holds; the quotient's is the highest at which the
remainder's denominator fits at both ends of the range. The tests compute each in their own arithmetic.

## CHANGE: the conversion's sweeps

The sweep under every source signature crosses two steps of exponent a side: minus one onto zero, where the
mode decides and no member leaves the range, and zero onto minus one, where every member past the target's
range on either end is completed by the policy. So it is where a result that read the source's mode or the
source's policy would disagree, which the paragraph now says of each.

A fifth sweep is added, at the positive edge: at each width, radix and pair of ranges, the source at the
highest exponent at which each end of its range lands on a position a slot index holds onto a target at
exponent zero, and a source at exponent zero onto the target at the highest exponent at which the
remainder's denominator fits, under the ten signatures of the target. A test holds both edges against the
format's verdict at every width, both ranges and the three radixes. The four sweeps become five.
