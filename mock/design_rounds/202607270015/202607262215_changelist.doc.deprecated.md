# Doc changelist: the release-blocker round

**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Phase:** DOC
**Source topic:** `202607262200_topic.release-blockers.md`
**Status:** living until locked

Writes the two correctness corrections into the design documents before the source moves, and states two
consequences of the strategy model that were true but undocumented.

## CHANGE: mock/crates/arvo/DESIGN.md.tmpl

Add `Requires` and `Satisfied` to the documented surface: the const-predicate witness and its marker, the
mechanism that lets an impl carry a compile-time condition and fail as a trait-resolution error with a
written explanation rather than as a const-eval panic after monomorphisation. Both are public because a
bound naming them appears in a public signature, and a consumer reading that signature needs them
resolvable.

State the rule they carry first: **`Identity` requires at least one integer bit.** `UFixed<0, F, S>` has
logical range `[0, 1)`, so it has no representable one, and its `Identity` impl does not exist. `ZERO` is
unaffected at every width. The document says why rather than only that, because a reader hitting the error
wants to know whether to change the type or change the call.

## CHANGE: mock/crates/arvo-strategy/DESIGN.md.tmpl

Two consequences of the strategy model, both already true, neither written down.

**Signed multiply at the container minimum.** The wrapping strategies produce the container minimum
directly rather than reaching it by negating its own magnitude, which is not representable. Documented
because it is the one input where the sign-magnitude path has no symmetric answer.

**Raw values may leave the declared logical range on `Hot` and `Cold`.** Wrapping happens at the container
width, not the logical width, so a multiply at logical 12 in a 16-bit container can return a raw above the
logical maximum of 4095. The existing text documents only that `Precise` clamps, which leaves a reader to
infer the others clamp too. They do not, and a consumer choosing a never-panic strategy is choosing this.

## Not changed

`mock/crates/arvo/README.md.tmpl` was on this list and comes off it. The summary describes the crate's
structure, what it re-exports and where the storage primitives live, and makes no claim about which
arithmetic has shipped. There was nothing stale to correct, so it is left alone rather than edited to
show the round touched it.

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl` describe the workspace rather than a crate contract,
and neither statement here changes a principle.

The repo-root `README.md` is a separate concern with its own set of corrections, tracked apart from this
round because it is a public-surface document rather than a crate contract, and it is not phase-gated.

The bit-matrix axis-naming question raised in the topic is deliberately absent. It changes no shipped
behaviour, so it is a design question to settle on its own rather than a correction to land under a
release.
