# Topic: the arms that guard a public surface are on it

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_ratio_coordinate.rs`,
`mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/mod.rs`

## Current state

A unit test lives under `src/`, so it ships in the package and a stranger reads it beside the rendered
documents. `documentation-writing.md` puts it on the same surface as a README and a `///`, which means
two classes are refused in it: the process noun, and the account of what an earlier version got wrong.

The arms landed by the round before this one carry six of the first kind and one of the second, and
the file they were split out of carries two more that predate the split.

```

the_ratio_coordinate.rs:32  one even negative denominator by accident and switched its value assertion off
the_ratio_coordinate.rs:178 the previous matrix would have failed.

the_ratio_coordinate.rs:293 near the top of the type the doubling `is_tie` used to perform left it.

tests/mod.rs:8              These replace four earlier tests that reached a declaration and stopped.
tests/mod.rs:118            2. the identity on the grid, which the old predicate only declared
```

**Line 330 is the hard one.** `round` in its workflow sense is a word this project uses for a ceremony
and a stranger reads as something else or as nothing, and it is inside an `#[ignore]` reason, which is
the one string in a test that gets printed to whoever runs the suite.

The rest are a reader being told that something here was recently wrong. That is a confession they
cannot act on, and what it tells them is that the author was lately mistaken about the subject they
are being taught.

## What is not lost by removing them

Each of the seven is doing a real job, and the job survives without the narration.

A test comment exists to say what the arm guards and why the case is hard. "This matrix carries both
parities because parity is what decides whether an exact form exists" says all of it. "The previous
matrix carried one by accident" adds only who was wrong.

The catalogued red's reason has to say what is unbuilt and what would build it. It does not have to
say which ceremony declined to.

## What this does not do

**The class is much larger than these two files and the rest is not swept here.** Twenty-five or so
instances sit across `arvo-format` and `arvo-strategy`, in `src/tests/the_inventory.rs`,
`src/tests/the_adaptation.rs`, `src/tests/the_identity.rs`, `src/tests/the_predicate.rs`,
`tests/compile_fail.rs`, `tests/matlab_fi_parity.rs`, `arvo-placement/src/tests.rs` and
`arvo-strategy/src/tests.rs`, plus one `ratified` in a `matlab_fi_parity.rs` ignore reason.

Sweeping them belongs to a round scoped to the class, with the whole surface in its changelist. The
two files here are in scope because one was written by the immediately preceding round and the other
was moved by it, and a file being edited is read whole rather than at the lines that brought you
there.

**A statement about the code keeps its reason.** The distinction the earlier sweep drew is unchanged:
what goes is the process noun and the confession, not the explanation. Several hits in the grep above
are hypothetical rather than historical, of the form "a constant that hardcoded it would have closed a
case", and those are statements about the design and stay.

## Changes proposed

Rewrite the seven so each says what its arm guards without naming a prior state, and take `round` out
of the catalogued reason. No assertion moves and no test name that describes current behaviour moves.
