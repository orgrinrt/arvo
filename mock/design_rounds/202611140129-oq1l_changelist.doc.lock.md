# Doc changelist: the digest rebuilt on the operations its owners already have

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Phase:** DOC CL
**Round:** `202611140129-oq1l`, job `arvo-digest`.
**Topic:** `202611140129-oq1l_topic.digest-on-the-owners.md`.

The first round's design gave `arvo-numeral` a byte and word door of its own and built the digest on
it. This changelist removes that door from `arvo-numeral`'s design, gives `arvo-bits` what the digest
needs from a container (D1), and rewrites the digest's design onto `Bits`, `Bytes` and `Num::mul`, with
the 64-bit FNV-1a and the switched digest waiting on `arvo-format` (D2, D3).

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and `README.md.tmpl`

Remove what round `202611140014-l3t2` added: item 6 of the list of host-type positions, the section on
the door where the host's bytes and words enter, and the readme paragraph naming that door. Nothing is
added: the digest uses `from_index`, `index` and `mul`, which `work/arvo-values` designs and the src
half of this round merges in before it is written.

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-bits/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and `README.md.tmpl`

1. What the crate is names `Bytes<'a>` beside `Bits`.
2. The dependency paragraph says the crate depends on nothing in this workspace and on notko, the
   foundation under the stack, for `Maybe`.
3. Under the type: `xor`, the bitwise exclusive or at one width, a bit operation that never carries,
   and `Bits::<64>::halves`, the door splitting a 128-bit host value into low and high `Bits<64>`.
4. A new section, the byte run: `Bytes<'a>` over `&'a [u8]`, `of` and `host` as its door, and
   `split_first` returning `notko::Maybe<(Bits<8>, Bytes<'a>)>` as the one way to walk it. No length
   question is designed now.
5. What the crate does not ship points hashing at `arvo-digest`. The surface lists the new items.
6. Three laws: `xor` agrees with the host's xor, is its own inverse and has zero as identity;
   `halves` loses nothing over the four placements of set bits; `split_first` yields exactly the
   slice's bytes in order and ends.

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-digest/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and `README.md.tmpl`

1. `Carrier` names `Bits<32>`, `Bits<64>` and `[Bits<64>; 2]`; `raw` returns that; `bits` is
   `Bits::cast`.
2. The input is `arvo_bits::Bytes`; xxhash-rust's result enters through `Bits::masked` and
   `Bits::halves`.
3. `Fnv1a<32>` is described on `Uint<32, Adapt<TowardZero, Wrap>, Quiet>::mul` and `Bits::xor`, with
   the state crossing through `index` and `from_index` and the host type inferred.
4. The 64-bit FNV-1a and the switched digest are described as waiting, each under a `FIXME` citing
   `arvo_format_admits_the_wrapping_product_over_the_unsigned_64`. The crossover's description stays,
   since it is what the switched digest is once it joins.
5. The tests paragraph and the readme follow.
