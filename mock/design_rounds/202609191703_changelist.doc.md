## Canon

Same governing row as the earlier platform-width changelists
(`a_platform_width_type_is_a_target_bound_member_of_the_format_family`). No row changes. Every edit
narrows a sentence to what a test or a probe arm shows; none states anything new about the design.

## `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the laws the applied map owes tests for

The oracle paragraph no longer says the oracle applies "each mode's and each policy's stated meaning".
It says two of the nine readings are taken from the implementation: `Clamp` read as `Saturate`, so the
`Clamp` cells check that collapse and nothing about a clamp to a declared bound, and a `HalfUp` tie read
away from zero, the reading `rounding.rs` documents while `half_up`'s meaning is open. The oracle is
said to be independent of the map in its arithmetic and not in those two readings. The sweep paragraph
adds the second, wider sweep the src changelist commits: distances up to `2^63`, the denominators 1, 2,
3, 4, 7 and `i64::MAX`, one-slot ranges at zero and at both ends of the index, `[0, 2^64 - 1]`,
`[-2^63, 2^63 - 1]`, and the surface at `USize` and `ISize`. Every broken map is reported by each sweep.

## `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the index's host integer

"the `Saturate` policy is the only place it pins anything" becomes the `Saturate` and `Clamp` policies,
which answer alike because the declared signature has nowhere to carry a clamp's bound.

## `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the platform-width points

The paragraph on the leading `::` no longer lists "an `extern crate`" among the shadows the leading `::`
closes, and no longer says the lint "refuses declaring, importing or aliasing anything to the name
`core` anywhere in this crate". It says the leading `::` closes a module and an import named `core`,
does not close an `extern crate ... as core` at the crate root or a manifest rename, and that the lint
refuses three source forms and reads nothing else, naming the manifest rename, a glob import, another
kind of item and a macro-expanded form as not refused by it.

## `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the laws the platform-width points owe tests for

"The same laws are stated over `UFixed<W, 0>` and `Integer<W>` at `W` in 16, 32 and 64" is narrowed to
what the suite asserts: the slot range's count and both ends at every admitted width, the error-word
membership at 16, 32 and 64, and the quantum, phase and magnitude count at the host's width only.

The manifest-rename sentence no longer says the rename needs the renamed crate to re-export the real
`core`'s contents. It says the rename reaches `::core` whenever the path the edition's prelude import
names, `core::prelude::rust_2024` at edition 2024, resolves in the renamed crate, and names the probe's
four outcomes: the real `core` whole, its `prelude` alone and an empty hand-written prelude each build
with the fake value read, and a crate with no `prelude` stops at the prelude import.

The closing paragraph no longer says the tests and the lint cover "every pointer width the ladder admits
against every shadow reached through one of those forms". It says they cover the pointer widths a target
has, 16, 32 and 64, against a shadow declared in this crate's source through one of the three forms.
