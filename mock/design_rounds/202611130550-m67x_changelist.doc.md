## CHANGE: `arvo-placement`, the sole carrier as a type

New `## The sole carrier is also a type`. `derive_sole` answers with a width; a value needs a type.
`SoleCarrier` gives the same answer per slot family as a ladder container, `carrier_agrees` compares it
with the derivation, and `SoleCarrier::ADMITTED` refuses a row that disagrees. Rows for `Signed<W>` and
`Unsigned<W>`, `W` from 1 to 64; the trait is open and an outside row owes `ADMITTED`.

Why it sits here, which is the check's section 3 on the value crate: the carrier table was the one part
of that crate that is a second home for something an existing crate owns. Which container a slot family
takes at sole occupancy is the placement's question; a table elsewhere kept equal to `derive_sole` by a
test over there is a second derivation. Here it is one derivation in two forms. The container is a type
of this crate and no host integer, so the crate still holds no host primitive, and no operation reads a
value through it.

`## The laws this crate owes tests for` gains the agreement of the two forms at all 128 rows, the
outside wrong row reported beside a right one, and the refusal as a compile-fail doctest on `ADMITTED`
beside a passing twin. `## The surface this design names` gains `SoleCarrier` and `carrier_agrees`.

## CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, the admission

`## What a value signature admits` is rewritten. `Sole` is implemented over every declared signature
whose slot family has a `SoleCarrier` row; `Sole::ADMITTED` checks what the signature declares: the
family's own `Slots::ADMITTED` and its row's `SoleCarrier::ADMITTED`, the whole signed or unsigned range
of the width, a policy declaring wrapping or saturation, a mode that reads no dither, a zero phase and a
constant quantum. `ValuePolicy` and `ValueRounding` leave the design. The refusal table moves `Clamp`,
`Stochastic` and a partial range to `Sole::ADMITTED`, and widths 0 and past 64 to the missing
`SoleCarrier` row. It says the price: a refusal by `ADMITTED` is a failed constant at the consumer's line
rather than a missing impl, as `arvo-format`'s `Slots` already pays.

The seal kept, with the reason: `Machine` stays sealed to the host's plain integers, since the bit copy
is sound only where every bit pattern is a value, and these are what the host offers rather than
instances of a contract the canon opens. `Held` is private for the same reason.

## CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, how the value holds its carrier

`## How the value holds its carrier` is rewritten. `Num<S: Sole, C: DebugCheck = Abort>` stores the
unsigned machine integer of the container `SoleCarrier` names, through `Held`. The sign is the slot
family's, `Sole::SIGNED`, read where an operation dispatches. `Carried`, its 128 rows and its
check against `derive_sole` leave the design; the placement holds that check now. The routes a consumer
is refused stay the same, with `Held` in place of `Carried`.

`## Where the host integers are named` names `Held` and its four rows in place of the carrier map, and
the dispatch by the carrier's size and the slot family's sign.

## CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, the debug check

`## The overflow markers` becomes `## The debug check`. `DebugCheck`, open, with `ABORTS`; `Abort` the
default and `Quiet` beside it, as `Num`'s second parameter. `Wrapping` and `Saturating` leave the
design, and `Wrap` and `Saturate` state the adaptation only.

The vehicle, chosen by what a consumer with no feature line can use and what keeps one signature one
behaviour: a type parameter with a default. The sketch `202611130550-m67x_the-debug-check-axis` builds
both. Under a feature a crate that names no feature has its sum complete once another crate in the
build turns the feature on (`outputs/app-both.txt`), and a consumer with no feature line cannot choose.
Under the parameter each choice is in the type its consumer spelled, and the default costs a consumer
that wants the abort nothing.

## CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, the aliases

`## Aliases` is rewritten. `Uint<BITS, A, C>`, `Int<BITS, A, C>`, `USize<A, C>` and `ISize<A, C>`, `C`
defaulting to `Abort`, `A` with no default and read through `NamesAdaptation`, which every `Adapt<R, O>`
implements for itself. The host doors are written on the value type over any adaptation rather than on
the alias.

How the strategy default is added without breaking a consumer: once `Warm`'s adaptation for integers is
claimed, the strategy implements `NamesAdaptation` by its own selection and `A` gains `= Warm`. The
sketch `202611130550-m67x_an-alias-that-takes-a-strategy-later` builds one consumer source, unedited,
against today's alias and the later one, and shows a consumer written against an alias without the
projection refused by the later one with E0277, which is why the projection ships now. The impl is for
`Adapt<R, O>` and not a blanket, so a strategy marker's impl overlaps nothing. The sketch has the value
crate depend on `arvo-strategy`; the direction is the map's open question and the design does not
settle it here.

## CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, the operations, the identity, what is proved

`## The operations`: the rung the dispatch takes is signed where the slot family is, so the order read
through it is the members'. The hand-written traits bound neither `S` nor `C` beyond the value's needs.
`## Identities`: `Num<S, C>` for every `S` and `C`.
`## What is proved, and how`: the bands run under `Quiet`; the debug check's tests replace the plain
markers' ones, `Abort` aborting at `add` and `adapted` under both policies, `Quiet` completing, `Abort`
quiet on what fits, and the two checks holding one member in one size. The compile-fail list gains the
outside slot family over part of a range refused beside one over the whole range, and the outside
policy that clamps refused beside one that wraps; `Held` replaces `Carried`. The consumer reaches
`DebugCheck` and implements `NamesAdaptation` and `DebugCheck` once each.

## CHANGE: the registry, what a datum of a format stands for

Two independent reads of `question::what_a_datum_stands_for` agree on its first option for formats: the
first read's `proposal::a_datum_of_a_format_is_one_point_of_its_ambient_domain`, and a cold second read
that derived before reading the first, committed as
`mock/research/202611130520_datum-a-cold-second-read.md`. Their intersection is ratified by experts in
one new ruling, `ruling::a_datum_of_a_format_is_one_point_and_a_type_may_hold_one_at_sole_occupancy`:
a datum of a format is one point of its ambient domain, a member of the representable set (a saturating
top is one point; what maps to it is the adaptation's fibre, and adaptation is not part of identity), and
a type may hold a datum as a value at sole occupancy. Its `promotion` names both reads. Its `gap` names
what neither reached or the two did not agree on, which stays the panel's: a composition's datum, values
at shared placements, what is stored (slot or pattern), one canonical encoding, the count and crate of
value types, the constructor-level option, and soundness against bestness.

The question row gains `answered` for the format arm only, worded so the open arms read open. It carries
no answer edge, because an edge would claim the whole question. The value proposal keeps `one_expert`;
its note says its `defines = "value"` reaches past its gap and that what it defines is a value type at
sole occupancy. The first read's other nineteen rows keep `one_expert`.
