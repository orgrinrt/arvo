# Src changelist: the two-limb division is a limb at a time

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** `202611132142-jb7q`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611132142-jb7q_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/wide.rs` and `src/wide/tests.rs`.

## CHANGE: `by_two_limbs`

The bit-serial loop is replaced by the normalised long division: the divisor shifted to its top bit, the
dividend into five limbs, three quotient limbs each estimated from the top two limbs over the divisor's
top limb and corrected against its low limb, then a multiply and subtract that does not borrow, and the
remainder shifted back. Its doc comment carries the argument that the add-back step is unreachable at two
limbs. `floor_divided`'s doc comment names the long division in place of "one bit at a time".

## CHANGE: the test

`src/wide/tests.rs` gains a sweep against `tests::big::Big::floor_over`: drawn four-limb dividends of both
signs over divisors at every normalisation shift from 0 to 63, the divisors `2^64`, `2^64 + 1`, `2^127`,
`2^128 - 1` and `2^128 - 2^64`, and dividends of a `B - 1` quotient limb times the divisor plus the
largest remainder, each quotient multiplied back.
