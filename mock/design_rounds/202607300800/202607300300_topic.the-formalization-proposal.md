# Topic: proposal, arvo's primitives become named compositions over a formalization

**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Status:** the agent's full proposal, put for op's agreement point by point. Nothing here is decided.
Rests on `202607300200_research.the-strategy-axes-against-the-field.md` and on D38, D39 and D47.

The research established that the field's axis decomposition is settled and that arvo's is incomplete,
and that the theory's own formalizations are more general than any of the industrial standards. This is
the proposal for what to do with that: stop shipping four numeric families with a preset marker, and
ship one formalization whose instances carry semantic names.

## The core observation the proposal rests on

Flocq, the Coq formalization underlying CompCert's floating-point reasoning, does not treat fixed point
and floating point as different subjects. One predicate covers both:

> `generic_format β fexp`, where a real number is in the format when it is left unchanged by truncating
> the significand associated to its canonical exponent, and `cexp β fexp x = fexp (mag β x)`.

The four standard formats are four choices of the exponent function. `FIX` is a constant exponent.
`FLX` is `e - prec`. `FLT` is `max(e - prec, emin)`. `FTZ` is flush-to-zero. So the difference between
a fixed-point number and an IEEE float is the value of one function parameter.

Rounding is the same shape: not a mode from a closed list, but a function `rnd : ℝ → ℤ` (`Ztrunc`,
`Zfloor`, `Zceil`, `ZnearestE`) whose lawfulness is carried by a `Valid_rnd` typeclass.

That is the mechanism this stack already believes in, arrived at independently by people formalizing the
mathematics: a function, plus a class asserting the law it obeys, so illegal combinations fail to
resolve.

## The proposed layering

Five layers, each depending only on the ones below it.

**Layer 0, the number systems.** Already settled as `arvo-num-systems` by D38, depending on the algebra
contracts by D39. The sets a value can inhabit: ℕ, ℤ, ℚ, ℝ, ℂ, ℍ, 𝕆, surreal, hyperreal, p-adic.
Membership means inhabits rather than equals.

**Layer 1, algebraic structure.** `arvo-algebra-contracts` per D22, populated to whatever depth the
mathematics goes per D47. Two kinds of declaration, and keeping them apart is what makes the rest work:
the structures (Magma, Semigroup, Monoid, Group, AbelianGroup, Ring, Field, FiniteField, Lattice,
BoundedLattice, and MV-algebra, which turns out to matter below), and the **laws as separate markers**
(`Associative`, `Commutative`, `Monotone`, `Idempotent`, `Distributive`, `Invertible`).

**Layer 2, the format.** New, and the heart of the proposal. A `Format` names which values of a number
system are representable. Parameterised by radix, by signedness, and by an exponent function. Instances:
`Fix<F>` with a constant exponent, `Flx<PREC>`, `Flt<PREC, EMIN>`, `Ftz<..>`, and any exponent function
a consumer writes. This is one concept where arvo currently has four families.

**Layer 3, the landing policies.** An exact arithmetic result is generally not in the format, and it can
fail to be in two independent ways: too precise, or too large. Both are maps from the exact value back
into the format, which is why they are one shape and not two subjects.

| Policy | Answers | Instances |
|---|---|---|
| `Rounding` | the result is too precise | `Trunc`, `Floor`, `Ceil`, `NearestEven`, `NearestAway`, `Convergent`, `Stochastic` |
| `Overflow` | the result is too large | `Wrap`, `Saturate`, `SaturateSym`, `SaturateZero`, `WrapSignMagnitude`, `Trap` |
| `Growth` | how much room an intermediate gets | `FullPrecision`, `KeepLsb`, `KeepMsb`, `Specify` |

`Rounding` is the axis arvo does not currently have at all. `Overflow` is the axis it has with two of
the field's five values. `Growth` is the axis currently buried inside the strategy markers, and D34
already had to prise it out by necessity to establish what `Warm` means above 64 bits.

**Layer 4, composition, and laws derived rather than asserted.** A concrete numeric type is a
composition of the layers below: a number system, a format, a signedness, a rounding, an overflow, a
growth. The law markers are then **computed from the composition** rather than declared per type:

| Composition | Structure it is | Laws |
|---|---|---|
| `Wrap`, any signedness | ℤ/2ⁿℤ, abelian group; a commutative ring with multiplication | associative, commutative, invertible |
| `Saturate`, unsigned | truncated addition, commutative monoid | associative, commutative, monotone |
| `Saturate`, unsigned, UNORM encoding | a **finite MV-chain**, the standard Łukasiewicz structure | as above, plus the MV operations |
| `Saturate`, signed, two-sided | monotone commutative unital magma on a bounded lattice | commutative, monotone, **not associative** |

So `Monoid<Add>` resolves for the first three and not the fourth, and a fold over a two-sided saturating
type fails to compile rather than returning a grouping-dependent answer. That is the whole point of the
exercise, and it is a guarantee no industrial fixed-point system offers.

The MV-chain row is worth its own sentence, because it closes something the round left open. The
standard MV-algebra is `[0, 1]` with `¬x = 1 − x` and `x ⊕ y = min(1, x + y)`, and finite Łukasiewicz
chains are its finite forms. arvo's `UFixed<0, F>` under the UNORM encoding, dividing by `2^F − 1` so
all-ones lands exactly on one, **is** such a chain; under arvo's own `2^F` encoding it is not, because
one is not representable. D36 recorded that the identity impl is incomplete and that the encoding is an
unnamed parameter. This says what the two encodings actually are: one of them is a structure with a
name and a literature, and the other is a half-open interval that is not.

**Layer 5, semantic names.** Everything arvo ships today becomes a named instance of a specific
composition. `UFixed<I, F, S>` and `IFixed<I, F, S>` are aliases for `Fix` compositions.
`FastFloat` and `StrictFloat` are aliases for `Flt` compositions. `Hot`, `Warm`, `Cold` and `Precise`
are documented as **presets** over (growth, overflow, container) rather than as an axis, which is what
the research showed they already are.

Per D48 the public spelling does not change: `UFixed<13, 3, Warm>` keeps reading exactly as it does now,
and the composition it expands to is internal.

## Where this goes beyond the standards, which was the explicit ask

1. **Law survival is tracked in the type.** SystemC hands a consumer `SC_SAT` and says nothing about
   associativity. This refuses the fold instead.
2. **IEEE 754 standardises one format.** `generic_format` covers the family, so arvo ships `FIX`, `FLX`,
   `FLT` and `FTZ` rather than fixed point plus an IEEE special case, and admits an exponent function
   nobody has standardised.
3. **Rounding as an open trait admits what no enum lists**, stochastic rounding being the live example,
   which is a current research staple and appears in none of the four industrial systems.
4. **Number-system membership as a bound.** No numeric library names dyadic-rational inhabitation or
   MV-chain membership. Both are decidable here from the type parameters.
5. **The composition is nameable**, so a consumer can construct a numeric system the field has not
   standardised and still get the laws computed for it.

## Three risks, stated because they are load-bearing

**It contradicts D29.** `arvo-float` was created two days ago on the reasoning that fixed point and
IEEE float are not the same kind of thing. Flocq says they are one thing at different exponent
functions. Under this proposal the crate split survives as packaging (floats carry hardware concerns:
infinities, NaN, and the specials that break membership) but the mathematical claim behind it does not.
That is op's to rule on, and it is the reason this is a proposal rather than a plan.

**The exponent function must not reach type position.** `fexp` computing a canonical exponent at compile
time is exactly the shape `generic_const_exprs` was forbidden for. It has to be an associated type or a
value-position computation, per the cure D2 settled and the sketch at
`202607282100_container-projection-without-gce`. This needs its own sketch before anything is built, and
if it cannot be expressed gate-free the proposal changes shape.

**The composition is a product of six axes.** `arvo-compile-time-last.md` licenses the cost explicitly,
and D47 requires each rung be sketched and benched rather than assumed. The mitigation is that the
composition is a type and the law markers are blanket impls over it, so nothing enumerates the product;
that claim is itself a sketch question.

## Sequencing the agent recommends

The current restructure lands first. It is mostly crate movement, it is nearly fully decided, and it
creates `arvo-num-systems`, `arvo-algebra-contracts` and `arvo-container`, which are the crates this
proposal fills. Folding the two together doubles the blast radius of a round that already spans sixteen
crates.

Then this is the round that opens next, starting with the `fexp` sketch, because the whole proposal
rests on that being expressible.
