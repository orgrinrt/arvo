   `UFixed`, so `arvo-bits` is at least as plausible as `arvo-numeric`.
2. **`Identity`, `Bounded`, `SignedIdentity`**. In `arvo-strategy` today. `202607281547` left open
   whether they move to the algebra crates; D20 sharpens it, since the algebra now holds euclidean
   division and the identity it is built on has a claim to sit beside it.

   plus `logical_width_unsigned` and `logical_width_signed`. Unstated in D2. `arvo-numeric` on
   subject matter.
4. **The const fns** `ufixed_bits`, `ifixed_bits`, `is_fractional`, `width_le_64`. Unstated.

6. **The float helpers** `abs_f32`, `abs_f64`, `sqrt_f32`, `sqrt_f64`. Unstated; `arvo-numeric` by
   subject matter.
7. **`EvenShares`**. A struct rather than a trait, so within the algebra pair it is a concrete and
