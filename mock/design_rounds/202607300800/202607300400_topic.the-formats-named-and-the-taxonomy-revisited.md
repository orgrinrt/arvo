## D50. The format concept unifies, the crate split survives as packaging

**Decision (op, 2026-07-30).** One `Format` concept parameterised by an exponent function, so the
mathematics is stated once. `arvo-float` stays as a crate, because floats carry hardware concerns the
formalization does not name: infinities, NaN, and the specials that fall outside every number system.

D29's placement stands. The reasoning recorded with it, that fixed point and IEEE float are not the
same kind of thing, is replaced: they are one thing at different exponent functions, and the crate
boundary is packaging rather than a claim about the mathematics.

## D51. Law markers are derived by blanket impls over the composition

**Decision (op, 2026-07-30).** The impls compute law membership from the axes rather than declaring it
per type. A new rounding or overflow instance gets its laws worked out rather than asserted.

This is D16's distinction applied: a derived property is computed and cannot lie, so it is a plain safe
impl. Asserting them per composition would make each an `unsafe impl` under D16, which is the wrong risk
class for something mechanically computable.

**Carries a sketch obligation.** Whether blanket impls resolve without overlap across six axes is a real
trait-solver question, and it is the second thing to sketch after the exponent function.

## D52. Compositions are public, semantic names are the default path

**Decision (op, 2026-07-30).** The full composition is nameable and bindable by anyone who wants it,
and the semantic aliases plus the strategy presets are what documentation and examples use.

Same relationship D40 gives `Rect` over `Orthotope`, and the same reasoning D31 used to keep the
ergonomic aliases. `arvo-toolbox-not-policer.md` decides it independently: sealing the composition would
be arvo deciding which combinations a consumer may want.

## The four formats, actually named

op asked where `FIX`, `FLX`, `FLT` and `FTZ` come from and what the real names are. Fetched from
Flocq's own source rather than expanded from the abbreviations, because the assumption that `FLT` means
"float" is wrong in a way that matters.

| Flocq | `fexp` | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `FIX` | `emin`, constant | fixed point. The exponent ignores magnitude entirely |
| `FLX` | `e - prec` | "floating-point format without underflow". Precision always exactly `prec`, exponent unbounded below, no subnormals |
| `FLT` | `max(e - prec, emin)` | "floating-point format with gradual underflow". IEEE 754's model: full precision to `emin`, then subnormals degrading |
| `FTZ` | `if e - prec < emin then emin + prec - 1 else e - prec` | abrupt underflow, flush to zero |

**The axis they differ on is what happens at the bottom of the exponent range.** Overflow is the top of
the range; underflow is the bottom. They are separate axes, and arvo's axis set names neither today.

Neither SystemC nor MATLAB has an underflow axis, because a `FIX` exponent is constant and there is no
range to fall out of. The moment format unifies, underflow becomes real.

### The proposed decomposition, one step past Flocq's own

Flocq names FLX, FLT and FTZ as three formats. They are one format with three underflow policies, and
Flocq bundles them because separate formats make theorems easier to state. That constraint does not
apply here, and op asked for the highest granularity available.

So, with names chosen for meaning rather than brevity, since nothing survives lowering:

```
Format    = Fixed<F>                       // constant exponent, no exponent range
          | Floating<PREC, U: Underflow>   // exponent varies with magnitude

Underflow = Unbounded                      // FLX: no floor, no subnormals
          | Gradual<EMIN>                  // FLT: subnormals, IEEE 754's choice
          | Flushed<EMIN>                  // FTZ: abrupt, flush to zero
```

All four are worth having. `Gradual` is IEEE. `Flushed` is what x86 and ARM actually do with the
FTZ and DAZ flags set, which games and DSP routinely enable, so a consumer can state it in the type
rather than in a comment. `Unbounded` bears directly on op's determinism point, since subnormal
handling is a classic source of platform-dependent behaviour and timing.

| Axis | Values | Status today |

## Calls this reopens, which is what op asked to see

Listed so the reconsideration is deliberate rather than discovered during the changelist.

**`arvo-strategy`'s identity, and possibly its name.** D23 left it holding the strategy axis plus the
container projection. Under this proposal it holds the **policy axes** (rounding, overflow, growth,
underflow) plus the **presets** plus the projection. That is a clearer identity than it has today, not
a muddier one, but the crate is then named after the presets rather than after its contents. Whether it
becomes `arvo-policy` with `Strategy` as one thing inside it is worth deciding rather than inheriting.

**A new crate for the format concept.** The `Format` trait, the exponent functions and the format
instances have no home in the current graph. Its dependencies are the law markers from
`arvo-algebra-contracts` and the width machinery, so it sits at the contracts layer. Provisionally
`arvo-format`. Its relationship to `arvo-num-systems` needs settling: a format determines which values
are representable, and membership is a statement about that set, so one of the two depends on the other
and the direction is not obvious from here.

**D32 and D33, the marker family.** `IntegerLike`, `FractionLike`, `FloatLike` and `BoolLike` were
placed by subject matter. Under the formalization they are **derivable from the format** rather than
asserted markers: a type is integer-like when its format is `Fixed<0>`, fraction-like when `F > 0`. If
they are derivable, D51 says they should be derived, and the placement question changes into whether
they exist at all.

**D45, the representational-limits contract.** Settled as a separate contract for saturation to read.
Under this proposal the representable range is a property of the **format**, computable from it, so the
contract may be a derived impl over the format rather than a declared trait. The decision stands; where
it lives and whether it is declared or derived moves.

**D36, the UNORM encoding.** Answered by this proposal rather than reopened. The encoding is a format
parameter, and the two encodings are different structures: divide by `2^F` and `UFixed<0, F>` spans
`[0, 1)` with no representable one, divide by `2^F − 1` and it is a **finite MV-chain**, the standard
Łukasiewicz structure, whose defining operation is exactly `x ⊕ y = min(1, x + y)`. One of the two has
a name and a literature and the other does not.

**D30, NaN as typestate.** Sharpened rather than reopened. Under the number-system layer, NaN and the
infinities are values that inhabit no set, so the membership machinery already refuses them and the
non-NaN marker is a membership witness rather than a bespoke float concept.

**D34, Warm's 2x headroom.** Consistent and now named: it is `Growth = FullPrecision`. The decision
stands unchanged and gains a vocabulary.

**D2's crate list.** Gains `arvo-format`, and `arvo-numeric` becomes a crate of semantic aliases over
compositions rather than a crate of primitive definitions, since the definitions move to the format
layer. Worth stating explicitly before the changelist treats it as a move of existing types.
