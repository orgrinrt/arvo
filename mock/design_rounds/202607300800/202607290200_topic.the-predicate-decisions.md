# Topic: decisions on the predicate concept

**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Status:** four decisions, all op's, recorded as made.

This carries op's answers on `202607290100_topic.one-predicate-concept-implemented-twice.md`. It is a
separate file because that topic had committed and a committed topic is frozen.

## D15. The predicate is the typestate predicate, and Family 1 is deleted

**Decision (op, 2026-07-29).** The question was posed as open between four shapes and it is not: the
sketches settled it, and Family 1 is dead regardless. The unified predicate is the typestate predicate
those sketches proved, carrying two things:

1. **Its argument list as a type-level hlist**, with `Deref` supplying ordinary `f(a, b)` call syntax
   at any arity and no feature gates. This is Shape F of
   `sketches/202607282230_hlist-arity-dissolution/`, complete and enforced, chosen over the
   arity-free Shape G because both existing callers perform a joint atomic test rather than a chain of
   refinements, and currying would express structure the domain does not have.
2. **Its properties as associated consts**, which is what the closure could never carry and the whole
   reason a typed predicate earns its existence. Verified in
   `sketches/202607290030_typestate-algorithm-selection/`: the discarded algorithm is absent rather
   than cold, and the value is that the property cannot desync from the thing it describes rather than
   any codegen win.

`arvo/src/predicate.rs` is deleted with its two callers rewritten. `IsZeroOf` and its four siblings
survive as the degenerate instance of the same concept, where the predicate has exactly one body and
the property is its name.

## D16. Derived properties are safe. Asserted properties are an `unsafe impl`

**Decision (op, 2026-07-29).** The two are different risk classes and the declaration has to show it.

A **derived** property is computed by the type from itself (`LANE_ALIGNED = N % 4 == 0`). It cannot
lie, needs no contract, and is a plain safe associated const.

An **asserted** property is a promise (`MONOTONE = true`) whose falsehood selects a different
algorithm and returns a **wrong answer** rather than a slow one. That is closer to an `unsafe` contract
than to a tuning knob, so asserting one is an `unsafe impl` carrying a stated contract. From a call
site the two mechanisms are indistinguishable, which is exactly why the distinction has to live at the
declaration where a reviewer sees it.

## D17. The concept lives in notko, on a truth contract shaped like `Cardinal`

**Decision (op, 2026-07-29).** notko is the home. The return-type blocker dissolves the same way the
count question did: notko declares the **contract for a truth value**, exactly as `Cardinal` is the
contract a count type implements per D6, and arvo's `Bool` implements it. The predicate's output names
that contract rather than a concrete type, so nothing in the signature needs bare `bool` and nothing
needs `Bool` to be reachable from notko.

Naming of the truth contract is not settled here. It follows D6's reasoning, which rejected an `-able`
suffix because the type **is** the thing rather than being capable of it.

The pending-obligation note at `notko/docs/202607290100_design.the-predicate-concept-may-belong-here.md`
predates this decision and states the fork as unresolved. It is superseded on the placement question
and should be updated to record the answer when the notko work opens.

## D18. Warm and Precise between 65 and 128 bits is not patched in isolation

**Decision (op, 2026-07-29).** Refusing, as the diagnostic promises, is the honest reading of the two
statements. It is not the action. When three artifacts disagree about a whole width band and no test
covers it in either direction, the answer is to **revisit the architecture rather than pick a patch**.

The container projection is already being rewritten: `202607282100_topic.remove-the-forbidden-feature-gates.md`
migrates `Project<TAG, Sign, BYTES, S>` to typestate, and the bucket table is what changes. The band is
decided there, with the architecture in view, rather than by amending `tag_warm_precise` first and
migrating a patched table afterward.

The test obligation stands either way and is the part that does not exist in any form today.
