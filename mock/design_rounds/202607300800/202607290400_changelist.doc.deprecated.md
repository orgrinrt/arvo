# Doc changelist: the widened restructure and the predicate concept

**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Phase:** DOC
**Status:** living. Not locked. Sections 1 and 2 are applied; sections 3 and 4 are blocked and say why.

Covers the decisions in this round's topic files: D1 to D13 in
`202607281220_topic.the-ndim-and-shape-design.md`, D14 in
`202607290050_topic.the-restructure-widens.md`, and D15 to D18 in
`202607290200_topic.the-predicate-decisions.md`.

D14 set the scope: the round widens rather than splitting into overlapping passes over the same
crates. This changelist is organised by that scope, in its four numbered items.

## Why this is not locked

Item 4, the forbidden-gate migration, is blocked on a decision that D18 deliberately places inside it:
what `Warm` and `Precise` mean between 65 and 128 bits. That band is decided when the bucket table is
rewritten, with the architecture in view, rather than patched first and migrated afterward. Item 3
depends on item 4 for the same reason, because the four new crates include `arvo-capacity` and the
container projection's move to typestate is the same work seen from the other side.

Sections 1 and 2 do not depend on either and are applied now. The changelist stays living until the
band decision unblocks the rest.

## 2. The predicate concept (per-crate, DOC phase)

Implements D15, D16 and D17 on the document side. Source is item 5, which is a src-CL concern.

### `mock/crates/arvo/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

**CHANGE: the `Pred<A>` / `Pred2<A, B>` / `Pred3<A, B, C>` entry.**

Located in the numeric-contract trait list. Currently describes three trait aliases over `Fn`,
blanket-impl'd, with "custom callable types implement `test(&self,...)`", which the shipped supertrait
makes impossible.

Replace with the unified typestate predicate: a named type carrying its argument list as a type-level
hlist and its properties as associated consts, callable with ordinary syntax through `Deref`. Record
that the three aliases are deleted and that the property mechanism, not the naming, is what a typed
predicate is for.

### `mock/crates/arvo-comb/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

**CHANGE: two signatures, `matrix_chain_dp` and `greedy_group`.**

Both currently show `feasible: impl Pred2<..>`. Update both to the unified predicate's bound.

**ADD: what the fresh-accumulator property buys.** `greedy_group`'s documented requirement, that a
well-formed predicate always accepts the first item of a fresh group, is currently prose backed by a
runtime guard. Record that it becomes a property on the predicate type, and that the guard is selected
away when the property holds.

### `mock/crates/arvo-numeric-contracts/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

**CHANGE: the `## Predicate family` section.**

Record that `Predicate` plus its five markers is the degenerate instance of the unified concept, where
the predicate has one body and the property is its name. Record D16: derived properties are safe
associated consts, asserted properties are an `unsafe impl` carrying a stated contract.

**ADD: that the markers currently gate nothing.** `predicates.rs:36` claims they gate which algorithms
accept the wrapper. No algorithm bounds on one. State the gap rather than repeating the claim.

## 4. The forbidden-gate migration (BLOCKED on the D18 band decision)

`202607282100_topic.remove-the-forbidden-feature-gates.md`. Three live `generic_const_exprs` gates in
arvo: `arvo-strategy/src/lib.rs`, `arvo/src/lib.rs`, and `arvo-hash/tests/fnv1a.rs`. The sketch at
`mock/research/sketches/202607282100_container-projection-without-gce/` proves the escape compiles with
no gate of any kind.

The blocker is not the mechanism. It is that rewriting the bucket table requires deciding what `Warm`
and `Precise` resolve to between 65 and 128 bits, which today is `WideBits` in the projection and
forbidden in both the design document and the `on_unimplemented` note. D18 places that decision here
on purpose.

Blast radius, measured: 487 references to `BitsContainerFor` across 34 files. The trait's signature
changes from `<const N: u16, Sign>` to a width-typestate parameter, so this is not a local edit.

**One of the three gates is probably free.** `arvo-hash/tests/fnv1a.rs` carries the gate at line 8 and
contains no const-expression-in-type-position construct of its own: no `[(); EXPR]:` bound, no
`cap_size`, no braced const-fn call in a type argument. It is there because the test consumes the
`arvo` facade and GCE bounds leak through the public API. If that reading holds it drops the moment
the facade's gate does, at no cost. Confirm by building the test with the gate removed once the facade
is migrated, rather than by assuming it.
