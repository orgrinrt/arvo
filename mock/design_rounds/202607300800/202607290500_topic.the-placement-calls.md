## D18b. The notko side splits between the two crates, and this was stated wrongly

**Correction (op, 2026-07-29).** A summary of this round put `Cardinal`, `Length`, `Cons` and `Empty`
in notko itself. They are **`notko-hlist`'s**, which is what D5 and D9 already say: notko-hlist is the
extracted crate and those are its contents, along with the folds `Concat`, `ContainsAll` and the
general `Fold` D13 assigns there.

**notko itself takes the predicates**, per D15 and D17, plus `Enumerator` per D19 below. So the split
is: notko-hlist holds the type-level list and everything that folds over it; notko holds the
properties and the predicate.

Recorded because the wrong version was stated aloud and would otherwise have reached the changelist.

## D19. `Enumerator` goes to notko

**Decision (op, 2026-07-29).** D2 said only that it leaves `arvo-tensor`, on the grounds that a blanket
trait over any `IntoIterator` is not a container concern. It goes to **notko**.

That is consistent with what notko already is: `Enumerator` has no numeric content, names a property a
type can have rather than a mathematical structure, and sits with `Boundable` and `NonZeroable` in the
same family. It also matches the reasoning D6 used to keep `Cardinal` in notko and leave `Natural` to
arvo, which is that notko takes the properties and arvo takes the mathematics.

## D20. The euclidean division family goes to the algebra crates

**Decision (op, 2026-07-29).** `ScalarEuclid`, `EuclidDiv`, `EvenShares` and `EvenSplittable`, today in
`arvo/src/traits/euclid.rs`, go to the algebra crates. The audit called this ambiguous between the
contracts crate and the concretes crate; **it is not ambiguous.** Euclidean division is algebraic
structure, which is exactly what `202607281547` created `arvo-algebra-contracts` to hold.

The contracts sit in `arvo-algebra-contracts` alongside `Semigroup`, `Monoid` and `Combine<Op>`. Any
concrete impls follow arvo's contracts-plus-sibling convention into `arvo-algebra`.

## D21. `Pred` is the typestate predicate, and this is not a new call

**Restated, not decided here.** D15 of `202607290200` already settled it: the family is deleted and
replaced by one named predicate type carrying its argument list as a type-level hlist and its
properties as associated consts. D17 places the concept in notko on a truth contract shaped the way
`Cardinal` is the contract for a count.

It is restated because D2 does not mention the family at all, and reading D2's silence rather than
D15's decision is how it got reported as unplaced. The restructure's account of `Pred` is D15 and D17,
not D2.

## D22. `arvo-algebra-contracts` and `arvo-algebra` are in the graph

**Restated, not decided here.** `202607281547` created both. They were absent from D2's drawing because
D2 predates them, and that absence was carried forward into later summaries as though they did not
exist.

`arvo-algebra-contracts` holds the algebraic structures: `Semigroup<Op>`, `Monoid<Op>`, the ladder
above them to whatever depth is wanted, `Combine<Op>` as the associative half that pairs with the
existing `Identity<Op>`, and now the euclidean family per D20. `arvo-algebra` is the concrete sibling
and exists only if there are concretes to ship.
