# Topic: shapes are N-dimensional, and the rank-limited pieces port onto them

**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** correcting and widening the preceding topic. Still no work, still nothing scheduled.
**Supersedes in part:** `202607281005_topic.arvo-shape-two-dimensional-primitives.md`, which named the
crate `arvo-shape` and scoped it to two dimensions.

## The correction

The preceding topic scoped shape primitives to two dimensions because the consumer that prompted them
is an application shell. That is the wrong shape. **There is no reason to limit this to 2D, and
N-dimensional abstractions are the right form.**

Everything else in that topic stands: arvo owns shapes because it is already free of hardcoded
scalars, layout stays in `lato`, and the split is that arvo declares what a shape is while `lato`
declares the things that use them. Only the dimensionality was wrong.

## What N-dimensional has to mean here

The abstractions serve **theoretical mathematics as readily as concrete spatial arithmetic**. A point
in a 17-dimensional parameter space and a point on a screen are the same construction at different
ranks, and a design that treats the screen case as primary and the general case as an extension will
have hardcoded the wrong thing in a way that is expensive to undo later.

Rank is therefore a parameter, in the same way width already is across arvo. The two-dimensional
instantiation that an application shell consumes is one arity of a general form, not the form itself.

## What already exists and has to port onto it

`arvo-tensor` is the rank-limited piece, and it says so plainly: "fixed-shape tensor wrappers with
type-level capacity: `Array<T, N>` (rank-1), `Matrix<W, N>` (rank-2)". Two ranks, each a separate
type, with no general rank between or above them.

That is a thing to generalise rather than a thing to sit beside. Once the N-dimensional abstraction
exists, `arvo-tensor` ports onto it, and `Array` and `Matrix` become the rank-1 and rank-2
instantiations they always were rather than two independent constructions that happen to look alike.

`arvo-comb`, `arvo-spectral` and `arvo-graph` all build on const-generic structures over arvo
numerics, so they are worth re-reading for the same pattern once the abstraction is settled. This
topic does not claim they need porting; it claims nobody has looked.

## Naming, left open

The preceding topic proposed `arvo-shape`. Whether the N-dimensional form keeps that name, or whether
the shape crate becomes the spatial instantiation of a more general abstraction that is named
separately, is not settled here and does not need to be until the work is scheduled.

## Status

Named, not scheduled, exactly as the preceding topic. Both exist so that the boundary and the
dimensionality are settled before `hökkeli` or `lato` improvises a local version, and so that
`arvo-tensor`'s two ranks are understood as an instance of the general form rather than as the
design.
