## Two branches answered one question and neither answer is whole

The trunk and this branch both rewrote `has_additive_identity` and they disagree about what it asks.

The trunk asks the membership predicate at the value zero, over both coordinates membership
quantifies over: some admitted magnitude and some admitted slot cancel the phase. This branch asks it
at magnitude zero only, and catalogues the rest as a red arm.

**The trunk's reading is the one the canon supports and this branch's is narrower than the row it
cites.** `proposal::membership_of_the_representable_set_is_one_affine_predicate`, ratified through
`ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon`, states the parameterisation as an affine slot function, **a
quantum per magnitude** and a phase. Membership is that predicate over the pair. A predicate over the
pair asked at one fixed member of one coordinate is a different predicate, and this crate's own
`contains` quantifies over both, which is why the two functions disagree about one question.

This branch's `format.rs` module doc already names the failure it then commits: the predicate is
written as `contains` asked where zero would sit, because the alternative, "its own conjunction over
the same coordinates, short one of them", had already happened once. It is short one of them again.
The coordinate it is short is the magnitude.

**Nothing in the branch's reading survives.** Its own catalogued red arm,
`the_identity_survives_a_shrinking_quantum`, asserts exactly what the trunk computes: at radix two
with a step law whose rate is minus one, a phase of one half is one whole step at magnitude one, so
`contains` at slot minus one and magnitude one holds and the identity is on the grid. The arm is the
specification for the repair, and it goes green rather than being rewritten.

## What this branch has that the trunk does not, and it is the reason to consolidate rather than pick

Every coordinate of a format here carries a type this crate owns: `Phase`, `Exponent`, `Magnitude`,
`MagnitudeCount`, `Slot`, `SlotCount`, `Radix`, `Width`, `Bool`. The trunk writes all of them as host
integers and a host `bool`.

`ruling::the_numeric_door_carries_the_coordinate_set_and_the_two_type_bound_is_not_canon` is ratified
and settles which of the two shapes the door has: *"What the door carries out is the coordinate set of
the ratified parameterisation, spelled in types the stack owns."* It reserves the count of types
explicitly, so how many is still open and this branch's answer to that is a proposal rather than a
settled thing. What is not open is that the trunk's ten bare associated constants are the shape the
ruling refused.

So the consolidation is one search, the trunk's, wearing this branch's types. Neither side is
selected and neither intent is dropped.

## The search, stated so the source is a transcription of it

Membership is `phase + slot * quantum(magnitude)`. Zero is a member exactly when

```
∃ m ∈ magnitudes, ∃ i ∈ slots :  phase + i * quantum(m) = 0
```

With `phase = (n / d) * radix^BASE` and `quantum(m) = radix^(BASE + SLOPE * m)`, dividing through by
`radix^BASE` and clearing the denominator leaves

```
i * d * radix^(SLOPE * m) = -n
```

`BASE` leaves the problem entirely, so the intercept moves no answer at any magnitude. The power
scales whichever side keeps both whole. A cancelling slot exists at a magnitude exactly when that
division is exact and the quotient is a value a slot index carries; the identity exists when some
admitted magnitude produces one the slot range admits.

**The two halves are named separately** because a law over the conjunction cannot say which half a
failure came from. `cancelling_slot` answers the first at one magnitude; `has_additive_identity`
conjoins it with the range over the admitted magnitudes.

**The search is bounded and the bound costs nothing.** The running product is carried one domain wider
than the coordinates, and at a radix of at least two it at least doubles per step, so it leaves that
domain within 127 steps and every magnitude past that answers no regardless. A radix below two scales
nothing at all and is answered before the loop rather than inside it.

## The phase coordinate loses a value it was handed, on two writable pairs

`Phase::of` normalises a non-positive denominator by moving the sign to the numerator, and two inputs
have no such normalisation inside the width the coordinates are declared in: a denominator of
`i64::MIN`, and a numerator of `i64::MIN` under a negative denominator. Both fall through to a
denominator of one with the numerator unchanged.

The current rustdoc says so and a test pins it, so this is declared rather than hidden. **It is still
wrong, and it is not cosmetic.** `Phase::of(3, i64::MIN)` names a tiny negative and answers `3/1`;
`Phase::of(i64::MIN, -7)` names a large positive and answers negative. And the trunk has arms over
exactly those pairs: `i64::MIN` over `i64::MIN` divides to a phase of one whose cancelling slot is
minus one and in range, and `i64::MIN` over minus one is a phase of `2^63` whose cancelling slot is
`i64::MIN`. Under this branch's normalisation the first loses its identity and the second answers no
rather than naming the slot. **The merge would have carried a silent regression on both.**

**The invariant the normalisation exists to hold is not needed.** Divisibility, `n mod d = 0`, is
independent of the sign of `d`, and so is the quotient. Nothing else reads the denominator's sign. So
the repair is to stop normalising: hold the pair as it was written, widen every read of it, and refuse
the one denominator that names no position at all.

**The refusal goes where this crate already puts refusals.** `Slots`, `Quantum` and `Ambient` each
carry what an implementor owes as a checked `ADMITTED` const with a verdict-returning form beside it.
`Format` is the fourth open contract here and is the only one without one. A denominator of zero names
no position on the grid, so the phase does not denote and the membership predicate has nothing to be
asked about, which is the same shape as a slot range that inverts or a step law over no magnitudes.

## The ambient domain owes something too, and this branch does not ask for it

The cancellation divides by powers of the radix. At a radix of one every magnitude names the same
value and the scaling is the identity; at zero the quantum is not a number at a negative exponent.
Neither describes a positional notation, and the search's termination argument assumes at least two.

`Ambient` here has no `ADMITTED`. The trunk's does, and it is the same shape as the three this branch
already carries, so it lands rather than being argued over.

## What is a public page and what a stranger may read on it

`documentation-writing.md` puts the rendered `docs/` tree, every `///` and `//!`, and the shipped
tests on the tier-1 surface. Two things do not belong there and both are present:

- **The process noun.** `round`, in its workflow sense, on a surface a stranger reads. Seven
  instances, in three crates: four in this crate's design template, one in a `tests/ui/` arm, one in
  the strategy crate's design template, one in the strategy crate's tests.
- **An account of what an earlier version got wrong**, which
  `the-tells-that-give-away-an-agent-wrote-it.md` names outright: a reader never learns that something
  used to be hardcoded or welded in, because a confession they cannot act on only says the author was
  recently wrong about the subject they are teaching. `until this round every one of them was declared
  with a host integer`, `it had a consequence nobody intended`, `Before it, the coordinate was an
  `i64` and this compiled`.

**The sweep two rounds ago covered `src/` and stopped there**, so it left the class standing in the
design template it was about and in the `tests/ui/` file written in the same body of work. The
distinction it drew stands and is not being reopened: a statement about the code keeps its reason, and
what goes is the process noun and the confession.
