# Src changelist: one search over both coordinates, in the coordinate types

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Round:** the identity search runs over the magnitude, and the coordinates are types
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/`, `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/`

The trunk merge, resolved by hand. Six source files conflict and none of the six is a choice between
two answers: on each one the trunk changed what a function computes and this branch changed what its
coordinates are typed as, and the two are about different things. What lands is the trunk's arithmetic
in this branch's types.

## CHANGE: `has_additive_identity` FROM one magnitude TO the range the quantum declares
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`
Verification: `the_identity_survives_a_shrinking_quantum` loses its `#[ignore]` and passes;
`the_identity_answer_is_membership_asked_at_zero` asserts the predicate against a walk of the declared
magnitude range over a twenty-format matrix.

This branch answered at `Magnitude::SMALLEST` and catalogued the rest. The catalogued arm is the
specification: at radix two under a rate of minus one, a phase of one half is one whole step at
magnitude one, `contains` at slot minus one and magnitude one holds, and the identity is on the grid.
The predicate said no.

**The existential runs over the pair `contains` quantifies over**, which is what makes this the
membership predicate asked at zero rather than a second reading of the coordinates. It walks the
magnitudes the quantum declares, asks for the cancelling slot at each, and answers yes at the first
one whose slot the range admits.

## CHANGE: `cancelling_slot` FROM absent TO the named half of the conjunction
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`
Verification: `cancelling_slot::<F>(magnitude)` answers `Maybe::Is` and `Maybe::Isnt` on constructions
that differ only in the magnitude, and `the_base_exponent_does_not_move_the_cancelling_slot` asserts
the intercept cancels out.

`pub const fn cancelling_slot<F: Format>(magnitude: Magnitude) -> Maybe<Slot>`.

Solving `i * d * radix^(SLOPE * m) = -n` exactly, in integers, one domain wider than the coordinates.
The power scales whichever side keeps both whole, each scaling step is refused before it would leave
the width rather than after, and the quotient is range-checked against what a slot index carries, so
an `Isnt` is always a decided answer and never a wrap.

**Named rather than folded into the predicate**, because a law over the conjunction cannot say which
half a failure came from.

**The search bound is a count of magnitudes and the scaling bound is a count of radix powers, and they
are not the same quantity.** They share the value 127 and are written as two constants, on the same
reading this crate already takes about an index and an extent. At a radix of at least two the running
product at least doubles per step, so it leaves the wider domain within that many steps and every
magnitude past the bound answers no regardless.

## CHANGE: `Phase` FROM a normalised ratio TO the pair as declared
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`
Verification: `the_extreme_phase_coordinates_are_answered_rather_than_overflowing` asserts the
cancelling slot at `i64::MIN` over minus one, over one, and over `i64::MIN`, and
`a_phase_keeps_the_value_it_was_declared_with` asserts the numerator and denominator come back
unchanged on both pairs the normalisation used to lose.

`of` normalised a non-positive denominator by moving the sign to the numerator, and two writable pairs
have no such normalisation inside the declared width: a denominator of `i64::MIN`, and a numerator of
`i64::MIN` under a negative denominator. Both fell through to a denominator of one.

**The answer moved on both.** `i64::MIN` over `i64::MIN` is a phase of one whose cancelling slot is
minus one and in range, and the normalisation turned it into a phase of `-2^63` with no identity.
`i64::MIN` over minus one is a phase of `2^63` whose cancelling slot is `i64::MIN` exactly, and the
normalisation turned it into one whose cancelling slot is `2^63` and out of reach. **Both are arms on
the trunk**, so the merge would have carried the regression rather than the fix.

The invariant bought nothing: `n mod d = 0` and `n / d` are both independent of the sign of `d`, and
nothing else reads it. So the pair is held as declared, `is_whole_multiple` computes one domain wider
because `i64::MIN % -1` overflows in the declared one, and the zero denominator is refused by the
contract rather than read as one.

## CHANGE: `Format` FROM three open contracts with an obligation TO four
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`
Verification: `is_admissible_format::<NoDenominator>()` is false and it is true for all four shipped
points; a `compile_fail` doctest on `Format::ADMITTED` refuses a zero denominator and the control at a
denominator of two builds.

`Slots`, `Quantum` and `Ambient` each state what an implementor owes as a checked const with a
verdict beside it. `Format` had none, and a phase denominator of zero names no position on the grid.

The refusal is a doctest rather than a `trybuild` case because the obligation is a const evaluated at
monomorphisation and `trybuild` runs `cargo check`, which skips codegen.

## CHANGE: `Ambient` FROM a radix with no bound TO a radix of at least two
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/ambient.rs`
Verification: `is_admissible_ambient` is false at `Radix::of(0)` and `Radix::of(1)` and true for the
three shipped domains; a `compile_fail` doctest refuses a unary domain through `radix` and the control
at radix two builds.

The cancellation divides by powers of the radix and the search's termination rests on the product
growing, and neither holds below two. `Radix` gains `is_positional`, so the condition is written over
the coordinate once and read twice, as the obligation and as the verdict, which is the shape
`Quantum` already carries.

## CHANGE: the quantum verdict FROM `is_admissible` TO `is_admissible_quantum`
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/quantum.rs`, `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/lib.rs`
Verification: `crate::quantum::is_admissible_quantum` resolves and no `is_admissible` remains in
`quantum`.

Three of the four verdicts are re-exported at the crate root and cannot share one name there. `Slots`
keeps the unqualified `is_admissible` because it is not re-exported, which is the whole of the
asymmetry.

**The quantum contract itself is unchanged and keeps both its conditions.** The trunk's carries one,
that the law ranges over a magnitude; this branch's carries that and the reach condition, that the
exponent at the largest admitted magnitude is one an `Exponent` holds. The wider one lands, and the
design paragraph that called the reach unaddressed is corrected in the same round rather than left
describing a crate that now checks it.

## CHANGE: `standards` FROM host integers TO the coordinate types
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/standards.rs`, `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/matlab_fi_parity.rs`
Verification: `cargo test --workspace` runs the fourteen parity arms and the two refusal arms, and
`FractionLength`, `Fi` and `Ufi` name no host type in any coordinate position.

`FractionLength<F>` declares `BASE` as `Exponent::of(-F)`, `SLOPE` as `Exponent::ZERO` and
`MAGNITUDES` as `MagnitudeCount::ONE`; `Fi` and `Ufi` declare `PHASE` as `Phase::ZERO`. The negation
stays an expression in an impl body rather than a const generic argument, which is the reason the law
is a type of its own and is unaffected by the coordinate carrying a type.

**This is the arm that says an outside convention can be declared at all.** It is the one shipped
member written from the coordinate set rather than from `points`, so it is where the door's shape is
exercised rather than described.

## CHANGE: the identity suite FROM the shipped points TO the coordinate matrix
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/tests/the_identity.rs`
Verification: the file carries a `Grid` format with every coordinate free and asserts the law over
radix, slope sign, slot family, phase denominator and magnitude count; both verdicts occur in every
sweep and each sweep carries the control that says so.

The trunk's arms land in full and the branch's stay. Neither set covers the other: the branch's carry
the wide-intermediate mutant and the superseded predicate as runnable controls, the trunk's carry the
axes the shipped points hold fixed, and a shipped point cannot reach a denominator other than two, a
slope other than zero or one, or a radix other than two.

`Grid` takes the phase as two const generic parameters and builds a `Phase` in the impl body, which is
the same construction `FractionLength` uses for its exponent and the reason the coordinate carrying a
type costs a sweep nothing.
