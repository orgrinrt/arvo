# Doc changelist: the constructor claim goes, the design under it stays

One sentence in `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, and the regeneration.

## `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

The paragraph opening `**The phase is one coordinate and one const.**` currently
reads, in part:

> `Phase` carries the numerator and the denominator as a pair, its constructors
> cannot produce a zero denominator, and the condition is a property of the type
> rather than a sentence in a doc comment asking for one.

It becomes:

> `Phase` carries the numerator and the denominator as a pair rather than as one
> host integer, so the two halves never have to be unpacked in a const, and the
> predicates below are total over the pair, including the zero denominator that
> names no position.

The clause about a doc comment goes with it, because the source's own doc comment
above `of` is exactly such a sentence and the design was contrasting itself with
something it does.

## Regeneration

`cargo mock`, with `docs/FORMAT.md` committed alongside.

## Verification

`grep -c 'cannot produce a zero' mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl docs/FORMAT.md`
returns zero for both, against a control of one on each before the edit.

And the claim itself, which is what made this a round: `Phase` has two
constructors, `format.rs:73` and `format.rs:79`. The first stores both arguments
unchanged. So `Phase::of(1, 0)` compiles and holds a zero denominator, which is
what the removed clause said could not happen.

## Not in scope

Whether `of` should refuse a zero denominator at const time. That is a canon
question, it is on the agenda, and this round neither answers it nor depends on
the answer.
