**Round:** `202611191340-whma`, job `arvo-stranded`.

## What the docs say

The digest crate was designed and built on `work/arvo-digest` in rounds `202611140014-l3t2` and
`202611140316-ys7k`, both closed and both on `dev` as the record (`3d95d776`). Its design,
`mock/crates/arvo-digest/DESIGN.md.tmpl` on that branch, declares `obligation::a_content_hash` and
says no ruling places a digest crate in arvo; op's answer of 13 November (xxhash-rust, BSL-1.0 on the
licence allow list) is the authority it builds on, and it is not a canon row. The source never reached
`dev`: the branch was cut from `work/arvo-values` before that branch's last rounds, and `dev` has since
taken the value tier from `work/arvo-values-next` (`d0161800`).

The branch held FNV-1a at 64 bits and the switched digest back on ticket
`arvo_format_admits_the_wrapping_product_over_the_unsigned_64`: `Uint<64>`'s wrapping `mul` was refused
at build. On `dev` that product is admitted (`d0161800`, closed ticket
`value_s_product_admits_the_unsigned_64_bit_wrapping_product`), so the first of the two holds no longer.
