## CHANGE: a private four-limb integer for the product's numerator

Files: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/multiplication/wide.rs`, new, declared `mod wide` in
`src/multiplication.rs`; its suite `src/multiplication/wide/tests.rs`, new.

`Wide` is sign and a magnitude of four `u64` limbs, zero never negative. `of(i128)`, `times`, `plus`
and `power(base, exponent)` (by squaring, a square formed only while a higher bit is left) answer `Isnt`
where the result leaves the four limbs; `floor_divided(u64)` is a long division of four limbs by one
with the floor and a remainder from zero up to the divisor; `index()` narrows to `i128` or answers
`Isnt`. Nothing outside `multiplication` sees it. Its suite checks every operation against the host's
128-bit arithmetic over a list through both signs, the limb boundary at `2^64` and the ends of the
index, and the four-limb edge on both sides: `2^255` and `10^77` held, `2^256` and `10^78` not, and
`2^256 - 1` plus one leaving.

## CHANGE: the product step over any phase and radix of a constant quantum

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/multiplication.rs`.

`Multipliable::<F>::STEP` is `step::<F>()`: the phase reduced to `n / d` with `d` positive, the radix
`r` and exponent `e`, `D = d^2 r^max(-e, 0)` when it is from one to `2^63 - 1` and `Isnt` otherwise,
`r^max(e, 0)` as a `Maybe<Wide>`, and the phase's term `n d r^max(-e, 0)`. `placed` forms
`(n + a d)(n + b d) r^max(e, 0) - n d r^max(-e, 0)` in `Wide` and floors it over `D`; a numerator past
the limbs or a floor past the index is `Placed::Past` on the position's sign. `product_position` forces
`ADMITTED` and turns the placement into an `Exact`, the remainder reduced by the greatest common
divisor, a past position saturated on its sign. `ADMITTED` carries four asserts in order: the phase
denotes; a constant quantum, with
`FIXME(arvo_product_over_a_magnitude_indexed_quantum_is_a_placehold)`; `STEP` held, with
`FIXME(arvo_s_exact_position_carries_no_remainder_denominator_past)`; the four corner positions held,
each one slot past its floor where its remainder is not zero, with
`FIXME(arvo_s_product_over_the_unsigned_64_bit_range_needs_a_slot_c)`. The last three messages say
not yet. `is_multipliable` asks the same four without forcing. The phase and radix refusals, the
`-62` exponent constant and the checked `i128` product are gone. `unforced_position`, test-only,
reads the step without forcing for the edge sweep. `Mul`, `product_position`, `mul` and
`is_multipliable` keep their names and signatures.

## CHANGE: the product's suite moves to the new conditions and widens

Files: `src/multiplication/tests/mod.rs`, `the_step.rs`, `the_oracle.rs`, `the_laws.rs`, and
`the_edges.rs`, new.

`mod.rs`: `quantum` reads the radix, `value` is `(phase + slot) * q`, and `exact_product` is the two
values' product over `q` less the phase. `the_step.rs`: the denotation keeps its 36 binary formats and
gains eleven at a phase or radix ten, with the slots'-product control wrong somewhere and never where
the quantum is one and the phase zero; the largest remainder at `2^-62`, `10^-18`, a phase over
`3037000499` and a half at `2^-60`; a phased floor; saturation past the index and past the four limbs.
The conditions: a zero phase denominator refused; phases and radix ten admitted; the remainder held at
`2^-62`, `10^-18`, `3037000499` and a half at `2^-60` and not one past each, the phase reduced first;
the carry at `Integer<64>`, `UFixed<64, 0>` out, `UFixed<64, -1>` and `UFixed<64, -62>` in,
`UFixed<63, 1>` in, `UFixed<63, 2>` out, and radix ten at `10^37` in and `10^38` out on a two-bit
range. Check-time consts gain a phased and a decimal product. `the_oracle.rs`: thirteen formats a
width from one to eight, both families at `2^0`, `2^-3`, `2^2` and `2^-1`, two phased binary, two
decimal and one phased decimal; the planted control gains a phase at quantum one. `the_edges.rs`: every
width from nine to 64 of both families at `2^0`, `2^1`, `2^-1` and `2^-62`, the verdict against the
corners read by a host 128-bit oracle and the unforced step at the edge slots against it, three formats
held out, a floor-only control wrong only at a fractional exponent; and the named edges through
`product_position`: `Integer<64>` `MIN * MIN` and `MIN * MAX`, `UFixed<63, 1>` and `UFixed<64, -1>` at
the top, `UFixed<64, -62>`. `the_laws.rs`: the wrapping fractional cell crosses every fraction width
from one to `W` at each width three to eight, signed and unsigned, 66 formats, each diverging.
