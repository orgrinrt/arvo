# The strategy axis, checked against its ten ratified rows

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-strategy/`, `mock/crates/arvo-placement/`
**Source topics:** `topic::the_strategy_axis`

## What this round checks

Ten `ruling` rows carry `topic = "the_strategy_axis"` and `rung = "ratified"`:
`the_strategy_intents_are_not_clear_cut`, `each_preset_names_a_stated_intent`,
`warms_objective_is_the_intuitive_best_choice`,
`hot_may_sacrifice_soundness_for_a_proven_meaningful_gain`,
`cold_is_for_cold_paths_and_cold_storage`, `cold_is_not_obliged_to_be_slow`,
`the_strategies_weigh_measurements_differently`, `cold_is_not_to_be_deprioritised`,
`arms_over_regions_are_the_fundamental_heart` and
`there_is_no_exchange_rate_because_there_is_no_generalisation`.

`mock/crates/arvo-strategy/` already carries a `DESIGN.md.tmpl` and a `src/`
built across several prior rounds. This round checks that design and that
source against the ten rows and fixes what does not match, rather than
starting over: nothing found here calls the crate's shape wrong.

## What was checked, row by row

Read against `mock/crates/arvo-strategy/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and
`mock/crates/arvo-strategy/src/lib.rs`, eight of the ten are already carried
correctly: the per-strategy statements are written as intents rather than a
behaviour table, each preset's rustdoc states the intent the corpus has for
it in close to the corpus's own words, the design states measurement decides
without asserting the disowned agreement clause, and a dedicated test
(`the_storage_binding_is_the_one_that_binds_to_footprint`) checks Cold is the
one preset reaching the footprint objective, which is a checkable encoding of
"not deprioritised."

Two findings, both real, neither about the crate's shape.

### Finding one: the crate claims a value it does not commit that it in fact commits

`DESIGN.md.tmpl` states: "Which overflow mode each preset names is not
written here... The crate carries the axis and the binding; the value is a
defaults question and filling it inside a design is how an open canon
question gets closed where nobody can see it happen."

`src/lib.rs`'s `presets` module assigns a concrete `Adaptation` to all four
presets: `Hot = Adapt<TowardZero, Wrap>`, `Cold = Adapt<TowardZero, Wrap>`,
`Precise = Adapt<HalfEven, Saturate>`, `Warm = Adapt<HalfEven, Wrap>`. That is
the value the design claims is not written. `question::does_warm_wrap_or_clamp`
is open in the registry (`topic = "overflow_policy"`, `decider = "panel"`,
unanswered), and its note records that op has already called the ratified
preset table's `Warm` cell stale. The source silently picks `Wrap` for
`Warm` with no marker naming the open question, which is exactly the failure
`mark-placeholders-fixme.md` and the design's own sentence warn against.

Rust requires a concrete associated type, so the presets cannot ship with no
value at all. The fix is honesty rather than abstention: the design's claim
is corrected to say what is actually shipped (a carried, non-canonical
default per `strategy.toml`'s `standing = "prior_attempt"`), and the source
gets a `// FIXME:` naming the open question by slug at the one cell that is
actually contested.

### Finding two: the ratified "arm" citation sits in the crate shipping no arm, and is absent from the crate shipping the only two

`arms_over_regions_are_the_fundamental_heart` is cited at the top of
`arvo-strategy/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and nowhere else in `mock/crates/`.
`arvo-placement/DESIGN.md.tmpl` names its `derive_sole` /`derive_shared`
pair "two arms" repeatedly, in exactly the ruling's own vocabulary
(a const predicate over `O::OBJECTIVE`, gating a region, the unselected arm
erased before the backend), and cites neither this ruling nor
`the_work_is_predicated_arms_composed`.

`arvo-strategy` supplies the const value (`Strategy::Objective`) that
`arvo-placement`'s arms are gated on; it does not itself contain a match
arm, because its own subject is a binding rather than a computation.
`DESIGN.md.tmpl`'s section heading "What an arm is, since this crate is
what selects them" overstates the crate's own role: the crate names the
concept and supplies the predicate value, and the selection itself happens
one crate down.

Fix: correct the heading and its section to state the division of labour
precisely and point at `derive_sole`/`derive_shared` by name, and move the
ratified citation to the crate that ships the arms, alongside the one that
explains why the crate above it does not yet ship any of its own.

## What this round does not touch

No arm ships in `arvo-strategy` in this round. Shipping one at the strategy
layer needs a measurement establishing a real region where a strategy's own
`Adaptation` should vary by something other than the strategy name (width,
signedness, hardware feature), and no such measurement exists in the corpus
yet. Building one now would be exactly the unjustified generalisation
`there_is_no_exchange_rate_because_there_is_no_generalisation` and
`bench-in-bench-harness-never-sketches.md` both refuse. The crate's existing
abstention, once its own claim about the mechanism is made precise, is the
correct reading of the ratified row rather than a gap in it.

`does_warm_wrap_or_clamp` and every other `overflow_policy` / `rounding`
question stay open. This round marks the one cell that is silently closing
one of them; it does not answer it, since that answer belongs to a
different topic and a different round.
