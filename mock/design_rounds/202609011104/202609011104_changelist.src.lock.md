**Date:** 2026-09-01

**Source doc CL:** `202609011104_changelist.doc.lock.md`

## What this changes

`arvo-strategy/DESIGN.md.tmpl` now says each preset's `Adaptation` is a carried,
non-canonical default rather than a settled answer, and that each is marked
`// FIXME:` at its declaration. Source does not yet carry those markers. This CL
adds them, one per preset impl, naming the open registry question by slug where
one exists (`Warm`, the cell `question::does_warm_wrap_or_clamp` already
disputes) and naming the general reason (carried default, not ratified) for the
other three, which have no open registry row disputing their specific cell but
are equally unproven defaults under the same design sentence.

Also corrects the module-level doc comment (`//!` at the top of `lib.rs`), which
carried the same false "not written here" claim the design document carried,
fixed there in the doc CL and left uncorrected in source until this CL.

No behaviour changes. No type, trait or signature moves. Four `// FIXME:` block
comments inserted immediately above their `impl Strategy for <Preset>` blocks,
plus one module-doc paragraph rewritten.
