# DOC changelist: the guarantee sentence says what is not enforced

The design states a guarantee about its own obligations that is false on both
halves, and it never says which functions force which obligation, which is the
whole observable content of the guarantee. Two readings of the canon, blind to
each other and separately dispatched, found the same thing; both are merged as
`#99` and `#100`.

This changelist corrects statements to what is enforced. **It chooses no new
locus and closes no hole**, because the routes that reach a binary are a design
decision the canon explicitly leaves to this tier, and that decision goes
through the two seats arguing against each other rather than being taken here.

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

### The claim, as it stands

Two occurrences, `:676` and `:728`:

> The guarantee is that an inadmissible declaration cannot reach a produced
> binary, and it can reach a passing check.

### Why it is false, both halves, measured rather than argued

**`Format::ADMITTED` is forced at exactly two sites crate-wide**, `format.rs:322`
and `:398`, established by `grep -rn "as Format>::ADMITTED" mock/crates/` and
read here rather than taken from either seat's report.

**An inadmissible declaration reaches a produced binary.** Three routes, each
measured under both `cargo check` and `cargo build` by seat B across eighteen
probe arms with controls, and one of them readable without a probe:
`contains` at `format.rs:250` is the crate's own spelling of the ratified
membership predicate, forces nothing, and answers for a format whose phase does
not denote. The other two are reading `PHASE` off the impl, and an implementor
writing `const ADMITTED: () = ();` to disarm the default.

**And `cargo check` does refuse**, from a const call site, which is the other
half of the same sentence and is false in the opposite direction. The same
document already states the mechanism correctly at `:255` and applies the right
reasoning to `adapt` at `:801`, so this is an internal contradiction rather than
a gap.

### What replaces it

**The guarantee stated as its extension rather than as a universal.** The
sentence says what forces what, names the two sites, and names the routes that
reach a binary without passing through them. A claim of totality names what
enforces it or it does not get written, and this one could not have named
anything, because the set was never in the document.

**The reproduction test is what this is really about.** A design is good enough
when two implementers produce working implementations of the same thing, and
the set of forcing sites is not recoverable from this document as it stands, so
an implementer reading it would not reproduce the enforcement it describes.

### What this changelist does not do

**It does not close the three routes.** `ruling::the_canon_does_not_police_what_shape_a_law_takes`
says the canon will not choose the shape a law takes, so the choice belongs to
this tier, and the two seats propose different things: one reports a residue
with no fix available, the other measures a blanket impl closing the disarm at
the same verbs and a const-generic denominator as the only shape reaching the
other two. **Two proposals that disagree are not a decision**, and the next unit
resumes both seats against each other rather than picking one here.
