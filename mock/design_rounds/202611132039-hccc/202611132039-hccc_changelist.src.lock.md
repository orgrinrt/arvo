# Source changelist: the quotient, the conversion and the door at a host width

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** 202611132039-hccc, branch `work/arvo-values`.
**Design:** `202611132039-hccc_changelist.doc.lock.md`, and under it
`mock/crates/arvo-numeral/DESIGN.md.tmpl`.

## Current state

A value has `add`, `sub`, `neg` and `mul` and their verdicts, the crossing to a sibling signature, and
the doors `constant`, `adapted` and `slot`, with `from_host` and `host` on the pointer-width aliases.
It has no quotient, no door onto another format, and takes a computed number only through `adapted`.
`src/num.rs` is at the size where a file wants splitting, so nothing more goes into it.

## Changes proposed

### `arvo-numeral`

`src/ratio.rs`, new: `div(by)` and `fits_div(by)` over `Divisor<S::Format>`, `convert::<T>()` and
`fits_convert::<T>()` for `T: Sole`. Each operation calls the format's own function on slots under
`FIXME(arvo_numeral_s_fixed_point_product_goes_through_the_128_bit)`, and each verdict asks
`panic_on_overflow` of the format's position. The mutants `mutant-div` and `mutant-convert` sit in
the two operations behind `cfg!(feature = ...)`.

`src/host.rs`, new: the trait `HostInteger`, sealed by its supertrait `Machine` and implemented for
`i8` to `u64`, `isize` and `usize`; `from_index::<H>(host)`, `holds_index::<H>(host)` and
`index::<H>()`. The host integer is widened to a 64-bit pattern with two flags for the cross-sign
cases, and the completion is the 64-bit rung's own `adapt_s64` and `adapt_u64`, so the door has no
second adaptation in it. `index` asserts in a constant that the slot range is inside `H`'s. The
mutant `mutant-index` sits in the saturating arm for an unsigned range.

`src/machine.rs`: `Machine` gains the associated constant `SIGNED`, which the widening and `index`
dispatch on beside the size.

`src/num.rs`: `at_index` and the alias `SlotsOf` become visible to the crate, for the two new modules.
Nothing else in it changes.

`src/lib.rs`: the two modules, and the re-exports of `HostInteger` and of `arvo_format::Divisor`.

`Cargo.toml`: the features `mutant-div`, `mutant-convert` and `mutant-index`.

`tests/the_quotient.rs`: `div` and `fits_div` against the format's `div` and `quotient_position`, at
widths 1 to 4 over every member divisor but zero and at widths 5 to 8 over the named set, both
families, both policies, five modes, every dividend; the two fixed-point formats at exponent `-3`.

`tests/the_conversion.rs`: `convert` and `fits_convert` against the format's `convert` and
`converted_position`, over the pairs of formats the design names, and `convert` against `under` on
one format.

`tests/the_host_door.rs`: `from_index` against `adapted` and `holds_index` against
`panic_on_overflow` over the host values the design names, at widths 1 to 8 and on the lattice above;
`index` returning what `from_index` was given.

`tests/the_debug_check.rs` and its release twin: `div`, `convert` and `from_index` under `Abort` and
under `Quiet`.

`tests/ui/`: `index::<u8>()` on a signed 8-bit integer beside `index::<i8>()`, and on a 9-bit
unsigned integer beside `index::<u16>()`, each refusal with its diagnostic. One pair more than the
design lists, for the seal it states: `from_index` of a 128-bit integer beside a 64-bit one.

`tests/consumer/`: a quotient by a constant divisor with its verdict, a conversion between an integer
and a fixed-point signature with its verdict, and the three host functions, with a function generic
over `HostInteger`.

`mock/research/sketches/202611132039-hccc_value-mutants/`: the red runs of the three mutants, with
what each shows. The 64-bit rung's mutant `mutant-64` removes the adaptation the host door calls, so
it turns the door's tests red as well, which is that mutant's own claim at one more caller.
