## `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

"The exact position": the carry paragraph stops saying the constructor saturates and stores a
different position. A carry past the end of the index pins the slot there and keeps the distance past
it, zero elsewhere and at most the carry. The two worked examples become what is stored and what the
map answers: `between(MAX, 9/4)` under `Ceil` and `Wrap` over a range ending at `MAX` wraps to two above
the lowest slot; `between(MIN, -7/4)` under `Floor` is out of range and a range starting at `MIN` wraps
it to 254 above its lowest slot. `slot()` answers the pinned slot. The sentence placing the constructor
on the index section's saturating list goes.

"The index's host integer": the saturating list is `Reach` and the addition step, with why the verdict
is right there (every range addition admits lies within `[-2^126, 2^126)`) and a pointer to the addition
section for what the wrap costs. `Exact::between` is taken off the list. The paragraph on the applied
map adds the distance: rounding reads the named slot's sign and parity from the pinned slot and the
distance, a position past the top is out of every range, one past the bottom is in range only at the
one step onto the index's lowest slot where the range starts there, the wrap reduces the distance with
the other two terms, and saturation pins to the end a position lies past.

"The laws the platform-width points owe tests for": the sentence saying a `cargo check` at a foreign
target type-checks assertions without evaluating them goes, and with it the one about a library check
at a 16-bit target. In their place: a `#[test]` arm runs only at its own target, a const assertion
would be evaluated by any build at that target, and this repository builds at the host only. The
lint paragraph states what the lint does, exactly: the declaration up to its semicolon across lines,
comments and whitespace dropped, the right-hand side compared against `UFixed<{usize::BITS},0>` and
`Integer<{usize::BITS}>`, a missing alias a finding, and what it does not guard.
