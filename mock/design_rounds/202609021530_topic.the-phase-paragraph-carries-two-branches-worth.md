# The phase paragraph has to carry what both branches established

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the phase paragraph

Two branches edited the same paragraph and both were right about different
things, so the trunk's copy and this branch's copy each hold half of what the
page should say.

The trunk's copy came in with the fraction work. It establishes that `Phase`
keeps the pair as it was written, that nothing normalises the sign and nothing
reduces the ratio, and that the two questions asked of a phase are independent
of the sign of the denominator. `format.rs:59` states the same thing from the
other side: total and lossless on every pair, so `of(3, i64::MIN)` is three over
the least value rather than a tiny positive wearing its sign.

This branch's copy drops the provenance the paragraph used to carry, which is
what the branch is for, and it corrects a claim that was false. The old sentence
said the constructors cannot produce a zero denominator. They can, and that is
deliberate: `format.rs:64` says a denominator of zero is not refused and is not
reinterpreted, it names no position, and `Format::ADMITTED` is where the
coordinates are checked together. So the predicates are total over the pair and
answer the zero case rather than assuming it away.

Neither half survives on its own. The trunk's keeps the history this branch is
removing, and this branch's is missing the sign and reduction facts. The
paragraph is rewritten once to carry both, in the voice the rest of the branch
uses.
