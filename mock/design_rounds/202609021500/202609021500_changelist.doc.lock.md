render to. The declaration block at the top of each stays untouched: a design has
to declare the canon it relates to, and that is not this round's question.

The verification below is the same on both files. Take everything from the first
`## ` heading onward, which is the body, and count registry addresses in it:

```
awk 'NR>2 && /^## /{f=1} f' <file> | grep -coE '(ruling|proposal|question|dimension|obligation)::'
```

from before the round.

## CHANGE: `arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl` FROM citing the rows behind each contract TO stating the contract
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: the body count above returns zero, from twenty-one. The declaration block still holds two `proposal::` and eight `ruling::`. Every backticked identifier that names an item in this tree still resolves.

Twenty-one addresses across sixteen passages. The usual shape is a sentence that
opens on a slug and the fact that it is settled, then says the thing. The thing
stays and the opening goes, so a rule about never emitting a runtime check
becomes the design saying so, which is what it meant to the reader anyway.

Four passages carry more than the framing and are rewritten rather than trimmed.
How many types the introduction door carries is genuinely unsettled and stays
unsettled, said as a design limit instead of as two derivations that reached
different answers and a count sitting outside what was stamped. The position
rule stays as the other shape the bound could take, named as open. The two
nearest-with-ties methods stay marked as open in both their readings. And the
width bound stays a property of this representation rather than a claim about
how wide arvo goes.

Two paragraphs go entirely, because there is no contract under either. One says
a dimension row still lists a value this design disagrees with and that the
repair belongs on that row; that is a note to whoever maintains the registry,
addressed to a reader who cannot see it. The other says an earlier version of
this page claimed something false. A reader who never saw it now holds two
designs to understand one, and learns the page was recently wrong about its own
subject. What the second one was guarding stays, in the sentence above it: the
two shipped shapes are not the two that can exist.
