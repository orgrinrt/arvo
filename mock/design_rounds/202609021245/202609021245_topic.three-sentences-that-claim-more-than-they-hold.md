# Three sentences in the fraction section claim more than they hold

## The spine is quoted with the clause that decides the case left out

The section opens by saying the format spine describes an operation onto a
representable set as an exact operation in the ambient domain composed with a
named total adaptation, and then that `Fraction::of` is one of those.

The spine says more than that, and what it says next is what settles this case:
the adaptation is a first-class object with its own laws, and an operation that
fuses one invisibly is an operation and an adaptation point wearing one name,
with the model's statements applying to it only when the two are separated.

`Fraction::of` is exactly that shape. It is one `const fn` performing the ratio
and the landing together, and there is no separated adaptation to point at. Nor
is `Fraction` a format: it has no ambient domain and no representable set in the
sense the spine's identity requires.

So the paragraph is a paraphrase that drops the load-bearing clause and then
draws a conclusion the full sentence forbids, which is the same defect the
`standing` paraphrase was, one tier up. The heading already says one tier down.
The sentence should say it too: the spine's shape recurs here, and the recurrence
is a resemblance rather than an instance.

## The exact region is described by the branch it does not take

The table says exact normalisation moves the sign to the numerator and cancels
the power of two the two operands share. It does neither where the denominator
is already positive, which is most of the region: there the constructor returns
the pair it was given, and a separate arm pins that.

Both halves of the description belong to the `i64::MIN` branch. The cell is
about a region and describes a corner of it.

## Half of what

`is_tie`'s comment says the stored remainder reaches one below the denominator,
so doubling it in the coordinate's own carrier leaves the type on any remainder
above half of it. Two readings, and only one is true.

Half the denominator is false: at `10` with a remainder of `6` the doubling is
`12`, which fits. Half the carrier's maximum is true and is the reason the
comparison widens. The sentence carries the justification for the change, so a
reader who takes the near antecedent gets a false statement in the place the
argument is made.
