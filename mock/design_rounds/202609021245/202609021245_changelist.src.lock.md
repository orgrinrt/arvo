# SRC changelist: half of what

## CHANGE: `is_tie`'s comment FROM an ambiguous antecedent TO the carrier
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`
Verification: the sentence names the carrier's maximum rather than `it`, and `grep -c "half of it" mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs` returns zero.

The comment said doubling the remainder leaves the type on any remainder above
half of it. The near antecedent is the denominator and that reading is false:
at a denominator of `10`, a remainder of `6` doubles to `12` and fits. The true
reading is half the carrier's maximum, which is why the comparison widens.

No assertion moves and no behaviour changes. What changes is that the sentence
carrying the justification says the thing that is true.
