## Canon

`ruling::the_derivation_is_a_placement_and_the_operation_set_is_an_admission_rule` governs the
admission, unchanged: the product is a function of the declared signature at every phase and radix of a
constant quantum, so those are admitted, and what this crate cannot carry yet is a declared placeholder
with its ticket rather than a refusal. `law::multiplicative_associativity_under_wrapping`,
`law::multiplicative_associativity_under_saturation` and
`law::distributivity_of_multiplication_over_addition` supply the cells the tests reproduce, unchanged.

## CHANGE: the product section is stated over any phase and radix

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the section retitled `## Multiplication, the product of
two members at a constant quantum`.

The lead states that the position `(phase + a)(phase + b) * q - phase` is a function of the signature at
every phase and radix, and that the step reads the phase, the radix and the exponent together.
`### The exact step is one division by a bounded denominator` replaces the binary move: the position over
`D = d^2 r^max(-e, 0)` at both signs of exponent, the slot the floor and the remainder reduced, the
four-limb numerator with its bounds (a factor under `2^96`, the product under `2^192`, the phase's term
at most `2^126`), the one long division by `D`, and saturation on the position's sign for a non-member.
`### What it refuses, and what is not yet admitted` replaces the five refusals with four conditions: a
zero phase denominator, refused by design; and three placeholders, each with its `FIXME` and ticket,
the magnitude-indexed quantum (`arvo_product_over_a_magnitude_indexed_quantum_is_a_placehold`), `D`
past `2^63 - 1` (`arvo_s_exact_position_carries_no_remainder_denominator_past`, reading `e >= -62` at
radix two, `e >= -18` at radix ten, and a phase denominator up to `3037000499` at exponent zero), and
a corner position the index does not hold, computed by the step itself and rounded one slot past
(`arvo_s_product_over_the_unsigned_64_bit_range_needs_a_slot_c`). The 64-bit partition is corrected:
`Integer<64>` admitted, `UFixed<64, k>` not yet for `k >= 0`, admitted for `k <= -1`, and
`UFixed<63, k>` admitted up to `k = 1`. The old statements that a nonzero phase and a radix other than
two are refused, and that `UFixed<64, _>` is refused at every exponent, are removed.
`### The laws this section owes tests for` gains the phase and radix ten in the denotation, the
compile-fail pair for a phase whose remainder denominator is too large in place of the phased pair,
the oracle sweep at both signs of exponent on both families with a phase and radix ten, the edge sweep
at widths nine to 64 against a host 128-bit oracle, the four-limb integer's own suite, and the wrapping
associativity cell crossed at every fraction width from one to `W`.
