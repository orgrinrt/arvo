# The trunk grew a second existential under this branch

## What happened

`fix/the-identity-turns-on-a-whole-multiple-phase` landed on the trunk while this
branch sat open, and it rewrote `arvo-format`'s `has_additive_identity` so the
search runs over slot and magnitude together rather than answering at one
magnitude. It gave `Ambient`, `Quantum` and `Format` the `ADMITTED` obligation
that `Slots` already carried, and it moved `Phase` off normalising arithmetic
onto a widened division that has no lossy pair.

None of that is this branch's work and none of it conflicts with it. What it
means for this branch is that `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/standards.rs`, which
this branch adds, now names a `Format` whose contract has moved underneath it.

## Why this needs a round rather than a merge

The trunk merge stages `crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`, and the gate reads
staged source against the phase without being able to tell a merge from an
edit. It is right not to try. So the window is opened the ordinary way, the
merge lands inside it, and the round records what was checked rather than
pretending a merge is not a change to this branch's tree.

## What this round has to establish

The claim worth pinning is not that the tree compiles. It is that the two
industrial conventions this branch names, MATLAB's `fi` and `fimath`, still
resolve against the moved contract, and that the parity numbers they are
measured against did not move with it.

- `Fi` and `Ufi` implement `Format` with the coordinates the trunk now
  declares, rather than the ones they were written against.
- The fourteen parity arms still reach MathWorks' published values, at the same
  widths and the same fraction lengths.
- The refusals `arvo-format` gained are refusals `standards.rs` does not trip,
  and where it would trip one that is a finding about the alias rather than
  about the refusal.

## What is out of scope

The three findings a review raised against this branch and marked as not
blocking: the sketch directory's `.md.tmpl` extension, the stale `gap` field on
the obligation row, and the dead second assertion in
`the_bound_is_attained_and_not_merely_respected`. They are filed and they are
somebody's next round, not this one's. This round carries the merge and the
verification that goes with it, and nothing else.
