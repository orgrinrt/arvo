# Doc changelist: the adaptation gains a verb, and one design document changes

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Round:** the adaptation round, opened after round `202608311902` closed
**Scope:** one `*.md.tmpl` under `mock/crates/`. No new crate, no new design document.

## What this changelist is

The slice was framed as an overflow and adaptation layer to design and build. It exists already, inside
`arvo-format`, by the design locked in round `202608311902`. What it lacks is the verb: an adaptation
is named everywhere and applied nowhere.

So this changelist edits one document rather than adding three. `arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl` gains the
statement of the map and the panic bound. Nothing else moves.

## The canon this design declares

Unchanged from round `202608311902` for the rows already declared, and adding three the earlier round
did not need:

`ruling::the_overflow_panic_is_permitted_and_bounded`, and
`ruling::the_panic_bound_names_a_concern_not_a_marker`, which corrects it and is why the bound is
stated loosely here rather than sharpened.

`proposal::the_realisation_map_is_one_map_with_two_regions`, which is what makes the map one thing with
two regions rather than a rounding mechanism beside an overflow mechanism.

`ruling::wrap_or_clamp_stays_open_and_both_get_priced` and
`ruling::precise_on_inexact_is_an_arm_question`, both named for what they forbid this round from
writing rather than for anything they let it write.

## Why no new crate, which was in scope to challenge and is the answer

An adaptation is a total map onto the representable set, so it cannot be stated without the format it
maps onto. A crate carrying it would be a crate that cannot be stated without its parent, which is the
test round `202608311902` used to refuse a five-crate split and which gives the same answer here.

**Keeping it where it is, is the result.** The brief put existence and locus in scope and this is the
finding: the boundary it implied would have split one sentence of ratified canon across two
compilation units.

## What is deliberately not here

**No preset is bound to an overflow policy.** `ruling::wrap_or_clamp_stays_open_and_both_get_priced` is
at `open` and says the bench decides, not this round and not op.

**No claim about what a saturated result denotes.** `question::what_a_datum_stands_for` is open with
the panel as decider and one reading makes the top an absorbing top. The map lands on the top slot and
this design says nothing about what that slot means.

**No widening of the rounding vocabulary.** `question::is_the_rounding_vocabulary_complete_at_six` is
open and challenges a ratified row; the ratified row governs until something overturns it.

**No arm.** Every region this round names is a region of one map rather than a selection between
competing implementations, and an arm still needs a measurement behind it.

## The inherited flat topic

`202608311948_topic.the-source-gate-is-untested-and-two-of-it-is-broken.md` is open and is named here
with its disposition, per the rule that a round consumes what was filed for it and never sheds it.

**It needs nothing from this round and is not consumed by it.** It is owned elsewhere: the two measured
lint holes it records are with the agent working on the lint suites, and the two mockspace lint defects
belong to that repository. It stays flat so the round that does own it inherits it the same way.

**It is not a stale filing.** Its subject, that the five lints gating `mock/crates/` have no tests, is
as true of this round's source as it was of the last one's.
