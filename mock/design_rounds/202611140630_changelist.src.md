## CHANGE: the member door suite samples both halves of the quantum and a whole phase

`src/division/tests/the_member_door.rs`: the format `Doubled`, `OneTo255` at `Constant<1>`, and the rows
`Byte, Doubled => [false, true, true, true]` and `Byte, FromOne => [true, false, true, true]`, which the
shipped verdict answers as stated. Two planted verdicts join the four: `VHalvesAlone`, which compares
`r^max(-e, 0)` alone through `v_half`, and `PhaseModuloASlot`, which compares the reduced phases' `Ratio`
modulo a whole slot; `the_table_reports_each_planted_verdict` asserts each disagrees with the table. The zero
column's search runs at seventeen formats, `Doubled` added. The table has eighteen rows.

`tests/ui/`: `a_member_divisor_from_an_exponent_above_zero_is_refused.rs` and
`a_member_divisor_from_a_phase_a_whole_slot_apart_is_refused.rs`, each one token from
`a_member_divisor_from_a_zero_free_range_on_the_grid_is_admitted.rs` (`Constant<1>`, `Phase::of(1, 1)`),
their stderr carrying the quantum's and the phase's own message. `tests/compile_fail.rs` registers both in
the divisor test, and the module doc names them.

## CHANGE: a member over a magnitude-indexed quantum is not yet admitted

`src/member.rs`: `MemberOf`'s obligation carries `FIXME(arvo_member_over_a_magnitude_indexed_quantum)`, and
its message says such a member is not yet admitted, since the applied map has no magnitude coordinate to
land one at, in place of saying a member holds a slot alone. `MemberOf`'s rustdoc, the module doc and both
doors' rustdoc say not yet admitted. `a_member_over_a_magnitude_indexed_quantum_is_refused.stderr` carries
the new message.

## CHANGE: the member door's obligation is its own module

`src/division/member_door.rs` holds `MemberDivisor`, `member_conditions`, `one_quantum`, `same_phase`,
`range_inside` and `no_member_names_the_zero`, moved unchanged from `division.rs`, with `MemberDivisor`, its
`ADMITTED`, `member_conditions` and `no_member_names_the_zero` at `pub(super)`. `division.rs` declares
`mod member_door`, imports `MemberDivisor`, and under `cfg(test)` the two the suite reads, so the suite's
paths are unchanged; `core::cmp::Ordering` leaves its imports. `division.rs` is 457 lines.
