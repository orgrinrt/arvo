## CHANGE: `round_slot` FROM the slot above as `down.saturating_add(1)` TO a `Rounded` pair

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`, `crates/arvo-format/src/addition.rs`

`pub(crate) struct Rounded { down: i128, up: Bool }` with `at`, `down`, `step`, `lands_within` and a
test-only `index`. `round_slot` returns it. `complete_slot` takes it: in range by `lands_within`; wrap as
`lo + ((down mod span) - (lo mod span) + step) mod span`; saturate and clamp on `down < lo`.
`panic_on_overflow` answers `!lands_within`. Addition's verdict reads the offset as
`(down - low) + step`. No wider integer is introduced; the pair names the one position past the index.

Verification: `grep -n 'pub(crate) struct Rounded' crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs` returns one, and
`grep -n 'saturating_add' crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs` returns one, `between`'s carry, which is
the chain of carries the design lists as saturating and not a position rounding names.

Landed in `7d737148`, which was committed while the now deprecated src changelist was the active one.

## CHANGE: the suites FROM pinning the saturated step TO the exact answers at the top

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_top_of_the_index.rs` (new),
`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_edges.rs`, `crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/the_ratio_coordinate.rs`,
`crates/arvo-format/src/apply/tests/mod.rs`, `crates/arvo-format/src/tests/the_inventory/the_range_obligation.rs`

`the_top_of_the_index.rs` runs every mode against `AtTheTop` and `AtTheBottom` (now `pub(crate)`):
ceil just under one past the top wraps to the lowest slot, saturate and clamp pin, `panic_on_overflow`
reports the step past the top, and `adapt` over the range at the top answers as the same range at zero
does, over six modes, three policies and offsets 0 to 255. `the_edges.rs`'s top-of-index arm asserts the
wrap of one past `i128::MAX` is 0 and is not -1. `the_ratio_coordinate.rs`'s carry arm reads the pair.

Negative controls, each run against the fixed source, restored and `touch`ed:

- the fixed source reverted to `saturating_add`: 4 failures in `the_top_of_the_index`;
- `lands_within` without the step check on the upper side: 16 failures;
- `lands_within` without the step-onto-`lo` branch: 2 failures;
- the wrap using `saturating_add` in place of the step: 4 failures.

## CHANGE: the pointer-width arms FROM an unstated gap TO `// FIXME:` markers

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/tests/the_platform_width.rs`,
`crates/arvo-placement/src/tests/the_platform_width.rs`

The 32-bit arms carry a `// FIXME:` saying they are compiled only by a hand-run
`cargo check --tests --target i686-unknown-linux-gnu`, and the 16-bit arms one saying they are never
compiled, a test harness needing `std` that no 16-bit target has. No lint or tool is added: a repo lint
runs on every commit and would put a cross-target build there, and a test cannot drive the build because
`forbidden-imports` refuses `std::process` at scope `*`.

Hand checks run for this changelist: `cargo check -p arvo-format -p arvo-placement --tests --target
i686-unknown-linux-gnu` and `cargo check -p arvo-format -p arvo-placement --target msp430-none-elf
-Zbuild-std=core`, both finished without error.
