# the-second-door-onto-the-width-obligation

`arvo-bits` has one obligation, `ADMITTED`, and two public constructions that
reach it. `masked` forces it directly and both of its refusals are pinned by
`trybuild` with their diagnostics checked, including the ordering that decides
whether the reader sees the intended `E0080` or the shift overflow `MASK`
would raise first. `cast` forces the same obligation at the target width, and
it forces it only by delegating to `masked`, with nothing holding that
delegation in place.

A post-monomorphisation refusal reached through a delegation is the kind that
survives a refactor without anybody noticing. The refusal does not disappear
loudly: the call keeps compiling and the diagnostic simply stops being there,
which no test in the crate would report.

The second half of the subject is the cast matrix. It walks all 4096 pairs of
admitted widths, which reads as exhaustive, and puts one start value through
each of them. Exhaustive over the type parameters and sampled over the values
is a shape worth stating rather than assuming, because the two halves are not
interchangeable and only one of them was actually swept.

The question the round settles is what the crate's own suite can currently
observe about `cast`, measured rather than argued, and what the cheapest thing
is that closes whatever it cannot.
