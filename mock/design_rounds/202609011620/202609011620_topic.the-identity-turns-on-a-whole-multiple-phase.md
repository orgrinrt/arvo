# The identity turns on a whole-multiple phase

## What this round does

The design says a nonzero phase removes the additive identity. Two instruments
that had not read each other measured that false, and the canon now carries the
correction: whether the set has an identity is decided by the phase being a whole
multiple of the quantum, not by its being nonzero. A phase of one whole step
shifts the grid onto itself and leaves zero on it, one slot down.

So the design tier states the corrected rule, and the function under it follows.
`has_additive_identity` tests `F::PHASE_NUM == 0`, which is the retired claim
written as code, and it is wrong wherever the phase is a nonzero whole multiple.

The test asserting the old claim is the third piece. Its three arms use phase
numerators 1, 1 and 3 against a denominator the type fixes at 2, so every phase
it tries is fractional and none of them enters the region where the function is
wrong. That is not a weak test, it is a test whose setup cannot reach the case,
which is why the defect survived a suite that looks thorough.

## What this round does not do

**It does not touch the stamped row.** `proposal::membership_of_the_representable_set_is_one_affine_predicate`
closes on the same clause and a ratified ruling stamps it, so correcting it wants
its own ruling rather than a design round's say-so. The sentence this round works
from is the one that row opens with, that membership is one affine predicate over
a phase, a quantum law and a slot range, which nothing here disturbs.

**It does not widen the region.** The measurement covers radices two and ten,
both signednesses, integer widths one to thirteen and exponents minus two to
eight. The corrected function is general because the arithmetic is, and the
claim about where it was checked stays where the evidence put it.

## Where it came from

A ratification seat reading the format proposals against the gate found the
grid's "contains neither zero nor one" clause false at the very geometry the
original sweep ran. A second seat, deriving the format layer cold with the
registry withheld, reached the same diagnosis independently and bounded it at
7375 of 23800 coordinate tuples. Both probes are committed with their controls.
