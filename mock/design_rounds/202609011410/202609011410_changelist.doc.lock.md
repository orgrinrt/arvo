# Doc changelist: the door sentence says what the crate actually holds

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Round:** the ratio fix stopped at the function a reviewer named
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

The design says the numeric introduction door is two types and that the coordinates are built through
it rather than widening it. Nine coordinate types each wrap a host primitive directly, so the crate
has eleven primitive introductions and the sentence is false.

## CHANGE: the door paragraph FROM claiming two primitives TO naming what the coordinates actually do
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: `grep -c 'built through the door rather than widening it' mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
returns zero, and the paragraph names `question::what_the_numeric_introduction_door_may_carry_out`.

The paragraph says that each coordinate introduces a primitive of its own rather than being built out
of `Width` and `Bool`, and that the question of what the door may carry out is open with the panel as
its decider.

**This corrects a statement about what shipped and does not answer that question.** The four options
it holds open are the panel's to choose among, and a design that picked one by describing the source
as though it had already been settled would be deciding it in the tier below where it belongs.
