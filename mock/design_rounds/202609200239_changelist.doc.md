## CHANGE: the bits crate says what the dependency would cost, not what a draft did
File: `mock/crates/arvo-bits/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, "Why it is not built on `arvo-format`", the opening
sentence.

"An earlier draft of this design gave `Bits<N>` a dependency on `arvo-format` for one thing, an
`arvo_format::Width` accessor reporting `N` back as a value in the stack's existing count-of-bits
vocabulary. Reconsidered against this stack's own stated principle that" becomes a statement in the
present about the dependency that is not taken: the one thing `arvo-format` would offer `Bits<N>` is an
`arvo_format::Width` accessor reporting `N` back in the stack's count-of-bits vocabulary, and it is
refused against this stack's stated principle that a consumer depends on the smallest slice it needs to
say one thing.

The rest of the section stands as written. It was already arguing from the cost and the erasure rather
than from the draft, and the tense is the only thing carrying history.
