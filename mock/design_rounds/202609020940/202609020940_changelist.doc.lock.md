# Doc changelist: the two architecture pages stop disowning themselves

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Round:** the surfaces a first release lands on, and what four of them say today
**Scope:** `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl`, and the two documents they render.

## CHANGE: drop the superseded banner FROM both templates TO nothing
File: `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl:1-7`, `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl:1-7`
Verification: `grep -c Superseded docs/DESIGN.md docs/PRINCIPLES.md` returns zero on both after
regeneration, and the two claims the banner names are corrected in the changes below rather than
merely unmarked.

Both public architecture pages open by telling the reader to treat nothing below as binding. The
banner was honest and it names exactly two stale claims, so removing it is only legitimate once
those two are fixed, which is why the three changes land together and not separately.

## CHANGE: the strategy count FROM a property of the design TO an instance count
File: `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl:98`
Verification: the sentence names no number, and `arvo-strategy/DESIGN.md.tmpl` is read against it
for agreement.

It reads "the four strategy markers and their codegen consequences". `arvo-strategy`'s own design
already states that the presets are instances of an open concept rather than members of an
enumeration, and that a consumer adding a fifth edits nothing, so a count in a sentence here
contradicts a design one layer down.

The deep dive it points at is about what a strategy bound costs at codegen, which is true of a
strategy rather than of there being four, so the fix is to say that and drop the number.

## CHANGE: the nightly feature set FROM seven features and const traits TO what the tree has
File: `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl:28-36`
Verification: three counts over `mock/crates/`, each of which must stay at zero for the replacement
to be true, and each of which is what falsified the original:

    #![feature(...)] attributes                    0
    occurrences of `const trait`                   0
    generic_const_exprs in any crate source        0

The paragraph states that every trait sensibly callable in const context is `pub const trait` on the
RFC 3762 line, and lists `adt_const_params`, `generic_const_exprs`, `const_trait_impl`,
`const_index`, `const_param_ty_trait`, `macro_metavar_expr_concat` and `try_trait_v2` as the flag
set, with a risk-class sentence attached.

None of it holds. The crates enable no nightly features at all and declare no const traits, and the
one mention of `generic_const_exprs` anywhere in the repository is `arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl:184`,
which says it is forbidden and that `min_generic_const_args` is the path. So the document does not
merely overstate the flag set: it names, as adopted, a feature the design one layer down rules out.

The replacement says what is true and no more: plain `const fn` throughout, no feature attributes,
and `generic_const_exprs` ruled out with the reason. It does not claim the crates build on stable,
which is unmeasured and is a different sentence.

## CHANGE: nothing in the registry
File: none
Verification: `mock/registry/*.toml` untouched, and `cargo mock` reports 708 rows across 10
namespaces before and after.

Nothing here is a canon act. The templates are a design surface and every change above brings one
into agreement with a design or a source count that already stood.

## CHANGE: nothing in the generated docs tree beyond these two files
File: none
Verification: `git status` after `cargo mock` names `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/PRINCIPLES.md` and the
`docs/STRATEGY.md` timestamp line, and nothing else.

The generated tree publishes the registry's provenance columns at a scale of several hundred
references across five documents, and that is filed for a human call rather than fixed here. It is a
question about what the generator emits, the rows themselves are the audit trail and are worth
keeping where they are, and it does not block the way these do: those two pages are wrong, where this
is internal detail on a page that is otherwise correct.
