# The surfaces a first release lands on, and what four of them say today

## SUBJECT: the public face is wrong in four places and no diff touches any of them

A release readiness read over the `dev` to `main` slice found four things that
block, and the thing they share is that none of them sits in anybody's change.
They are the surfaces a stranger reads and that no work is ever *about*, so
nothing prompts a look at them and no gate reads them.

This round fixes all four.

## The two architecture pages open by disowning themselves

`docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/PRINCIPLES.md` are the two documents somebody opens
after the readme, and both begin:

> **Superseded. This document describes a design that is being replaced, and it is
> not current.** ... until it lands, treat nothing below as binding.

Shipping a first release whose architecture page says to disregard itself is
worse than shipping no page. The banner was honest when it was written and it
names exactly two claims that had gone stale, so the repair is to fix those two
claims and drop the banner rather than to delete the documents or to leave the
warning standing over content that is mostly fine.

Both claims are measured rather than assumed.

**The strategy set.** `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl:98` calls them "the four strategy
markers". `arvo-strategy`'s own design already says the presets are instances of
an open concept rather than members of an enumeration, and that a consumer adding
a fifth edits nothing, so the count is not a property of the design.

**The nightly features.** `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl:29-36` states that every trait
sensibly callable in const context is `pub const trait`, and lists seven nightly
features as the flag set. Against the tree:

    #![feature(...)] attributes in mock/crates/    0
    occurrences of `const trait`                   0
    generic_const_exprs in any crate source        0

The one mention of `generic_const_exprs` anywhere is
`arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl:184`, which says it is **forbidden** and that
`min_generic_const_args` is what there is. So the paragraph does not merely
overstate: it names a feature the design rules out, as part of a set the crates
do not use at all.

## Sixteen doc comments carry how the project decided things

`documentation-writing.md` puts rustdoc at tier 1 and forbids provenance there,
and rustdoc is the larger public surface because it renders on docs.rs and is
where a reader spends longest.

Worst first: `arvo-format/src/apply.rs:323` names op on a `///` over a public
function and closes on "would go past what was blessed";
`arvo-strategy/src/lib.rs:141` names op and a prior panel; `arvo-format/src/width.rs:8`
names a workspace tool and one of its configuration sections. Then the `ratified`
family across `format.rs`, `overflow.rs`, `apply.rs`, `arvo-placement/src/lib.rs`,
`arvo-strategy/src/lib.rs` and `arvo-strategy/src/tests.rs`.

**Every one of them survives the removal**, which is what makes this cheap: "the
factoring requires" says the same thing as "the ratified factoring requires", and
the second word carries no information a reader can use. What is being cut is who
decided and when, never what the contract is.

## The package version was never a decision

`mock/Cargo.toml:47` carries `version = "0.1.0"` and every crate inherits it.
`git log -S` puts it at the initial seed commit, so it has never moved and is a
scaffold default rather than anything anybody chose. It also contradicts the
readme three paragraphs above it, which says the api has not settled.

`0.0.0` is what the design phase carries, with the release named by its tag.

## What this round does not do

**The generated `docs/` tree publishes the registry's provenance columns**, at a
scale of several hundred references across five documents. That is a locus
question about what the generator emits rather than a wording fix, the rows
themselves are the audit trail and are worth keeping where they are, and it is
filed for a human call. **It is not in this round**, and it does not block the
same way the four above do, because those are wrong while this one is merely
internal.
