# Doc changelist: the width law was sampled, and the widened lint had no arm

## `README.md` (update)

Hand-written rather than generated, so it is edited directly and no regeneration reaches
it.

- "Where this is right now", the paragraph enumerating the crates that build: append one
  sentence for `arvo-bits`, saying it sits beside the other three rather than under them
  with no dependency of its own, and what it is: an exact-width bit container,
  `Bits<const N: u32>` for `N` from one to sixty-four, masked construction and masked
  cast between declared widths, no arithmetic and no sign. The clause saying why is the
  one the design already gives, that a field a consumer pins to a width and aliases is a
  different thing from a number.
- Same section, the caution paragraph: "the shape above these three" becomes "these
  four". A count in prose that disagrees with the tree is the shape this round is here
  to fix, so it is not left to a second pass.

## `crates/arvo-bits/DESIGN.md.tmpl` (no change)

Stated here so its absence is a decision rather than an omission. The laws it states over
"any value" and "every admitted `M`" are correct as written; what was wrong was the suite
claiming to have pinned them. The document does not weaken to match a sample, and it does
not need to strengthen either, because it already says the thing the sweeps now check.

## The generated pages (regenerate, no hand edit)

`docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/PRINCIPLES.md`, `docs/STRUCTURE.md` and the three
`STRUCTURE.GRAPH` renderings all describe three crates. Their templates already describe
four: `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl` says "Four crates" and `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl` says "Four
crates" with the `arvo-bits` paragraph under it, both landed by the round that shipped
the crate.

So nothing is authored here. `cargo mock` regenerates them, which produces the page that
is true now rather than a hand edit that would be overwritten. The defect was that the
regeneration never ran, and the pages are the only place it showed.

## What this changelist does not touch

The primitive-vocabulary table in `PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl` sits inside the shared
cross-ecosystem block synced from the workspace, marked not to be edited in place, and
the previous round already recorded that as a known gap rather than an oversight. It is
unchanged for the same reason and the fix, if one is wanted, is still a workspace-level
one.

The `[primitive-introductions]` entry in `mockspace.toml` is configuration rather than a
document and is out of this changelist regardless; whether it should be crate-wide is a
question for op, recorded in the topic file.
