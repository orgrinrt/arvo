# addition-admitted-on-a-declared-signature

The ratified rows bind the arithmetic and there is no arithmetic. `ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon`
states arithmetic as an exact ambient operation composed with a named total adaptation, the crate ships
the adaptation as `adapt`, and nothing composes it with an operation. The one `Operation` in the tree is
test-local, `Add<F>` in `src/tests/the_adaptation.rs:229`, and its test reads its own declaration back.
Two ratified laws, the additive verdict and absorption, are statements about an addition that does not
exist, and the symmetry section of the design says so itself: no code reads the relocation predicates,
"because the consumer that would is an operation and there are none here".

This round builds addition as an admitted operation, the exact step it composes with the adaptation, the
obligation that refuses a format it cannot be stated over, and the absorption verdict that says where
the reduction is associative. It also settles where addition lives.

## The canon it rests on

All ratified unless stated.

- `ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon`: arithmetic factors as exact operation then adaptation; the
  concept is closed and the inventory open.
- `ruling::the_derivation_is_a_placement_and_the_operation_set_is_an_admission_rule`: an operation is
  admitted exactly when it is a function of the declared signature. The design ships a rule, not a list.
- `ruling::behaviour_is_stated_per_declared_signature_and_the_premise_dissolves`.
- `ruling::the_additive_and_absorption_verdicts_are_canon`, ratifying
  `proposal::an_additive_verdict_is_independent_of_the_fraction_width` and
  `proposal::absorption_decides_associativity_of_a_clamped_reduction`. Its note records that the
  additive row carries no `rounding` axis.
- `law::additive_associativity_under_wrapping`, `law::additive_associativity_under_saturation`,
  `law::additive_associativity_recovered_under_declared_nonnegative_operand_window`.
- `ruling::the_overflow_panic_is_permitted_and_bounded`, `ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path`,
  `ruling::the_ambiguous_rounding_word_is_retired_for_six_explicit_names`.

"Reduction" in the absorption row is the adaptation map onto the representable set, not a fold. Its
statement is `adapt(adapt(x) + c) == adapt(x + c)` over reachable exact `x` and stored `c`, which is the
relocation law `adaptation_relocates` already expresses, read at the positions and translations addition
reaches. A fold is a different object and stays out: `question::reduction_order_or_associativity` and
`question::what_a_numeral_guarantees_to_a_fold` are open.

## Canon gate

Aligned, on a first read. A second independent read is owed before anything here is cited as the canon's
permission.

Admitted addition is one instance under the admission rule, as each point in `points` is one instance
under the format concept, so shipping it does not ship an operation list. The locus is `arvo-format`:
the exact step reads the phase and the slot range and nothing else, and the rule's own test, a function
of the declared signature, is kept by construction only in the crate that cannot reach a carrier.
`arvo-placement` can see a carrier, which is exactly the dependency the floor forbids an operation to
have, and `arvo-strategy` is where defaults are bound, which addition is not.

Refused, because the canon does not license it: a numeral value type carrying a stored slot. No row
licenses one, and a value type in this crate would be a second placement beside the one
`the_derivation_is_a_placement` derives by occupancy. Addition takes and returns `Slot`, the coordinate
the format is stated in. Mixed-signature addition is refused too: `question::mixed_numeral_addition` is
open, so there is one signature parameter.

## What the round builds

Addition's exact step, from the denotation. Two members `(p + a) q` and `(p + b) q` sum to
`(p + (a + b + p)) q`, so the exact position is `a + b + p` in slot units: the whole part of the phase
added to the slot, the fractional part as the remainder. The quantum and the radix do not appear, which
is the additive verdict's scale independence as a property of the arithmetic rather than of a sweep.
The sketch confirms the step against the canon's counts.

An admission obligation, forced where the step is taken, refusing two things: a magnitude-indexed
quantum, because at a different magnitude a slot names a different value and the applied map carries no
magnitude coordinate to land the sum at; and a sum the slot coordinate cannot carry. No shipped point
reaches the second, and the sketch shows an outside format does.

`Add<S>`, the operation, declaring its signature and its arity, replacing the test-local one. Its arity
is what the reach reads, so it is not decoration.

The absorption verdict. The reach of addition is known from the signature: sums of two members span
`[2 MIN + k, 2 MAX + k]` and the translation is a member plus `k`, where `k` is the phase's whole part.
When the phase is whole, every position is on the grid and the verdict is the completion half of
`adaptation_relocates`. When it is fractional, the rounding adds a fixed `delta` of zero or one wherever
`rounding_is_translation_equivariant` holds over those positions, and the verdict is the same completion
question at `k + delta`; for stochastic rounding, whose `delta` is the dither's, it must hold at both.
Where the rounding varies across the reach the verdict refuses, and brute force counts what that costs.

`Reach` gains a grid state. Today a reach may carry a tie or not; an on-grid reach says the rounding
region is never entered, which makes the rounding half hold for every mode and moves the upper
excursion test from "at or above the highest slot" to "above it". Without it the verdict gets
`Window<-4, 0>` wrong. Three states replace the tie flag, so "on grid with a tie" cannot be written.

`panic_on_overflow` takes the position and the dither rather than a slot. The design says the verdicts
refuse a position; a slot is what a caller cannot produce before rounding, which is private. Addition
itself does not panic: the ruling leaves the debug panic to the caller's build, and the verdict takes
exactly what `add` takes.

## Findings, with their predicates

Every predicate below names only what was run, and a dimension absent from one does not hold.

1. The exact step reproduces the saturation witness. Holds for `total_width: W in {4, 8}`,
   `fraction_width: F in {0, 2, 3}`, `signedness: signedness in {unsigned, signed}`,
   `overflow_policy: overflow policy in {wrap, saturate}`, `rounding: rounding = floor`,
   `phase: phase = 0`, `operation: operation = add`, `arity: arity = 3`, `threads: threads = 1`.
2. A signed range can be associative under saturation. Holds for `total_width: W = 3`,
   `fraction_width: F = 0`, `signedness: signedness = signed`, `overflow_policy: overflow policy = saturate`,
   `operand_window: operand window = full range`, `phase: phase = 0`, `operation: operation = add`,
   `arity: arity = 3`, at the range `[-4, 0]`, against `[-1, 7]` which is not.
3. The carry refusal is unreachable from a shipped point. Holds for `total_width: W in 1..=62`,
   `phase: phase in {h/2 : h any i64}`, `operation: operation = add`, `arity: arity = 2`.

## Two registry proposals, not made

`proposal::an_additive_verdict_is_independent_of_the_fraction_width` and
`proposal::absorption_decides_associativity_of_a_clamped_reduction` were ratified as law-tier
propositions and still sit in `proposal.toml`. They should move into `law.toml` as `law` rows: the
ruling calls them law-tier, their predicates passed its gate as written, and a reader looking for the
laws of addition looks in the law namespace and finds the rows that cite them rather than the rows
themselves. The additive row would also answer the ruling's own note by naming `rounding`: at `phase 0`
addition never rounds, so `rounding any` is established there and nowhere else. Editing the registry is
not this round's.
