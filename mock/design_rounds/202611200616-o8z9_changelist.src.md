## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/Cargo.toml`

Takes `notko-hlist.workspace = true`, declared in the mock workspace with its default features off.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/src/word.rs`

`Lane` stays; its impl for `Bits<N>` reads `WIDTH` through `BitWidth::WIDTH`.

`Field` takes `Copy` as a supertrait and names `type Value: const Lane` and nothing else; `Layout` names
`type Container: const Lane` and `type Fields: Fields`. `BELOW` and the field's `Layout` go.

The seal is implemented for `notko_hlist::Empty` and `Cons<H, T>`. `Fields` keeps `SUM`, its fold
asserting each field's width is one to sixty-four; `COVER` and `OVERLAP` go. `Below<P>`, with the
supertrait `Fields`, gives `BELOW` for `Cons<H, T>` at `Here` and at `There<P>`. `Holds`, `Listed`,
`placed` and the tuple list go.

`Word::TILED` asserts the container's width is one to sixty-four, then that `SUM` equals it. `get` and
`with` take the field marker by value and a place `P`, bound `L::Fields: PlaceOf<F, P> + Below<P>`, with
no const bound. `get` masks what it moves down to the field's width before `from_low`. The two mutant
flags stay at the same lines of the read and the write.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/src/lib.rs`

Re-exports `Below` in place of `Holds`.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/tests/the_packed_word.rs`

The layouts are rewritten in the new declaration and the tests in the new call shape. A macro runs the
round trip and the raw-bit pinning over every container width from one to sixty-four, a single `Bits<N>`
field and, from two up, a `Bits<N - 1>` under a `Bool`. The alias test goes; a test lists one field
type in two layouts and reads and writes it at its place in each.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/tests/the_packed_word_with_no_gates.rs`

New, with no feature line: the handle written and read in constants.

## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/tests/compile_fail.rs` and `tests/ui/`

The packed word's cases are rewritten in the new shape. The overlap case and the past-bit-sixty-four
case go, as states that cannot be written. New cases: a field listed twice, a consumer's `Lane` of width
zero and of sixty-five as a field value, and as a container.

## `mock/research/sketches/202611200603_packed-word-mutants/`

The two flags are rerun over the new tests and the outputs and counts replaced, the sketch's text
saying which round reran them.
