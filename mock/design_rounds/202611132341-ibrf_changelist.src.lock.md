**Date:** 2026-11-13

## CHANGE: `src/apply/landing.rs` is rewritten from the locked sections

`Scaled` holds its denominator as `NonZeroU128`, and `Scaled::of` takes one and checks nothing.
`position_at` forms `num q radix^(t + c) - p den radix^(b + c)` over `den q radix^c` with
`c = max(0, -t, -b)`, `b` left out at a zero phase, and returns the end of the index on a side where the
numerator is provably past it (`past`, `under_half`). `first_candidate` takes the ceiling of `log2 radix`
for a negative shift and returns zero for a falling law with a phase. `land` lands at the top magnitude
when the walk finds nothing. `Landable::ADMITTED` carries the three compile-time refusals: a range not
holding zero over more than one grid, with `FIXME(arvo_land_refuses_a_format_whose_slot_range_does_not_hold_ze)`;
wrapping off one progression; and wrapping on a magnitude-indexed one-progression set, with
`FIXME(arvo_land_does_not_wrap_on_a_one_progression_set_over_severa)`. The FIXME naming
`arvo_landing_a_value_on_a_magnitude_indexed_format_is_a_call` is gone.

## CHANGE: the landing's tests

`src/apply/landing/tests.rs`: the oracle is rewritten from the membership predicate in the four limbs
and names the top magnitude's position past an end, and positions are compared as reduced remainders;
`Far` gains numerators past 100 bits; a falling phased law and a zero-phase 64-bit decimal law join the
sweeps; a phased decimal law over 50 magnitudes is swept on `Near`, the shifts where its denominator
`10^(s - BASE)` stays in the four limbs, since one over 300 magnitudes forms no position past the 77th; the review's two points are tests of their own; the
zero-denominator test goes with the convention. `tests/the_members.rs` lands every member of four laws
on itself. `tests/past_the_ends.rs` covers saturation and clamping past each end, on a rising, a
falling and a phased law, a position past the four limbs, and wrapping on a constant quantum.
`tests/the_one_progression.rs` checks `is_one_progression` against the set written out.
`tests/the_walk_start.rs` holds the decimal law under a negative shift, moved from `tests.rs`, and a
falling phased law over 101 magnitudes whose members on the finest step sit `2^99` from zero, where a
walk start reading only the point's distance from zero skips 33 magnitudes.
