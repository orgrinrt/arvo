## CHANGE: the directions are aliases, and ties away runs on decimal

File: same, "The rounding directions, and where a result lands".

The four directions are type aliases rather than renamed re-exports, with the reason: a re-export under
another name becomes the path the compiler prints for the mode. `roundTiesToAway` stays the MATLAB Round
alias, and the decimal arms run it. The landing composition is written once over anything that says
whether a position overflows and what it reads off as.

## CHANGE: where the expected side comes from

File: same, "Where the IEEE expected side comes from".

Binary32 and binary64 run under the four binary directions against the host. Binary16 reads its
published encodings and the digit rule at radix two under the four. Decimal runs the digit rule under
all five. The rule is written once over any radix with each format's numbers written in, and reads no
arvo code; a separate arm pins the format's own range to those numbers. The fimath sweep runs at a
matrix of shapes.
