# The second base review

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Scope:** `arvo-format`'s wrapping admission sentence, the wrapping sweep's formats, the IEEE 754
underflow draws, and the comments the second review of `work/arvo-format-base` named.

The second review of `work/arvo-format-base`, recorded in
`mock/research/202611140340_review-arvo-format-base-2/REVIEW.md`, passed the canon gate as the owed second
read and found the arithmetic right as far as it could break it. It found three claims the tree makes
and does not keep, and four small comments.

The design says `UFixed<64, k>` is admitted under wrapping at every `k`, and `mul`'s doc says the same at
every exponent at or above zero. At `k = 129` the top corner, `(2^64 - 1)^2 2^129`, passes `2^256`, so the
step is not formed and the verdict is false there; at `k = 128` it is formed. The range is `k` from 0 to
128. `mul`'s doc also still says the floor is reduced modulo the range's count onto the range, the shape
round `202611140209` replaced with the floor modulo twice the count on its own side of zero.

Round `202611140209`'s sweep of the wrapping product names its dimensions, signed and unsigned, both ends
of the index, odd and even counts, but the formats it sweeps do not cross them. Past the index no pair
has a negative floor with a remainder below a half, and none is a negative tie on an odd count, which is
the cell the floor's negative side exists for. The range at the bottom of the index has no inexact
position at all. The fix is formats that reach those cells, and a test that counts the pairs in each
cell so the sweep's coverage is asserted rather than claimed: an odd range of nearly the whole 64-bit
width, `-(2^63 - 1)` to `2^63 - 1`, at phase one half and `2^2` and at phase one quarter and `2^3`, and
the bottom range at `2^-1` and `2^-2`. A phased range at `i128::MIN` is refused as unformed, so the bottom
range takes a zero phase.

The same round says no IEEE 754 underflow draw falls where the search hands its stand-in. The draws start
one exponent below the first unit past the slot index, and for binary that unit is `2^128`, past the
unsigned 128-bit integer, while for decimal the first unit past the index, `10^39`, is past it already.
The stand-in is sound, since the far points already lean on it, but the sentence says the draws avoid
it and they do not. The draws are moved rather than the sentence: they start at the lowest exponent
whose unit the unsigned 128-bit integer holds, which is the first unit past the index for binary and one
above it for decimal.

The comments: the IEEE rule's note that `2 |c|` is at most `2^128` now reads below, since `-2^127` is
refused; the case of `UFixed<64, -300>` names its exponent rather than a phase; and the multiplication
module's doc names `MultipliableUnder` and `is_multipliable_under` beside the obligation it already names.

The review's fourth finding, `Remainder::of` taking two host integers, is the rule task
`arvo_format_s_coordinate_reads_return_host_integers` already holds, and the task is bumped with it. It is
not this branch's work.
