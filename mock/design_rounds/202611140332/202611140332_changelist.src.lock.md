# Src changelist: the second base review

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140332`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140332_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/` and `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/`.

## CHANGE: where the unsigned 64-bit product is admitted

`src/multiplication.rs`: `mul`'s rustdoc says the floor past the index is reduced modulo twice the
range's count, kept on its own side of zero, and that the unsigned 64-bit range is admitted at exponents
from 0 to 128, in place of the count onto the range at every exponent at or above zero. The module doc
names the second obligation, over the signature, which `mul` forces and `is_multipliable_under` asks.

`src/multiplication/tests/the_wrap_past_the_index.rs`: a new test,
`the_admission_ends_where_the_step_stops_forming`, holds `UFixed<64, 128>` admitted under wrapping and
`UFixed<64, 129>` refused, `UFixed<64, -255>` admitted and `UFixed<64, -256>` refused through
`is_multipliable` and under wrapping and saturation, and `UFixed<63, k>` admitted at `1` and `-255` and
refused at `2`. The comment on `UFixed<64, -300>` names its exponent's `D` rather than a phase.

## CHANGE: the wrapping sweep's cells

`src/multiplication/tests/the_wrap_by_every_mode.rs`: four formats join the sweep under every mode, the
odd range `-(2^63 - 1)` to `2^63 - 1` at phase one half and `2^2` and at phase one quarter and `2^3`, and
the 200 slots at the bottom of the index at `2^-1` and `2^-2`. A new test,
`the_sweep_reaches_every_sign_and_side_of_a_half_past_the_index`, counts the swept pairs past the index
off the grid by the floor's sign and the remainder against a half, asserts every one of the six cells is
reached, and that the odd wide range at phase one half reaches a negative tie. Its control is the range
at the bottom at exponent zero, which has no position off the grid and reaches no cell. The module doc
says the dimensions are crossed.

## CHANGE: the IEEE 754 underflow draws

`tests/ieee754/mod.rs`: `held_below` names the lowest exponent whose unit the unsigned 128-bit integer
holds, and the 500 underflow draws start there rather than one below the first unit past the index. The
rule's comment says `2 |c|` is below `2^128`. `tests/ieee754_underflow_parity.rs`: its module doc says the
sweep runs down to that exponent, and a new test, `no_draw_falls_where_the_search_hands_its_stand_in`,
holds the bound at `qmin - 127` for binary64 and `qmin - 38` for decimal64, checks it against a power
written in the test, and checks every draw of four seeds over binary32, binary64 and decimal64 is at or
above it. Its control is the far points, which are below it. The test failed with the draws started two
exponents lower: `tests/ieee754_underflow_parity.rs`, the draw assertion.

## NO CHANGE: the arithmetic and the bare reads

No arithmetic changes. `Remainder::of` taking two host integers is task
`arvo_format_s_coordinate_reads_return_host_integers`, bumped with it.
