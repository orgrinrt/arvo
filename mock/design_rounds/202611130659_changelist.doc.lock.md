## Canon

`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` is why the step's branch on a zero factor goes
rather than waiting on a blessing: the canon does not settle whether that branch is a check, and the
divisor's admission can be drawn so the branch is dead for every value the type can hold.
`ruling::the_derivation_is_a_placement_and_the_operation_set_is_an_admission_rule` is unchanged in what
it admits. The registry is silent on the remainder's public door, so the stance is taken here and the
existing proposal row `a_quotient_s_divisor_is_non_zero_by_type` is amended to the narrower admission.

## CHANGE: the remainder and its position are public

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the exact position's section.

The paragraph saying no public constructor takes a wide remainder is replaced. `Remainder` is public,
`Remainder::of(num, den)` is the residue `num` leaves over `den` with a zero denominator reading as
`Remainder::ZERO`, `numerator` and `denominator` read it back, and `Exact::at(slot, part)` is the
position over it, with no carry and nothing past the index. The product, the quotient and the
conversion build through these two. The sentence calling the remainder private to the crate names it
instead.

## CHANGE: `of` is admitted only where no slot names the zero

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the divisor's section.

`of` is admitted exactly where the reduced phase denominator is past one. The paragraph on the
non-member zero saturating toward the positive end is removed: every divisor names a non-zero factor
by construction, and the step does not ask. The compromise is restated: a whole-phase format divides
only by constants, a range leaving the zero out included, under the ticket
`arvo_a_runtime_divisor_over_a_whole_phase_range_without_the`. The laws paragraph names the new
compile-fail pair, `of` over a whole phase beside the same range at a half-offset phase.

## CHANGE: one array of verdicts per obligation

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the refusals of the quotient and of the conversion.

Each says the obligation and its verdict function read one array of the four verdicts.

## CHANGE: the readme's divisor sentence
