# Src changelist: the remainder carries a 128-bit denominator

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** step AR6 of the roadmap, its first round, on `work/oracle-fraction-wide`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611130438_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply.rs`, `src/apply/position.rs`, `src/addition.rs`,
`src/multiplication.rs`, `src/format.rs`, `src/lib.rs`, `src/wide.rs` (moved), the tests under
`src/`, `tests/compile_fail.rs` and `tests/ui/`. No public item is added, renamed or removed;
`Fraction` and every public signature are unchanged.

## CHANGE: the stored remainder is a coordinate of its own

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/apply/position.rs`, `src/apply.rs`.

`Remainder` is crate-private: a numerator below an unsigned 128-bit denominator. `within(num, den)`
is total, keeping the residue of a numerator at or past the denominator and reading a zero
denominator as `ZERO`, so every value is in `[0, 1)`. It answers `is_zero`, `against_half` (`num`
against `den - num`) and `is_above(dither)` (a 64-bit by 128-bit cross product held in two words), and
compares equal to the `Fraction` whose pair it holds. `Exact.part` is a `Remainder`; `between`
stores the caller's residue in it exactly, and the crate-private `Exact::held(slot, part)` takes one
directly, carrying the `FIXME` for the missing public constructor under
`arvo_has_no_public_constructor_for_an_exact_position_whose_r`. `is_on_grid` and `is_tie` read it,
and `round_slot` reads `against_half` and `is_above` in place of the 128-bit products it formed.

## CHANGE: the four-limb integer moves to the crate and divides by two limbs

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/wide.rs`, `src/wide/tests.rs`, moved from `src/multiplication/`.

It gains `negated` and `unsigned`, and `floor_divided` takes a `u128` divisor and returns a `u128`
remainder: limb by limb under `2^64`, a bit at a time with a carried top bit from there. The floor
adjustment is unchanged. `greatest_common_divisor_unsigned` joins `greatest_common_divisor` in
`src/format.rs`, and the signed one now calls it.

## CHANGE: addition refuses only a phase that does not denote

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/addition.rs`.

`remainder_is_held` is removed; the obligation's first assert and `is_addable` read `denotes`, with a
message naming a zero denominator. The `FIXME` on the `2^63` half goes. `positioned` and
`addition_is_associative` build the remainder with `Exact::held` through one `remainder_of`.

## CHANGE: the product's denominator moves to `2^128 - 1`

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/multiplication.rs`.

`Step.divisor` is `u128` and `Step.offset` a `Wide`; `D` and `n d r^k` are formed in four limbs and `D`
narrowed with `unsigned`. The obligation's message names `2^128 - 1` and the unsigned 128-bit
denominator, under the same ticket. `positioned` reduces with the unsigned gcd and builds the
position with `Exact::held`.

## CHANGE: the shared wide oracle and the applied-map oracle widen

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/tests/big.rs` (moved from `src/multiplication/tests/`),
`src/tests/ratio.rs`, `src/apply/tests/the_oracle.rs` and its construction sites.

`Big` is shared by the product's edge sweep and the four-limb suite. It gains `of_unsigned` and
`unsigned`, and `floor_over` takes any positive `u128`: bit-serial under `2^64`, and from there a
search over the quotient's bits that reads only a product and a comparison, so it is another
algorithm than the step's on each side. `Ratio::position` builds with `Exact::held`. The oracle's
`Point` carries `i128` and is documented to a denominator of at most `2^63`.

  in `Big` at denominators from 2 to `2^128 - 1`, 41 remainders and nine dithers, each beside a

  equality with `Fraction` and the residue and zero-denominator readings of `within`.

  `negated` at their ends.

  `2^128 - 1`.
- `src/multiplication/tests/the_step.rs`: the remainder at `2^-127`, `10^-38` and a phase of

- `src/addition/tests/the_step.rs`: the refusal test admits every denoting phase; the largest

- `src/addition/tests/the_signed_oracle.rs`: sum, negation and difference against the applied map's

## CHANGE: the compile-fail pairs move to the new boundaries
