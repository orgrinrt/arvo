The source follows the design as the doc changelist locked it. Changes are in `arvo-placement` (the sole
carrier as a type, with its tests) and in `arvo-numeral` (the admission, the carrier, the debug check,
the aliases, with their tests and the consumer crate), plus the mutant outputs. The registry, principles
and format design changes of the doc changelist touch no source.

## Current state

`arvo-placement` gives the sole carrier only as a width, `derive_sole`. `arvo-numeral` keeps its own map
`Carried`, 128 rows checked against `derive_sole` by its own test. `Sole` is implemented per listed slot
family through the sealed `ValuePolicy` and `ValueRounding`. `Wrapping` and `Saturating` carry the debug
abort inside the overflow policy. The aliases are `Uint<BITS, P>` and the rest over a policy, and
`USize` and `ISize` fix one adaptation.

## Changes proposed

### CHANGE: `arvo-placement`, `SoleCarrier`

`src/sole.rs` declares `SoleCarrier { type Carrier; const ADMITTED: Bool; }` and `carrier_agrees`, and a
`sole!` macro writes the rows for `Signed<W>` and `Unsigned<W>`, `W` from 1 to 64, each naming the
ladder container `derive_sole` gives. `src/lib.rs` adds the module and exports both.
`src/tests/the_sole_carrier.rs` checks agreement at all 128 rows, an outside wrong row reported beside a
right one, and the `ADMITTED` refusal as a compile-fail doctest beside a passing twin.
`src/tests/the_open_inventory.rs` follows the new export.

### CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, the admission and the carrier

`src/admit.rs`: `Sole` is a blanket over every `DeclaredSignature` whose slot family has a
`SoleCarrier` row, with `ADMITTED`, `WIDTH`, `POLICY` and `SIGNED`, and `whole_range` checking the range
covers the whole signed or unsigned range of its width. `ValuePolicy` and `ValueRounding` go.
`src/map.rs`: `Carried` and its rows go; `Held` maps each ladder container to its unsigned machine
integer, four rows, private. `src/machine.rs`: `Machine` loses `SIGNED`. `src/num.rs`:
`Num<S: Sole, C: DebugCheck = Abort>` stores `ReprOf<S>`, dispatches on the carrier's size and
`Sole::SIGNED`, aborts only where `C::ABORTS`, and its trait impls run over `<S, C>`.
`src/identity.rs` follows. `src/rung.rs` changes its doc only.

### CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, the debug check

`src/check.rs` declares `DebugCheck { const ABORTS: Bool; }`, `Abort` and `Quiet`. The overflow
messages name the `Quiet` check as the way to ask for the completion.

### CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, the aliases

`src/alias.rs` declares `NamesAdaptation`, implemented for every `Adapt<R, O>` and no blanket, and
`Uint<BITS, A, C = Abort>`, `Int<BITS, A, C = Abort>`, `USize<A, C = Abort>` and `ISize<A, C = Abort>`
over `<A as NamesAdaptation>::Adaptation`. The host doors are written on `Num` over any adaptation at
the platform width. `src/lib.rs` takes its new doc and exports `Sole`, the aliases and
`NamesAdaptation`, `Bool`, the checks, the identities and `Num`.

### CHANGE: the tests

`tests/common/mod.rs` runs the bands under `Quiet` over `Wrap` and `Saturate`; the band files follow,
`band_1_to_8.rs` reading `USize` through an alias with its adaptation named.
`tests/the_plain_markers_assert.rs` becomes `tests/the_debug_check.rs`: `Abort` aborts at `add` and
`adapted` under both policies, `Quiet` completes, `Abort` is quiet on what fits, and the two checks hold
one member in one size. The compile-fail cases are ported to the new spellings with their `.stderr`
regenerated; two new pairs, an outside slot family over part of a range refused beside one over a whole
range, and an outside policy that clamps refused beside one that wraps; the map-by-path refusal names
`Held`. `tests/consumer/src/main.rs` uses `Quiet`, and implements `NamesAdaptation` and `DebugCheck`
once each.
