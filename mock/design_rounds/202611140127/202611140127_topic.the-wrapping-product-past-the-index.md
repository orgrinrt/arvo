## What the design says now

The product's obligation refuses a format where a corner position leaves the 128-bit slot index, and that
is where the 64-bit ranges part: the signed range's largest position is `2^126` and is held, the unsigned
range's top corner is `(2^64 - 1)^2`, just under `2^128`, and is not. The open item says admitting it is a
widening of the slot coordinate past `i128`, which every computation over a slot would then owe. The design
says nothing of how a wrapping completion could be admitted without that widening. So the silence is named
here, and the admission below is the minimal one.

## What wrapping reads of a position past the index

The numerator is formed in four limbs over `D`, so its floor is known there even where the index does not
hold it. Wrapping reads only the floor's residue modulo the range's count, and the remainder over `D`, and
both are in the range: the floor reduced modulo the count onto the range, the remainder kept, is the
position `adapt` wraps to the same member as the true one. Rounding that takes it one slot up takes the
true floor one slot up too, and `adapt` wraps one past the top to the bottom as it wraps any position past
the range. So under wrapping the product needs no wider slot coordinate. It needs the floor, which the
step already has and dropped.

`land` reduces a floor past the index the same way since round `202611140055`. The reduction is one
function in `placing.rs`, read by both, and `Placed`'s past arm carries the floor and the remainder rather
than only the sign, so any step that places through `over` has them.

## The admission

The obligation moves to the signature for `mul`: a phase that denotes, a constant quantum and a step
formed at the four corners, as before, and the carried condition only where the policy does not wrap.
`product_position`, which returns a position without a policy, keeps the format's obligation whole, since
a position past the index has no `Exact` to be. `is_multipliable` keeps asking the format's question.

Saturation and clamping on the same range would be sound by the same reading: a floor past the index is
past the range on its side, and both pin at that end. They are left out because the ask is the wrapping
product; that is task `44c483a9f0d5`.

## Tests

The top corner `(2^64 - 1)^2` under wrapping on `UFixed<64, 0>` is `1`, and `(2^64 - 1) * 2` is
`2^64 - 2`, the residues the host's own `wrapping_mul` on `u64` gives; a sweep of pairs near both ends
against `u64::wrapping_mul`, and at a positive exponent against the same with the scale applied. The failing
control is the same sweep with the reduction replaced by the index's end, which is what the step produced
before, and has to disagree. A compile-fail pair: the saturating product over that range refused, the
wrapping one admitted, differing in the policy.
