## CHANGE: the floor past the index is kept

`Placed::Past` carries the floor and the residue over the divisor, both in the four limbs, where it carried
the sign. `over` fills them, `is_held` reads it as not held, and `positioned` saturates on the floor's
sign as before. `wrapped`, crate-private in `placing.rs` and generic over the slot range, reduces a
floor past the index modulo the range's count onto the range with its remainder kept, positions a held
one, and answers nothing for a step not formed.

## CHANGE: one policy check

`adapt::wraps`, crate-private, answers whether a signature's overflow policy wraps. `mul` reads it; this branch
carries no `land`.

## CHANGE: the product's obligations

`Multipliable::FORMED` holds the phase, the constant quantum and the formed step; `ADMITTED`, forced by
`product_position`, adds the carried condition under its existing FIXME. `MultipliableUnder<S>`, forced by
`mul`, holds `FORMED` and the carried condition unless `wraps::<S>()`, under a FIXME citing
`arvo_format_admits_the_saturating_and_clamping_product_past`. `mul` places the product and, when the
signature wraps, reads it through `wrapped`, falling back to the saturated end for a step not formed,
which only a slot that is not a member reaches. `is_multipliable` is unchanged.

## CHANGE: tests

`the_wrap_past_the_index.rs`: the top corner on `UFixed<64, 0>` wraps to 1 and `(2^64 - 1) 2` to
`2^64 - 2`; pairs within 40 of each end and three across the range at exponents 0, 3 and 63 against
`u64::wrapping_mul`. The compile-fail pair `multiplication_saturating_past_the_index_is_refused.rs`
with its `.stderr`, and `multiplication_wrapping_past_the_index_is_admitted.rs`, differing in the
policy, in `multiplication_over_an_uncarried_product_is_refused_and_one_bit_narrower_admitted`.

## The failing control

`wrapped` answering the index's end for a floor past it, which is what the step produced before, reddens
both wrapping-product tests. The two multiplication snapshots that
refuse at `FORMED` now name it, with the same messages.
