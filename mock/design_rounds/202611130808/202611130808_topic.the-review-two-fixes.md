# the-review-two-fixes

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** the fix-up after review pass two of `work/oracle-fraction-wide`, on the branch with `dev`
merged in, `arvo-numeral` among it.

## What pass two found

The rustdoc of `quotient_position` still says the one non-member that names the zero saturates toward
the positive end. No `Divisor` names that slot since round `202611130659`, and the branch is gone, so the
clause describes nothing.

The comment on the quotient's private step says every caller passes a `Divisor`'s slot or a member
`extreme_divisors` keeps. The test-only `unforced_position` passes raw slots too, and its callers skip
the pole, so the sentence names one door short.

The format's section on the platform-width obligation says no crate holds a value of any format yet and
that what one datum of a numeral stands for is an open question. With `dev` merged that is false on
both counts: `arvo-numeral` holds one member of a format's representable set at sole occupancy, and
`ruling::a_format_datum_is_one_point_and_a_saturating_end_is_one_point` settles a format's datum as one
point of its ambient domain. What a composition's datum stands for, what a value stores and how many
value types there are stay open, as the ruling says.

## What changes

The sentence in the design says what holds now: a format holds no value, the value crate does at sole
occupancy, a format's datum is one point, and the rest of the question is open. The rustdoc clause is
deleted, and the step's comment names the test door beside the other two. Nothing else moves.
