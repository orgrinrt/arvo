## CHANGE: `core::fmt::Debug for Bits<N, S, Sign>` in arvo-storage

Generic blanket impl bounded by `<S as BitsContainerFor<N, Sign>>::T: core::fmt::Debug`. Projects via reference-cast through the `repr(transparent)` layout (no transmute_copy needed for references).
