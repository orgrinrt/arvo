## The test gate failure, stated plainly because it is my own

`arvo-format` ships four properties as `matches!` expressions over an enumeration, and four tests
asserting what those expressions match:

```
is_monotone(policy)               = !matches!(policy, Policy::Wrap)
is_identity_inside_range(policy)  =  matches!(policy, Wrap | Saturate | Clamp)
is_deterministic(mode)            = !matches!(mode, Mode::Stochastic)
is_directed(mode)                 =  matches!(mode, TowardZero | Floor | Ceil)
```

**Nothing anywhere applies a `Policy` or a `Mode` to a value.** The only use of `Policy` outside a test
in the entire tree is the array literal `SHIPPED_POLICIES`. So every one of those tests reaches a
declaration and stops, which is the closed loop the test discipline names: a law relating two
declarations to each other constrains neither, and the test is whether the law reaches, through
however many hops, something the code does.

`is_identity_inside_range` is the purest form. It names all three variants of a three-variant
enumeration, so it is the constant `true` in a costume, and the test asserting it holds for all three
asserts that true is true.

**Measured rather than asserted**, in `mock/research/sketches/adaptation-gate/p1_the_shipped_law_surface_certifies_a_falsehood.rs`,
which carries a control that fails if the diagnosis is wrong. It states the monotonicity predicate
backwards, claiming wrapping transports order and saturation does not, which is false about arithmetic
in both directions. **With its own test updated to match, that backwards declaration is green.** The
probe then builds a real wrapping and a real saturating map over a small window and measures order
transport against them, and the measured answer disagrees with the backwards declaration at all three
policies and agrees with the shipped one at all three.

**So the shipped declarations are correct and nothing checked them.** That distinction is the whole
finding: the defect is not that they are wrong, it is that the suite would not have noticed if they
were, and I reported "49 passing" last round as though it meant something about them.

## Existence and locus, which the brief put in scope and which the answer is to keep

**This layer should not be its own crate, and the boundary the brief implied is the wrong one.**

An adaptation is a total map **onto the representable set**. It cannot be stated without the format,
because the set is what it maps onto, so a crate carrying it would be a crate that cannot be stated
without its parent. That is the same test round `202608311902` used to refuse splitting into five
crates, and it gives the same answer here.

`arvo-format` already carries `Adaptation`, `Adapt`, `Overflow`, `Rounding`, `Policy` and `Mode`, by
the design locked in that round. **Keeping them there is this round's result on locus**, and the
canon's ratified factoring is why: arithmetic on a format is an exact operation in the ambient domain
composed with a named total adaptation onto that set, which is one sentence about one thing.

**So the slice is smaller than it was framed.** It is not a layer to build. It is one missing verb on
a layer that exists.

## What is actually missing, which is the verb

The layer names an adaptation and never applies one. There is no function taking an exact result and
returning a member of the representable set, which is the thing the ratified factoring is about.

**One map with two regions rather than two mechanisms.** That is
`proposal::the_realisation_map_is_one_map_with_two_regions`, which stands at three or more
independent instances: rounding between grid points, completion outside the range, and a magnitude
bound switches off the completion and not the rounding while a grid bound does the reverse.

So the map takes an exact position and returns a slot, and the two regions are where the rounding mode
decides and where the overflow policy decides. Both regions are already parameterised, so this adds a
verb rather than a coordinate.

**The debug panic is the second missing piece**, bounded as above: the go-to for what the typestate
cannot catch, on debug builds, with the declared mode standing in its place on release. It is not an
overflow policy and cannot be one, because the ratified factoring requires an adaptation to be total
and a panic diverges. That was established in round `202608311902` and is unchanged.
