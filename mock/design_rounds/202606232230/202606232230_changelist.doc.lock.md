## CHANGE: arvo-strategy/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- After "## Const-trait arithmetic surface", add a "## Scalar euclidean raw contract" section: the inner
  per-width capability traits `ScalarEuclidRaw<N>` (signed) and `UScalarEuclidRaw<N>` (unsigned), separate
  from `IArith`/`UArith`. Macro-impl'd per strategy x native container (widths 1..=128), they supply the
  concrete-container operations the outer fixed-point euclid traits need: `div_euclid_scalar` /
  `rem_euclid_scalar` (raw container divided by an integer count, ULP granularity) and `div_euclid_whole` /
  `rem_euclid_whole` (a `FRAC` const carrier, fixed-point divisor, quotient floored to whole units). The
  bodies are strategy-independent (division shrinks magnitude, so no logical-bound clamp is needed even for
  Precise) with a div-by-zero guard returning the numerator. N > 128 (WideBits) is excluded, matching the
  arith surface (#320/#321). This is the array-`Capacity`-contract pattern: a contract for the specific
  capability, concrete impls per width, outer traits blanket-bound on it.

Backticked names introduced: `ScalarEuclidRaw`, `UScalarEuclidRaw`.

## CHANGE: arvo/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- In "## Composable traits", add three single-method-family traits, each separately importable and
  prelude-exported, blanket-impl'd over `IFixed<I,F,S>` / `UFixed<I,F,S>` (bound on the inner raw contract):
  - `ScalarEuclid`: `div_euclid_scalar(self, n: USize) -> Self`, `rem_euclid_scalar(self, n: USize) -> Self`.
    Euclidean division by an integer count at ULP granularity (the as-equal-as-possible split base/remainder).
  - `EuclidDiv`: `div_euclid(self, rhs: Self) -> Self`, `rem_euclid(self, rhs: Self) -> Self`. std-parity
    euclidean division by a `Self` divisor; quotient floored to whole fixed-point units (the coarse but
    conserving variant). rustdoc names the granularity difference from `ScalarEuclid`.
  - `EvenSplittable`: `split_evenly(self, n: USize) -> EvenShares<Self>`, a no-alloc iterator yielding `n`
    shares that sum to `self` exactly (the `self % n` remainder distributed one ULP to the first `r` shares
    by index, deterministic). Built on `ScalarEuclid`. `EvenShares<T>` is the iterator type.

Backticked names introduced: `ScalarEuclid`, `EuclidDiv`, `EvenSplittable`, `EvenShares`.
