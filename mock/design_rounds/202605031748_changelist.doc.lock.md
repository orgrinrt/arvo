Rewrite captures:

  parallel structs.
- Concrete W types in regular use: `Bits<64, Hot, Unsigned>` (formerly
  Mask64's payload) for one-word storage on Hot/Unsigned; `Bits<256,
  Hot, Unsigned>` (formerly Mask256's payload) for MultiContainer

- Width up to 256 supported by Round 2's `MultiContainer` BitPrim
  plumbing (#311). Wider widths require either WideBits-backed Bits
  (already shipped in #316) or new per-width container support; not in
  Round 3 scope.
- `arvo-mask-contracts::MaskOps<const W: Width>` is the abstract const
  trait surface; `arvo-bitmask::Mask<W>` is the concrete chassis. The
  rename eliminates the naming clash.
