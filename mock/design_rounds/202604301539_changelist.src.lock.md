## arvo-bits-contracts/src/widen.rs

### Update module doc and trait doc to bit-pattern-preserving contract

The module doc-comment (`//!`) at the top of `widen.rs` and the
`Widen<T>` trait doc-comment both currently claim sign-extension
semantics for sub-carrier-width signed `Bits<M, ...>` widening. The
impl does not implement that; it forwards through the carrier-side
`as` cast which preserves the carrier bit pattern. The DESIGN.md
prose is being aligned in the doc CL; this edit aligns the
in-source doc-comments to match.

Replace:

> "For sub-carrier-width `Bits<M, S, Signed>` the cross-Bits impl
> masks bits 0..M of the source carrier and sign-extends bit M-1,
> using M from impl context."

With prose matching the DESIGN.md.tmpl Widen-section update:

> "For sub-carrier-width `Bits<M, S, Sign>` the substrate's contract
> is bit-pattern-preserving on both `Narrow` and `Widen`. The cross-
> Bits forwarder calls the carrier-side primitive trait method
> without explicit M-bit sign-extension; bits above M in the source
> carrier are forwarded to the destination carrier as-is. For values
> constructed via `Narrow` (which zero-pads above the logical width
> via `(source as unsigned) & ((1 << N) - 1)`), this produces zeros
> above the logical width in the widened result regardless of the
> source's logical signedness."

In `widen_to`'s method-level doc, replace "Sign-extends for signed
source-target. For sub-carrier-width `Bits<M, ...>` source the body
masks bits 0..M before the cast." with: "Sign-extends at the
carrier's high bit for signed primitive sources. For sub-carrier-
width `Bits<M, ...>` source the body forwards the carrier bit
pattern through the carrier-side widen; sub-carrier high bits are
preserved as-is, not sign-extended at bit M-1."

In `widen_to_unmasked`'s method-level doc, drop the source-side mask
language (there is no source-side mask in the cross-Bits impl
anyway): the unmasked variant exists for symmetry with `Narrow`'s
unmasked variant and is hot-path equivalent to `widen_to` on this
trait family.
