## `mock/crates/arvo-bits/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

The packed word section. The declaration paragraph says that a field names `Value` alone, that a layout
names `Container` and `Fields`, a `notko_hlist` list of field markers from the low bit up, and that a
field's position is the sum of the widths listed before it, so a field names no layout and one marker
can sit in several. `Fields` stays, sealed, and folds the list into its summed width; `Below<P>`, sealed
through `Fields`, gives the position of the member at the place `P`. `Holds<F>` and the tuple list go.

The `Lane` paragraph keeps its content and adds that `Lane::WIDTH` for `Bits<N>` is `BitWidth::WIDTH`,
which forces the container's own refusal of its width.

The `Word` paragraph says that `get(field)` and `with(field, to)` take the field's marker by value, the
place inferred through `notko_hlist::PlaceOf`, so `Field` takes `Copy`; that `Field::Value` and
`Layout::Container` are bound `const Lane` on the declaring traits, so `get` and `with` carry no const
bound and are plain `const fn`s a consumer with no feature line calls in a constant; what that costs a
consumer with a value type of its own; and that a read keeps only the field's bits of what it moves down,
as a write does of what it puts.

The checking paragraph says that one assertion stays, the widths summing to exactly the container's,
and why it is equality; that each field's width and the container's width are asserted one to sixty-four
because `Lane` is open; that an unlisted field is refused at type check as a member the list has no
place for, and a field listed twice at the access as an ambiguous place; and that overlap and a field
past its container cannot be written.

The surface paragraph names `Below<P>` in place of `Holds<F>`, the `notko_hlist` list in place of the
tuple, and the dependency on `notko-hlist`.

The laws paragraphs say the laws run over every container width from one to sixty-four as well as over
the handle, its wide twin and the 64-bit edges; that one field type in two layouts reads at its place in
each; that a consumer crate with no feature line reads and writes in a constant; that the refusals
pinned are the short sum, an unlisted field, a field listed twice, a field and a container of width zero
and of width sixty-five, and an impl of the fold by hand; and that the two mutant flags stay.
