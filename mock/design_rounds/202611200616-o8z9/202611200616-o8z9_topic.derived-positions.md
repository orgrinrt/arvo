## Decision 1: a field's position is derived from the list order, low bit first

The sketch `202611200614_packed-word-const-reach` takes the second seat's shape over the real `Bits`, `Bool` and `notko-hlist` and it builds. A field names only `Value`; a layout names `Container` and `Fields`, a `notko_hlist` list of field markers from the low bit up; a field's position is the sum of the widths listed before it. The widths in order are the whole content of a layout, and offsets declared beside them stored it twice, which is why the old shape needed an overlap check, a cover check and a membership by place and width, and why the review found the last admitting an alias of another value and a field of another layout. Derived, overlap and a field past its container cannot be written, membership is `notko_hlist::PlaceOf` by type, and one check stays: the widths sum to exactly the container's width, which also refuses a dropped or resized field moving the ones above it. A field names no layout, so one marker can sit in several layouts. The second seat names what would argue for declared offsets, a real layout whose reserved gaps outnumber its named fields; hilavitkutin's two shapes have no gap. `BELOW`, `Holds` and the tuple list go.

A field is passed to `get` and `with` as its marker value, `word.get(Kind)`, `word.with(Kind, to)`, the place inferred, so `Field` takes `Copy` as a supertrait. A field listed twice is refused at the access as an ambiguous place.

## Decision 2: the const impl is required once, on the declaring traits

The sketch shows the branch's accessors refused from a consumer crate with no feature line, `E0658` "cannot call conditionally-const method", which is `obligation::the_unstable_machinery_does_not_reach_a_consumer` failing. `Field::Value` and `Layout::Container` are bound `const Lane`, and `get` and `with` carry no const bound and are plain `const fn`s, which the same consumer calls in a constant. A consumer whose own type is a field's value writes a `const impl` of `Lane` and carries the gate in its own crate; one using `Bits` and `Bool` carries nothing. A test with no feature line pins it.

## Decision 3: an open `Lane`'s width is asserted where the list is folded

`Bits` refuses a width of zero or past sixty-four itself, and `Lane::WIDTH` for `Bits<N>` is read through `BitWidth::WIDTH`, which forces that refusal. `Lane` is open, so a consumer's own type can claim any width; the fold asserts each field's width is one to sixty-four and `Word` asserts the container's, each with its own message and its own `compile_fail` case.
