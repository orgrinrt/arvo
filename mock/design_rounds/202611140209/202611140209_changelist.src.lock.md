# Src changelist: the remainder has no unwrap door, and the wrapping product keeps the floor's sign and parity

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140209`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140209_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/` and `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/`.

## CHANGE: nothing reads the remainder's pair back out

`src/apply/position.rs`: `Remainder::numerator` and `Remainder::denominator`, the two `Maybe<u128>`
doors, are removed, and the type's rustdoc says nothing reads the pair back out and that a consumer
observes a remainder through `Exact::is_on_grid`, `Exact::is_tie` and `adapt`. No crate code read
either door. `tests/ui/the_quotient_and_conversion_surface_is_reached_from_the_root.rs` reads the
remainder through the position instead: a third is neither a tie nor on the grid, a half is a tie,
and a zero denominator reads as on the grid.

## CHANGE: wrapping reduces the floor modulo twice the count on its own side of zero

`src/placing.rs`, `wrapped`: the count is formed in `Wide` from `S::MAX` and `S::MIN` without negating
`S::MIN`, so a range from the bottom of the index no longer overflows. The floor past the index is
reduced modulo twice the count into `[0, 2 count)`, then moved into `[-2 count, 0)` when the floor is
negative, and handed to `adapt` with its remainder. That keeps the floor's class modulo the count, its
sign and its parity, which are what the six modes and the wrap read. The rustdoc says so in place of
the sentence claiming wrapping reads only the residue.

`src/multiplication.rs`: the rustdoc of `MultipliableUnder` and the obligation's message say modulo
twice the count.

Failing control, run on the tree before the fix with the new suite in place:
`the_wrap_by_every_mode::a_negative_tie_toward_zero_wraps_from_its_own_floor` got `2^63 - 3` where the
reference is `2^63 - 2`; `a_range_from_the_bottom_of_the_index_wraps_without_a_panic` panicked at
`placing.rs` on `-lo`; `a_tie_on_an_odd_count_goes_to_its_own_floor_s_even_neighbour` got the two
members one apart from the reference's; `the_wrapping_product_is_the_reference_under_every_mode` had
six strays under half to even at the odd count at the top. All four pass after it.

## CHANGE: the verdict on a declared signature

`src/multiplication.rs`: `is_multipliable_under<S: DeclaredSignature>() -> Bool`, the three conditions
every product owes and the carried one only where the policy does not wrap, in the order `mul` forces
them. `src/lib.rs` re-exports it beside `is_multipliable`.
`the_wrap_past_the_index::the_signature_verdict_turns_on_the_policy_past_the_index` holds it on both
sides: `UFixed<64, 0>` admitted under wrapping and refused under saturation and clamping, `UFixed<64, -1>`
admitted under both, and `UFixed<64, -300>`, whose step leaves the four limbs, refused under wrapping
too. Failing control: the test did not compile before the function existed.

## CHANGE: the clamping product refused beside the saturating one

`tests/ui/multiplication_clamping_past_the_index_is_refused.rs` and its `.stderr`, registered in
`tests/compile_fail.rs` beside the saturating case; its twin that compiles is
`multiplication_wrapping_past_the_index_is_admitted.rs`, differing in the policy only. The two
existing files' comments and the saturating `.stderr` follow the obligation's new message.

## CHANGE: the wrapping product under every mode

`src/multiplication/tests/the_wrap_by_every_mode.rs`, registered in `src/multiplication/tests/mod.rs`:
`mul` under wrapping against a reference in `Big` that reads no step code, under all six modes and
three dithers, at a half and a third and a quarter phase, signed and unsigned, on ranges at the top and
the bottom of the index, on odd and even counts, at the ends, beside them, the middle and zero. Its own
control, `the_reference_tells_the_modes_apart`, shows the reference separates toward zero from floor
and half to even from half up on the pairs swept.

## CHANGE: the IEEE harness

`tests/ieee754/mod.rs`: `round_off` refuses a significand of `-2^127`; the underflow draws run from one
past the first unit past the index to twenty-one below the smallest subnormal, so no draw falls where
the search hands its stand-in. `tests/ieee754_underflow_parity.rs`: the decimal64 sweep is dropped, since
the decimal suite already takes these inputs through the shared ones; its pinned point and its wrong
direction control stay. The module doc says what is swept and where.
