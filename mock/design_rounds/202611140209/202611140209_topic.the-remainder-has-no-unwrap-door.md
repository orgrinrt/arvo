# The remainder has no unwrap door

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Scope:** `arvo-format`'s `Remainder::numerator` and `Remainder::denominator`, the design paragraph that
names them, and the root-surface compile case that reads them.

Two reviews of the format branch read the two accessors against `no-bare-primitives`. Both return the
host's unsigned 128-bit integer inside a `Maybe`, and the doc comment calls each an unwrap door, declared
as one. The first review reads them as a violation outright. The second reads them as unwrap doors that
the rule's six definition-site exceptions do not list, so also a violation. The rule's exceptions are a
closed list, and an unwrap door is not on it. Coord took the two readings as settled for this half of
ticket `388c088fee6c` and asked for the fix on this branch: an arvo carrier, or no door at all where
nothing needs one. The other half, `Scaled::of`'s parameters, belongs to `land`, which this branch does
not carry.

Nothing needs them. `Exact` hands no `Remainder` out: its public reads are `slot`, `is_on_grid` and
`is_tie`. So the accessors only ever read back the pair a caller passed to `Remainder::of`, which the
caller already holds, and inside the crate nothing calls them; the remainder's own comparisons are
crate-private. The one reader is the compile case that checks the surface is reached from the root.

There is no arvo carrier to hand the pair out in, either. `arvo-format` depends on no crate with an
integer type, the remainder's parts reach `2^256 - 1`, and the crate has no public four-limb integer, a
decision its open items already leave until a consumer asks for one. A carrier would be a new type with
nothing reading it.

So the two accessors go, with the design sentence that names them, and the compile case reads the
remainder through what a consumer can observe: a third is not a tie, a half is, and a zero denominator
reads as the grid point.

The same rule read across the crate finds thirteen more public reads returning a host integer:
`Slot::index`, `SlotCount::count`, `Exponent::power`, `Magnitude::index`, `MagnitudeCount::count`,
`Phase::numerator` and `denominator`, `Width::count`, `Bool::get`, `Radix::base`, `Fraction::numerator`
and `denominator`, and `Arity::count`. Each is the read of one of the crate's own coordinate types, the
bottom of the tower this crate is, and neither review raised them. Whether the bottom-out exception
covers the read as well as the field is the question the `Scaled::of` half already asks of op, so they
are left as they are under task `5ce2bb7e67a4` rather than changed on one reading.
