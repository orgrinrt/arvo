# Doc changelist: the inventory of conventions gets a crate, and its first member

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Round:** the standards-alias round
**Scope:** one new crate's design and readme, and one sentence in the root design.

## What this changelist is

`obligation::every_standard_convention_expressible_as_an_alias_over_the_primitives` wants each
industrial convention writable as a first-class alias over arvo's own primitives, with a parity suite
running the same computation through both. Nothing in the tree answers it, and the floor it would be
written over is built.

So a design lands for the layer that holds those aliases, and for its first member, MATLAB's `fi` and
`fimath`. It says what a convention alias is, where the parity bar sits, and what the alias does when
the convention names something the floor cannot express.

## The canon this declares

`ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon`, ratified, for the two halves this rests on. Arithmetic on a
format is an exact operation in the ambient domain composed with a named total adaptation, which is
what makes `fimath` an adaptation rather than a setting. And the concept is closed while the inventory
of admitted instances is open, which is what makes an alias an admission rather than an amendment.

`ruling::behaviour_is_stated_per_declared_signature_and_the_premise_dissolves`, ratified, which
ratifies `proposal::every_operation_arvo_declares_is_a_function_of_the_declared_width`. That proposal
carries the typed edge to this obligation and its `because` is the adequacy argument the whole round
is built on.

`ruling::the_standards_bound_starts_at_two_and_reserves_the_rest`, op's, for the scope: MATLAB and
IEEE 754 are the core and the other three named conventions are reserved rather than excluded.

`ruling::the_standard_is_parity_in_output_not_in_the_internals`, op's, for the bar: parity in output
rather than in internals, and a standard may ship with marked holes because the framework is what has
to be exhaustive.

`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path`, ratified, for why a convention the floor cannot
express is refused by the type system rather than reported by a constructor.

## Why a crate rather than a module in `arvo-format`

`arvo-format` already ships instances of its own concept in `points`, so instances living beside the
concept has precedent here and the question is real rather than rhetorical.

The split follows the ratified sentence. The concept is closed and the inventory is open, and those
are two different things with two different futures: the concept is four contracts that do not grow,
and the inventory is five named conventions of which two are in scope and three are reserved, each
carrying marked holes for as long as the floor has gaps. Putting a growing, hole-carrying inventory
inside the crate that defines the closed concept makes one crate mean both, and the first thing that
gets read wrong is which half a `FIXME` is about.

The dependency edge stays honest either way: the crate needs `arvo-format` and nothing else. Stating a
convention is stating what values exist, so it reaches for the declared signature and never for a
placement or a strategy, and it sits beside `arvo-placement` rather than above it.

`points` stays where it is. Those four are the parameterisation points the canon names, so they are
the concept worked rather than an inventory, and moving them would say the opposite.

## The bar a parity arm has to meet, stated so the source changelist can be checked against it

An arm cites the convention's own documented answer, not a value read back out of arvo. Where MathWorks
documents what `fi` returns for an input, that number is the assertion and arvo is what has to agree
with it. An arm that computes both sides through arvo has asserted that arvo agrees with itself.

## What is deliberately not here

**IEEE 754.** In scope by op's bound and not in this round. Its rounding surface sits behind two open
registry rows, and its interchange formats want the indexed quantum family, which is a different
reading of the same floor and wants its own round.

**Any change to the rounding vocabulary.** `question::is_the_rounding_vocabulary_complete_at_six` and
`question::which_tie_direction_an_unqualified_nearest_names` are open and are the panel's. The design
records that the convention needs a mode the floor does not name and stops there.

**Any change to the width ladder.** The design records that the convention declares widths the floor
refuses and stops there.

**No change to `arvo-format`, `arvo-placement` or `arvo-strategy`.** The alias is written over the
surface those already have. If it could not be, that would be the finding, and the round would be
about the floor instead.
