# Topic: the source gate is still untested, third filing

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `mock/lints/`, and two mockspace lints
**Status:** re-filed, unchanged, owned elsewhere

Third filing of one subject, and the reason each time is the same mechanism rather than new work.
`cargo mock close` sweeps every loose flat file in `design_rounds/` into the closing round's archive,
whatever round the file belonged to, so a filing left deliberately in the way is taken out of the way
by the next round that closes.

The previous filing said exactly that would happen. It then happened, to that filing, on the next
close. So the sentence is evidence now rather than a prediction, and it is the whole argument for the
fix belonging to mockspace rather than to whoever keeps re-filing.

The content is at
`mock/design_rounds/202608312030/202608312008_topic.the-source-gate-is-still-untested.md` and the
original at
`mock/design_rounds/202608311957/202608311948_topic.the-source-gate-is-untested-and-two-of-it-is-broken.md`.

**In one paragraph so this is not a bare pointer.** Five lints gate every line of source under
`mock/crates/` and none has a test: `no_std_enforcer`, `no_alloc_enforcer`, `no_dynamic_dispatch`,
`no_runtime_grow`, `arvo_bits_traits_only`. Two have measured holes, `no_std_enforcer` missing every
qualified path expression and `no_alloc_enforcer` matching against the untrimmed line. The other three
are unexamined, which is not the same as sound. Two further defects sit in mockspace's own lints:
`undocumented-type` cannot be satisfied by a type name with no lowercase letter, and `no-std` reads
only the first thirty lines of a crate root.

**Owned elsewhere.** The two measured holes are with the agent on the lint suites; the two mockspace
defects belong to that repository. Nothing here is this arc's to fix, and what is this arc's is that
the subject stays visible.
