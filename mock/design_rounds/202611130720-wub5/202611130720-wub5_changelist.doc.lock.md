`ruling::the_derivation_is_a_placement_and_the_operation_set_is_an_admission_rule`: both presuppose a

## CHANGE: the ruling, narrowed to the datum clause

`ruling::a_format_datum_is_one_point_and_a_saturating_end_is_one_point`. The row is new on this branch,
so the id changes with the clause it no longer carries; every citation in the registry and the designs
follows, and the locked changelist of the round before keeps the old id as the record of what it said.

`says` keeps the datum clause, the set-valued arm and the saturating end, and drops "a type may hold a
datum of a format as a value at sole occupancy, and such a type is the placement derivation's own
sole-occupancy arm". `promotion` says plainly that the first read's questions reached it worded toward
their answers, quoting its note, that its answers are argued from the rows but its wording was handed
to it, and that the second read derived before opening it; so the sole-occupancy clause had one cold
read and is not ratified. `because` drops the sole-occupancy grounds. `gap` gains the clause as a
statement of its own, at one cold read, and names the two presupposing rows as what a value type
builds on. `note` records that an earlier wording on this branch ratified the clause and that the
coordinator narrowed the gate. The provenance anchor of the second read's sole-occupancy section goes.
The section header loses "and the type that holds one".

`question::what_a_datum_stands_for` changes only the id it cites. The value proposal's note says the
ruling ratifies the datum clause only, that the two rows presuppose the sole-occupancy type, and that

## CHANGE: `arvo-placement`, what the carrier obligation guarantees

`## The sole carrier is also a type` no longer says the crate building a value forces `ADMITTED` and so
stops an outside wrong row. `ADMITTED` is provided, so an outside row may replace it with a constant
that checks nothing; a crate needing the agreement asserts `carrier_agrees` itself.

## CHANGE: `arvo-numeral`, licence, seals, admission, proof

The canon list cites the two presupposing rows as the licence for a value type, says the clause as a
statement of its own is open with one cold read, cites the narrowed ruling for what the value holds,
and cites the stated row and the check proposal for the debug check. `## How the value holds its
carrier` says the same where it names its choices. The seals kept on `Machine` and `Held` cite

`## What a value signature admits`, item 1: the row's agreement is asserted by `Sole::ADMITTED` as
