# Doc changelist: addition admitted on a declared signature

Consumes `202609111010_topic.addition-admitted-on-a-declared-signature.md`, the one open topic, including
the section it gained after the first doc changelist locked. It rests on the sketch
`mock/research/sketches/202609111010_addition-obligation-and-window/`, which establishes that the
obligation shape refuses at the tool the round pins it with, that a window family of slot ranges builds
without `generic_const_exprs`, that the exact step reproduces the canon's witness counts, and that the
carry refusal is reachable only from an outside format.

## Comparison to deprecated changelist

`202609111027_changelist.doc.deprecated.md` carried the whole of the design below except three things, and
its template edits landed while it was active, in `915c26d9` and `abf0b845`. They stand: nothing here
takes any of them back.

What it missed, found while planning the source against it:

- A third refusal. A phase declared over `i64::MIN` with an odd numerator has a fractional part whose
  reduced denominator is `2^63`, which no `Fraction` holds. `Fraction::of` saturates it, at the largest
  odd numerator to exactly one, and `Exact::between` carries that into the slot, so the position lands a
  whole slot above the sum. Built to the deprecated changelist, the source would have had to refuse this
  without the design saying so, or return the wrong slot on an admitted format. A zero denominator is the
  other pair with no remainder, and addition's obligation carries that condition itself rather than
  forcing `Format::ADMITTED`, whose force sites the design names as exactly two.
- The force sites. It named `sum_position` as the verb forcing the obligation. `addition_reach` and
  `addition_is_associative` compute the same positions and would otherwise narrow them into a `Slot` for a
  format whose sum leaves the coordinate.
- The operands. `add` takes slots, and nothing makes a slot a member. The carry obligation bounds the sum
  of members only, so for two slots outside the range the wide sum can leave the coordinate, and
  narrowing it with a cast would wrap without a word. The step saturates it into the coordinate instead,
  which keeps `add` total, and the design states what that costs under wrapping.

Everything else is carried over unchanged, and is listed again below so this changelist stands alone.
