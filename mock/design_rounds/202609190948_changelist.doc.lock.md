## `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

"The index's host integer": the list of what saturates is stated as the whole list (non-member
positions, chains of carries), and a new paragraph says the applied map computes no saturating position:
rounding answers with the slot at or below and a step bit, the completion's in-range test, wrap and
saturation are stated over that pair, and `panic_on_overflow` asks the same question of it. The
previous claim that the wrap was exact "at every position the integer holds", which the saturated step
falsified, is replaced by exact at every position rounding can name.

"Where the exact step happens": the intermediate is described as the pair, one position wider than a
`Slot`, and "widening it removes the case" becomes carrying the intermediate in a form that holds every
position the step reaches, which for rounding is the pair and not a wider integer. The two sections now
give one account.

The quantum-law paragraph no longer cites "the slot range's own count condition", which is gone; it says
the span condition gets the same guarantee by checked arithmetic.

The capacity paragraph states only the canon: no capacity type is built, the platform-width ruling puts
one out of its scope, the door ruling says what a capacity is and not what type spells it, and the
obligation excepts a const generic parameter with the bare form used only where genuinely painful.
Whether a capacity is such a position is left unanswered. The `adt_const_params` fact stays, as a fact
about this crate.

The platform-width laws no longer claim the alias is compiled at all three pointer widths. They say which
arms any check can compile, that the 16-bit test arms cannot be compiled because no 16-bit target has a
test harness and are marked where they stand, and that one ignored catalogue test runs the checks that
can run.

## `PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl`

"No `.0` field-access": `USize`, `ISize` and `UFixed` leave the list of value types, with a paragraph
saying they are zero-sized format points with no constants, constructor, arithmetic or field, and that
the value question is `question::what_a_datum_stands_for`, open. The `USize::ONE`/`ZERO`/`MAX`, the
`Deref` on `USize` and the `USize(N)` constructor examples go. The type taxonomy's newtype row drops
`UFixed` and `USize`. The primitive table's `usize` row says the points name the format and no value
type exists, and states the exception with its bound. "Sizes are const" restores the bound and drops
"rather than a stack type standing in for it".
