## CHANGE: the alias is swept against the integer rule, with six planted spellings
File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/the_ties_away_alias.rs` (new)
The sweep the design names. Both spellings against `sign(x) floor(|x| + 1/2)` computed as a step above
the slot, itself checked against a brute-force reading of the whole position wherever that fits, then
completed over the format's range. Slots at both ends of the index and around zero, denominators of both
parities from one to `i64::MAX`, numerators at both ends and at the midpoint, `Integer<8>` and
`Integer<64>` under `Wrap` and `Saturate`, and a second domain whose numerators carry out of `[0, 1)`.
Six planted spellings, each required to be reported: `half_up` alone, `toward_zero` where `floor`
belongs, a zero slot read as negative, the shift always upward, the shift composed with `floor`, and the
doubled shift. The one region where `shift` cannot be formed is asserted as a region rather than as three
cells, with the tie-freeness of that region asserted beside it.
Verification: `cargo test -p arvo-format --test the_ties_away_alias` passes; commenting out any one
planted spelling's arm fails it.

## CHANGE: a lint refusing a published surface that names the mode without the note
Files: `mock/lints/half_up_carries_its_note.rs` (new),
`mock/lints/half_up_carries_its_note/tests.rs` (new)
`half-up-carries-its-note`, at hard error. It reads the repository's root `README.md`, every `.md.tmpl`
under the mock directory and every doc comment in a crate's own `src/` there, outside `research/`,
`design_rounds/`, `agent/`, `target/` and hidden directories. A markdown document naming any spelling of
the mode carries `HALF_UP` somewhere in it. A doc passage carries it where the passage states the
denotation, which is where it names the mode, in its own words or in the item it sits on, and says the
tie goes toward positive infinity or writes `floor(x + q/2)`. It does not read the registry, the
generated documents under `docs/`, plain comments, or a crate's `tests/` and `benches/`, none of which
rustdoc prints. The spellings and the passage reader come from the sibling lint rather than a second
copy, whose `SPELLINGS`, `spellings_in` and `passages` widen to `pub(crate)` for it, and `Passage` gains
a field saying whether rustdoc prints it.
Verification: `cargo test --manifest-path mock/target/mockspace-lints/Cargo.toml half_up_carries`
passes; `cargo mock --lint-only` reports every lint passing over this tree; planting the note-free
sentence in a template and in a doc comment fires, and the same sentence with the note does not.

## CHANGE: the magnitude lint's reader binds an escape to what it governs
File: `mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule/reading.rs`
A clause is cut into segments at `,`, `(`, `)`, `{` and `}`. A negator voids a pairing where it sits in
the reading's segment on either side of the reading, or in the name's segment ahead of the name, or
between the two where the reading comes first, since a negator opening a list after the assertion takes
every name in that list out of it. A conjunction between the negator and what it would void ends its
reach in all three. `listed_apart` becomes a test over bare items: three segments or more, the reading's
item holding nothing but the reading, and a neighbouring item holding at most one word, which is what
makes a run of commas a list rather than a sentence with an aside in it. `in magnitude` counts as a
reading only behind a direction word, and a reading inside a code span with a naming word beside it is a
mention rather than a reading. A clause with no name of its own takes the previous clause's where it
opens with a pronoun, bounded at a blank line and at anything else the previous clause named after the
mode.
Verification: the corpus below passes, and every mutant of the new arms is caught, which is
`mock/research/sketches/the-half-up-lint-reader-mutants/`.

## CHANGE: `term_at` FROM a lookup over both vocabularies TO `reading_at` over one
Files: `mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule/reading.rs`,
`mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule/tests.rs`,
`mock/lints/half_up_is_not_a_magnitude_rule/sentences.rs`
Mutating the reader found this rather than a missing test. `term_at` looked a term up among the
spellings and the readings and folded case to do it, and it is reached from one place, on a reading's
own offset, with every reading written lower case already: both the spellings half and the folding were
unreachable, so breaking either changed nothing anywhere. It becomes `reading_at` over the readings, and
the invariant that makes the folding unnecessary is asserted in
`every_reading_is_written_lower_case_so_a_lowered_clause_can_be_searched_for_it`, with the same filter
run over the spellings as its control. Four more pairs join the corpus, one for each arm that survived
its mutant: the alias clause from `symmetry/tests/the_classification.rs`, the reflection partition from
`arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, a negator in an aside between the name and the reading, and a list item
carrying more than the reading it holds. Three of those four are prose this repository carries, and the
arms holding them were driven in by watching the lint over the tree rather than by writing the sentence
down, so nothing but the tree was holding them.
Verification: the corpus runs to 25 pairs and the count is asserted; each of the fourteen mutants in
`mock/research/sketches/the-half-up-lint-reader-mutants/out/runs.txt` fails at least one test.
