## What the canon says, and only that

`ruling::a_platform_width_type_is_a_target_bound_member_of_the_format_family`, in its note: "`Cap` is out
of scope. The question's own keywords carry it beside `USize`, and it names a different position, a
const-generic capacity, which `ruling::the_numeric_door_carries_the_coordinate_set_and_the_two_type_bound_is_not_canon`
already answers as a coordinate the door carries rather than a numeral. Nothing here touches it."

`obligation::a_primitive_for_every_position_a_bare_number_would_take`: "A const generic parameter is
excepted, and even there the bare form is used only where the alternative is genuinely painful." Op's
words in its `quote`: "No bare usize other than in const generics for smoother and more ergonomic api,
and even there, only when truly painful otherwise."

No row rules against a capacity type and no row rules for one. The platform-width ruling puts it outside
its own scope, the door ruling says what a capacity is (a coordinate, not a numeral) and not what type
spells it, and the obligation excepts the const-generic position with a bound on the exception.

## What round `202609190825` said instead, and where it still stands

Its topic, both its changelists, `rules/cookbook.md.tmpl` ("there is no `Cap` type, by ruling"),
`PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl` ("it takes the host's `usize` rather than a stack type standing in for it"),

platform-width ruling puts one out of its scope, a const-generic position is the obligation's excepted
position with the bare form used only where the alternative is genuinely painful, and whether a capacity
position is such a case is not answered here.

## What `USize` and `ISize` are, against what the surfaces say they are

The shipped points are aliases, `USize = UFixed<{ usize::BITS }, 0>` and `ISize = Integer<{ usize::BITS }>`,
and both name zero-sized format markers. They hold no value. The older principles and agent templates
describe a `USize` value newtype that is not in the tree: `USize::ONE`, `USize::ZERO` and `USize::MAX`,
a `USize(N)` constructor, `impl const` arithmetic, a `Deref` to `usize`, "newtype, fixed rep, validation
at construction", `get(USize) -> &T` indexing, and a review table that tells an agent to replace a
`usize` with `arvo_format::points::USize`, which at a value position compiles to a zero-sized field
carrying nothing.

The value half is `question::what_a_datum_stands_for`, open, with the panel as its `decider`, and the
option set one op refused to bound. So the surfaces say
what the points are, say that no value type of either exists, and say the question is unanswered. They
do not describe a value type, and they do not pick one of the options that question carries.
