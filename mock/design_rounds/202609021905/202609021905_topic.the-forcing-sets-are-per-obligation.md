# The forcing sets are per obligation, and `contains` has one

## What is wrong

The previous round replaced a claim about when the format's obligation fires
with a claim about which verbs force it. That part holds and a reviewer
re-derived it. What it also wrote, in five places, is that `contains` forces
nothing, and that is false.

`contains` is `magnitude_in_range::<F::Quantum>(magnitude).and(slot_in_range::<F::Slots>(slot))`
at `format.rs:256`. `magnitude_in_range` opens with `let () = Q::ADMITTED;` at
`quantum.rs:381` and `slot_in_range` with `let () = S::ADMITTED;` at
`slots.rs:306`. So `contains` forces two obligations and not the two the round
was about.

The claim that survives is narrower and is the one the round needed: `contains`
reaches a value without meeting `Format::ADMITTED` or `Ambient::ADMITTED`. The
sweeping version is the same class of error the round was opened to fix, written
while fixing it.

A second claim contradicts the round's own new text 68 lines later in the design.
The new paragraph says the four obligations have different forcing sets; the
older one still says each fires exactly where the slot range's does. Measured,
the four sets are pairwise disjoint from the slot range's.

## What this round changes

- The five sites stating that `contains` forces nothing, narrowed to name which
  obligations it does force and which it does not.
- The surviving sentence in the design that says each obligation fires where the
  slot range's does.
- One test comment that generalises from the format's obligation to all of them,
  which is what let the fixture pass without entering the path that would expose
  it.
- A `trybuild` arm for `cancelling_slot`, the other of the two verbs forcing
  `Format::ADMITTED`. Only `has_additive_identity` is pinned today, so deleting
  the force site at `format.rs:327` leaves the suite green.

## What it does not change

The forcing-set claim itself, which is correct and was verified independently.
The three runtime arms on the routes, which are correct about `Format::ADMITTED`.
