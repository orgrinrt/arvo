## Why three crates and not one, and not five

The decomposition is this round's call and it is derived from the floor rather than carried from the
tree that was deleted. The floor separates two things that had been argued about as one: what
behaviour is stated over, and what the carrier comes out of. That separation is the seam, and a third
crate sits on top of both because something has to bind a name to a choice.

| Crate | What it is | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| `arvo-format` | The declared signature: the representable set as an affine predicate, and the adaptation that maps an exact result onto it. What behaviour is stated over. | `notko` |
| `arvo-placement` | The placement derivation, keyed on occupancy. The carrier at sole occupancy; the carrier, access width and stride at shared. No operation reads it. | `arvo-format` |
| `arvo-strategy` | The binding from a name to a placement objective and an adaptation selection. The presets are instances of an open inventory. | `arvo-format`, `arvo-placement` |

**Not one crate**, because the floor's whole content is that the declared signature and the placement
are different objects, and putting them in one compilation unit invites an operation to read the
carrier, which is the thing the MATLAB divergence refutes. The dependency edge is what enforces it:
`arvo-format` cannot name a carrier because it does not depend on the crate that has one.

**Not five**, because splitting the format from the adaptation, or the signature from either, produces
crates that cannot be stated without each other. The adaptation maps onto the representable set, so it
needs the format in the same breath; the signature is the pair. One topic, one crate.

## What is deliberately not here

**The chain**, which is `topic::the_chain` and out of this round's scope.

**The strategy object's component count**, which is `topic::the_strategy_object` and out of scope.

**Which mode each preset names.** `does_warm_wrap_or_clamp` is open in the registry, and
`ruling::the_strategy_intents_are_not_clear_cut` forbids writing the per-strategy statements as clear
cut and settled. The design carries the axis and the binding mechanism; it does not fill in a table of
four names against four behaviours.

**Every other open registry question the topic file lists**, each named there rather than filled here.
The correction this round was given after its refusal is that where the registry holds a question
open, the design says so and stops.

**Any arm.** This changelist writes the concepts the arms will be gated over. An arm carries a
predicate over a region and its own justification in words, and none is written until there is a
measurement to justify it.
