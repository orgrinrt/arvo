# Topic: the base primitive design is blocked on the container premise, and the block is checkable

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** the base numeric primitives, meaning what a format is as a type, how the six rounding
modes and the overflow policies are carried, and how a strategy selects among them
**Status:** refused at the canon gate, filed so the next round has to deal with it

This round was opened to take arvo's base numeric primitives from the ratified canon through the
design ceremony. It did not get past the canon gate, and this file records why, with the compiler
output that makes the reason checkable rather than arguable. The round stays in TOPIC. No doc
changelist was authored, no template was edited, no source was written.

The short version is that the design's first sentence, the one that says what parameters the format
type carries, is a sentence the canon says is not writable yet, and writing it anyway would answer
two open canon questions as a side effect of choosing a type signature.

## What is ratified and what it reaches

The format spine is ratified and it is real ground. `ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon` carries four
propositions, and the two that bear here say that a format is identified by its ambient domain and
its representable set, and that membership in that set is one affine predicate over one
parameterisation with a slot function, a quantum per magnitude and a phase. That fixes what a format
*is*. `ruling::the_ambiguous_rounding_word_is_retired_for_six_explicit_names` fixes the rounding
vocabulary at six names. `ruling::the_overflow_panic_is_permitted_and_bounded` fixes the panic to
debug builds and puts a declared mode in its place on release.

What none of them fixes is the type. A format's identity being the pair does not say how many type
parameters the Rust type has, and that is a separate call the canon has a separate row for.

There is one place the canon hands a surface call to a design round explicitly, and it is worth
naming because it is the thing that made this round look ready.
`question::which_width_coordinates_a_consumer_writes` is answered, and its answer says the projection
choice, which pair a consumer writes at the surface, "belongs to the design round for the format".
So a design round on the coordinate surface is licensed. The parameterisation is a different
question and it is not.

## What blocks it

`topic::the_container_premise` says it in the canon's own words:

> Whether a declared numeral's behaviour is stated over its declared width or over the container that
> carries it. **Blocking: no wording of several downstream clauses is true on both branches.**

`question::the_container_premise` carries no `answered` field. Its `unblocks` reads "The statement of
what a primitive is, which is the panel's central noun and which four topics have deferred to." Six
proposal rows answer it, all from seat 210, all at `standing = "one_expert"`, and no ruling ratifies
any of them. Seat 221 read them and said the thing that settles their standing, in
`221_dolan_the_numeric_fundamentals.md` under "The container premise, and why I am not a second
instance on it":

> `question::the_container_premise` is answered by six rows, all of them seat 210's, and **seat 210 is
> this persona.** [...] **A genuinely independent second read is owed and it should not be Dolan.**

So under `ruling::two_experts_converging_is_a_ratification_and_the_coordinator_holds_the_gate` the
premise stands at one expert and is not canon. And this seat cannot be the second instance either,
because it read the six rows before forming any view of its own, which makes anything it says
confirmation from a second reader of one page rather than an independent arrival.

The consequence lands directly on the design's first line.
`proposal::an_axis_the_realisation_map_does_not_read_is_not_a_type_parameter` is the row that would
say what parameters the format type carries, and its own `note` says the sentence is missing:

> Under one branch of the container premise the clause's first sentence is false and no wording is
> true on both branches until that is ruled on, so the first sentence is not in this file at all.

The sentence that is not in the file is "an axis the realisation map does not read must not be a type
parameter". That is the rule deciding whether the carrier, and with it the strategy that derives it,
is a parameter of the format type. It is the design's first line and the canon says it has no wording
yet.

## The second question, which decides the same thing under another name

`question::container_derivation_output_count` is open, its decider is the panel, and its first option
is "One output, the carrier alone, which occupies more memory than the strategy promises because the
map from width and strategy to carrier is not injective." Its note carries the standing disagreement
that decides this round's third scope item outright:

> whether the strategy is an upstream selector the ladder never sees or a key of the ladder itself.

That is exactly "how a strategy selects among them", and it is recorded as a standing disagreement
that neither of the two files on the subject addresses.

`question::which_operation_set_the_design_ships` is the third, also open, also panel-decided, and its
note says no measurement can supply it because the question is a decision. Its `unblocks` says it
"decides whether footprint is observable, and with it one clause of the primitive candidate, the
contested one-vocabulary-or-two fork, and whether the count of primitives is container-relative."
The count of primitives is 32 under one reading and 64 under the other, per
`proposal::a_primitive_is_a_value_set_with_one_realisation_map`. How many base primitive types there
are is the first thing a base primitive design states, and it moves with a question nobody has
answered.

## Why deferring is not available, with the compiler saying so

The obvious escape is to write a design that is invariant under the branch, so the round proceeds and
the premise gets ruled later without invalidating anything. That escape was tried rather than
reasoned about, and it does not exist. The probes are in
`mock/research/sketches/base-primitives-gate/`, committed with their output.

`p1_branch_arity.rs` builds both branches and runs six checks, including a control that fails loudly
if the two carriers it separates are indistinguishable. Both branches compile. The branch-A shape,
carrier not in the type, has one footprint per declared width across every width shape tested. The
branch-B shape carries two footprints at one declared width. The consumer signature under branch A
takes two generic parameters and under branch B takes three, and the third is the threaded parameter
the refusal row prices.

The route through a default was the one worth trying, since it would let the surface read as branch A
while staying branch-B-capable. It does not defer, it picks branch B and hides it: two footprints stay
reachable at one declared width through `FixedD<13, 0, Narrow>`, and the default never reaches the
polymorphic position, so every generic consumer downstream threads the parameter anyway.

Two compile refusals settle the rest, and they are the strongest results in the file because they say
the sentence has no expressible form rather than a wrong value.

`p2_branch_a_cannot_express_two_carriers.rs`, which must not compile and does not:

```
error[E0080]: evaluation panicked: if this compiles, the branch-A shape distinguishes two footprints
at one declared width, and the claim in this file's header is refuted
```

That matters beyond tidiness, because `ruling::cold_is_for_cold_paths_and_cold_storage` is ratified
and its subject is the footprint. On branch A the design has no signature that distinguishes the
thing that ratified intent is about.

`p3_branch_b_splits_a_homogeneous_container.rs`, which must not compile and does not:

```
error[E0308]: mismatched types
   expected `FixedB<13, 0, Narrow>`, found `FixedB<13, 0, Wide>`
```

That is the third arm of the cost the refusal row names, "no repair at a homogeneous container", and
it lands on the storage path, which is the path the cold intent exists for.

So the two branches are not two spellings of one design. They differ in the type constructor's arity,
in what a consumer can express at one declared width, and in what a storage column can hold. A base
primitive design has to pick, and picking is answering `question::the_container_premise` by fiat,
inside a design, where nothing records that a canon question was decided.

## The ordering, which op stated

`ruling::the_next_unit_is_chosen_bottom_up` is his, verbatim:

> Whatever makes sense next and is natural and gives most answers that settle downstream questions at
> the same time and enable/unblock further design and exploration work. We want to build from ground
> up, so once the ery fundamentals and the basic paradigms and conventions are in place, we move one
> tier up to actually use those to define the things arvo needs defined and then one tier up etc etc.

`proposal::the_topics_form_a_stack_a_frame_and_the_canons_own_machinery` records what that order runs
over, and the floor of the stack is the container premise, with the format directly above it and the
primitive above that. Doing this round now is one tier up before the tier below it is in place.

`ruling::the_canon_is_written_once_at_the_end` carries no verbatim and is recorded as him choosing the
third of three options, so it binds more weakly, but it points the same way: nothing moves into the
canon until every topic is done.

## What is not blocked, recorded so the next round does not re-derive it

The overflow and rounding half of this scope is in much better shape than the format half, and it is
worth writing down which parts are already reachable from ratified text.

`does_warm_wrap_or_clamp` is answerable now from three ratified rows without waiting on anything,
per `221_dolan_the_numeric_fundamentals.md` section 7: the mode is declared, so a strategy does not
define an overflow mode and at most states a default over an axis declared separately.
`where_wrapping_lives` is answered by the ratified factoring, since wrapping and saturation are both
total maps onto the representable set and therefore both members of the one slot.

Neither of those can be written into a design yet, because both are statements about what the format
type carries, and that is the blocked part. They are recorded here as ready rather than as done.

One finding against a canon row, which seat 221 reached independently and this seat agrees with after
reading it, so it is confirmation rather than a second instance: `dimension::overflow_policy` lists
`panic` among the axis values, and under the ratified factoring an adaptation is total while panic
diverges, so a predicate span reading `overflow policy = panic` is saying something about the build
profile in the overflow vocabulary. The dimension set is append-only, so the repair is a note rather
than an edit.

## The two open topics this round inherits and does not shed

`design-round-consumes-its-inheritance` says every flat topic is this round's work, so both are named
here with their disposition rather than left silent.

`202607270711_topic.the-lints-can-see-the-whole-crate-now.md` files 128 lint findings across the crate
tree, per crate. Every crate it counts was deleted on 2026-08-08 when `mock/crates` was cleared for
the canon work, so the findings it names have no files to sit in. It is not shed and not closed: what
it records is that the lints now read every source file rather than only `src/lib.rs`, and that fact
binds whatever gets written next. It should be re-filed as a fresh flat topic against the new tree
once one exists.

The third topic it names, `202607300100_topic.warm-and-precise-above-64-bits.md`, was swept into
`mock/design_rounds/202607300800/` when that round closed. That round locked both changelists, so the
sweep is the archive doing what it does rather than anything lost, and the topic is readable there.
Whether that round's changelists actually addressed it is not something this file checked, and
somebody re-filing it should read the two locked changelists first rather than assume either way.

That same sweep produced a defect worth naming, because it is the exact failure
`design-round-consumes-its-inheritance` warns about.
`202607270711_topic.the-lints-can-see-the-whole-crate-now.md` exists twice in this tree,
byte-identical: flat in `mock/design_rounds/`, where it reads as an open filing, and inside the closed
archive `mock/design_rounds/202607300800/`, where it reads as finished history. `diff` reports no
difference between the two. So one open topic is simultaneously filed as done, whichever copy a
reader opens first decides what they believe about it, and nothing in the tree says the other copy is
there. This file does not repair it, because deleting either copy is a call about somebody else's
audit trail.

`202607311100_topic.lint-pack-rename.md` records the rename to `mockspace-extra-lints` and a blessed
per-instance `--no-verify` for that migration commit. The rename has landed, `mockspace.toml` names
`mockspace-extra-lints` under `[lint-crates]`, and `cargo mock check` reports the lint pipeline green
at strict today. The debt it describes was the old crate tree's and went with it. It needs nothing,
and that is written here rather than assumed.

## What would unblock this round

In order, and the first one is the whole of it.

1. **A genuinely independent second read of the container premise, by a persona that is not Dolan and
   that derives before reading seat 210's six rows.** Seat 221 asks for exactly this and names the
   constraint. Everything else here is downstream of it.
2. **`which_operation_set_the_design_ships`.** No measurement supplies it, the canon says so. It is a
   decision with three options and a panel decider, and observability has no truth value until the
   signature is named, per `proposal::observability_is_relative_to_a_declared_signature`.
3. **`container_derivation_output_count`**, and with it the standing disagreement its note carries
   about whether the strategy is an upstream selector or a key of the ladder, which is what decides
   how a strategy selects.

With those three closed, the design's first line has a wording, the count of primitive types is
fixed, and the strategy's position in the derivation is stated. The rest of this scope, the six
rounding names and the overflow policies, is already reachable from ratified text and was blocked
only on having something to carry it on.

## What this round did not do, plainly

It did not write a design. It did not open a doc changelist. It did not pick a branch of the premise
and mark the pick as derived, which was available and would have been wrong: the canon does not
merely fail to reach this question, it names the question and says the wording does not exist yet,
and a derived call inside a design is the one place a canon decision leaves no record that it was
made.
