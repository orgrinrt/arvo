# Src changelist: the remainder is carried over four limbs

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** `202611132240-c5qc`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611132240-c5qc_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `arvo-format`'s `wide`, `placing`, `apply::position`, `apply::landing`, `multiplication`,
`division` and `conversion`, their tests, the oracle in `tests/big.rs`, and four compile-fail cases.

## CHANGE: `Wide` divides by up to four limbs

`floor_divided` takes a positive `Wide` divisor and returns `Divided { quotient: Wide, remainder: Wide }`.
One limb divides limb by limb; two to four go through `by_limbs`, Knuth's algorithm D at the divisor's
own limb count with the add-back, which replaces `by_two_limbs`. New `ordering`, `scaled_against` (both
products in five limbs), `common_divisor` (the host's 128-bit Euclid where both fit, the four-limb
division otherwise) and the private `shifted_up`, `ordered` and `scaled`; `is_below` goes, `plus` reads
`ordered`. The module doc says a step reads `Isnt` as a step not formed.

## CHANGE: `Remainder` is two four-limb values

`Remainder { num: Wide, den: Wide }`. `of(u128, u128)` and `ZERO` stay; the crate-private
`reduced(Wide, Wide)` builds one in lowest terms. `numerator` and `denominator` return `Maybe<u128>`.
`is_zero`, `against_half` and `is_above` read the four limbs, the last through `scaled_against`;
`widened` goes. `PartialEq<Fraction>` compares through `Wide::of`.

## CHANGE: `Placed::Unformed` and the shared ending

`placing::Placed` gains `Unformed(bool)`, a step whose numerator left the four limbs, beside `Past`.
`over` and `positioned` take a `Wide` divisor; `is_held` is false and the new `is_formed` false for
`Unformed`; `positioned` saturates it on its sign, as `Past`.

## CHANGE: the three operations form and ask `D` in four limbs

`multiplication`, `division` and `conversion` keep `D` as a `Wide`, return `Unformed` where a numerator
or a product leaves the limbs, and replace the `D`-fits condition with `product_is_formed` (four
corners), `quotient_is_formed` (the denominator at the two range ends, the numerator at the four
range-end corners) and `conversion_is_formed` (the two ends). Each FIXME on
`arvo_s_exact_position_carries_no_remainder_denominator_past` and each obligation message names
`2^256 - 1` and the four limbs. `landing::position_at` keeps `D` in four limbs and reads `Unformed` as
no position.

## CHANGE: the tests

`wide/tests.rs` goes through a `floor` helper narrowing the remainder; new
`wide/tests/the_wide_divisor.rs`: three- and four-limb divisors at every normalisation, the add-back
case, `B - 1` quotient limbs, the order, the scaled comparison and its fifth limb, the common divisor,
each against `Big`. `Big` gains `floor_over_big`, `negated`, `common_divisor`, `limbs` and `wide`, and
loses the `unsigned` nothing reads any more. The multiplication, division and conversion edge oracles
form `D` in `Big` and reduce with its Euclid; the sweeps add `2^-128`, `2^-200`, `2^-255` and `10^-77`
for the product and `2^-200` onto `2^40` for the conversion, with named boundaries at `2^-255`/`2^-256`,
`10^-77`/`10^-78`, the quotient at `2^192`/`2^193` and the conversion at `2^127`/`2^128`. The
obligation boundary tests move to the four-limb edges, the phase's term leaving before `D` among them.
The landing gains a point whose remainder's denominator passes 128 bits. The compile-fail pairs move to
`2^256`, and the root-surface case reads the accessors' maybes.

## Verification

`cargo test -p arvo-format`, lib and `compile_fail` and the four parity suites, the planted arms still
red and binary128 and decimal128 still refused; clippy `-D warnings`; `cargo mock --lint-only`. Mutants:
the add-back removed from `by_limbs`, `scaled` cut to four limbs, and `Unformed` read as `Past` in
`is_formed`.
