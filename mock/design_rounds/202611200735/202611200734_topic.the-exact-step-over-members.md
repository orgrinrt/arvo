## 1. The format's steps over members

`Member<F>` gains `product(self, rhs: Member<F>) -> Exact`, `quotient(self, by: Divisor<F>) -> Exact` and `converted::<T: Format>(self) -> Exact`. Each is the position its slot step gives for the same slots, `product_position`, `quotient_position` and `converted_position`, and each forces that step's obligation. Over members the range bounds the operands, so the bound the value read in `room.rs` is a constant of the format here, with no test of an operand: where the largest numerator two members reach and the denominator fit the host's unsigned 128-bit integer and their quotient is below `2^127`, the position is formed there, in the machine's 64-bit integer where both terms fit it, and elsewhere it is the slot step's. The divisor is in the range at a zero phase because each of its doors puts it there: a constant is checked against the range, `Divisor::member` is admitted only for a range inside it, and `Divisor::of` is refused where the phase is whole.

The code is what `room.rs` held, moved to `arvo-format/src/member/step.rs`, and the value keeps none of it. Its tests move with it: the bound at its edge at radix two, ten and three, and the 64-bit division where the terms fit. They gain the law the format owes for a second formation of the same position: every pair of members at six bits in both ranges, three radixes and four exponents, and the 64-bit corners either side of each edge, give the slot step's position, the floor and the remainder's ratio. The two mutants move too and are renamed for the format, `arvo_mutant_member_step` and `arvo_mutant_member_step_edge`.

## 2. A slot on the grid lands without the four limbs

`Member::landed::<S>(slot)` is the member `adapted::<S>(Exact::on_grid(slot), _)` gives, for any slot and any dither: a position on the grid does not round, so only the policy's completion acts. The completion of a position the index holds is formed in the index, by one function, `complete_index`, and `adapt` takes the same function wherever its rounded position fits the index, so the completion keeps one home and the four limbs are left to positions past the index. `landed` is not a door that classifies a slot read at run time: it is the applied map at a position on the grid, total, and a slot already in the range lands on itself. A value's own slot reaches its member this way, and so does the divisor a value is divided by at run time.

## 3. The value calls the format, and the sentence comes back

`arvo-numeral` lands its slot with `Member::landed` and calls the three steps, then `adapt` as before. `room.rs` and its two mutant flags go. The value's design states the product's second arm as the format's step over two members, called as it stands, and keeps the sentence the second review's round deleted: the rounding has one home, the format's applied map, and a second one written here to be quick would be a second arithmetic kept equal to the first by tests alone. The quotient by a value read at run time lands its divisor with `Member::landed` rather than `Member::adapted`, which is the cheap landing `member_adapted_lands_an_on_grid_slot_through_the_four_limb_a` asks for.
