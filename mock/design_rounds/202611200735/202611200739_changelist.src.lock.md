**Topic:** `202611200734_topic.the-exact-step-over-members.md`. **Doc changelist:** `202611200735_changelist.doc.lock.md`.

## `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/`

ADD: `member/step.rs`, holding `Member::landed`, `Member::product`, `Member::quotient` and
`Member::converted`, the ratio and the bound over the range that pick the host's integer, moved from
`arvo-numeral/src/room.rs` with its two mutant flags renamed `arvo_mutant_member_step` and
`arvo_mutant_member_step_edge` (topic decisions 1 and 2).

ADD: `member/step/tests.rs`: every pair of members at six bits, both ranges, radix two, ten and three,
exponents -5, -1, 0 and 2, and the conversions down three, up four and onto itself, against the slot
steps' positions, each format shown to take the host's integer first; the 64-bit corners either side of
the quotient's and the product's edge at radix two, and at radix ten and three either side of the
quotient's edge and at the product's, which are the corners that reach the 128-bit division at those
radixes; the bound at its edge at radix two, ten and three; the bound at its edge and one past it for the
product, the quotient and the conversion at widths one, eight, sixteen, 32, 63 and 64, both ranges, the
three radixes, against edges the tests work out in their own arithmetic; the 64-bit division where the
terms fit; `landed` against `adapted` on the grid under each policy, at one, six, eight and sixty-four
bits in both families, over the slots around the ends, around zero and far past, which holds the hand-off
from `adapt` to `complete_index` rather than the completion (decisions 1 and 2).

CHANGE: `member.rs`'s doc on `adapted` is wrapped to the crate's width.

CHANGE: `member.rs` declares `step`, which reads `MemberOf` as a child module does, and its docs name the
third door and the steps (decision 2).

ADD: `apply.rs`'s `complete_index`, the completion of a slot the index holds; `complete_slot` takes it
wherever the rounded position fits the index, keeping the four-limb path for positions past it (decision 2).

ADD: `apply/position.rs`'s `Exact::same`, for tests only: the same floor, and remainders of one ratio.

CHANGE: `multiplication.rs`, `division.rs` and `conversion.rs` open `Multipliable`, `Divisible` and
`Convertible` and their `ADMITTED` to the crate, so a member step forces its slot step's obligation in
both arms (decision 1).

CHANGE: `Cargo.toml` declares the two mutant flags to `check-cfg` (decision 1).
