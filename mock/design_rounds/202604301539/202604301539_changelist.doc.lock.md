## arvo-bits-contracts/DESIGN.md.tmpl

### Update Widen section: clarify bit-pattern-preserving contract

Existing prose in the `## Widen` section claims:
> "For sub-carrier-width `Bits<M, S, Signed>` the cross-Bits impl
> masks bits 0..M of the source carrier and sign-extends bit M-1
> across the destination carrier's high bits, using M from impl
> context."

Replace with explicit bit-pattern-preserving framing:
> "For sub-carrier-width `Bits<M, S, Signed>` the cross-Bits impl
> preserves the carrier bit pattern. Bits above the logical width
> M are forwarded from the source carrier as-is (typically zero
> for values constructed via `Narrow`, since the signed Narrow
> macro zero-pads above the logical width). Consumers needing
> logical-value-preserving signed conversion (explicit sign-
> extension at bit M-1) compose `Narrow` plus sign-extension plus
> `Widen` at the application layer; the substrate's refit family
> is bit-pattern-preserving by contract on both directions."

Add a new note at the end of the `## Widen` section explaining the
asymmetry-with-Narrow paragraph applies to logical-width handling
specifically; the cross-Bits forwarder for both Narrow and Widen
forwards through the carrier-side primitive trait without any
sub-carrier-width sign machinery.

### Verify Narrow section consistency
