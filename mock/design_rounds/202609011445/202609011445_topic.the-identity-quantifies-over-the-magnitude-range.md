## What the previous round fixed, and the half of it that stayed

The round before this one corrected `has_additive_identity` from turning on
whether the phase is nonzero to turning on whether it is a whole multiple of the
quantum. That correction is right and stands. What it did not do is notice that
the function reads no coordinate of the quantum at all.

`format.rs:67` reads `PHASE_NUM`, `PHASE_DEN` and the slot range. It never
touches `MAGNITUDES`, `SLOPE` or `BASE`. So it answers about the grid at
magnitude zero, while its name, its rustdoc and the design's sentence all
quantify over the format's representable set, which spans every admitted
magnitude.

## What the canon says membership is

`ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon` is ratified and carries four propositions as
one shape. The second is
`proposal::membership_of_the_representable_set_is_one_affine_predicate`:
membership is one predicate over one parameterisation, an affine slot function, a
quantum per magnitude and a phase, and the phase is stated explicitly because it
decides whether the set carries an additive identity at all.

The design writes that predicate out at `DESIGN.md.tmpl:29`. A value `v` is
representable exactly when there is a magnitude `m` and a slot `i` within the
slot range such that `v = phase + i * quantum(m)`. The existential runs over
both coordinates.

So zero is in the set exactly when some admitted pair cancels the phase, and
that is one question with one answer. Solving it in exact integers, from
`(PN/PD) * r^BASE + i * r^(BASE + SLOPE*m) = 0`, dividing through by `r^BASE`
and clearing the denominator:

```
i * PD * r^(SLOPE*m) = -PN
```

`BASE` cancels and never reappears, so the answer does not depend on it at any
magnitude. What is left depends on the phase, the radix, the slope and the slot
range, and the shipped function reads two of those four.

## What follows, measured

`mock/research/sketches/phase-over-the-magnitude-range/` carries two ad-hoc
spikes and their output. The first compares the shipped function, transcribed
verbatim, against an enumerator over the real grid that never mentions it, which
is the control the canon's own phase probe used: a predicate checked against an
enumeration written independently of it cannot be agreeing with itself.

Three disagreements, in both directions.

**A whole phase whose cancelling slot is out of range at magnitude zero and in
range higher up.** Phase four quanta, `Indexed` at slope one, `Signed<2>`. At
magnitude zero the cancelling slot is -4 and the range stops at -2. At magnitude
one the quantum has doubled, so slot -2 cancels the same absolute phase and is
the range's own lowest index. The function says no identity; there is one. Every
coordinate here is one this crate ships, so an outside `Format` reaches it with
no outside `Quantum` at all.

**A fractional phase that becomes whole at a higher magnitude.** Phase one half,
against a quantum whose step shrinks with magnitude. At magnitude zero the
cancelling slot is minus one half and is not a slot. At magnitude one the quantum
has halved, so the same absolute phase is one whole step and slot -1 cancels it.
The function says no identity; there is one.

That second case refutes the design's own gloss, which says only a phase with a
fractional part takes the identity off the grid. It is true of the constant
family and false of a magnitude-indexed one, because "the quantum" is not a
single thing there. The gloss was written for the family the shipped points
mostly inhabit, and the code implemented the gloss rather than the predicate
above it.

**A format admitting no magnitudes at all.** `MAGNITUDES = 0` with a zero phase.
The set is empty, so it contains nothing, and the function says it carries an
additive identity. Over-reporting rather than under-reporting, and from the same
root: with no existential over the magnitude there is nothing to notice that no
magnitude is admitted.

Three controls passed. The enumerator separates a zero phase from a half-step
phase, so it is not stuck at one verdict. It agrees with the shipped function on
all five shipped points, so what is being measured is the cases under test rather
than the instrument. Moving `BASE` by seven moves no answer, which checks the
derivation rather than asserting it.

## The class underneath, which is wider than one function

`MAGNITUDES = 0` is reachable because nothing refuses it. The `Quantum` trait's
own rustdoc says "never zero: a law over no magnitudes describes no values", and
that sentence is a request rather than a check. `Format::PHASE_DEN` says "Never
zero" in the same way. `Ambient::RADIX` states no bound at all, and a radix below
two is not a positional notation and makes the cancellation equation degenerate.

`Slots` in the same crate does not do this. It carries `ADMITTED`, a const every
function reading the range forces, so an implementor that does not meet it stops
the build at the use site. The design argues for that at `DESIGN.md.tmpl:374`,
and the argument is not about slot ranges: it quotes
`proposal::the_concept_is_closed_and_the_inventory_is_open`, ratified through the
format spine, which says a new instance earns admission by supplying the
concept's obligations, and that closing the concept while opening the inventory
is what makes admission a check rather than a negotiation.

So three of this crate's four open contracts request their obligations in prose
and one checks them. That asymmetry is the design's own principle applied once.

This matters to the widened predicate rather than merely sitting next to it. The
arithmetic above is sound when the radix is at least two, and shipping a widened
totality claim that rests on an unchecked declaration is the defect this design
has already had to correct once in writing, at `DESIGN.md.tmpl:369`: a sentence
claiming something was total when nothing was checking.

## What this round proposes

Widen the computation rather than narrow the claim. The design's normative
predicate quantifies over the magnitude, the function is named in the design's
surface list as one of the membership predicate's neighbours, and a function
answering only about magnitude zero would answer a question the design never
asks.

Name the intermediate. The slot that cancels the phase at one magnitude is the
concept the predicate turns on, and having it as its own function makes both
halves testable separately rather than only their conjunction.

Give the three unchecked contracts the obligation the fourth already has, each
asserting what its own rustdoc already claims and nothing further: a radix of at
least two, at least one magnitude, a nonzero phase denominator. Each gets the
verdict-returning form beside it, as `Slots` has `is_admissible`, so a
construction that compiles and is wrong can be reported on at check time without
forcing the const that refuses it.

Keep the total branches even where an obligation makes the case unrepresentable
in a built binary. The design is explicit at `DESIGN.md.tmpl:385` that an
obligation fires at codegen and not at `cargo check`, so the predicate still
needs a defined answer for a declaration a check-time evaluation can reach.

Fix the design's gloss so it states the predicate over the magnitude range
instead of over "the quantum", which is not a single thing in the indexed family.

## The tests this round owes

The phase law is currently asserted through `Biased`, and `Biased` fixes the
denominator at two, the quantum at `Constant` and the slots at `Signed`. The
other three points all set the numerator to zero. So every arm that has ever
exercised a nonzero phase ran at one denominator, in one quantum family, over one
slot family.

That is the same shape as the gap the previous round closed, one axis over.
That round found that every phase tried was fractional because the numerator was
always odd; the axis it did not check was the denominator, the family and the
range. A law sampled at one point of three axes is a law nobody has tested.

So the sweep runs both quantum families, both slot families and a spread of
denominators, and it carries the cases no arm reaches today: a phase denominator
of zero, an integral phase whose cancelling slot falls outside the slot range, a
format admitting no magnitudes, and the two witnesses above. The wrong
constructions stay in the file permanently and are asserted to be rejected,
rather than being built to look at and deleted.
