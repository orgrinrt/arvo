## CHANGE: `a_translation_band_with_no_positive_translation_licenses_a_low_excursion` FROM absent TO declared

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/symmetry/tests/the_reach.rs`
Verification: `cargo test -p arvo-format --lib a_translation_band` passes, and it fails with `TowardZero refused a low excursion that no translation in the band can undo` when the low side's second disjunct is deleted. Both runs are in `research/sketches/the-symmetry-predicate-mutations/`.

`completion_is_translation_homomorphic` decides the low side by
`reaches_below(lowest).not()` or `reaches_a_positive_translation().not()`, and
the second is never true in any of the cross's 432 cells: `cell_reach` hands
every cell the range's highest slot as its upper translation bound, and that is
positive on all three ranges. The high side's mirror is exercised both ways,
through the restriction axis that turns negative translations off, which is what
made the gap invisible. Half of a symmetric pair was covered and the coverage
control could not see the difference, because it counts triples, excursions,
ties and negative positions and asks nothing about the translation band's sign.

A fourth restriction axis would reach it and would double the cross for one
cell's worth of information. This arm reaches it directly, with three controls:
that the same positions with a positive translation in the band are refused,
which is what says the yes came from the disjunct rather than from a vacuous
reach; that the positions stop one slot short of the top so the high side is
decided by `reaches_above` and the verdict rests on the low side alone; and that
under wrapping the answer is yes whichever band it is handed, so the refusal is
the clamp throwing a distance away rather than anything about the reach.

## CHANGE: `Reads::Parity` FROM documenting a region TO carrying a `FIXME` naming what would express it

File: `crates/arvo-format/src/symmetry.rs`
Verification: `grep -c 'FIXME' mock/crates/arvo-format/src/symmetry.rs` returns one.

The variant's own documentation says half-even is defeated by a domain whose
translations are all even. `Reach` carries no parity of the translation band, so
`rounding_is_translation_equivariant` has no disjunct for it and answers no on a
domain where the law holds.

Conservative, so nothing unsound rests on it, which is why it went unnoticed: a
predicate that refuses too much fails no cross and costs a lowering rather than
a result. A region an arm could gate on is going unused, and prose alone is not
greppable, so the marker names the fourth fact on `Reach` that would express it
and names `the_classification` as the instrument that already walks that domain.
