# Source changelist: the standard targets

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** `202611131910-ni9o`, job `arvo-standards`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611131910_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/standards.rs` and the new `src/standards/`, the crate's
`tests/`, and the regenerated `docs/`.

## Comparison to deprecated changelist

`202611131910_changelist.src.deprecated.md` carried the same scope and the same changes, and the source
it describes landed at `dac1d014` under it. It was deprecated only because the doc reopened to correct
one sentence about `Floating`, which the binary32 arm showed to be false. Nothing in the source moves for
that correction; this changelist restates the scope and adds the assertion the correction names.

## CHANGE: `standards` splits into one module per standard

`src/standards.rs` keeps the module documentation and declares `matlab` and `ieee754`.
`src/standards/matlab.rs` holds `FractionLength`, `Fi`, `Ufi`, `FiMath`, `UfiMath`, `rounding_method`
and `overflow_action` as they were, moved and not changed: `FractionLength` is still the `Quantum`
whose exponent is the negated fraction length, and `Fi` and `Ufi` still each implement `Format` over
`Signed` and `Unsigned` `Slots`. Every path that read them, the parity file and the word-length
compile-fail case, reads `standards::matlab`.

## CHANGE: `standards::ieee754`

`ExponentRange<P, EMAX>` implements `Quantum`, `Significand<A, P>` implements `Slots` and
`Interchange<A, P, EMAX>` implements `Format`, each deriving its coordinates in its impl body.
`Binary`, `Decimal`, `Binary16`, `Binary32`, `Binary64`, `Decimal32`, `Decimal64` and `Attributed`
are aliases. `rounding_direction` re-exports the four modes under the standard's names.

## CHANGE: the parity suites

`tests/matlab_fi_parity.rs` gains a planted-mode arm over its five published declarations.
`tests/matlab_fimath_parity.rs` sweeps the five method names, the Round alias and both overflow actions
against MathWorks' definitions as integer rules, each with a planted arm.
`tests/ieee754/mod.rs` holds the composition that lands a value at the finest magnitude in range.
`tests/ieee754_binary_parity.rs` holds binary16, binary32 and binary64 against the encodings and the
host, every direction on binary32, the overflow refusal, and the comparison with `Floating<25, -149,
254>`: the same law, one slot apart, and the extra slot at the top magnitude naming `-2^128`, which
binary32 rounds to an infinity. `tests/ieee754_decimal_parity.rs` holds decimal32 and decimal64 against
the standard's digit rule. Each carries planted arms required to be reported. `tests/compile_fail.rs`
gains the binary128 and decimal128 cases under `tests/ui/`.

## What `arvo-format`'s source gains, named for T2

The `standards::ieee754` module and the move of MATLAB's declarations into `standards::matlab`, and
nothing else. No item outside `standards` changes.
