# Src changelist: the IEEE harness reaches below the smallest subnormal

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Round:** `202611140156`, job `arvo-format`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611140156_changelist.doc.lock.md`.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/tests/ieee754/mod.rs` and the new
`tests/ieee754_underflow_parity.rs`. Nothing under `src/` changes.

## CHANGE: the search positions a value below the finest step

`position` forms the scaled denominator in the unsigned 128-bit integer through the new
`unsigned_power`. Where it fits `i128` the position is as before. Where it fits only `u128` the floor is 0
or -1 and the remainder exact; past `u128` the floor is the same and the remainder `1 / u128::MAX`, or
`(u128::MAX - 1) / u128::MAX` below zero, which the doc comment states keeps the comparisons against
zero and a half. `land`'s doc names what still answers nothing.

## CHANGE: the rule reads the dropped unit in `u128`

`round_off` takes the unit from `unsigned_power` and works on the magnitude in `u128`, comparing the
rest against the unit less the rest, so a unit past the index whose half `|c|` reaches is read, and the
everything-dropped arm holds only past `u128`.

## CHANGE: the inputs

New `underflow(f, seed)`: the far value, the significand bound, `i128::MAX` at the first unit past the
index and one past it, ones there, the tie at half that unit and its neighbours where the index holds
it, and 500 draws from four hundred to twenty-one exponents below the smallest subnormal. `inputs`
extends with it. New `misses_over` takes the inputs; `misses` reads it. The module carries the
`dead_code` allow the ties-away module carries, for the same reason.

## CHANGE: the underflow suite

`tests/ieee754_underflow_parity.rs`: binary32, binary64 and decimal64 under the four directions over
`underflow` against the digit rule; binary64 at `2^-1400` toward positive, toward negative and ties to
even; decimal64 at `10^-480`; the tie at `2^126` over `2^127` steps and one past it; and a wrong
direction missing on binary64 and decimal64.

## The failing control

The search answering nothing below the index again reddens six of the suite's seven arms; the rule's
old unit, `power` cast up, reddens the binary32 and binary64 sweeps.
