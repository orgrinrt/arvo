## `PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl`

"No `.0` field-access": the list of numeric value types is replaced by the two value types the tree
has, `Bool` and `Bits<N>`, neither a numeral, with their inherent `const fn`s and one unwrap door each.
The next paragraph covers every format point, `Integer`, `UFixed`, `Biased`, `Floating`, `USize`,
`ISize`, as zero-sized markers holding no value, says `IFixed`, `FastFloat` and `StrictFloat` are not in
the tree, and keeps the value question open. The unwrap bullet names `Bool::get()` and `Bits::raw()` in
place of a `Deref` on `Bool` and a `Transparent::raw`, and the arithmetic bullet names inherent
`const fn`s in place of `impl const` operators.

"Sizes are const at type level": `Seq<T, N>`, `Map<K, V, N>` with `N` a type-level parameter; that
parameter is the excepted const generic position with the bare form only where genuinely painful; what
type spells it is open, no capacity type is built, and whether a capacity is a painful case is
undecided. The sentence that called a capacity the excepted position and then said whether it is such a
position is not settled is gone.

Primitive table: the `u8..u128` row says `Integer` and `UFixed` hold no value and no numeric value type
exists; the `f32`/`f64` row stops offering `FastFloat`/`StrictFloat` and names `Floating` as a format.

"arvo: the fixed-point arithmetic stack": "every numeric primitive flows through `UFixed` / `IFixed` /
`FastFloat` / `StrictFloat`" becomes the format a numeral declares, the points listed as markers, and
no numeric value type yet. "All arvo types are `repr(transparent)`" becomes the two value types being
so and the format points being zero-sized.

## `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

The ratio coordinate: the carry paragraph says the constructor saturates where the sum leaves the index,
that this happens only within `2^63` of either end, that a range may be admitted there, what is stored
for `i128::MAX` with `9/4` and for `i128::MIN` with `-7/4`, and that the map adapts the stored position,
with the `Ceil`-and-`Wrap` consequence named. "Everywhere a slot any range admits could start from" and
"like every other position the crate computes" go.

"The index's host integer": the list of saturating sites names `Exact::between`'s carry beside the
non-member operand and the chain of carries, and says the map's answer is exact for the stored position
rather than the named one.

"The platform-width points", capacity paragraph: the door ruling's answer is attributed to the
platform-width ruling's note reading it, the door ruling's own text is said to name no capacity, the
capacity is a type-level parameter whose spelling is open, and the bound on the exception is kept.

"The laws the platform-width points owe tests for": the per-target arm paragraph is replaced. No arm per
pointer width; why such an arm is redundant with the spelling over `usize::BITS`; why no host arm can
catch a literal width, why `cargo check` at a foreign target evaluates no assertion, and why a 16-bit
library check proves nothing about an alias. The spelling is guarded by the lint
`the-platform-width-points-read-the-pointer-width`, and the host tests and the family laws cover the rest.
The `msp430-none-elf` claim goes with the paragraph.
