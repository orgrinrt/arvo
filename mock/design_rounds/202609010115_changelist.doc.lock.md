# Doc changelist: the exact step happens where it fits

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Round:** the value-path round, opened on the fifth review pass over #28
**Scope:** one crate design document.

## What this changelist is

The ratified factoring says arithmetic on a format is an exact operation in an ambient domain composed
with a total adaptation onto the representable set. The design says that and the source does the
opposite: it performs the exact step in the representable set's own carrier, where it does not fit.

So the design gains the sentence that makes the factoring mechanical rather than descriptive, and says
that the two regions of the map are not public.

## The canon this declares

`proposal::arithmetic_on_a_format_factors_as_an_adaptation_of_an_exact_operation`, ratified through
`ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon`. The exact step and the adaptation are two acts, and the exact one
does not happen in the target set.

`proposal::the_realisation_map_is_one_map_with_two_regions`, for why the two regions are not two
public functions.

`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path`, ratified, for why the answer is a wider
intermediate rather than a check: with the intermediate wide enough there is nothing to check.

## Files changed

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl` | states that the exact step is performed in a domain wide enough to hold it and the adaptation lands the result, states that this is what makes the map total for every input without a check, and states that the two regions are internal |

## Why a wider intermediate rather than a guard

A guard on the value path would be a runtime check where the canon forbids one, or a compile-time
refusal of inputs that have a correct answer. **Wrapping `i64::MAX` into a small range has an answer**;
the code simply computes it in a carrier that cannot hold the intermediate step.

Widening the intermediate is the ratified sentence read literally, and it makes the function total on
every input of its parameter type rather than on a subset the documentation would then have to name.

## What is deliberately not here

**No change to what the adaptation means.** The rounding modes, the overflow policies and which arm
packs are untouched.

**No new public surface.** The change removes two public functions and adds none.
