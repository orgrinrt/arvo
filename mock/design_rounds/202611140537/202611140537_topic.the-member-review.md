# The member review answered

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Scope:** `arvo-format`'s member type, the divisor's member door, their tests, and the registry row that
recorded the divisor's stance.
**Source:** `mock/research/202611140540_review-arvo-format-member-1/REVIEW.md`, pass one of
`work/arvo-format-member`, which passed the canon gate and failed the test gate.

The review found no wrong verdict in the obligation and failed the suite on three counts. The member's own
tests could not fail: they read constants chosen inside the range back through a helper, and the planted
door they reported was the helper reporting itself. The member door's sweep built its expected answer from
the divisor format's value at the divisor's slot, so the grid claim the door rests on, that a slot of `G`
denotes what the same slot of `F` does, was assumed by the oracle rather than checked against it, and only
two pairs on one grid were swept. The totality walk asserted the range on `adapt`'s output and reached the
member only through an equality with it.

The answers. The member's suite becomes the constant door at both ends of the range at every admitted width
of both families, written as literal widths, with the refusal pinned on both sides by compile-fail pairs one
past the top and one below the bottom. The sweep takes the divisor's value from `G`, asserts it equal to
`F`'s at every member, and runs over every admitted pair of the door's table plus a negative exponent and
radix ten. The zero column is checked against the oracle's search over every member, beside the hand
statement. The walk asserts the range on the member's own slot. The check that each condition flips alone
reads the table's literals, so it says something about the table rather than repeating the shipped verdict.

Two findings are drift. Neither door forced the format's own obligation or asked for a constant quantum,
though the design says the member serves a constant-exponent quantum only; so a member built over a phase
that does not denote, or over a floating format where membership needs a magnitude. Both doors now force
one obligation, the format's own and a constant quantum, refused at build with a compile-fail pair for
each. The registry row `proposal::a_quotient_s_divisor_is_non_zero_by_type` still records two doors and the
compromise this round's predecessor removed, so a superseding proposal on the same topic records three.

The design's reason for leaving the coordinate table alone, that a member is a value type rather than a
coordinate, sat in a changelist and read against the design's own sentence. The design now says it: a
member is a slot refined by its range, which no contract declares a format with, so it is not one of the
coordinates in that table. Three rustdoc summaries run past a line and are split.

The tenth finding is outside the diff: `arvo-numeral`'s consumer test is red because its lockfile pins
notko over ssh while the mock workspace declares https, the rewrite every test run in this container makes.
It goes on the ticket that rewrite already has.
