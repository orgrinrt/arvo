## CHANGE: both member doors force what a format owes

`src/member.rs`: a private `MemberOf<F>` whose `ADMITTED` forces `<F as Format>::ADMITTED` and asserts
`is_constant_family::<F::Quantum>()`, with a message naming the magnitude a member would need and the constant
quantum to declare instead. `Member::constant` and `Member::adapted` each force it first. The test-only
`assumed` is removed with the planted door that was its one caller. The module doc names what both doors
refuse, and the rustdoc summaries of `Member`, `constant` and `adapted` are split so each first line stands
alone.

`src/division.rs`: the rustdoc of `Divisor::member` is split into the summary "The divisor at a member of
`G`." and the paragraphs after it. No code changes.

## CHANGE: the member's own tests

`src/member/tests.rs` is rewritten. One test names the constant door at both ends of the range at every
admitted width, 1 to 64, of `Integer<W>` and `UFixed<W, 0>`, each end a literal expression of the width,
compared with the `MIN` and `MAX` the format declares; the literal list is checked against `ADMITTED_WIDTHS`.

`src/apply/tests/totality.rs`: at every fed position the walk asserts `Member::adapted`'s own slot is within
the range, beside its equality with `adapt`'s. The module doc says so.

## CHANGE: the member door's sweep reads the divisor's value from its own format

`src/division/tests/mod.rs`: `quotient_by::<F>(a, by)` is the exact position of the member `a` of `F` over the
value `by`, and `exact_quotient` calls it with `F`'s own value.

`src/division/tests/the_oracle.rs`: `pair::<S>(a, divisor, by, planted)` takes the divisor's value and builds
both the oracle's position and the planted step from it. The sweep passes the dividend format's value, as
before.

`src/division/tests/the_member_door.rs`: `ByMembers<G>` computes `value::<G>(b)`, asserts it equal to
`value::<S::Format>(b)`, and passes it to `pair`. `swept::<F, G>()` checks the cell count and the pair count
from the two ranges. Six sweeps, one test each: `Positive` by `Positive`, `Byte` by `Positive`, `Biased` by
`Quarters`, `FromOne` by `FromOne`, `ByteInQuarters` by `PositiveInQuarters` at exponent `-2`, and
`ByteInTenths` by `PositiveInTenths` at radix ten and exponent `-1`. The table is one macro, `the_table`,
naming twelve pairs, the six swept ones answering true throughout; `disagreements` and the control read it
through two callers. The control reads the table's literals and requires, for each condition, a row stating
that condition false and every other true. A new test checks `no_member_names_the_zero::<G>()` against
`members::<G>().all(|b| value::<G>(b).num != 0)` at all thirteen formats the table names. The three planted
verdicts stay.
