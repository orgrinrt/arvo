# Doc changelist: the ratio coordinate's constructor is an adaptation

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Round:** the ratio coordinate saturates its magnitude instead of dropping it
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

Three paragraphs in the coordinate-types section, all in one place, all describing a constructor the
crate does not have. Two of them are about the phase and are stale; the third is about the ratio and
is about to become stale by decision.

## CHANGE: the phase paragraph FROM a constructor that refuses TO an obligation that refuses

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: `grep -c 'its constructors cannot produce a zero denominator' mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl` returns zero, and the section states that the phase holds the pair it was handed and that `Format::ADMITTED` is what refuses a denominator naming no position.

The paragraph says `Phase` "carries the pair, its constructors cannot produce a zero denominator, and
the condition is a property of the type rather than a sentence asking for one". The first clause is
right and the rest is not. `Phase::of(1, 0)` builds, the crate's own compile-fail doctest on
`Format::ADMITTED` writes exactly that, and the refusal is the obligation's. What is true is the
shape the phase round actually landed: the pair is held, the predicate is on the coordinate as
`denotes`, and the contract that reads the coordinate is what refuses.

## CHANGE: the negative-denominator paragraph FROM the phase's rule TO the ratio's rule

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: `grep -c 'stay whole-numbered' mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl` returns zero, and the sentence about a negative denominator naming a value exactly names `Fraction` rather than sitting under the phase paragraph where a reader takes it for the phase.

It says a negative denominator "moves its sign to the numerator and keeps the magnitude" and that two
pairs "stay whole-numbered because the exact form is not representable". `Phase::of(3, -7)` returns
three over minus seven, so the first half is false of the coordinate the reader has just been reading
about. The second half is true of `Fraction` today and stops being true in this round.

## CHANGE: the ratio coordinate FROM two pairs staying whole-numbered TO an adaptation with two regions

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: the section states the four properties the constructor holds, names the two regions, and gives the residue and its bound. `grep -c '1 / i64::MAX\|relative' mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl` returns at least one.

The design tier has to say this before the source may, and it is a contract change rather than a
correction: the constructor's answer on two families of input moves.

What goes in, and no more than this:

- The constructor is an adaptation in the sense the ratified factoring already names, mapping a ratio
  in the ambient rationals onto the pairs the coordinate represents.
- It has two regions. Exact normalisation moves the sign and cancels the power of two the operands
  share. Magnitude saturation pins the magnitude to the largest the coordinate carries, which is what
  `complete_slot` already does to a slot leaving its range, one coordinate up.
- The four properties: positive denominator, the named sign, exact wherever the coordinate admits an
  exact form, and a relative error of at most `1 / i64::MAX` where it does not.
- Which inputs are in the second region, stated as the condition rather than as a count: one operand
  is `i64::MIN`, a negative denominator, and the other operand odd.
- A zero denominator keeps its own answer and is not one of the regions, because it names no ratio
  and there is nothing to be near.

What stays out: the counts, the widths the properties were checked at, and the instruments. Those are
this round's evidence and they live in the sketch. The design says what the constructor is, and a
number measured at a moment is not that.

## CHANGE: nothing in the registry

File: none
Verification: `cargo mock` reports 708 rows across 10 namespaces before and after.

The constructor's rule is derived from `ruling::the_format_spine_is_canon`, which is ratified and
already says an operation onto a representable set is an exact operation composed with a named total
adaptation. Naming one more instance of a shape the canon already carries is design work, not a canon
act, and the canon gains nothing from a row saying the format crate did what the spine says.

`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` and
`ruling::accuracy_is_never_bought_with_performance` are the two other ratified rows the choice answers
to, and neither is amended: the rule adds no check and gives up no speed on any path.
