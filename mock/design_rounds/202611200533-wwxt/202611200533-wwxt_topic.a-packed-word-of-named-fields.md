## What is asked

Hilavitkutin's identity handle is one 32-bit word holding a 28-bit id, a 3-bit kind and a 1-bit flag, each read and replaced by name, with the layout fixed when the consumer builds and the one-bit field typed as a bit and not as a number (`hilavitkutin-sym/src/handle.rs:19`, `:73`; `hilavitkutin-sym/src/shape.rs:185`, at hilavitkutin `dev` `ad6da29e`). It wrote that word with the removed tree's `bitfield!`, which is a dead tier and is read here only as what the consumer writes today. The nearest row, `obligation::an_exact_width_container_a_consumer_can_alias_and_pin`, asks for the container only, and `refsto` on it reaches `the_first_slice_answers_to_hilavitkutin_and_kaski`, `the_old_bits_crate_returns_in_slice_one` and `ingest_is_the_consumers_and_the_c_abi_is_where_it_ends_up`, none of which names a field.

## What the canon says

Nothing on a word divided into fields. The registry's nearest terms are the lens and shared placement: `proposal::the_lens_degenerates_to_an_ordinary_value_at_sole_occupancy` and `proposal::at_shared_occupancy_no_per_element_footprint_observation_exists`, both `one_expert`, both proposals and so not canon, and the value-stance row's gap that "shared placement, the packed column, is not covered and waits on the carrier deferral". A packed word is not that case. Its fields are read out into values of their own, a `Bits<W>` or a `Bool` at sole occupancy, and the word itself is one `Bits<N>` at sole occupancy; no value is held at a shared placement and none is reached through a carrier in place. So the round builds nothing on the lens rows and leaves the packed column where its gap puts it.

What binds the shape is in force already: no runtime check, a refusal at build in its place; sizes const; monomorphisation as the dispatch; the stack's own primitives at every public position (the repo's `CLAUDE.md`, "The constraints that are not open"). `ruling::ingest_is_the_consumers_and_the_c_abi_is_where_it_ends_up` holds for the word as for the container: construction from the container takes every bit pattern, since the fields tile it, so there is no validating entry point.

## The decisions

Placement and shape are not settled by the canon, so each went to stack-coord for a stamp on 20 November, with the sketch, per the workspace rule on a provisional stamp while op is away, and goes to op as a question ticket after.

1. The obligation. A new row, `obligation::a_word_divided_into_named_fields_of_declared_widths`, states the need in the consumer's terms and names no mechanism: a word of a declared total width, named fields of declared widths, each read and written in a constant, a one-bit field with its own type, the widths summing to the word checked at build, the whole word converted to and from the exact-width container of its width at no cost. Consumer hilavitkutin, topic `the_format` as its nearest sibling has, quoting `hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl` on `dev`.

2. The placement. `arvo-bits`, in a module of its own beside the tier, since the word is held in the bit container, its one-bit field reads as the tier reads one bit, and it adds no arithmetic. `tier.rs` is not touched; the lane holding it is `arvo-numeral-doors`.

3. The shape, as the sketch built it. A consumer names a layout and its fields as marker types. `Layout` gives `Container`, the type the word is held in, and `Fields`, a list `(A, (B, (C, ())))` in any order. `Field` gives `Layout`, the word it belongs to, `Value`, what it reads as, and `BELOW`, a `Width` counting the word's bits under it. `Word<L>` is `repr(transparent)` over `L::Container`, with `of` and `bits` the moves in and out, and `get::<F>` and `with::<F>` reading and replacing one field, each a `const fn`.

4. The one-bit field reads as `arvo_format::Bool`, the type `BitAccess::bit` already returns. No `Bit` type is added.

5. One contract, `Lane`, for what goes in the low bits of the sixty-four-bit carrier: a `WIDTH` and the way in and out. `Bits<N>` and `Bool` implement it, so a container and a field value are one kind of thing, and a consumer's own lane type implements it in its own crate.

6. The refusals. Every entry point forces a `const` item that asserts the fields share no bit, their widths sum to the container's width, and their union covers it; `get` and `with` force one more that asserts the layout lists a field at that place and width. Each is a build refusal with a message naming what is wrong. Pinned by `compile_fail` cases for a short sum, an overlap and an unlisted field, each beside a case that builds.

7. Equality, copying and the debug view of `Word<L>` are written by hand and bound on `L::Container`, never derived, since a derive bounds them on the layout marker.

8. The cost. `of` and `bits` are moves of a `repr(transparent)` value. The claim that the round trip and one field's read and write lower to a move, a shift and a mask is the bench's to make, with `cargo mock bench disasm`, and the design states only what the disassembly shows.

## Weighed and not taken

Fields placed in order from the low bit with an index the compiler infers: no `BELOW` to write, but every read is `get::<F, _>()` and the layout's order becomes a wire fact nobody reads at the field. A declarative macro writing the impls: arvo ships no proc macro (`proc_macro_crates = []`), and a macro hides the contract a consumer generic over a word would bound on.
