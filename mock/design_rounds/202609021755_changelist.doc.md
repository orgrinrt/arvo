# DOC changelist: name the two verbs, and an override defeats rather than bypasses

The round before this one replaced a false guarantee with one stated over the
functions that force the obligation, which is the right shape. It then named the
wrong functions, and it filed an override as a route that misses the forcing site
when the override is what the forcing site evaluates. Both are corrected here
against the source rather than against either report.

**This changelist closes no route and chooses no locus.** That decision belongs to
this tier and is still open, per
`ruling::the_canon_does_not_police_what_shape_a_law_takes`.

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

### The claims, as they stand

At `:676`:

> `Format::ADMITTED` is forced at two of them, `apply` and the identity search.
> `Ambient`, `Quantum` and `Slots` each force their own beside those.

At `:679-684`, listing three routes that "reach a value without passing a forcing
site", the second of them being "an implementor writing an empty `ADMITTED` over the
default".

### Why each is wrong, measured

**The two verbs.** `grep -rn "as Format>::ADMITTED" mock/crates/ --include='*.rs'`
returns exactly two lines, `format.rs:325` and `format.rs:401`. The functions
enclosing them are `cancelling_slot` and `has_additive_identity`. `apply` is a
module; the obligation forced inside it is `<<S::Format as Format>::Slots as
Slots>::ADMITTED` at `apply.rs:355`, which is a different obligation.

**The `Slots` clause.** `Slots::ADMITTED` is forced at `slots.rs:306`, `:317` and
`:324`, and at `apply.rs:355`. None of those is beside either `Format` site, so
"beside those" states an adjacency that does not hold.

**The override.** An implementor writing `const ADMITTED: () = ();` does not miss the
forcing site. `has_additive_identity::<F>` evaluates `<F as Format>::ADMITTED` for
whatever `F` supplies, so the site fires and the constant it fires on is empty. The
distinction is the whole content of the guarantee: two routes never reach a forcing
verb, and one reaches it and finds nothing there.

### What replaces them

The two functions by name, `Slots` stated with where it is actually forced, and the
routes split two and one. The sentence then holds over the verbs for an implementor
that does not override, and says so, rather than claiming a guarantee it does not have.

## CHANGE: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/format.rs`

The rustdoc on `Format::ADMITTED` at `:179` carries the same two sentences and is the
half that ships to a reader on the registry. Same correction, same wording, because a
reader landing on one and then the other should not have to work out which is current.

## What is not touched

The `compile_fail` doctest below that rustdoc, which is correct as written: it pins
that a zero denominator is refused at a forcing site, and that is the property.
