# Source changelist: the carrier stays inside the value

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Round:** 202611130513-jy34
**Doc changelist:** `202611130513-jy34_changelist.doc.lock.md`
**Topic:** `202611130513-jy34_topic.the-carrier-stays-inside-the-value.md`

The source follows the design as the doc changelist locked it: the carrier doors go, the carrier's
type becomes unnameable outside the crate, and `Hash` and `Debug` go through the slot. Every change is
in `arvo-numeral`, its tests and its consumer crate, plus one comment in `mockspace.toml`.

## Current state

`Num<S: Sole>` stores `S::Repr`. `Sole` declares `type Repr: Machine`. The crate root re-exports
`Carried` and `Machine`. `Num::from_raw` takes `S::Repr` and `Num::raw` gives it. `Hash` hashes the
carrier and `Debug` prints it. `USize` and `ISize` build their host doors on `from_raw` and `raw`.

## Changes proposed

### CHANGE: `Sole` names the map only in its supertrait list

`src/admit.rs`. `Sole` loses `type Repr` and becomes
`Sole: DeclaredSignature<Format: Format<Slots: Carried>>`, keeping `WIDTH`, `POLICY`, `ASSERTS` and
`ADMITTED`. The blanket impl loses its `type Repr` line. `Carried` is reached as
`crate::map::Carried` inside the crate.

### CHANGE: `Carried` and `Machine` are sealed in modules the crate does not export

`src/lib.rs` drops `pub use machine::Machine` and `pub use map::Carried`. Both traits stay `pub` in
their private modules, so they can appear in `Sole`'s supertrait list without a `private_bounds`
warning and are reachable by no path from outside.

### CHANGE: `Num` stores the carrier through a private alias

`src/num.rs`. A crate-private alias `ReprOf<S>` is `<SlotsOf<S> as Carried>::Repr`; `Num<S: Sole>`
stores it, and `dispatch!` is called with it in place of `S::Repr`.

### CHANGE: the carrier doors go

`src/num.rs`. `Num::from_raw` and `Num::raw` are removed. The rung adaptation functions stay, since
`add` completes through them.

### CHANGE: the host doors build from the carrier inside the crate

`src/num.rs` gains crate-private `from_carrier` and `carrier`, the bare constructor and field read.
`src/alias.rs` builds `USize::from_host`, `USize::host`, `ISize::from_host` and `ISize::host` on them
with the bit copy, as before.

### CHANGE: `Hash` and `Debug` go through the slot

`src/num.rs`. `Hash` hashes `self.slot().index()`; `Debug` prints `self.slot()`. `Clone`, `Copy`,
`PartialEq` and `Eq` keep reading the carrier.

### CHANGE: the named surface in `mockspace.toml`

The comment on `arvo-numeral`'s `[primitive-introductions]` row names the host doors and no longer the
carrier doors, and says the map is sealed inside the crate. The row stays as it is.

### CHANGE: the tests follow the surface

`tests/common/mod.rs` `check_value` checks the round trip through the slot,
`Num::<S>::adapted(Exact::on_grid(v.slot()))` against `v`, in place of `from_raw(v.raw())`.
`tests/band_1_to_8.rs` loses `from_raw_adapts_bits_outside_the_width_by_the_policy` and gains `the_hash_and_the_debug_read_the_slot_and_not_the_carrier`, which fails while either reads the carrier.
`tests/the_plain_markers_assert.rs` loses the three `from_raw` tests and the `PAST_THE_TOP_BITS`
constant; `the_plain_markers_do_not_assert_on_what_fits` adapts its in-range position instead.

### CHANGE: the compile-fail pairs

The twins `the_raw_door_named_from_outside_compiles` and
`the_raw_door_where_a_host_integer_goes_compiles` become `the_slot_named_from_outside_compiles` and
`the_slot_where_a_slot_goes_compiles`, each reading `slot`. Four new pairs, each refused case with a
twin naming the slot instead: `the_carrier_by_projection_is_refused` (`S::Repr` with `S: Sole`),
`the_carrier_by_a_fully_qualified_projection_is_refused` (`<X as Sole>::Repr`),
`the_carrier_map_by_path_is_refused` (`arvo_numeral::Carried`) and
`the_machine_integers_by_path_is_refused` (`arvo_numeral::Machine`), with their `.stderr` files
committed. The four refusals at the `Carried` bound, the two widths, `Clamp` and `Stochastic`, keep their
place and now name the map by its sealed path, `arvo_numeral::map::Carried`, and their `.stderr` files
follow.

### CHANGE: the consumer crate

`tests/consumer/src/main.rs` stops importing `Carried` and `Machine` and stops calling `raw` and
`from_raw`. It reads `Sole`'s `WIDTH`, `POLICY`, `ASSERTS` and `ADMITTED` and `ValuePolicy::ASSERTS`
generically, round-trips a value through its slot with `adapted`, and observes the carrier's size only
through `size_of`.

### CHANGE: the mutant outputs

`mock/research/sketches/202611130513-jy34_value-mutants/` holds the runs of the four value mutants on
the new surface, with its `FINDINGS.md`.

### CHANGE: the generated documents

`docs/` regenerates by `cargo mock` once the round is closed.
