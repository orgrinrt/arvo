## CHANGE: the product step, its obligation and the operation

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/src/multiplication.rs`, new; `src/lib.rs` declares `pub mod
multiplication` and re-exports `Mul`, `is_multipliable`, `mul` and `product_position`.

`Mul<S>` is an `Operation` of arity two. `product_position::<F>(a, b)` forces `Multipliable::<F>::ADMITTED`,
whose five asserts refuse, in order, a nonzero phase, a radix other than two, a magnitude-indexed
quantum, an exponent below `-62`, and a range whose corner products, moved up by the exponent where it
is positive, leave `i128`. The step is `a * b` moved `e` binary places: up onto the grid for `e >= 0`,
down for `e < 0` as the arithmetic shift with the low `-e` bits as the remainder over `2^-e`, reduced
by its trailing zeros. A slot product or an up move past the index saturates on the product's sign. No
division anywhere. `mul::<S>` is the step composed with `adapt::<S>`. `is_multipliable::<F>` asks the
same five questions without forcing them.

## CHANGE: the shared rational oracle and the pair table move out of the addition suite

Files: `src/tests/ratio.rs` and `src/tests/table.rs`, new, registered in `src/tests/mod.rs`;
`src/addition/tests/mod.rs`, `the_signed_laws.rs`, `the_verdict/{brute_force,counts,negative_controls}.rs`.

`Ratio` moves whole and its methods become `pub(crate)`, and `Table::of(min, max, op)` builds the pair table from any binary
operation, so the product's suite reads the same oracle and the same table the sum's does rather than a
copy. The addition suite's `sums` and `stepped` build through `Table::of`. Its 40 tests stay green.

## CHANGE: the product's suite

Files: `src/multiplication/tests/{mod,the_step,the_oracle,the_laws}.rs`, new.

`the_step`: the denotation, two values multiplied with the quantum in and read back as a position, at
exponents `-8` to `8` over `Signed<3>` and `Unsigned<3>` plus `Integer<4>` and `UFixed<4, -2>`, with a
quantum-free control that disagrees somewhere and nowhere the quantum is one. The largest remainder
denominator at `2^-62`, floor and reduction on a negative product, saturation past the index, each
refusal on both sides of its boundary through `is_multipliable`, and the step and verdict bound in
`const` items.

`the_oracle`: every member pair at widths one to eight, signed and unsigned, at exponents `0`, `-3`,
`2` and `-1`, every mode, every shipped policy, four dithers for the stochastic mode, slot and overflow
verdict against `apply`'s oracle fed the exact rational position. A planted product without the
quantum agrees at a quantum of one and is reported at `2^-2` and `2^1`.

`the_laws`: wrapping associativity holds at widths three to eight on the integer grid, signed and
unsigned, and fails at every width three to seven with fraction bits. Saturating associativity holds
unsigned at three to eight and fails signed at 28, 160, 780 and 3516 triples at three to six, with the
row's witness `(7 * 7) * -1 = -7` against `7 * (7 * -1) = -8`. Distributivity holds unsigned under
wrapping and saturation at three to eight, and fails signed under saturation at 120, 1414, 13594,
119422 and 1000674 triples at widths three to seven.

Finding: the distributivity row's fails cell names widths six and seven and puts 34.52% at six. Both
probes it cites measured 34.52% at four bits, 1414 of 4096 (`93_probes/p7_signedness_breaks_the_congruence.out:23`,
`97_probes/p2_congruence_predicts_the_laws.out:209`), and 45.56% at six. The suite asserts the measured
counts, which include six and seven. The registry is not edited here: ticket
`law_row_distributivity_names_w_6_and_7_for_a_failure_measure`.
