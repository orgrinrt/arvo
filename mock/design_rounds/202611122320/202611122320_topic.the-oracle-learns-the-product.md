# Topic: the oracle learns the product

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Round:** step AR3 of the roadmap, round two of two.
**Scope:** `mock/crates/arvo-format`, a new `multiplication` module beside `addition`.

## Governing canon

`ruling::the_derivation_is_a_placement_and_the_operation_set_is_an_admission_rule` governs, as it did
for addition: an operation is admitted exactly when it is a function of the declared signature. The
laws the tests reproduce are `law::multiplicative_associativity_under_wrapping`,
`law::multiplicative_associativity_under_saturation` and
`law::distributivity_of_multiplication_over_addition`, none edited. Division is out of scope.

## The exact step

At a constant quantum `q`, two members are `(phase + a) * q` and `(phase + b) * q`, so their product
is `(phase + a)(phase + b) * q^2`, which is the member at position `(phase + a)(phase + b) * q - phase`.
Unlike the sum, the product reads the quantum, and with a phase it reads it twice over in a way that
makes the position a polynomial in the phase. The step admitted here takes the case the brief names:
radix two and zero phase, where the position is `a * b * 2^e`, `e` the quantum's exponent. That is the
product of the two slots, moved by `e` binary places: up, on the grid, for `e >= 0`; down, with a
remainder of denominator `2^-e`, for `e < 0`. No division appears: the down move is an arithmetic
shift for the slot and a mask for the remainder.

The remainder reduces to lowest terms before it is stored, so the position equals the one any other
path to the same rational builds, which is what an `Exact` compared with `==` needs.

## What the obligation refuses

Five conditions, one obligation, forced by the exact step and by the composition, and asked without
forcing by an `is_multipliable` verdict, the shape addition has.

The phase is zero. A phased product's position is `(phase + a)(phase + b) * q - phase`, a function of
the declared signature still, but not the step stated here; the refusal says so and does not pretend
the product is undefined.

The radix is two. At another radix the down move is a division by a power of that radix, which the
brief keeps out, and its remainder's denominator is not a power of two.

The quantum is constant. A magnitude-indexed family names a different value per magnitude, and the
applied map carries no magnitude coordinate, for the reason addition refuses it.

The remainder is held. `Fraction::of(num: i64, den: i64)` (`src/apply/position.rs:69`, the brief's
`:68` moved by one line) carries a positive denominator in a signed 64-bit integer, so the largest
power of two it holds is `2^62`. The quantum's exponent is therefore at least `-62`. At `-63` the
remainder's denominator would be `2^63`, which no positive signed 64-bit integer holds, so that format
is refused rather than answered wrongly.

The product is carried. The slot index is `i128` (`src/slots.rs:46`). Over a box of slots the extreme
products sit at the corners, so the obligation multiplies the four corners checked and moves each
by `max(e, 0)` places checked; any overflow refuses. The signed 64-bit range's corners give at most
`(-2^63)^2 = 2^126`, which the index holds. The unsigned 64-bit range's top corner is
`(2^64 - 1)^2`, just under `2^128`, past `i128::MAX`, so `UFixed<64, _>` is refused for the product
and `UFixed<63, _>`, whose top corner is under `2^126`, is admitted. Holding it would take a slot
coordinate wider than `i128`; that widening is a ticket, not this round.

The bound the brief asks for: a double-width exact sum at width 64, that is two signed 64-bit
products added before any adaptation, reaches `2 * 2^126 = 2^127` at `a = b = c = d = -2^63`, one
past `i128::MAX`. Nothing this round builds forms such a sum, because every composition adapts each
product before the next operation reads it, so the bound is stated and the obligation does not need
it. A fused multiply-add at width 64 would need the widening above.

## Non-members

For slots outside the range, which no member pair reaches, the step still answers: an overflowing
slot product saturates at the end of the index on the product's sign, on the grid, and an overflowing
up move saturates the same way. The answer is total, as `sum_position`'s is.

## The surface

`Mul<S>` of arity two, `product_position::<F>(a, b)`, `mul::<S>(a, b, dither)` and `is_multipliable::<F>()`,
all `const fn`, re-exported from the crate root. No reach and no associativity verdict: the reach
serves addition's translation argument, which has no counterpart for a product, and the verdict would
need a relocation law for multiplication that the canon does not state.

## Tests

The denotation, values multiplied with the quantum in and read back, at exponents on both sides of
zero and both families, with a control that leaves the quantum out and must disagree wherever the
exponent is not zero. Each refusal pinned on both sides of its boundary through `is_multipliable`.
Every member pair at widths one to eight against the apply oracle, every mode, every shipped policy,
at whole and fractional exponents. The three law rows' counts, at the cells they state. Three
compile-fail fixtures, a phased format, a magnitude-indexed quantum and the unsigned 64-bit range, each
with a twin differing only in the offending token that compiles, and the diagnostic committed.

The `Ratio` oracle and the triple table move out of the addition suite into the crate's shared test
module, since the product suite needs both, and the addition suite reads them from there.
