## CHANGE: a section states the product at a binary quantum

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, new `## Multiplication, the product of two members at a
binary quantum`, before `## Open items`, with four subsections.

The lead states the product's position, `(phase + a)(phase + b) * q - phase`, why it reads the quantum
where the sum does not, and the case the step takes: radix two and zero phase, position `a * b * 2^e`.
`### The exact step is a product moved by the exponent` states the up and down moves, the remainder
reduced to lowest terms, no division, and saturation for non-members. `### What it refuses` states the
five refusals of one obligation, forced by the step and asked by `is_multipliable`, and the 64-bit
bound: `Integer<64>` admitted at `2^126`, `UFixed<64, _>` refused, `UFixed<63, _>` admitted, and the
double-width sum at width 64 reaching `2^127`, which nothing here forms. `### The operation and the
surface` names `Mul`, `product_position`, `mul` and `is_multipliable`, and why there is no reach and no
verdict. `### The laws this section owes tests for` lists the denotation and its control, the
refusals and three compile-fail pairs, the oracle sweep at widths one to eight, and the law rows'
cells with their counts.
