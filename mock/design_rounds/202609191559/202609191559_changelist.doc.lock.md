## `crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the laws the platform-width points owe tests for

The sentence claiming no form in "the four forms the lint named below enumerates" resolves the
leading-`::` path to anything else is replaced: the alias is the pointer width wherever the leading-`::`
path resolves to the real `core`, and whether it does is the question the lint's second arm answers.

The paragraph naming four forms "of a crate reference bound to the name `core`", "the raw identifier
`r#core` included on each", as "what the leading-`::` path does not resist on its own" is replaced by
three paragraphs. The lint refuses three forms, read as tokens after comments and literals are stripped:
a module named `core`, a `use` item binding `core` anywhere in its tree (alias, unaliased last segment
with or without braces, `self` under a `core` segment), and an `extern crate` aliased `as core`, with
`r#core` counting as `core` in each. The leading `::` resists the first two on its own, since each binds
only in its own module, and they are refused for the bare spelling there. `extern crate self as core` at
the crate root reaches `::core`. A macro-expanded `extern crate self as core` is refused by the compiler.
A manifest rename to the key `core` reaches `::core` when the renamed crate re-exports the real `core`'s
contents, and is unguarded. The unguarded list names glob imports, other item kinds named `core`,
macro-expanded forms, the manifest rename and a third alias.

The closing paragraph says three forms instead of four.
