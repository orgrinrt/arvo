## CHANGE: a member is a slot the type knows is in range

A new subsection at the end of `## The slot range carries the declared width, and where it stops`,
`### A member is a slot the type knows is in range`. `Member<F>` is a slot of `F`'s range with a private
field, two doors and one accessor. `Member::<F>::constant::<B>()` is refused at build outside the range.
`Member::<F>::adapted::<S>(exact, dither)`, for a declared signature over `F`, is `adapt` returned as a
member, in range by the map's totality, which `apply/tests/totality.rs` holds; it serves a
constant-exponent quantum only, as `adapt` does. A slot on the grid and in range reaches its member
through `Exact::on_grid`. `Member::slot` reads it back. No door classifies a slot read at run time, under
`ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` and `ruling::the_ingest_door_waits_on_a_known_use`.
The guarantee is stated over its perimeter, the private field, the two doors and the copy `slot` returns,
with the test that feeds both doors the edges and the ends.

## CHANGE: the divisor's third door

`### The divisor is a type, and its zero is refused before the program runs`: three constructors in place
of two. `Divisor::<F>::member::<G>(m)` is admitted at build where `G` has `F`'s radix, constant exponent and
reduced phase, `G`'s range lies inside `F`'s, and no member of `G` names the zero, the phase not whole or
its zero slot `-n` outside `G`'s range. The range condition keeps the quotient exact, since a divisor slot
past `F`'s range saturates; the topic's appended section records it, and the sketch's `wider-range` case
fails with its own message. The paragraph on `of` points at the member door for a whole-phase range that
leaves the zero out. The sentence calling the whole-phase restriction a compromise, and its ticket
reference, are replaced: such a format divides at run time by a member of a zero-free range on its grid,
and never by a bare `Slot`.

## CHANGE: the laws the member door owes

`### The laws this section owes tests for`, under division: the zero-free range 1 to 255 on a byte's grid
divided by its own members and a byte divided by them, against the oracle at every mode and shipped
policy; both member doors fed the edges and the ends at every admitted width of both families, every slot
read back in range; `adapted` at `Exact::on_grid` returning its slot in range; the member door's verdict
on both sides of each of its five conditions; compile-fail pairs for a constant past the top and for
`member` from a range holding its zero slot, another radix, exponent or phase, and a wider range; and four
planted verdicts reported.

## CHANGE: the canon header

`ruling::the_ingest_door_waits_on_a_known_use` joins the rulings the design declares, for why a member
has no runtime classifying door.

## CHANGE: the crate summary

`README.md.tmpl`: the quotient's divisor is a constant the build checks, a slot of a format in which no
slot names the zero, or a member of a range on the same grid that leaves the zero out.

## NO CHANGE: the rest

`Divisor::constant`, `Divisor::of`, the exact step, the quotient's conditions and every other operation
stand. `Member` is a value type rather than a coordinate, so the coordinate table and the contracts table
are unchanged.

## The canon

No canon row changes. `ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` is what rules out a classifying
door, and `ruling::the_ingest_door_waits_on_a_known_use`, at `stated`, sets a known use as its bar; the
values lane, the one consumer the door was asked about, needs none. The member door admits only divisors
whose zero the build has refused, so nothing the canon governs is widened.
