# Src changelist: the room at its edge

**Date:** 2026-11-20
**Round:** `202611200013`, job `arvo-numeral-doors`.
**Doc changelist:** `202611200019_changelist.doc.lock.md`.

No source the crate ships changes: the arm's arithmetic, its bounds and its markers stand as round
`202611192051` left them. Everything below is a test.

## `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/src/room.rs`, the `bound` test module

CHANGE: `the_product_is_formed_here_at_every_negative_exponent_of_the_64_bit_widths` becomes
`the_product_is_formed_here_down_to_where_the_radix_power_passes_128_bits` and asserts the edge: the
product formed here at -127 and not at -128, at 64 bits both ranges and at 8 bits signed.

## `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/tests/common/edges.rs`

ADD: `host_bound` (the largest `k` with `m r^k` below `2^127`), `power_bound` (the largest `k` with `r^k`
a 128-bit integer), and from them `quotient_room` and `product_room`, the deepest exponent at which the
value forms each in the host's integer, clamped to the format's deepest.

## `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/tests/every_shape/quotient.rs`, `product.rs`, `tests/common/shapes.rs`

CHANGE: each family gains `room` between `half` and `deep`; `SIGNATURES_OF_A_BAND` is six exponents,
1920 signatures. The six suite files `the_quotient_at_every_shape_*` and `the_product_at_every_shape_*`
name the sixth exponent in their module docs.

## `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/tests/the_conversion_at_the_room_edge.rs`

ADD: from exponent zero at 32 and 64 bits onto the last exponent inside the host's bound and the first
past it, both source ranges onto both target ranges, radix two, ten and three, the ten signatures of
the target, 160 signatures a radix; and the room's edges written out as numbers.

## `mock/crates/arvo-numeral/tests/the_quotient_by_a_value.rs`

CHANGE: the unsigned dividends divide by `Unsigned`, 1 to 255, and by `Positive`, 1 to 127; no value is
skipped before landing. Each landing is checked to be the value or the end of the divisor's range it
passed, the quotient is checked against the oracle's by the landed slot, and the count of values landed
from past the range is asserted.
