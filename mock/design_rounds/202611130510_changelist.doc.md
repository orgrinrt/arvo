## Canon

`ruling::the_derivation_is_a_placement_and_the_operation_set_is_an_admission_rule` admits both new
operations: each position is a function of the declared signature, or of the source format and the
target signature, wherever it is defined. `ruling::never_a_runtime_check_and_one_lowered_path` is why
the divisor is a type with compile-time constructors rather than a value tested at the call. The
registry is silent on division, the divisor's zero and conversion by name, so three stances are taken
here, each with its compromise in the design: the divisor non-zero by type, conversion as a single
step across any radix pair, and, carried from the previous round, the remainder's 128-bit width.

## CHANGE: division is added

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, new `## Division, the quotient by a member that is
non-zero by type`.

The position `(phase + a) / ((phase + b) q) - phase` and its admission. `Divisor<F>` with
`Divisor::constant::<B>()`, refusing a `B` out of range or at the zero member, and `Divisor::of(slot)`,
admitted exactly when `F` has no zero member, and the compromise that a range holding the zero
divides only by constants. The non-member zero slot saturates toward positive. The step's numerator
and per-call denominator. The four conditions of the obligation: a phase that denotes (by design), a
constant quantum, the denominator at the larger range end, and the eight corners. `Div`,
`quotient_position`, `div`, `is_divisible`. The laws owed tests.

## CHANGE: conversion is added

File: `mock/crates/arvo-format/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, new `## Conversion, one member placed on another
signature's grid`.

The position `(phase_F + a) q_F / q_T - phase_T`, a single step and why a chain is the consumer's
composition. The step over `d_F V_F U_T d_T`, radixes read separately. The four conditions: both phases
denote (by design), both quanta constant, the pair's denominator, and the two range ends. `Convert`,
`converted_position`, `convert`, `is_convertible`. The laws owed tests.
