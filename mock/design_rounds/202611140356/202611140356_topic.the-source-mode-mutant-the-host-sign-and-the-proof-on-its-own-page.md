## Current state

The sweep under every source signature crosses two steps of exponent. On the step from zero onto minus
one, `arvo_mutant_convert_policy` shows that a conversion completing by the source's policy disagrees
with it, and its red run is committed. On the step from minus one onto zero the design says a conversion
that rounded by the source's mode would disagree, and no mutant shows it. The design also says the
policy mutant passes every other sweep, while the committed run holds three binaries and no more, and
it says every member past the target's range on either end is completed by the policy, which an
unsigned range, leaving only at its top, does not bear out.

`Machine`, the sealed trait every host integer and carrier is, gained `SIGNED` on this branch as a bare
`bool`. Every position takes the stack's own primitive, and `Bool` is the one `arvo-format` ships.

`DESIGN.md.tmpl` of `arvo-numeral` is 785 lines, past the 500 a file holds, and its section on what is
proved and how is about 240 of them, one subject of its own.

The generated documents under `docs/` were rendered before the last two rounds, so the published page
still names the mutants as cargo features and lacks what those rounds changed.

## Changes proposed

`arvo_mutant_convert_mode` rounds the conversion by the source's mode in place of the target's,
declared to the configuration check beside the others, with its red run committed. The policy
mutant's red run covers every conversion sweep, so the claim that it passes the rest is a record. The
design's sentence on the range-losing step names the ends a range can leave by.

`Machine::SIGNED` is a `Bool`.

The section on what is proved and how moves whole into `DEEPDIVE_PROOF.md.tmpl` of the crate, which
renders as a page of its own, and the design names it where the section stood.
