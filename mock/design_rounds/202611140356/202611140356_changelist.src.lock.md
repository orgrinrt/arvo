## CHANGE: the mode mutant

`Cargo.toml` declares `arvo_mutant_convert_mode` to the configuration check beside the other flags.
`src/ratio.rs` gains its arm in `convert`: under the flag the conversion is computed onto
`WithRounding<T, R>`, where `R` is the source's rounding, and so rounds by the source's mode where the
target's should. `Adaptation` and `WithRounding` join the file's imports from `arvo-format`.

## CHANGE: the machine integer's sign is a `Bool`

`src/machine.rs`: `Machine::SIGNED` is a `Bool`, each row `Bool::of` its sign. `src/host.rs` reads it
with `get` at the two dispatches and at the bounds of a host integer. The two compile-fail cases of
the index door quote that bound in their expected output, `tests/ui/the_index_of_nine_bits_as_a_byte_is_refused.stderr`
and `tests/ui/the_index_of_a_signed_range_as_an_unsigned_integer_is_refused.stderr`, and each takes the
new line; the refusal and its message are unchanged.

## CHANGE: the red runs

`run.sh` runs `arvo_mutant_convert_policy` over every conversion binary of the crate, each
`tests/the_conversion*.rs`, and `arvo_mutant_convert_mode` over the two binaries under every source
signature. `FINDINGS.md` gains what each shows, and `outputs/` the two runs.

## CHANGE: the generated documents

`docs/` is regenerated from the templates as they stand, which brings the published page up to the
last three rounds: the mutants as flags, the conversion on the open question, the five sweeps, and the
proof on its page.
