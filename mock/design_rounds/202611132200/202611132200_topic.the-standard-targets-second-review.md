## Changes proposed

The significand's bounds move to a range of their own that carries the slot range's obligation as it
is. The significand reads its bounds from that range and states its own `ADMITTED`, which forces the
sign and magnitude obligation and then the range's. `adapt` and the range predicate force the
significand's `ADMITTED`, so they still refuse, and `slots::is_admissible` reads the bounds without
forcing anything. Beside the new obligation stands its verdict, `is_sign_and_magnitude`, and a test
reports the wrong constructions through both verdicts without refusing the build. The generic
`is_admissible_format` answers the format's own obligation and nothing further, as the design says each
verdict does.

Every direction arm on both decimal formats gets a planted wrong mode, and the short precision is
planted on both.

Each hole carries a `FIXME` naming its ticket: `arvo_the_ieee_754_infinities_and_signed_zero_have_no_member`,
`arvo_binary128_and_decimal128_need_a_slot_ladder_past_64_bit` and
`arvo_no_arithmetic_runs_through_both_sides_of_the_ieee_754_p`. A test asserts the signed zero: the
host answers negative zero and the landing answers the one zero.

The sweep's sentences name thirteen shapes. The summaries are shortened. The crate readme names both
standards. `agrees` stays: past the range the standard's finite answer is the largest member, and
saturation is the only way the format reaches it.
