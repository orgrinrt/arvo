## CHANGE: the significand's bounds and its verdict

`src/standards/ieee754.rs`: `SignificandBounds<A, P>` implements `Slots` with the bounds and width
`Significand` had, under the default obligation. `Significand<A, P>` reads its `MIN`, `MAX` and `WIDTH`
from it and overrides `ADMITTED`: the sign and magnitude assertions, then the bounds' `ADMITTED`. The
inherent `SIGN_AND_MAGNITUDE` const goes. `is_sign_and_magnitude<A, P>() -> Bool` is the verdict beside
the obligation. The `.stderr` of the four compile-fail cases that force it are regenerated, the 128-bit
pair naming `SignificandBounds` where they named `Significand`.

## CHANGE: the holes carry their tickets

`src/standards/ieee754.rs`: the module documentation names four holes, and three `FIXME` markers name
`arvo_no_arithmetic_runs_through_both_sides_of_the_ieee_754_p` at the module,
`arvo_binary128_and_decimal128_need_a_slot_ladder_past_64_bit` at `SignificandBounds` and
`arvo_the_ieee_754_infinities_and_signed_zero_have_no_member` at `Interchange`. The module summary and
the `Attributed` summary are shortened under 80 characters.

## CHANGE: the tests

`tests/ieee754_binary_parity.rs` gains `the_standards_negative_zero_lands_on_the_one_zero` and
`a_significand_that_is_not_sign_and_magnitude_is_reported_by_its_verdict`.
`tests/ieee754_decimal_parity.rs`: `every_direction_planted` plants a wrong mode on all five directions
of both formats, and the short precision is planted on both.

## CHANGE: the obligation

the sweep's thirteen shapes.
