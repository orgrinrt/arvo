## What this crate is

A value of a declared signature: one member of the format's representable set, held alone in the
carrier the placement derives at sole occupancy. `arvo-format` says which values exist and how an exact
result is put back onto them; `arvo-placement` says how many bits one of them takes when it sits alone.
Neither holds a number. This crate does, and holds nothing else.

Every operation on a value is defined as the format's own operation on its slot, so the value never
states a behaviour of its own. Its sum is the oracle's sum, `add::<S>` in `arvo-format`, and the
contract is an equality: the slot of `a.add(b)` is `add::<S>(a.slot(), b.slot(), Dither::UNUSED)` for
every pair of members of every admitted signature. The carrier arithmetic is the mechanism that meets
that equality fast, and it is free to be anything that does.

It depends on `arvo-format` and `arvo-placement` and adds nothing to either. `no_std`, no allocation,
no feature gate, no unstable feature, and every operation a `const fn`.

## What a value signature admits

`Sole` is the bound a value is generic over. It is implemented once, over every `DeclaredSignature`
that meets three conditions, so nothing outside this crate implements it and code generic over a
value's signature bounds it by `Sole` and nothing more.

The format's slot family is on the map, which is `Signed<W>` and `Unsigned<W>` for every `W` from 1 to
64, through `Carried`. The overflow policy is a `ValuePolicy`: `Wrap`, `Saturate`, `Wrapping` and
`Saturating`. The rounding mode is a `ValueRounding`: `TowardZero`, `Floor`, `Ceil`, `HalfUp` and
`HalfEven`. All three traits are sealed, so the set is what this crate can compute and no more.
`HalfUp` here is the format's `half_up`, a tie toward positive infinity at every sign, and not the
`HALF_UP` of Java or Python: a tie at -2.5 goes to -2.

On top of those bounds, `Sole::ADMITTED` asserts that the format's quantum is constant and its phase is
zero. That is where the sum of two members is the member at the sum of their slots, which is what lets
the carrier add stand in for the format's addition. Every door forces it, so no value of another
format can be built. Integers, fixed point at any constant exponent, and scaled integers at any radix
pass; a phase or an indexed quantum is refused at build with that assertion's message.

What is refused, and why, so that each refusal reads as a decision rather than a gap:

| Refused | Where it fails | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Clamp` | `ValuePolicy` bound | With no bound to carry, clamping is saturation under another name. |
| `Stochastic` | `ValueRounding` bound | It reads a dither, and a value has nowhere to carry one. |
| A phase, an indexed quantum | `Sole::ADMITTED` | The sum of two slots is not the slot of the sum. |
| Width 0, width past 64 | `Carried` bound | No slot range, or no ladder carrier. |

## How the value holds its carrier

`Num<S: Sole>` is a `repr(transparent)` newtype over `S::Repr`, its field private. `S::Repr` comes from
the map: `Carried` names the ladder carrier for each slot family, the narrowest of 8, 16, 32 and 64 bits
that holds the width, signed exactly where the family is. Each row of the map carries a check-time
assertion that the carrier's size in bits is what `arvo_placement::derive_sole` derives for that
family's canonical point, so the map cannot drift from the placement it transcribes.

A `const fn` cannot call a trait method on `S::Repr`. So each operation is written once: it moves the
bits to the machine integer of the same size and signedness, calls that rung's function, and moves the
result back. The match it dispatches on is over constants of the instantiation, so one arm survives
monomorphisation and the backend sees one call. The move is a bit copy and the crate's one `unsafe`. It
is bounded on `Machine`, sealed to the plain machine integers, where every bit pattern is a valid value,
and guarded by an assertion that the two sizes are equal; that assertion is an ordinary one rather than
an inline `const`, because the dead arms are monomorphised as well and would fail it, and on the arm
taken it holds and folds away.

The rung functions are written once per carrier width and expanded by one macro. Below the carrier's
width two members sum without leaving the carrier, so `add` is the machine's wrapping add followed by
the adaptation onto the declared width. At the carrier's width the machine's own wrapping or saturating
add is already the answer.

## Where the host integers are named

This crate is where the carriers the placement derives meet the host's machine integers, so it is
declared beside `arvo-format` and `arvo-bits` as introducing the numeric category, and the
bare-primitive lints read that declaration as their scope. The declaration covers a named surface and
nothing else:

1. `Num`'s representation, `S::Repr`, and the map that names it: `Carried`, its rows from `Signed<W>`
   and `Unsigned<W>` to the ladder carriers, and `Machine` with the bit copy between two of them.
2. The rung arithmetic, written once per carrier width, and the dispatch onto it by the carrier's size
   and sign.
3. The doors to the carrier, `raw` and `from_raw`, and to the host, `from_host` and `host` on `USize`
   and `ISize`.
4. The slot index, a bare `i128` in `arvo-format`'s `Slot`, where `constant` takes it as a const
   parameter and the value is built from one.
5. The signatures core fixes for the traits `Num` implements, `PartialEq::eq` returning `bool` among
   them, and the crate's own tests.

Everything else is written in the stack's own types: `Bool` for every predicate, `Width` and `Policy`
from the signature, `Ordering` for the order, and `Num` itself at every position a consumer sees.

## The overflow markers

Each value policy has two markers over one `Policy`.

Under the plain spellings, `arvo_format::overflow::Wrap` and `arvo_format::overflow::Saturate`, a
result that leaves the range asserts in a debug build: in `add`, and in every door that adapts. That is
the letter of `ruling::the_overflow_panic_is_permitted_and_bounded`.

`Wrapping` and `Saturating` are declared here, implementing `arvo_format::Overflow` with the same
`POLICY`. Under them the completion is the meaning of the operation and nothing asserts in any build
profile, the way the host's own `core::num::Wrapping` and `core::num::Saturating` behave. The choice is
in the type, never in a build flag.

The assertion never lives inside the total operation. It asks a verdict, `fits_add`, which says whether
the exact sum of two members is a member, and only under a plain marker and `debug_assertions`; in a
release build, and under the second markers in every build, the branch is a constant false and clears
at lowering. The doors that adapt ask `arvo_format::panic_on_overflow` of the position they adapt, the
same verdict the format ships.

The compromise, stated: a consumer writing `Wrap` gets a debug abort on a sum that wraps. That is the
ratified row's letter, and the marker that turns it off is one word away.

## The doors

Every door is a `const fn` and every one is total or refused at build. None checks at run time.

`Num::constant::<V>()` takes a slot index as a bare `i128` const parameter and is refused at build, as
a failed constant, where `V` is not a member. `Num::adapted(exact)` ingests an `arvo_format::Exact`
through the format's own `adapt`, so a position between two slots is rounded by the signature's mode and
a position outside the range is completed by its policy. `Num::from_raw(raw)` reads carrier bits as the
member they name after the rung's adaptation onto the declared width, so it is total over every bit
pattern, and `from_raw(v.raw())` is `v` for every value.

`Num::slot` and `Num::raw` are the unwrap doors and say so: `slot` gives the member's `arvo_format::Slot`,
`raw` the carrier bits, and the carrier is the one place this crate shows a machine integer.

`USize` and `ISize` carry two more, `from_host` and `host`, taking and giving the host's `usize` and
`isize`. Their carrier is the ladder's at the pointer width and every host value is a member, so both
are bit copies.

## The operations

`add` is the sum, total, and equal to the oracle on every pair. `fits_add` is its verdict, returning
`Bool`.

`eq` returns `Bool`, and two values are equal exactly when their slots are, because the carrier is
canonical: one member, one bit pattern. `cmp` returns `core::cmp::Ordering`; `lt`, `le`, `gt` and `ge`
return `Bool`; `min` and `max` return the value. At a zero phase and a constant quantum the map from slot
to member is monotone, and the carrier is signed exactly where the slot family is, so the carrier's order
is the members' order and these read the carrier.

`Num` implements `Clone`, `Copy`, `PartialEq`, `Eq`, `Hash` and `Debug` through the carrier, written by
hand so that `S` itself is not bounded. It implements no operator trait beyond equality, no ordering
trait, no `Default` and no `From` of a host integer. An operator trait cannot be called in a `const fn`,
so the operations are the inherent ones above. The ordering traits are left out because core's
`partial_cmp` answers in a bare `Option`, which this workspace refuses in every crate that does not
introduce fallibility, and `Ord` cannot be had without it; the order is the inherent `cmp`, which a
consumer passes wherever a comparison is taken. A default would be a policy decided for the consumer,
and a conversion from a host integer is the door `from_raw` already is, named.

## Identities

`Identity<M>` has one associated constant, `IDENTITY`, and `Additive` is the one `M` this crate declares.
The call shape is `<Num<S> as Identity<Additive>>::IDENTITY`.

`Num<S>` implements it for every `S`. The constant asserts `arvo_format::has_additive_identity` of the
format before anything else, so on a format without one the refusal is a failed constant naming the
missing identity rather than a missing impl. Within what `Sole` admits zero is always slot zero and a
member, so the refusal fires only where the admission would refuse as well, and fires first.

The identity is computed from the format's own predicate rather than declared, so it is not an author's
marker that nothing checks. Associativity stays the verdict it is, `arvo_format::addition_is_associative`,
and no marker for it is declared here. The multiplicative identity waits for the product on values.

## Aliases

`Uint<BITS, A>` is a value of `UFixed<BITS, 0>` under the adaptation `A`, and `Int<BITS, A>` of
`Integer<BITS>`. `USize` and `ISize` are values of `arvo_format::points::USize` and
`arvo_format::points::ISize` under `Adapt<TowardZero, Wrap>`. The const parameters are bare integers.

`Bool` is `arvo_format::Bool`, re-exported, and not a value over a declared signature. It is the type
every predicate and verdict returns, the format's and this crate's alike: `eq`, `lt`, `fits_add` and the
format's own `panic_on_overflow` all give it. A boolean over a one-bit value would be a second type for
the same thing, and none of those would return it. A one-bit value's sum is also not how a yes or no is
read: under wrapping it is exclusive or, under saturation it is or. So a consumer finds the boolean
beside the numerals, and it is the one the stack already answers in.

## What is proved, and how

The suite is split into one test binary per width band, 1 to 8, 9 to 16, 17 to 32 and 33 to 64, so a
band's run is its own and the narrow bands stay quick.

Widths 1 to 8 are exhaustive: every signature over both slot families, both value policies under the
non-asserting markers and all five modes, every pair of members, through the value type and checked
against the oracle. The same widths are computed in each of the four rungs, which is the derived carrier
checked against every wider one, the 64-bit carrier among them. Widths 9 to 12 are computed in every
rung wide enough to carry them, the 16, 32 and 64-bit ones, both slot families, both policies, every
pair, against the oracle.

So the rungs are exhaustive at widths 1 to 12, which covers the 8-bit carrier at its full width and the
wider carriers below theirs, and the value type is exhaustive at 1 to 8. Widths 13 to 64 are sampled,
and every full-width branch of the 16, 32 and 64-bit carriers, the machine's own wrapping or saturating
add, falls among them: at width 16 alone there are four billion pairs per family and policy.

Widths 9 to 64 run through the value type on an edge lattice, each end of the range and its neighbours,
zero and its neighbours, and every single-bit value, against the oracle, plus pairs drawn from a seeded
generator whose seed is committed in the test.

`fits_add` is checked against `panic_on_overflow` on the same pairs. `from_raw(v.raw()) == v`, and
carrier equality is slot equality.

The debug assertion is observed where it lives. One test per site, `add`, `adapted` and `from_raw`, and
per plain marker, `Wrap` and `Saturate`, gives the site an input that leaves the range and expects the
site's own message; the panic is the plain markers' specified behaviour in a debug build, so these are
`#[should_panic]` tests. Each site has a twin under `Wrapping` and `Saturating` taking the same input
and completing it, and one test shows the plain markers stay quiet on what fits. The binary is compiled
only under `debug_assertions`, since a release build has no assertion to observe.

One mutant per rung, behind the committed features `mutant-8`, `mutant-16`, `mutant-32` and `mutant-64`,
removes the rung's adaptation. The tests assert agreement with the oracle under every build and expect
nothing of a mutant, so each mutant turns red the tests that run through its rung and leaves the others
green. The red output of each mutant's run is committed.

`law::additive_associativity_under_wrapping` and `law::additive_associativity_under_saturation` are
reproduced through the value type, with the registry's own counts.

Compile-fail pairs under `tests/ui/`, each beside a passing twin that differs in the offending token,
with the diagnostic committed: width 0 and 65, `constant` of a non-member, the additive identity on a
format without one, `add` across two signatures, a host integer where a value goes and a value where a
host integer goes, the field named from outside, `Clamp`, `Stochastic` and a phased format.

A consumer is a crate of its own, outside this workspace, that depends on `arvo-numeral` with no feature
line and reaches every public item: each type, trait and alias, each associated item of `Sole`,
`Carried`, `Machine` and `ValuePolicy`, each door and operation, the identity, the host doors of `USize`
and `ISize`, and the derived traits. A test builds and runs it, and it asserts what it reads.

The disassembly of `add` at 13 bits in 16 and at 64 bits beside the host's `wrapping_add` is committed as
an ad hoc reading, and called that: the bench harness cannot yet commit the disassembly of a named
function.

## What is not here

Subtraction, negation, the product, the exact accumulator and `Lane` are the next round's, and nothing
here presumes their shape. A value at shared occupancy, packed beside others, is a different placement
and not this crate's.
